L4 and L5 - cheap per dollar, useless at scale

It is also capped at CNY 100,000 per person per calendar year (S07), so USD 400,000 takes 27 years. Carrying cash is cheaper still per dollar (spread only, and the CNY 10 permit fee is waived until 2026-12-31) but limited to USD 10,000 per trip (S08), so USD 400,000 means 40 trips.
