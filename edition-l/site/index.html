Getting money out of China: what it actually costs

A US citizen with CNY in a Chinese bank faces two different problems, and they are usually confused. Tax is not the main one. The binding constraints are (1) proving the money is yours and lawful, and (2) the payments rules that decide how much can be converted. The conversion itself costs a measured fraction of one percent.

The answer in five lines

  1. Establish the route before optimising the price. A US passport holder is an overseas individual (S02 Art. 37(2)), whose purchase rule is documentary - lawful current-account RMB income plus tax vouchers (S02 Art. 13; S04). The USD 50,000 annual total is written for settlement by anyone plus purchase by a domestic individual (S03 Art. 2). That distinction decides whether USD 400,000 is a one-time file or eight years of wires.
  2. Price the wire, not the rumour. BOC charges 0.1% of the CNY amount, floored at CNY 50, capped at CNY 260, plus CNY 150 cable (S11). Bank of America charges USD 15 to receive it (S14). Those three numbers plus the spread are the whole published cost.
  3. The conversion penalty is about a fifth of one percent. BOC's own board on 2026-11-10 showed it would buy USD at 665.71 and sell at 668.51 per USD 100; half of that CNY 2.80 bracket is 0.2099% of the amount. That is the entire "conversion" cost at the counter - the tariff charges no separate conversion commission (S11 item 210107011).
  4. Do not confuse the daily fixing with a mid-market rate. The CFETS parity for the same day was 6.7167 while BOC sold at 6.6851, so the bank's offer was below the fixing. Computing a "penalty against parity" produces nonsense; measured against the bank's own bracket it does not. See F20.
  5. The real penalties are elsewhere. PRC administrative fines reach 30% of the amount for evasion or disguised FX dealing (S01 Arts. 39, 45); a property sale is taxed at 20% of the gain (S10 Art. 3(3)); and US reporting failures run to USD 16,536 non-willful or USD 165,353 willful per FBAR (S19). None of those is a conversion tax, and all of them are avoidable by paperwork.
