Repository audit

This edition was built inside an existing repository that already contains nine earlier editions, a consolidated portal and a shared coordination board. This page records what a line-by-line review found. Nothing outside edition-l/ was edited except the append-only coordination board and, at integration time, the portal's navigation.

Scope and method. Files were read directly. Rates and tariffs were re-fetched from the issuing institution and compared with what the repository publishes. Counts (HTTP links, stale board entries, duplicate grids) were produced with greps over the working tree and are reproducible from the commands quoted on the methodology page. Prior editions' own verifiers were executed and pass: no finding below disputes that their arithmetic is internally consistent; the findings concern sourcing, labelling and consistency with the documents re-read here.

Detail

A01 · Consolidated cost table leaves the FX cost blank although an official board supports measuring it

Finding. integration/costs.json writes null into fx_cost_usd, downstream_usd, documentation_usd, tax_usd and all_in_usd for all 40 rows, and integration/verify.py asserts that those fields stay null. The published BOC board gives both a bid and an offer for the same moment, so a one-way half-spread is measurable. The result is that the most-read dashboard shows the smallest component of the cost and hides the largest published one.

Where. integration/costs.json (40 rows); integration/verify.py (assertions on null fields); index.html

Official comparator. S12 ↗ S11 ↗

Recommendation. Either compute the half-spread from a timestamped board or label the column as deliberately unmeasured. This edition does the former and states the formula.

A02 · 'Cash ask' label and unstated snapshot time in the root executive summary

Finding. EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md describes CNY 669.03 per USD 100 as 'BOC's observed 2026-11-10 cash ask'. On the board these figures come from, the cash and spot selling rates for USD are identical, so 'cash' adds no information; and the time of observation is not given in the sentence itself. The same board showed CNY 668.51 at 17:39:58, a 0.08% move within the day.

Where. EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md (most-important-result section)

Official comparator. S12 ↗

Recommendation. Say 'selling rate observed at HH:MM China time' and drop 'cash' for USD.

A03 · Four different same-day USD rates are published across editions with no reconciliation

Finding. Searches over the data files return CNY 669.19, 669.03, 669.29 (6.6929) and 6.6919 for the same 2026-11-10 session, and this edition's fresh fetch gives 668.51 at 17:39:58. Each was correct when captured, but a reader comparing editions sees inconsistent prices for one day.

Where. edition-h/data/*, edition-i/data/*, edition-j/data/*, integration/costs.json

Official comparator. S12 ↗

Recommendation. Show the capture time next to every rate and never compare two grids captured at different times.

A04 · The USD 50,000 tier is described without saying whose purchase it covers

Finding. The consolidated evidence entry describes S03 as the 'USD50,000 facilitation tier'. The rule itself applies the annual total to settlement by any individual and to FX purchase by a domestic individual (S03 Art. 2). A US-citizen reader can reasonably conclude that a passport holder has a USD 50,000 purchase allowance; the text does not say that.

Where. integration/build.py (S03 scope string); integration/costs.html wording; several archived editions

Official comparator. S03 ↗ S02 ↗

Recommendation. State the two limbs separately and add the overseas-individual route (S02 Art. 13; S04) beside them.

A05 · A use restriction on purchased FX is asserted without a source that supports it in this review

Finding. Edition J's quota method lists as a drawback that purchased FX 'cannot include overseas property, securities or insurance' and cites its own S08. That specific source was not re-fetched here and no source read in this session contains that restriction, so the claim is neither confirmed nor refuted - it is unverified and should be labelled as such.

Where. edition-j/data/methods.json (M02 cons)

Official comparator. S03 ↗ S02 ↗

Recommendation. Re-retrieve the instrument that imposes the use restriction before repeating it as fact.

A06 · 368 plain-HTTP external links remain in archived editions

Finding. Counted by directory: edition-b 82, edition-d 76, edition-g 45, edition-f 43, edition-j 37, edition-i 35, dossier 34, edition-c 8, docs 4, edition-e 2, edition-h 2. The consolidated verifier enforces HTTPS-only for its own pages, so the repository applies two different link standards.

Where. edition-b/** through dossier/** (generated pages)

Official comparator. S01 ↗ S11 ↗

Recommendation. Upgrade archived links to HTTPS or mark them as historical. This edition uses HTTPS only.

What the prior editions got right

  • No invented numbers. Across editions B-J the unpriced components are consistently blank rather than zero, and the earlier audit pages already flag the personal-fact gap. That discipline is preserved here.
  • The 2026 virtual-currency repeal claim is correct. Prior editions state that Yinfa [2026] No. 42 repealed the 2021 notice; the source text confirms it (S09, final clause).
  • The section 988 nuance is correct. Prior editions warn that the USD 200 personal-transaction rule is not a universal allowance; S15 (e)(3) supports that.
  • The 2004/2014 staging conflict is handled correctly. Prior editions flag that Art. 6 of the 2004 measures was superseded; S06 VI.2 confirms the staging was cancelled.
