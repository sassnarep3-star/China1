S02Measures for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange (PBOC Order [2006] No. 3) ↗People's Bank of ChinagovernmentArts. 2, 7, 9, 12, 13, 14, 20, 22, 26, 34, 37Domestic vs overseas individuals are treated differently (Arts. 2, 37); splitting a transaction to evade limits and false documents are prohibited (Art. 7); an annual total applies to settlement and to domestic-individual FX purchase (Art. 9); an overseas individual may buy FX and remit lawful current-account RMB income with ID plus supporting documents (Art. 13); overseas individual's proceeds of selling domestic commercial housing may be purchased and remitted with SAFE approval (Art. 22); lawful property transfer abroad follows the property-transfer rules (Art. 26); overseas individuals are passport holders, i.e. a US citizen is an 'overseas individual' (Art. 37(2)).

S09Notice on Further Preventing and Handling Risks Related to Virtual Currency and Related Activities (Yinfa [2026] No. 42) ↗PBOC and seven other authoritiesgovernmentSections 1, 3(6), 6(18)-(19); final repeal clause; effective 2026-02-06Virtual currency has no legal-tender status; exchange between fiat currency and virtual currency carried on inside mainland China, and related intermediation, pricing and tokenisation activity, are illegal financial activity that is strictly prohibited; financial institutions may not provide account, transfer or settlement services for it; the 2021 notice (Yinfa [2021] No. 237) is repealed and replaced by this notice; civil acts contrary to public order are void and losses fall on the investor.

S1526 U.S.C. section 988 - Treatment of certain foreign currency transactions ↗U.S. Government Publishing Office (2024 edition of the U.S. Code)governmentSubsections (a)(1), (b), (c)(1)(C), (e)Disposing of nonfunctional currency is a section 988 transaction and currency gain or loss is ordinarily ordinary income or loss (subsection (c)(1)(C)); for an individual's personal transaction the general rules do not apply and no gain is recognised from exchange-rate changes - but that exclusion does not apply if the gain would exceed USD 200 (subsection (e)(1)-(2)); 'personal transaction' excludes a transaction to the extent allocable expenses meet section 162 or section 212 (subsection (e)(3)), so a USD 200 allowance cannot be assumed for every holding.
