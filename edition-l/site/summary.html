Executive summary

The question was "what is the least-tax way to get CNY out of China". The honest answer is that there is no conversion tax to optimise. There is a measured spread, a short list of published bank charges, a payments-policy gate, and a set of reporting duties whose penalties are far larger than the transfer cost.

Bottom line for a US citizen with a CNY balance. The cheapest published route is a documented purchase and single outward wire: about $68.42 on $10,000.00 (0.68%) falling to $915.77 on $400,000.00 (0.23%) because the commission caps at CNY 260. Whether that route can carry the whole balance is a documentation question (S02 Art. 13; S04), not a tax question. If the bank instead applies the domestic-individual annual total, the same USD 400,000 needs 8 wires and costs $1,450.09.

Decision order

  1. Classify the balance, not the currency. Ask the bank, in writing, whether it treats you as an overseas individual and which current-account item the money is. Everything downstream depends on the answer (F01, F02).
  2. Assemble the evidence before asking for a quote. Contract or other transactional proof, plus the China tax record or withholding voucher (S02 Art. 13; S04). A quote you cannot fund is not information.
  3. Get executable quotes on the same day for the same amount from BOC, your current bank, and any provider you are eligible for. Compare amount received, not headline rate (S12 disclaimer).
  4. Choose cost allocation deliberately. SHA leaves correspondent deductions in the amount received; OUR moves them to you at a known published price at BOC (USD 15 or 25) and an undisclosed pre-collection at HSBC (CNY 200) (F18).
  5. Do the reporting in the same year. FBAR by 15 April with an automatic extension to 15 October (S16); Form 8938 with the return; Form 3520 for a large foreign gift (S18).
  6. Only then consider a capital-account route. Emigrant transfer, inheritance, or a property sale are entitlements with their own tests (S05, S06, S02 Art. 22), not faster wires.

What would change this answer

  • The source of the balance. Salary, a property sale, an inheritance, a dividend and a gift each have a different route and a different tax result. No personal fact was supplied, so no determination is made (F22).
  • The bank's classification of you. Overseas individual versus domestic individual changes the applicable purchase rule and the amount of paperwork (F01).
  • The day. The BOC selling rate for USD moved from 669.19 to 668.51 per USD 100 on 2026-11-10 across the snapshots recorded in this repository. On USD 400,000 that is about USD 400 of movement - more than the entire fixed-fee component (F19).
  • Whether the balance was ever reported in the US. If the Chinese account was unreported, the dominant cost is not the transfer at all but the FBAR and Form 8938 exposure (S16, S17, S19).
  • Whether you have genuinely emigrated and cancelled PRC household registration. That is the gate for the large capital-account route (S05 Art. 8; F10).
What this edition does not do. It does not decide your eligibility, does not quote a live rate, does not compute a tax on an unknown gain, and does not certify that a branch will accept a file. It prices what is published and marks the rest blank.
