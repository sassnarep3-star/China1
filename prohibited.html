Prohibited and high-risk

Six routes that appear in every search result

These are not options. They are here because they are widely recommended, and because the penalty attaches to the whole amount rather than to any gain — which makes them the most expensive things on this site by a very wide margin.

The arithmetic that settles it A lawful route costs you a spread and some professional fees, both of which scale with the amount. A prohibited route exposes you to 30% of the amount, or 30–100% if serious, plus confiscation. The expected value is negative at any plausible detection probability. There is no balance size at which "clever" beats "documented".

Why the crypto route deserves its own warning

The advice you will find online almost always cites 银发〔2021〕237号 — the "924 notice". That document was repealed on 2026-02-06 by 银发〔2026〕42号.

The replacement is not more permissive. It keeps virtual-currency business activity an illegal financial activity, bars financial institutions and non-bank payment institutions from providing account opening, fund transfer or clearing and settlement services for it, provides that individuals' virtual-currency investment acts offending public order are void with losses borne by the investor, and adds new prohibitions on real-world-asset tokenisation and RMB-referenced stablecoins.

Note the two layers of failure The obvious failure is that the counterparty is engaged in an illegal financial activity. The second, quieter one is that the payment leg — RMB out, foreign currency in — is 变相买卖外汇 under FX Regulations Art.45 in its own right, regardless of what instrument sits in the middle. Putting a token in the path does not change the character of the currency exchange. See S14 and S01.

And the dead end that isn't even illegal

"Just send it to a relative in China." Art.67 of the 2020 Guidelines treats a transfer between a 境内个人 account and a 境外个人 account as a cross-border transaction, so it must satisfy the same outward current-account test as any other remittance. It is not a shortcut; it is the same queue with an extra form.

And if the point of the exercise is to consume the relative's USD 50,000 allowance, it stops being a dead end and becomes 分拆 under Art.62 — risk letter first, then watch-list designation for the current year plus two more, with full documentary review of every subsequent transaction. See S03.

What to do instead Go back to Channels and match the money to its source. If no row matches, the honest conclusion is that no lawful channel reaches it — and the correct response is to leave it, spend it in China (C09/C20), or get specific advice. Not to buy a workaround.
