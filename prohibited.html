Named, not explained. Prohibited Prohibited unverified 0% on the conversionCharged as unauthorised FX trading under Art. 45: warning, confiscation of illegal gains, fine of up to 30% of the amount, 30%–100% if serious, plus criminal liability.

Named, not explained. Prohibited Prohibited unverified 0% on the conversionCharged as 逃汇 under Art. 39: order to repatriate plus a fine of up to 30% of the amount, 30%–100% if serious, criminal liability where it is a crime.
Source: S01
 No US tax arises on moving your own capital. Separately, fabricated invoicing can create US exposure under US false-statement rules, which are outside the scope of the sources retrieved here.
Source: S24
 FBAR / Form 8938 as applicable.
Source: S17 S20
 Art. 39 evasion: up to 30% of the amount, 30%–100% if serious. Art. 12 requires a genuine, lawful transaction basis for every current-account payment, and Art. 48 separately penalises submitting inauthentic documents (up to RMB 50,000 for an individual).
Source: S01

  • Circumvents the quota by pretending the transaction is current-account.
  • Directly targets the authenticity review banks are required to perform (Art. 12).
  • Escalates to 逃汇 with a 30%–100% fine range.
  • Customs and tax data are cross-checked against declared invoices.

 Treated as illegal financial activity: strictly prohibited and subject to banning, with criminal liability where it constitutes a crime. Financial institutions and payment providers may not process it, which is why onshore bank rails to an exchange do not exist.
Source: S13
  • None that are lawful onshore. The onshore and offshore legs cannot both be done through regulated PRC rails.
  • Exchanging legal tender for virtual currency is illegal financial activity in the PRC.
  • Offshore exchanges serving PRC residents over the internet are likewise illegal.
  • Banks and payment institutions are barred from processing it, so it relies on unlicensed counterparties.
  • Civil acts are void and losses are borne by the party, so there is no recourse if the counterparty defaults.

This is expressly one of the six prohibitions the individual affirms on the FX purchase application. Prohibited Prohibited unverified 0% on the conversionEnforcement is administrative, not tax-based.  FBAR does not reach directly held foreign real estate, but Form 8938 reporting can attach if the property is held through an entity or account.
