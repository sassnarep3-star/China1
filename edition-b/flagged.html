FX Regulations Art. 52(2) defines a 境内个人 (domestic individual) as a PRC citizen, or a foreign national continuously resident in China for one year (excluding diplomats and international-organisation representatives). Huifa [2007] No.1 Art. 2 applies the USD 50,000 annual quota to settlement by any individual and to FX purchase by 境内个人 specifically, while Art. 13 governs a 境外个人 converting lawful RMB current-account income in China. Both the quota route (M01) and the foreign-individual income-conversion route (M03/M04 and Art. 13) are documented. No single answer is asserted for the reader. A US citizen who is a 境外个人 with lawful RMB income in China is on a materially different (and in some respects easier) footing than a PRC citizen subject to the quota. Getting this classification wrong invalidates the whole plan.

What a blank means

A cell reading unverified is not a missing datum to be filled in later by guesswork. It is an assertion that the official record does not state a number, or that no official page could be retrieved for it. Filling those cells is exactly how the bad advice circulating on this topic is produced.

The four blank value families this project deliberately left empty:

  • Retail CNY/USD spread and wire fees — commercial, unpublished, and moving. No official schedule exists.
  • PRC exit tax on cancelling household registration — a tax settlement is required; whether it operates as an exit tax is unresolved.
  • Treaty interest rate — largely moot because savings interest is already exempt.
  • Penalty provision for virtual-currency conversion — the activity is prohibited; mapping it to a specific FX fine is legal characterisation, not a sourced fact.

What this site is. A documented evidence pack. Every figure traces to an official government source listed on the Sources page. Where no official source confirmed a value, the cell says unverified and the item appears on Flagged for review. Nothing was inferred, estimated or filled from memory.

What it is not. It is not tax, legal or investment advice, and it is not a plan for your specific facts. Classification as a domestic vs foreign individual, prior-year filing history, and willfulness are decisive and cannot be determined from this dataset. Consult a qualified adviser.
