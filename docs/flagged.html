F01 — The bank's retail CNY-to-USD conversion spread is not fixed by any official instrument

material value-left-blank

What is unresolved

The Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration Art.27 provides only that the renminbi exchange rate follows a managed floating regime based on market supply and demand, and that the foreign exchange authority is responsible for its implementation. No circular in this register sets a retail buy-sell spread or a maximum conversion charge for individuals. Every published percentage on this site therefore excludes the conversion spread, and the spread is shown as blank rather than estimated.

Where it appears

  • penalty.html cost layer 1 and 3
  • executive-summary.html

How to resolve it

Obtain the current published CNY and USD buy and sell rates from the handling bank, or from the China Foreign Exchange Trade System central parity publication, and compute the spread for the actual transaction size.

F02 — Two official definitions of 'domestic individual' disagree about a foreigner living in China

material conflict-between-official-sources

What is unresolved

Regulation Art.52(2) defines a domestic individual to include a foreign citizen who has continuously resided in China for one full year, and an overseas individual as a foreign citizen residing outside China. Measures Art.37(2) defines an overseas individual as any foreign citizen presenting a passport, home-return permit or compatriot permit, with no residence test. The Measures are the later and more specific instrument and are the ones SAFE branch guidance applies, but the conflict is unresolved on the face of the texts and determines which remittance tier applies: Art.15(1) of the Detailed Rules lets an overseas individual remit FX abroad on identity document alone, while Art.14 subjects a domestic individual to a USD 50,000 per day ceiling on the same transaction.

Where it appears

  • individual-channels.html routes R01, R04
  • flagged.html

How to resolve it

Confirm the classification with the handling bank's FX compliance desk in the specific branch, and in a material case seek written confirmation from the local SAFE branch.

F09 — Two official SAFE sources disagree on whether an overseas individual needs SAFE approval to remit property sale proceeds

material conflict-between-official-sources

What is unresolved

Huifa [2006] No.47 Art.5 requires the SAFE branch where the property is located to review and confirm the FX purchase application, the transfer contract and the tax-paid certificate before the proceeds may be converted and remitted. The SAFE Henan branch's 2018 service guidance states that an overseas individual realising lawfully owned domestic commercial property needs no SAFE application and faces no time limit, and may deal directly with the bank. Measures Art.22 still says the purchase of FX and remittance of proceeds is subject to SAFE approval. The 2025 operational guidelines issued with Huifa [2025] No.43 set out a bank-side document checklist consistent with the Henan position.

How to resolve it

Confirm with the handling bank which procedure applies in the specific city, and obtain the final text of Huifa [2025] No.43 to establish whether Art.5 of the 2006 notice has been superseded.

F10 — There is no express channel for a non-immigrant overseas individual to transfer general property out of China

material regulatory-gap

What is unresolved

Measures Art.26 routes an overseas individual's outward transfer of legitimate domestic property to the Interim Measures for the Transfer of Individual Property Abroad. Those Interim Measures cover only two situations: emigration transfer, which requires public-security proof of cancellation of Chinese household registration and a settlement-abroad certificate, and inheritance transfer, which requires an estate inherited from a decedent. A US citizen who never held a PRC hukou and has no inheritance has no route under either limb. Deposits, investment returns and general savings that are not current-account income therefore fall outside the express channels, which is the structural reason the trapped-balance problem exists.

Where it appears

  • executive-summary.html
  • asset-channels.html routes R05, R06

How to resolve it

This is a gap in the scheme rather than an unresolved question. The practical responses are the routes that do exist: current-account income conversion, asset monetisation through a sale, or a corporate or trade structure.

F11 — The 1984 treaty names Chinese taxes that no longer exist in that form

minor drafting-anachronism

What is unresolved

Art.2 of the Agreement names the individual income tax, the income tax concerning joint ventures with Chinese and foreign investment, the income tax concerning foreign enterprises and the local income tax. Those instruments were superseded by the 2008 Enterprise Income Tax Law and the 2018 amendment to the Individual Income Tax Law. Coverage of the current taxes relies on Art.2(2), which extends the Agreement to taxes imposed after signature that are substantially similar, and on the competent authorities notifying each other of substantial changes. Any treaty claim should address this expressly.

Where it appears

  • tax-treaty.html

How to resolve it

Confirm with the competent authority, or rely on Art.2(2) with a documented substantially-similar analysis.

F17 — An overseas individual holding a Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card appears to lose the Art.13 treatment

material conflict-within-official-guidance

What is unresolved

The SAFE Xiamen branch guidance states that an overseas individual who holds a Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card of the People's Republic of China is subject to the annual facilitation quota when purchasing foreign exchange. Read against Measures Art.37(2), which classifies any foreign citizen with a passport as an overseas individual, and Art.13, which exempts legitimate current-account income from the quota, the guidance creates a distinction that turns on the identity document presented rather than on residence. The same guidance confirms that an overseas individual converting earned income does not complete the Individual FX Purchase Application form.

Where it appears

  • individual-channels.html routes R01 and R02

How to resolve it

A permanent resident should present a passport rather than the permanent resident card when converting earned income, and should confirm the treatment with the branch before transacting.

F19 — Current central parity and market exchange rates are not stated

minor value-left-blank

What is unresolved

No rate is quoted anywhere on this site. Fashi [2019] No.1 Art.11 requires conversion at the central parity rate published by the China Foreign Exchange Trade System or a PBOC-authorised institution on the date of discovery, and Art.16 of the Individual Income Tax Law requires foreign-currency income to be converted at the RMB central parity rate. Both are rules about which rate to use, not a rate itself, and rates move daily.
