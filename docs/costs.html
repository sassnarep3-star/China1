Quote-comparison methodology

For the same total CNY debit, request the actual USD amount credited after all charges. Record the quote timestamp, rate direction (CNY per USD or USD per CNY), sending fee, intermediary allocation and receiving fee. Do not deduct a fee twice if it is already included in the net quote.

Effective CNY per USD = total CNY debited ÷ actual USD credited. Lower is better for the same amount and timing. This is an analytical definition, not an official tariff. Tax liabilities must be modeled separately by year and income category; do not add historical taxes already paid as if charged again by the wire.

No live quote was requested because amount, bank, beneficiary and eligibility are unknown. No hypothetical numerical example is presented as a factual price.
