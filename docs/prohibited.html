Prohibited and high-risk channels

5 channels in this category. Each is stated with its legal basis, its limit where one is fixed by an official instrument, its tax cost, the documents it requires, and its pros and cons.

Documented for completeness and for avoidance. Every channel here is unlawful, and each carries published administrative penalties and a defined criminal threshold.

Underground banking R29

地下钱庄

verified Severe Prohibited
FieldValue
Legal basisProhibited by Measures Art.30 and Regulation Art.45; criminal under Fashi [2019] No.1 Art.2 and Criminal Law Art.225(4)
How it worksThe customer pays RMB to a domestic account controlled by the operator and an associated party credits foreign currency to the customer's offshore account. No cross-border movement of the same funds occurs, which is why the arrangement is described as disguised buying and selling of FX.
Stated limitNot applicable. S03 S01 S12 S10 S11 S09
Chinese taxNot applicable. The arrangement is unlawful and no tax treatment is asserted.
US taxNot applicable as a lawful channel. Receipt of funds through such a channel raises US money-laundering and reporting exposure independent of the Chinese offence.
DocumentationNot applicable.

Pros

  • Pro. None. Listed only so that the reader can recognise and avoid the channel

Cons

  • Con. Regulation Art.45: warning, confiscation of illegal gains and a fine of up to 30% of the illegal amount, rising to between 30% and 100% in serious circumstances, with criminal liability where a crime is constituted
  • Con. Fashi [2019] No.1 sets the criminal threshold at an illegal business amount of RMB 5,000,000 or illegal gains of RMB 100,000, and 'especially serious' at RMB 25,000,000 or RMB 500,000
  • Con. Published fines are large relative to the amounts moved: RMB 24,970,000 on RMB 312,000,000 of private FX purchase used to buy overseas property; RMB 1,300,000 on RMB 10,817,900 moved through a Macau casino underground bank; RMB 968,500 on RMB 13,835,800
  • Con. Offenders are placed on the SAFE watch list and the penalty is entered into the PBOC credit-reference system, which damages future banking, borrowing and FX access
  • Con. No legal recourse if the operator absconds with the funds, since the underlying contract is unlawful

Sources: S03 S01 S12 S10 S11 S09

Splitting transfers across multiple individuals' annual quotas R30

分拆购汇（蚂蚁搬家）

verified Severe Prohibited
FieldValue
Legal basisProhibited by Measures Art.7; penalised under Regulation Art.39
How it worksSeveral individuals each use their own USD 50,000 annual quota to purchase FX and remit it to the same overseas account, so that a single beneficial owner moves an amount far above any one quota.
Stated limitNot applicable. S01 S03 S09 S10 S11
Chinese taxNot applicable.
US taxWhere US persons lend their identity to such a scheme, they carry their own FBAR and Form 8938 exposure on accounts over which they have signature authority, and potential aiding exposure.
DocumentationNot applicable.

Pros

  • Pro. None. Listed only so that the reader can recognise and avoid the channel

Cons

  • Con. Measures Art.7 expressly prohibits evading quota supervision by splitting and prohibits using false commercial documents or vouchers to evade authenticity review
  • Con. Regulation Art.39: SAFE orders repatriation within a time limit and imposes a fine of up to 30% of the evaded amount, rising to between 30% and 100% in serious circumstances, with criminal liability where a crime is constituted
  • Con. Published penalties: 34 individuals' quotas and USD 2,446,200 moved, fined RMB 830,000; 102 individuals' quotas and CAD 6,753,600 moved, fined RMB 1,727,800; 173 individuals' quotas and CAD 12,056,500 moved, fined RMB 3,009,000; 21 individuals' quotas and USD 1,049,600 moved, fined RMB 330,000; 32 individuals' quotas and USD 1,582,500 moved, fined RMB 536,400
  • Con. A bank that facilitates the splitting is also fined: one bank was fined RMB 1,000,000 for processing split purchases and payments for a client using 303 individuals' annual quotas
  • Con. The individuals whose quotas were used are themselves exposed, and every offender is placed on the SAFE watch list and in the PBOC credit-reference system

Sources: S01 S03 S09 S10 S11

Cryptocurrency purchase in China and offshore sale R31

境内购买虚拟货币后境外出售

verified Severe Prohibited
FieldValue
Legal basisProhibited by Yinfa [2021] No.237
How it worksCNY is used to acquire a virtual currency through a peer-to-peer or exchange channel and the asset is then sold offshore for USD.
Stated limitNot applicable. S16
Chinese taxNot applicable. No lawful tax treatment arises from a prohibited activity.
US taxNot applicable as a lawful channel. A US person holding convertible virtual currency has separate US reporting and taxation exposure.
DocumentationNot applicable.

Pros

  • Pro. None. Listed only so that the reader can recognise and avoid the channel

Cons

  • Con. Para 1(2): fiat-to-crypto exchange, crypto-to-crypto exchange, acting as central counterparty, providing information intermediary or pricing services, token issuance financing and crypto derivatives are all illegal financial activities, strictly prohibited and to be banned, with criminal liability where a crime is constituted
  • Con. Para 1(3): overseas exchanges serving mainland residents over the internet are equally illegal
  • Con. Para 1(4): civil acts of investing in virtual currency that offend public order and good morals are void and losses are borne by the participant, so there is no legal remedy for a failed or fraudulent trade
  • Con. Para 4(9): banks and non-bank payment institutions must not open accounts, transfer funds or provide clearing and settlement for such activity, so the on-ramp itself is closed
  • Con. Stablecoins including Tether are expressly named as having no legal-tender status

Sources: S16

Fabricated trade background and false commercial documents R32

虚构贸易背景对外付汇

verified Severe Prohibited
FieldValue
Legal basisProhibited by Regulation Arts.12 and 39; Measures Art.7
How it worksFalse or invalid contracts, invoices or bills of lading are presented to a bank to justify an outbound payment as a current-account trade settlement.
Stated limitNot applicable. S03 S01 S09 S10 S11
Chinese taxNot applicable.
US taxNot applicable as a lawful channel.
DocumentationNot applicable.

Pros

  • Pro. None. Listed only so that the reader can recognise and avoid the channel

Cons

  • Con. Regulation Art.12 requires current-account FX receipts and payments to have a genuine and lawful transaction basis, and Art.39 penalises evasive transfer of capital abroad by deceit with a fine of up to 30% of the evaded amount, rising to between 30% and 100% in serious circumstances
  • Con. Published corporate penalties: USD 92,858,000 paid out on false bills of lading, fined RMB 37,340,000; USD 27,349,000, fined RMB 9,602,000; USD 17,726,400, fined RMB 5,978,800; USD 15,656,200, fined RMB 5,098,400; USD 9,555,000, fined RMB 3,097,400; USD 6,190,000, fined RMB 2,136,500
  • Con. Prepayment for goods that never arrive was separately penalised: USD 2,560,000 paid as advance payment with no delivery, fined RMB 837,200
  • Con. Banks that process payments against false bills of lading are also fined under Art.47, and one was fined and had RMB 800,000 confiscated
  • Con. Penalty information is entered into the PBOC credit-reference system

Sources: S03 S01 S09 S10 S11

Overseas card cash-out through fictitious point-of-sale transactions R33

境外违规刷卡套现

verified High Prohibited
FieldValue
Legal basisMeasures Art.16; Regulation Art.39
How it worksA mainland card is used abroad for transactions that are not genuine consumption, generating cash or an offshore credit and moving capital out under the cover of card spending.
Stated limitNot applicable. S09 S01 S03
Chinese taxNot applicable.
US taxNot applicable as a lawful channel.
DocumentationNot applicable.

Pros

  • Pro. None. Listed only so that the reader can recognise and avoid the channel

Cons

  • Con. SAFE's 2022 circular records an individual who made 188 overseas card transactions totalling CAD 1,222,000, found to have evaded FX supervision under Art.16 of the Measures and fined RMB 325,600 under Art.39 of the Regulation
  • Con. The penalty is entered into the PBOC credit-reference system and the individual is placed on the SAFE watch list
  • Con. Genuine consumption by card is lawful; the violation is the fictitious character of the transactions, which is what monitoring models target

Sources: S09 S01 S03
