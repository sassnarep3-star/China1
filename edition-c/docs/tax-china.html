The provisions that gate the conversion

  • Art.13(1) of the Detailed Rules makes a tax voucher part of the evidence an overseas individual must produce to convert legitimate current-account income. S02
  • Art.15 of the Individual Income Tax Law requires the registration authority to verify the individual income tax paid certificate before registering a transfer of immovable property, and the market entity registration authority to verify it before registering an equity transfer. Tax cannot be deferred past the point of transfer. S13
  • Art.13 paragraph 5 requires a taxpayer who cancels Chinese household registration because of emigration to complete tax clearance before cancellation. This is the gate on the emigration property transfer route. S13
  • Art.8 permits adjustment plus interest for non-arm's-length related-party dealings, for non-distribution by an enterprise in a markedly low-tax jurisdiction controlled by a resident individual, and for arrangements lacking reasonable commercial purpose. S13
  • Art.49 of the EIT Implementation Regulations makes management fees between enterprises, and rent and royalties between operating units of the same enterprise, non-deductible. This is the constraint on the service-fee and royalty routes. S14
  • STA and SAFE Announcement 2021 No.19 requires tax filing before a single outbound payment above USD 50,000 equivalent, once per contract before the first such payment, with reinvestment of legitimate direct-investment income in China exempt. S15
