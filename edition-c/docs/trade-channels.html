Trade and current-account channels

3 channels in this category. Each is stated with its legal basis, its limit where one is fixed by an official instrument, its tax cost, the documents it requires, and its pros and cons.

Channels built on a genuine transaction in goods or services. The Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration provides that the State does not restrict current-account international payments and transfers.

Tourism shopping trade settlement by an overseas individual R13

境外个人旅游购物贸易方式项下结汇

verified Low Very narrow
FieldValue
Legal basisDetailed Rules Art.9(3)
How it worksAn overseas individual settles foreign exchange earned under the tourism shopping trade mode against a valid identity document and a personal tourism shopping customs declaration.
Stated limitNone stated beyond the customs declaration requirement. S02
Chinese taxDepends on the trader's status and the goods; not determined by this route.
US taxBusiness income is US taxable.
DocumentationValid identity document; personal tourism shopping customs declaration.

Pros

  • Pro. A named, narrow current-account route with a simple document set

Cons

  • Con. Tied to a specific customs supervision mode and to declared goods
  • Con. Small in practice and unsuited to material balances
  • Con. Not a route for converting an existing deposit; it settles trade receipts

Sources: S02

Export of goods, receiving USD proceeds R22

货物出口收汇

verified Low for genuine trade; severe for fabricated trade High structurally; requires a real business
FieldValue
Legal basisRegulation Arts.5, 12 and 13; Detailed Rules Arts.9 and 25
How it worksA Chinese exporter ships goods and receives payment in USD from the overseas buyer. Current-account FX receipts may be retained or sold to a financial institution operating settlement and sale of FX, and the transaction is processed against genuine commercial documents.
Stated limitNo individual annual quota applies to trade FX receipts and payments through an FX settlement account, which is administered as an institutional account. S03 S02 S09 S10
Chinese taxVAT on exported goods is zero-rated subject to State Council exceptions, so export sales generally carry no output VAT and may attract refunds of input VAT. Corporate income tax at 25% on the profit, or the preferential rate where the entity qualifies.
US taxThe US buyer's cost is generally deductible; the Chinese seller's profit is US taxable to its US owners with a foreign tax credit.
DocumentationSales contract; commercial invoice; customs declaration; transport documents; the bank reconciles the receipt against the declaration.

Pros

  • Pro. Creates USD abroad directly, with no CNY-to-USD conversion step and no SAFE quota at all
  • Pro. The cleanest structural answer: the money never needs to leave China because it is earned outside it
  • Pro. Zero-rated VAT on exports makes the goods channel tax-efficient on the indirect tax side

Cons

  • Con. Requires a real export business with goods, logistics and an overseas customer
  • Con. Fabricated trade backgrounds are the most heavily fined pattern in the SAFE circulars, with corporate penalties of RMB 37.34 million, RMB 9.602 million and RMB 5.9788 million published for fictitious bills of lading
  • Con. Working capital, customs and product compliance are ongoing costs
  • Con. Does nothing for money already sitting in a Chinese account except by generating new export revenue

Sources: S03 S02 S09 S10

Export through an agent by an individually-owned business R23

个体工商户委托代理出口收结汇

verified Low for genuine trade Medium
FieldValue
Legal basisDetailed Rules Art.9(2), as amended by Huifa [2016] No.13
How it worksAn individually-owned business entrusts an enterprise holding foreign trade operating rights to export on its behalf. It may receive and settle the FX through its own FX settlement account, and settlement is processed against the export agency contract and transport documents issued by the logistics company.
Stated limitNo individual annual quota applies; the FX settlement account is managed as an institutional account. S02 S13
Chinese taxBusiness income at 5% to 35% progressive rates; VAT and surcharges on the goods.
US taxBusiness income, US taxable with a foreign tax credit.
DocumentationExport agency contract or agreement with the agent enterprise; transport documents issued by the logistics company, per the 2016 amendment replacing the earlier customs declaration requirement.

Pros

  • Pro. Lighter than forming a company while still accessing the institutional-account treatment
  • Pro. No annual quota against genuine trade documents
  • Pro. The 2016 amendment simplified settlement documents to contract plus transport documents

Cons

  • Con. Still requires real goods and a real agent
  • Con. Depends on the agent's compliance and on the agent reporting the arrangement to the local SAFE branch
  • Con. Business income rates of 5% to 35% can exceed the 25% corporate rate at scale

Sources: S02 S13
