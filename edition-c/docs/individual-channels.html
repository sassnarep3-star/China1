Individual and personal channels

11 channels in this category. Each is stated with its legal basis, its limit where one is fixed by an official instrument, its tax cost, the documents it requires, and its pros and cons.

Channels available to a natural person. For a US citizen the controlling legal question is whether Chinese foreign exchange law classifies you as an overseas individual or a domestic individual.

Convert and remit legitimate current-account RMB income as an overseas individual R01

境外个人经常项目合法人民币收入购汇汇出

verified Low High, where the money is earned income
FieldValue
Legal basisMeasures for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange Art.13; Detailed Rules (Huifa [2007] No.1) Art.13(1)
How it worksA US citizen is an overseas individual, defined at Art.37(2) of the Measures as a foreign citizen holding a passport. Art.13 lets an overseas individual convert legitimate current-account RMB income earned in China into foreign exchange and remit it, presenting a valid identity document and supporting evidence of the transaction, including tax vouchers. Detailed Rules Art.13(1) repeats this and expressly names the tax voucher.
Stated limitNo annual quota is stated for this route. Art.9 of the Measures applies annual total-amount management to individual FX settlement and to domestic-individual FX purchase; an overseas individual purchasing FX against earned income is not a domestic individual. S01 S02 S05 S21 S35 S36
Chinese taxIndividual income tax on the underlying income must already have been paid and evidenced. Wage and salary income of a non-resident is taxed per month at 3% to 45% on revenue less RMB 5,000; remuneration for personal services and royalties are taken at 80% of revenue and taxed per item.
US taxThe income is already US taxable in the year earned. Converting and remitting it is not a second taxable event. Foreign tax credit under IRC s.901, or the foreign earned income exclusion where the taxpayer qualifies.
DocumentationPassport; employment contract or service contract; proof of income; tax voucher issued by the tax authority; bank's authenticity review.

Pros

  • Pro. The principal route for a US citizen and the only one with no stated annual ceiling
  • Pro. Processed at the bank counter, not by SAFE approval, so it is fast once documents are in order
  • Pro. No capital-account approval and no separate SAFE registration
  • Pro. The Xiamen branch guidance confirms no Individual FX Purchase Application form is required for an overseas individual
  • Pro. The Henan branch confirms no statutory time limit on converting previously earned income, provided the supporting evidence survives

Cons

  • Con. Only current-account income qualifies. Capital-account receipts such as sale proceeds, dividends or investment returns do not
  • Con. The bank must be satisfied the income is legitimate and taxed; missing or stale tax vouchers stop the transaction
  • Con. Each bank applies its own authenticity review, so outcomes vary by branch and by officer
  • Con. Employment contract, payroll records and tax certificates must be assembled for every year being remitted

Annual facilitation quota of USD 50,000 per person per year R02

个人年度便利化额度

verified Low if used within the quota and for a permitted purpose Low as a primary route; useful as a supplement
FieldValue
Legal basisMeasures Art.9; Detailed Rules Art.2
How it worksWithin the annual total amount an individual may settle or purchase FX on a valid identity document alone. Above it, current-account purchases require evidence of the transaction amount and capital-account items follow the capital-account chapter.
Stated limitUSD 50,000 equivalent per person per year, set by Detailed Rules Art.2. SAFE may adjust it in light of the balance of payments. Art.9 applies it to individual FX settlement and to domestic-individual FX purchase. S01 S02 S04 S21
Chinese taxNone on the conversion itself.
US taxNone on the conversion itself. The account is reportable on FinCEN Form 114 and possibly Form 8938.
DocumentationValid identity document within the quota.

Pros

  • Pro. Simplest possible mechanics: identity document only, no supporting evidence
  • Pro. Available at any authorised bank and through personal internet banking
  • Pro. Unchanged since 2007 and stable to plan around

Cons

  • Con. The declared purpose may not be used for overseas real property, overseas securities investment or overseas dividend-paying insurance
  • Con. USD 50,000 a year is immaterial for a large trapped balance; draining USD 1,000,000 at this rate takes twenty years
  • Con. For a US citizen holding only a foreign passport this quota is not the binding constraint on earned-income conversion, which is a widely misunderstood point
  • Con. Xiamen branch guidance states that an overseas individual who holds a Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card is subject to the annual facilitation quota for FX purchase, so permanent residents do not enjoy the R01 treatment

Reconvert previously exchanged but unused RMB back into foreign exchange R03

原兑换未用完的人民币兑回

verified Low Narrow; only where the funds originated offshore
FieldValue
Legal basisMeasures Art.15; Detailed Rules Art.13(2)
How it worksWhere RMB was originally obtained by converting foreign exchange and has not been spent, it may be converted back on presentation of the original exchange receipt. The receipt is valid for 24 months from the exchange date. Daily cumulative reconversion up to USD 500 equivalent, and up to USD 1,000 equivalent at a pre-customs departure-port location, may be done on a valid identity document alone.
Stated limit24-month validity of the original exchange receipt; USD 500 per day and USD 1,000 per day at departure ports on ID alone. S01 S02 S21
Chinese taxNone.
US taxNone on the conversion itself.
DocumentationValid identity document and the original exchange receipt.

Pros

  • Pro. Restores funds that were converted into RMB in the first place, so it is not new capital leaving China
  • Pro. Small amounts need no supporting evidence beyond the receipt

Cons

  • Con. Useless unless the money genuinely originated as foreign exchange that was converted into RMB
  • Con. The 24-month window closes and the right is lost
  • Con. Cannot be used to move money that was earned in RMB

Remit foreign exchange already held in an overseas individual's FX savings account R04

境外个人外汇储蓄账户内外汇汇出

verified Low High once the FX position exists
FieldValue
Legal basisDetailed Rules Art.15(1)
How it worksAn overseas individual remitting foreign exchange abroad from an FX savings account does so on presentation of a valid identity document. No quota and no supporting evidence are stated for this leg.
Stated limitNone stated for an overseas individual. By contrast Detailed Rules Art.14 caps the domestic individual's equivalent leg at USD 50,000 equivalent per day on ID alone, above which current-account authenticity vouchers are required. S02
Chinese taxNone.
US taxNone on the transfer. The receiving account is US reportable.
DocumentationValid identity document.

Pros

  • Pro. Once funds are already in foreign currency, the outbound leg is administrative rather than substantive
  • Pro. Markedly lighter than the equivalent rule for a domestic individual

Cons

  • Con. Presupposes the money is already denominated in foreign exchange inside China, which is the hard part
  • Con. A domestic individual and an overseas individual may not freely transfer FX between their savings accounts; Detailed Rules Art.28(3) manages such transfers as cross-border transactions

Overseas cash withdrawal against a mainland bank card R08

持境内银行卡在境外提取现金

verified Low within the caps; high if cards are pooled Very low as a repatriation route
FieldValue
Legal basisHuifa [2017] No.29; Measures Art.33 and Art.38; Detailed Rules Art.30
How it worksA cardholder withdraws foreign currency cash at an overseas ATM against a mainland-issued card. The issuer settles the withdrawal, and the RMB cost is debited domestically.
Stated limitAll cards in the cardholder's own name, including supplementary cards, may not together withdraw more than RMB 100,000 equivalent per calendar year overseas. The per-card daily cap is RMB 10,000 equivalent for both RMB cards and foreign currency cards. Exceeding the annual cap suspends overseas cash withdrawal for the current and the following year. S08 S01 S02
Chinese taxNone.
US taxNone on the withdrawal. FinCEN Form 105 applies if more than USD 10,000 in currency or monetary instruments is transported into the United States at one time.
DocumentationCard and PIN; the issuer enforces the caps through the SAFE card-overseas-transaction system.
Officiality caveatThe regulator's own text of Huifa [2017] No.29 was not located on safe.gov.cn during this session; figures are taken from Bank of China's republication of the notice and of the SAFE spokesperson's answers. F08

Pros

  • Pro. Immediate physical access to cash outside China with no application or approval
  • Pro. RMB 100,000 a year is roughly USD 14,000, usable for living expenses

Cons

  • Con. Far too small to repatriate a material balance
  • Con. Borrowing another person's card, or lending your own, to evade the cap is expressly prohibited and is itself punishable
  • Con. Breaching the cap loses overseas cash access for two years and may attract penalties under the Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration
  • Con. ATM and issuer fees plus the card network's conversion rate apply on every withdrawal

Overseas card spending on current-account consumption R09

个人持境内银行卡境外消费

verified Low for genuine consumption; high for cash-out or capital movement High for spending; none for repatriation
FieldValue
Legal basisHuifa [2017] No.29 SAFE spokesperson answers; Measures Art.38
How it worksA mainland-issued card pays for travel, business, study, food, lodging, transport and shopping abroad. SAFE states that such current-account card use is unaffected by the cash-withdrawal rules and does not consume the USD 50,000 annual facilitation quota.
Stated limitNo stated cap on overseas current-account card consumption in the sources reviewed. Reporting thresholds and issuer limits apply commercially. S08 S09 S01
Chinese taxNone.
US taxNone. Card repayment from a domestic account is not income.
DocumentationCard; the transaction must be a genuine current-account consumption.

Pros

  • Pro. Discharges US dollar living costs directly from the RMB balance, which is economically equivalent to repatriating the same amount
  • Pro. Does not consume the annual facilitation quota
  • Pro. No application, approval or supporting documents

Cons

  • Con. Only usable for consumption. It does not produce USD in a US bank account
  • Con. Using overseas card transactions to move capital rather than to consume is a violation: in the 2022 SAFE circular an individual who made 188 overseas card transactions totalling CAD 1,222,000 was found to have evaded FX supervision and fined RMB 325,600
  • Con. Issuer and network conversion spreads apply

Withdraw and carry foreign currency banknotes out of China R10

个人购汇提钞并携带外币现钞出境

verified Medium to high Very low; unsuitable for material amounts
FieldValue
Legal basisMeasures Arts.33 and 34; Detailed Rules Art.30; Regulation Art.15 and Art.42
How it worksAn individual withdraws foreign currency cash domestically, within the amount permitted to be carried out of the country, or above that amount after prior filing with the local SAFE branch, and carries it across the border with a customs declaration.
Stated limitDetailed Rules Art.30: cumulative withdrawals up to USD 10,000 equivalent per day may be handled directly at the bank; above that, the individual must file in advance with the local SAFE branch and present the stamped foreign-currency-cash withdrawal filing form. Detailed Rules Art.31: cash deposits up to USD 5,000 equivalent per day may be made directly. S01 S02 S03 S29
Chinese taxNone on the withdrawal.
US taxFinCEN Form 105 must be filed where currency or monetary instruments totalling more than USD 10,000 at one time are transported into the United States.
DocumentationValid identity document; proof of the withdrawal purpose above the threshold; the SAFE filing form; customs declaration on exit and on US entry.

Pros

  • Pro. Produces physical USD with no counterparty and no wire trail to reconstruct later
  • Pro. Available in modest size without SAFE involvement

Cons

  • Con. Carrying FX across the border in violation of the rules attracts a warning and a fine of up to 20% of the illegal amount under Art.42 of the Regulation
  • Con. Amounts above USD 10,000 must be declared to US Customs and Border Protection; failure is a separate US offence
  • Con. Theft, loss and seizure risk, plus deposit difficulty at a US bank without a documented source of funds

Insurance proceeds paid to an overseas beneficiary R11

境外个人作为保险受益人的理赔款购汇汇出

verified Low Narrow; contingent
FieldValue
Legal basisDetailed Rules Art.10(4) and Art.13; SAFE Henan Q&A (I)
How it worksWhere the beneficiary of a policy is an overseas person, the claim proceeds may be converted and remitted if the purchase of the insurance was itself lawful and compliant, processed under the policy for legitimate RMB income of an overseas individual against the insurance contract and the insurer's payment proof.
Stated limitNone stated for this route. S37 S02 S13
Chinese taxInsurance indemnity is exempt from individual income tax under Art.4(5) of the Individual Income Tax Law.
US taxLife insurance proceeds paid by reason of death are generally excluded; other indemnities are analysed by type. The conversion is not a taxable event.
DocumentationValid identity document; the insurance contract; the insurer's payment proof.

Pros

  • Pro. Exempt from Chinese individual income tax as insurance indemnity
  • Pro. Uses the same lightweight overseas-individual income machinery as R01

Cons

  • Con. Requires a lawful underlying policy and an insurable event; not a planning tool
  • Con. SAFE guidance makes legality of the original purchase a precondition
  • Con. Domestic individuals paying FX premiums to a domestic insurer is a separate route with its own rules and does not move money out

Individual foreign-trade operator FX settlement account R12

个人对外贸易经营者外汇结算账户

verified Low for genuine trade; severe for fabricated trade High where a genuine trade exists
FieldValue
Legal basisMeasures Arts.10 and 29; Detailed Rules Arts.9(1) and 25; SAFE spokesperson answers of 5 January 2007
How it worksAn individual who completes commercial registration and files for foreign trade operating rights may open an FX settlement account. That account is administered as an institutional account and is used for trade FX receipts, payments and settlement, whether trading directly or through an agent.
Stated limitSAFE's own English explanation states that, unlike individual FX savings accounts and capital-account items, purchases and sales of FX under an FX settlement account are not subject to the annual quota and are handled against valid trading documents regardless of amount. S01 S02 S04 S13 S09 S10
Chinese taxBusiness income of an individual is taxed at 5% to 35% progressive rates on annual revenue less costs, expenses and losses. VAT and surcharges apply to the trade.
US taxBusiness income is US taxable; a foreign tax credit is available. If the activity runs through a foreign entity, Forms 8858, 8865 or 5471 may be required.
DocumentationIndividual business licence or other practice certificate; foreign trade operating rights registration filing with the commerce authority; the export agency contract and the agent's customs declaration for settlement.

Pros

  • Pro. No annual quota, and no stated cap, provided the trade documents are genuine
  • Pro. Converts a person into a current-account trader, which is the category Chinese law is most willing to let through
  • Pro. Can be combined with an individually-owned business structure

Cons

  • Con. Requires a real, documented export or import business, not a paper one
  • Con. Fabricating trade documents to move money is the single most heavily penalised pattern in the SAFE circulars, with corporate fines reaching RMB 37.34 million on USD 92.858 million of fictitious payments
  • Con. Registration, bookkeeping, tax filing and customs compliance are ongoing obligations
  • Con. Settlement is tied to specific customs declarations and transport documents

Outward direct investment by a domestic individual R27

境内个人对外直接投资

verified Medium Low
FieldValue
Legal basisMeasures Art.16; Detailed Rules Art.16
How it worksOutward direct investment by a domestic individual requires SAFE approval: the FX needed may be purchased or remitted from own FX only after the local SAFE branch approves, and the individual must complete outward direct investment FX registration.
Stated limitCase-by-case SAFE approval. No general quota is stated. S01 S02
Chinese taxReturns are taxed under normal rules; Art.10(4) of the Individual Income Tax Law requires self-filing on overseas income.
US taxA US person owning a foreign corporation generally files Form 5471 and may have GILTI or Subpart F inclusions.
DocumentationSAFE approval; outward direct investment FX registration; project approval or filing with the commerce authority where required.

Pros

  • Pro. Where approved, moves a defined amount out against a real investment project

Cons

  • Con. Requires SAFE approval, which is discretionary and rarely granted for individuals
  • Con. Applies to a domestic individual, so a US citizen treated as an overseas individual follows a different path
  • Con. Detailed Rules Art.16 also sweeps in overseas individuals who habitually reside in China for economic interest and set up or control a special purpose vehicle for round-trip investment
  • Con. Registration and ongoing reporting obligations follow the investment

Outward donation or property transfer by a domestic individual R28

境内个人对外捐赠和财产转移购付汇

verified Medium Low to medium; discretionary
FieldValue
Legal basisMeasures Art.20; Detailed Rules Art.10(1) and Art.10(3)
How it worksA domestic individual's outward donation or property transfer requiring FX purchase must comply with the relevant regulations and be approved by SAFE. Donations above the annual total amount require a notarised donation agreement or contract, and inheritance income requires the legal instrument or notarial certificate of inheritance.
Stated limitSAFE approval required; no general quota stated. S01 S02
Chinese taxDepends on the underlying asset.
US taxA gift to a non-US person may trigger US gift tax and Form 709; a US recipient of a large foreign gift or bequest has its own reporting obligations. Not quantified here.
DocumentationNotarised donation agreement or contract, or the inheritance legal instrument or notarial certificate; SAFE approval.

Pros

  • Pro. A lawful route for genuine donations and family transfers above the quota

Cons

  • Con. Requires SAFE approval and notarisation
  • Con. The donation must comply with State regulations, and authenticity is examined closely because it is a known quota-evasion pattern
  • Con. US gift and generation-skipping rules may apply to the donor independently of Chinese approval
