## Verification boundaries and flags

- Local commands `integration/verify.py`, `edition-i/build/verify.py`, `assets/check.py`, and `edition-d/build/verify.py` passed. These test structural integrity, selected arithmetic and local links, not every underlying legal assertion, external-link availability, or an individual's eligibility.
- A BOC schedule, 2014 onward, was directly read; its continued availability does not establish the current applicable price for this customer. The site's dated rates are illustrative, not executable quotes. Intermediary/receiving charges, underlying taxes and personal penalties remain **unverified** and cannot be summed into an exact personal cost.
- An official source or bank must confirm the source-of-funds category, relevant documentation and quoted amounts before any transfer. Do not split transactions among people to evade controls. Professional China/US tax advice is appropriate; this review is not individualized advice.
- Published inherited [Edition I audit](../edition-i/audit/REPO_AUDIT.md) records that many earlier editions were not re-certified line by line. The present review does not remove that limitation.
