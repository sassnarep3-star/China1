S04: 个人财产对外转移售付汇管理暂行办法 (Interim Measures for Sale and Remittance of Individual Property Transferred Abroad), effective 2004-12-01 government

Defines emigration property transfer (Chinese emigrants to foreign countries/HK/Macau settling abroad, pre-emigration assets) and inheritance property transfer (foreign citizens/HK/Macau residents inheriting mainland estate). Property must be lawful, undisputed, not under judicial restriction; applications at former household registration / decedent household registration SAFE offices. Original Art. 6 staged remittance schedule (first remittance ≤50% of approved total; after 1 year ≤50% of remainder; after 2 years the rest) — see flag F09 (superseded in part by S03 Section VI).

S05: Can I buy foreign exchange using the proceeds from selling my house? (Shanghai municipal government FAQ, source: Bank of China) government

Non-resident selling a mainland house may purchase FX and remit proceeds. Steps: ownership transfer; settle tax at local tax authority and obtain tax-payment proof; obtain Tax Filing Form for External Payments (services/other accounts) when transaction exceeds USD 50,000; purchase FX and remit at bank with passport/ID, transfer contract + registration certificate, tax voucher (and filing form if > USD 50,000), notarized power of attorney if an agent acts.

S09: Individual Income Tax Law of the People's Republic of China (English, 2018 amendment) government

Art. 2 lists nine taxable income categories (salary; personal-service remuneration; author's remuneration; royalties; business operation; interest/dividends/bonuses; leasing; transfer of assets; incidental income) — currency conversion is not a taxable category. Art. 3 rates: comprehensive income 3–45% progressive; business operation 5–35%; flat 20% for interest/dividends, leasing, asset transfer and incidental income. Art. 1 resident definition (183 days).

S10: Report of Foreign Bank and Financial Accounts (FBAR) government

U.S. persons must file FinCEN Form 114 if aggregate value of foreign financial accounts exceeded USD 10,000 at any time in the calendar year; due April 15 with automatic extension to October 15; filed through FinCEN BSA E-Filing; civil and criminal penalties apply to violations; late filing should be corrected as soon as possible.

https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/report-of-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts-fbar ↗

S11: Do I need to file Form 8938, Statement of Specified Foreign Financial Assets? government

FATCA reporting thresholds: unmarried living in the U.S. — more than USD 50,000 on the last day of the tax year or more than USD 75,000 at any time; married filing jointly living in the U.S. — USD 100,000 / USD 150,000; taxpayers living abroad — USD 200,000 / USD 300,000 (unmarried) and USD 400,000 / USD 600,000 (joint). Form 8938 attaches to the income tax return; no filing required if no income tax return is required.

https://www.irs.gov/businesses/corporations/do-i-need-to-file-form-8938-statement-of-specified-foreign-financial-assets ↗

S12: Internal Revenue Manual 5.21.6 — Foreign Financial Account Reporting (FBAR vs Form 8938 comparison) government

Penalty comparison: Form 8938 failure to disclose — up to USD 10,000 plus USD 10,000 per 30 days of non-filing after IRS notice, potential maximum USD 60,000, criminal penalties may also apply. FBAR — non-willful up to USD 10,000; willful up to the greater of USD 100,000 or 50 percent of account balances; criminal penalties may also apply. Threshold comparison FBAR USD 10,000 at any time vs 8938 thresholds above.

https://www.irs.gov/irm/part5/irm_05-021-006 ↗

Amounts on U.S. tax returns must be expressed in U.S. dollars; foreign-currency income/expenses translated at the prevailing rate when received, paid or accrued; U.S. tax payments must be remitted in U.S. dollars.

S15: Foreign Tax Credit government

Credit or deduction available for foreign income taxes paid or accrued on income also subject to U.S. tax; individuals claim on Form 1116; generally only income, war profits and excess profits taxes qualify; credit not allowed for taxes on income excluded under the foreign earned income exclusion.

https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-tax-credit ↗

S16: LB&I Practice Unit — Disposition of Nonfunctional Currency (IRC 988) government

Disposing of nonfunctional currency (including bank deposits) is a section 988 transaction; gain/loss equals amount realized minus adjusted basis; character generally ordinary; basis of withdrawn currency may use a consistently applied reasonable method (FIFO/LIFO/pro rata); virtual currency is not section 988 currency (cites Notice 2014-21).

https://www.irs.gov/pub/fatca/int_practice_units/fcu_t_018_02_01_08.pdf ↗

S17: Guide to CNY transfers (Sending from CNY) provider

Sending CNY from China is limited to personal accounts of non-mainland-Chinese senders (Taiwan/HK/Macau eligible) who worked in China and hold a China tax record showing tax paid within the last 3 years; funds may only be sent to an account the sender owns; single-transfer maxima CNY 300,000 (China bank app) and CNY 50,000 (Alipay); overall limit derived from after-tax salary proven by tax records; service provided with a PBOC-regulated licensed third-party payment provider; exact fees shown only when setting up a transfer (no fixed published fee table).

https://wise.com/help/articles/2955298/guide-to-cny-transfers ↗

S21: 关于进一步防范和处置虚拟货币等相关风险的通知 (Notice on preventing virtual-currency and related risks), PBOC et al. Yin Fa [2026] No. 42 government

Current authority in force from 2026-02-06 (fetched 2026-11-10). Virtual-currency business activities remain illegal financial activities strictly banned (Sec. 1(1)); offshore entities and individuals may not provide virtual-currency services to domestic parties in any form; fiat-pegged stablecoins may not be issued offshore pegged to RMB without approval; RWA tokenization activities are banned absent approval; financial institutions may not provide accounts, transfers or settlement for virtual-currency business (Sec. 3(6)); penalties incl. criminal liability (Sec. 6); civil acts violating the notice are void with losses borne by the parties (Sec. 6(19)). S19: 行邮监管：出境、入境可以携带多少现金？ (Customs passenger cash-carrying FAQ) government

RMB carrying limit CNY 20,000 per crossing (PBOC Announcement 2004 No. 18). Outbound FX cash: up to USD 5,000 equivalent no permit; above USD 5,000 up to USD 10,000 requires a bank-issued FX Carrying Permit (携带外汇出境许可证); customs will not clear cumulative bank permits over USD 10,000; above USD 10,000 requires a SAFE-issued permit. Inbound FX cash over USD 5,000 equivalent must be declared in writing. Exemptions for same-day/short-term repeat crossings.

http://guangzhou.customs.gov.cn/guangzhou_customs/381679/3795484/3800229/index.html ↗
