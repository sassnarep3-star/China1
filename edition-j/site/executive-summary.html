Executive summary

Reader: a U.S. citizen with CNY in a Chinese bank account. Decision date: 2026-11-10. Currency controls depend on immigration/residence and the money's source, not U.S. citizenship alone.

Most important result: there is no verified universal cheapest route. First establish that the bank can document the transaction, source of funds and tax treatment. Then request executable quotes for the same settled amount from every eligible provider and compare the FX rate plus every sending, intermediary and receiving fee — not just a headline commission.

Tax and reporting — do not assume zero

Moving your own already-taxed principal is not automatically new U.S. income, but characterise the original income, interest, dividends, asset gains and any currency disposition separately. §988's personal-transaction exclusion fails when gain exceeds USD200 — it is not a universal USD200 deduction. FBAR and Forms 8938/3520 are separate reporting duties. The new 1% U.S. remittance tax concerns remittances sent from the U.S., not this direction. S10 ↗ S11 ↗ S12 ↗ S13 ↗ S16 ↗
