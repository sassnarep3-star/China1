Step-by-step procedures

Every lawful procedure remains subject to the handling bank's and competent authority's current review. Large or complex capital transactions need advice from the bank, a China tax adviser and a U.S. international tax adviser before execution.

M01Current accountOverseas-person lawful RMB income — purchase + remittance
  1. Confirm your bank classifies you as 境外个人 (passport) and will accept the income category.
  2. Gather: passport, employment/labour contract, payroll records, China IIT payment records (Art. 59 requires tax vouchers).
  3. At the bank: apply to purchase FX against the income, then remit to your own overseas account (Art. 65: ID only for existing FX).
  4. Keep purchase slip, SWIFT copy and tax records; reconcile U.S. reporting (FBAR/8938, worldwide income).

M02Current accountExisting FX remittance (FX already held)
  1. Confirm FX balance is in an FX account under your own name.
  2. Instruct remittance to your own overseas account; 境内个人 over USD50,000/day needs transaction evidence (Art. 64).
  3. Retain remittance receipt for U.S. reporting reconciliation.
S01 ↗ S02 ↗
M03Current account境内个人 annual facilitation quota (USD50,000/yr)
  1. Confirm classification with the bank first — do not assume it.
  2. If 境内个人: purchase within USD50,000/yr, remit; over quota needs transaction evidence (Art. 56).
  3. Never borrow another person's quota.

M05RailsWise from-CNY transfer
  1. Verify identity + tax records with Wise (1–2 working days first time).
  2. Start transfer from China bank app or Alipay; confirm remaining overall limit covers the amount.
  3. Destination must be your own overseas account/Wise account.

M06RailsBank telegraphic transfer (BOC / HSBC China)

  3. Confirm the executed FX rate and all deductions on the receipt.

M13RailsOverseas ATM cash withdrawal (China card)
  1. Withdraw within CNY10,000/day and CNY100,000/year across all your cards.
  2. Never use another person's card (prohibited).
  3. Declare cash over USD10,000 entering the U.S. (FinCEN 105) — see flag F08.
