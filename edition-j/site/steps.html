Step by step

Exactly how each route works, in order

Twenty-four lawful or conditional routes, each as a numbered procedure with the provision that authorises the step. The six prohibited routes are excluded by design - this page does not explain how to break the law.

These are the procedural steps described in the cited instruments, not a substitute for your bank's current checklist. Banks apply their own documentary standards and may ask for more.

R01 · Bank purchase and TT remittance against documented current-account income lawful

个人外汇管理办法 Art. 13; 经常项目指引(2020) Art. 59 · CN-PBOC3 ↗CN-GUIDE2020 ↗

  1. Classify the balance by source and period; separate documented income from pre-emigration savings.
  2. Ask the bank to confirm you are booked as 境外个人 (foreign individual) on the account, because the quota rules differ.
  3. Assemble the documentary set: passport, China tax record or withholding slips, employment or service contract, and bank statements showing the credits.
  4. Submit the purchase (购汇) application; above USD 50,000 the bank performs enhanced review under Art. 59.
  5. Settle the FX at the counter/online rate, request the TT, and keep the 结售汇 slip and the remittance receipt.
  6. Report the account and the transfer on your US return: FBAR if the aggregate exceeded USD 10,000 at any point, Form 8938 if the threshold applies.

R02 · Annual facilitation-quota purchase (USD 50,000 per person per year) conditional

经常项目指引(2020) Art. 54; 个人外汇管理办法 Art. 9 · CN-GUIDE2020 ↗CN-PBOC3 ↗

  1. Confirm with the bank whether you are recorded as 境内个人 or 境外个人 - this determines whether the quota is even available to you.
  2. If available, submit the purchase application with your valid ID inside the annual quota.
  3. Record the quota usage; it is aggregated per person across all banks.
  4. Remit and retain the slips.

R03 · Salary, bonus and severance paid from a mainland employer lawful

个人外汇管理办法 Art. 13; 经常项目指引(2020) Art. 59; IIT Law Art. 2(1) · CN-PBOC3 ↗CN-GUIDE2020 ↗CN-IIT ↗

  1. Obtain the employment contract, the payroll history and the annual 个人所得税 withholding record.
  2. Have the employer or the tax bureau confirm the tax-paid position for the period.
  3. Present the package to the bank with the purchase application.
  4. Convert, remit, and reconcile the gross and net amounts in USD on your US return.

R04 · Service-trade income: consulting, freelance, royalties lawful

经常项目指引(2020) Art. 49 and Art. 59 · CN-GUIDE2020 ↗CN-PBOC3 ↗

  1. Gather contracts, invoices, payment receipts and the tax treatment of the service income.
  2. Confirm whether the payment needs the 服务贸易等项目对外支付税务备案表.
  3. Apply at the bank for purchase and remittance.
  4. Retain everything; report the income and the account in the US.

R05 · Rent collected from mainland property you own lawful

个人外汇管理办法 Art. 13; IIT Law Art. 2(7) and Art. 3(3) · CN-PBOC3 ↗CN-IIT ↗

  1. Register or confirm the lease and the rental tax payments.
  2. Present the lease, the tax record and the bank statements showing rent credits.
  3. Apply for purchase and remittance of the net rent.

R06 · Deposit interest and dividends (bank deposits, fund distributions, FIE dividends) lawful

IIT Law Art. 2(6) and Art. 3(3); 财政部 税务总局公告2026年第27号 · CN-IIT ↗CN-DIV2026 ↗CN-PBOC3 ↗

  1. Identify each distribution and whether 20% was withheld at source.
  2. Obtain the withholding certificates.
  3. Apply at the bank for purchase and remittance of the net amount.
  4. Claim the Chinese tax as a foreign tax credit on the US return where eligible.

R07 · Sale of mainland residential property by a non-resident owner lawful

个人外汇管理办法 Art. 22; IIT Law Art. 2(8) and Art. 6(5) · CN-PBOC3 ↗CN-SHFAQ ↗CN-IIT ↗

  1. Sign the sale contract and complete the ownership-transfer registration at the real-estate authority.
  2. Settle property transfer taxes at the local tax bureau and obtain the tax-payment proof.
  3. Obtain the 服务贸易等项目对外支付税务备案表 for amounts above USD 50,000.
  4. Present passport, contract, registration certificate and tax voucher to the bank.
  5. Purchase FX and remit to your own overseas account; keep the 结售汇 slip.

R08 · Emigrant property transfer with SAFE approval (移民财产转移) conditional

PBOC公告〔2004〕16号 Arts. 2, 8, 10; 汇发〔2014〕2号 Section VI · CN-PROPERTY2004 ↗CN-CAP2014 ↗CN-PBOC3 ↗

  1. Confirm eligibility: settled abroad and Chinese household registration cancelled.
  2. File the written application and the two prescribed forms with the FX bureau at your former household-registration location.
  3. Provide identity documents, the consular certificate of settlement abroad, and the property-rights and realisation documents.
  4. Provide the Chinese tax certificate or tax-paid vouchers for the property being transferred.
  5. Obtain the approval and 核准件, then apply at the designated bank to purchase FX and remit.
  6. Keep the approval, the 核准件 and each remittance receipt for the US side.

R09 · Inheritance transfer with SAFE approval (继承财产转移) conditional

PBOC公告〔2004〕16号 Arts. 2, 9, 10; 汇发〔2014〕2号 Section VI-三 · CN-PROPERTY2004 ↗CN-CAP2014 ↗

  1. Obtain the inheritance documentation and the decedent's property-rights documents.
  2. Obtain the decedent's Chinese tax certificate or tax-paid vouchers.
  3. File the application at the FX bureau where the decedent's household was registered.
  4. Receive approval, then purchase and remit at the designated bank.
  5. File Form 3520 on the US side if the bequest exceeded USD 100,000.

R10 · Company liquidation, capital reduction or equity-sale proceeds conditional

外汇管理条例 Art. 22 final paragraph; 个人外汇管理办法 Art. 31 · CN-FXREG ↗CN-PBOC3 ↗

  1. Complete the liquidation or capital reduction and obtain the tax clearance.
  2. Update the capital-account registration with the FX bureau.
  3. Present the liquidation documents, tax clearance and registration to the bank.
  4. Purchase FX and remit the distributable amount.

R11 · Sell the mainland asset to a non-resident buyer who pays you offshore conditional

外汇管理条例 Art. 22; 个人外汇管理办法 Art. 26 · CN-FXREG ↗CN-PBOC3 ↗

  1. Confirm with a Chinese lawyer whether the specific asset class can be transferred to a non-resident with offshore settlement.
  2. Complete any required capital-account or real-estate registration.
  3. Settle offshore in USD and document the arm's-length price.

R12 · FIE dividend distribution to a foreign individual shareholder lawful

财政部 税务总局公告2026年第27号; IIT Law Art. 2(6), Art. 3(3) · CN-DIV2026 ↗CN-IIT ↗

  1. Pass the profit-distribution resolution.
  2. The company withholds 20% and files within 15 days of the month after payment.
  3. Obtain the withholding certificate.
  4. The company or you apply at the bank to remit the net dividend.

R13 · Foreign-investor special account (资本金/外投) after approval conditional

个人外汇管理办法 Art. 31 · CN-PBOC3 ↗

  1. Obtain the direct-investment approval from the competent authority.
  2. Apply to the FX bureau to open the special account.
  3. Apply for approval to convert the account balance.

R14 · Licensed outbound investment product bought while still resident (QDII and similar) conditional

个人外汇管理办法 Art. 17; 外汇管理条例 Art. 17 · CN-PBOC3 ↗CN-FXREG ↗

  1. Confirm your 境内/境外 classification at the time of purchase.
  2. Buy through a licensed domestic institution within its approved quota.

R15 · Domestic FX deposit account inside China (convert now, remit later) lawful

个人外汇管理办法 Arts. 27, 32 · CN-PBOC3 ↗

  1. Open or confirm the 外币储蓄账户 at the bank.
  2. Purchase FX into the account and keep the 结售汇 slip.
  3. Remit later within the Art. 64 rules, or convert back if needed.

R16 · B-share or cross-border securities account for a foreign individual conditional

个人外汇管理办法 Arts. 17, 23 · CN-PBOC3 ↗

  1. Confirm eligibility for the specific account type.
  2. Open through a licensed domestic securities institution.

R17 · Spend on a mainland-issued card abroad (no conversion by you) lawful

SAFE 汇发〔2017〕29号 Q&A; 经常项目指引(2020) Art. 76(3) · CN-CARDS2017 ↗CN-GUIDE2020 ↗

  1. Use the card for genuine consumption abroad.
  2. Keep the statements for US reporting and for the foreign-account question.

R18 · Overseas ATM cash withdrawal on a mainland card lawful

SAFE 汇发〔2017〕29号 · CN-CARDS2017 ↗

  1. Withdraw within the per-card daily and per-person annual limits.
  2. If carrying more than USD 10,000 into the US, file FinCEN 105.

R19 · Carry cash across the border within the permit band lawful

经常项目指引(2020) Arts. 86-89; 外汇管理条例 Art. 42 · CN-GUIDE2020 ↗CN-FXREG ↗

  1. Withdraw with prior FX-bureau filing if above USD 10,000 a day.
  2. Obtain the 携带外汇出境许可证 in the applicable band.
  3. Declare to China Customs and, on entry to the US, file FinCEN 105 if above USD 10,000.

R20 · Alipay or WeChat cross-border remittance conditional

经常项目指引(2020) Arts. 120-137 (payment institutions) · CN-GUIDE2020 ↗

  1. Confirm the wallet's eligibility and per-transfer and annual limits.
  2. Complete identity and purpose verification.
  3. Send and keep the electronic receipts.

R21 · Licensed remittance provider with a China tax record (e.g. Wise from-CNY) conditional

经常项目指引(2020) Art. 120-137; provider terms · CN-GUIDE2020 ↗WISE-CNY ↗

  1. Collect China tax records covering the last three years.
  2. Complete provider verification (1-2 working days).
  3. Link your Chinese bank account or Alipay as the funding source.
  4. Send to your own overseas account and download the receipt.

R22 · International postal money order or courier of a monetary instrument conditional

外汇管理条例 Art. 42; 31 CFR 1010.340 · CN-FXREG ↗US-1010340 ↗

  1. Do not use for material amounts without written guidance from the bank and the FX bureau.

R23 · Leave the CNY on deposit and spend it on future trips to China lawful

个人外汇管理办法 Arts. 27-36 (accounts); no conversion occurs · CN-PBOC3 ↗

  1. Keep the account compliant and file FBAR/8938 as applicable.

R24 · Convert inside China into USD and hold the USD in a mainland bank lawful

个人外汇管理办法 Art. 32; 经常项目指引(2020) Art. 64 · CN-PBOC3 ↗CN-GUIDE2020 ↗

  1. Purchase FX into the foreign-currency savings account.
  2. Hold or remit later under Art. 64.

R25 · Buy a globally priced asset in China (e.g. gold) and sell it abroad conditional

个人外汇管理办法 Art. 30; 外汇管理条例 Arts. 8, 39 · CN-PBOC3 ↗CN-FXREG ↗

  1. Do not treat this as a conversion channel without written customs and FX advice specific to the asset.

R26 · Use the balance for genuine obligations that are payable in CNY lawful

外汇管理条例 Art. 5, Art. 12 (genuine current-account payments) · CN-FXREG ↗

  1. Identify genuine CNY obligations and pay them from the account.
  2. Keep the invoices and contracts.

26 procedures. Four routes carry no procedure because they are unlawful, and two because they involve no transfer.
