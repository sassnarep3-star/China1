Tax and reporting

Two tax systems, four forms, one rule: report first

China taxes the income. The US taxes your worldwide income and, separately, punishes failure to report the account. None of these is a fee for converting, and all of them are larger than the bank charges.

Why FBAR dominates every other number in this project. At a USD 400,000 balance, one willful violation has a statutory maximum of USD 200,000. The published bank cost of moving the same USD 400,000 through BOC is about USD 61. Filing on time is the highest-return action available, and it does not require moving anything.
The clean sequence. Report the account while it is still in China (FBAR, 8938). Pay or confirm Chinese tax on the income. Then convert through the route whose documentary basis matches the source, and claim the Chinese tax as a foreign tax credit. The order matters because the reporting duty attaches to the account, not to the transfer.
