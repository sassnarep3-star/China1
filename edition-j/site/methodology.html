Methodology and limitations

Source hierarchy

  1. Government, regulator, statute, court and official tax guidance for legal/tax claims.
  2. A bank or provider's own tariff/help page for its own prices and product terms only.
  3. No blog, forum, aggregator or law-firm summary supports a numeric or legal row.

Cost model (worked example: USD 10,000, BOC single wire)

  • P = 10,000 × 6.6866 = CNY66,866.00.
  • Commission = 0.1% × P = CNY66.87 (within CNY50–260). Sender fee = 66.87 + 150 = CNY216.87.
  • FX margin = 10,000 × 0.209813% = USD 20.98 vs midpoint (indicative).
  • Partial subtotal = 20.98 + 216.87/6.6866 = USD 53.41. All-in remains blank (receiver/intermediary/tax unknown).
  • One transfer assumed. Tranche ladders (L3/L4) repeat the fee per USD50,000 wire — shown to expose the cost of splitting, not to recommend it.

What verification proves (and does not)

Mechanical verification proves deterministic arithmetic, source presence, blank semantics and local-link integrity. It does not establish legal correctness, personal eligibility, bank acceptance, an executable quote, or future external-link uptime. Earlier editions are preserved for provenance and are not re-certified here (F13).

Safety and legality

Quota splitting, underground settlement, fabricated trade and crypto/stablecoin bridges are not tax-saving recommendations. Prohibited candidates have no operational steps. Business, investment and cash routes require real underlying transactions and cannot be used to relabel an unrelated personal balance.
