Table 8 — claim-to-source mapping
SourceSupports
CN-FXREGThe statutory penalty ladder: 逃汇 (Art. 39) up to 30% then 30%-100%; 非法套汇 (Art. 40) same; cash carried in/out of the border (Art. 42) up to 20%; private/illegal FX dealing (Art. 45); and Art. 5 free current-account payments.
CN-PBOC3The 境内个人 / 境外个人 split (Art. 2, Art. 37 definitions), current-account convertibility versus capital-account 'convertibility process' (Art. 3), the no-splitting rule (Art. 7), the quota/over-quota structure (Art. 9), the overseas individual documented-income route (Art. 13), property-sale remittance (Art. 22) and outward property transfer (Art. 26).
CN-GUIDE2020Annual facilitation quota of USD 50,000 per person per year for 个人结汇 and 境内个人购汇 (Art. 54); 境外个人 purchases against documented current-account income with no stated annual cap (Art. 59); service-trade documentary threshold of USD 50,000 (Art. 49); splitting/'watch list' rules (Art. 62); the USD 50,000 outward-remittance documentary threshold (Art. 64); cash rules: USD 10,000 per day withdrawal/deposit and the USD 5,000 inbound declaration and USD 5,000-10,000 carry-permit bands (Arts. 86-89); payment-institution single-transaction cap of USD 50,000 (Art. 132).
CN-PROPERTY2004Defines emigrant transfer and inheritance transfer (Art. 2); original staging (Art. 6); document list including the tax certificate/完税凭证 (Arts. 8-9); the RMB 500,000 approval threshold (Art. 10); ban on taking out cash in China (Art. 11).
CN-CAP2014Cancels the RMB 500,000 national-level filing (VI-一), cancels mandatory staged emigrant releases so an approved amount may be remitted once or in instalments (VI-二), allows merged applications from different decedents (VI-三), and removes the notarisation requirement for property-rights documents (VI-四). This supersedes the staging in CN-PROPERTY2004 Art. 6.
CN-PROPERTYQAConfirms, as of March 2026, that the operative definition of 个人财产对外转移 still rests on PBOC Announcement [2004] No. 16 and covers emigrant and inheritance transfers. It does not restate the 2014 staging removal, so the two must be read together.
CN-IITNine taxable income categories (Art. 2) - a currency exchange is not among them; 20% flat rate on interest/dividends, asset leasing, asset transfer and incidental income (Art. 3(3)); 3%-45% progressive on comprehensive income (Art. 3(1)); taxable-income computation for asset transfers net of original value and reasonable expenses (Art. 6(5)); the exit filing trigger when emigrating and cancelling household registration (Art. 10(5)).
CN-DIV2026From 2026-09-01, dividends paid by a foreign-invested enterprise to a foreign individual are taxed at 20% under 'interest, dividends and bonuses', withholding applies, and the 1994 exemption in 财税字〔1994〕20号 Art. 2(8) is repealed.
CN-SHFAQThe operational sequence for a non-resident selling mainland property: ownership transfer, tax settlement and tax-payment proof, the external-payment tax filing form above USD 50,000, then purchase and remittance. Government-hosted but content attributed to a commercial bank, so it evidences procedure, not statute.
CN-CARDS2017Aggregate overseas cash withdrawal on a person's domestic bank cards is capped at CNY 100,000 per calendar year; per-card daily limit is CNY 10,000 equivalent; exceeding it suspends overseas cash access for the current and following year; card spending abroad does not consume the USD 50,000 facilitation quota.
CN-VA2026Virtual-currency business activity in mainland China is characterised as illegal financial activity and strictly prohibited; financial and payment institutions may not provide account, transfer or settlement services for it; it took effect 2026-02-06 and repealed 银发〔2021〕237号. The PBOC original is the primary source; this government portal reprint was used because the PBOC page was not retrievable from this sandbox.
BOC-BOARDOnly the published bid/ask numbers on that timestamp. It is not a quote for any particular customer, amount or date after the timestamp.
BOC-TARIFFCross-border telegraphic transfer: 1 per mille of the remittance, minimum CNY 50, maximum CNY 260, plus cable CNY 80 (Hong Kong/Macau/Taiwan) or CNY 150 (elsewhere). Domestic interbank: tiered CNY 2/5/10/15 up to CNY 50,000, then 0.03% capped at CNY 50. Intra-bank foreign-currency transfer: 1 per mille capped at CNY 200. Note 1 allows separate agreements, discounts and local differences.
CFETS-FIXThe PBOC-authorised central parity, and the published methodology (market-maker quotes, highest and lowest dropped, weighted average).
WISE-CNYEligibility for sending from CNY is limited to non-mainland-Chinese senders who work or have worked in China and hold a China tax record showing tax paid in the last three years; the recipient must be an account the sender owns; CNY 300,000 per transfer via a China bank app, CNY 50,000 via Alipay; an overall limit based on after-tax salary. Fees are quote-based, not a published schedule - the page says to check live pricing.
US-988§988(e)(2): for an individual's personal transaction in nonfunctional currency, no gain is recognised from exchange-rate movement between acquisition and disposition, and §988(e)(3) defines 'personal transaction'. §988(e)(2) withdraws that relief when the gain would exceed USD 200. Cornell is a republication of the Code, used here because the uscode.house.gov granule URLs returned HTTP 404 and govinfo was unreachable.
US-5321FBAR civil penalty structure: non-willful capped at USD 10,000 per violation with a reasonable-cause exception; willful raised to the greater of USD 100,000 or 50% of the account balance at the time of the violation, and the reasonable-cause defence is unavailable for willful violations. Statutory caps are inflation-adjusted annually.
US-1010821Current inflation-adjusted FBAR maxima for penalties assessed on or after 2025-01-17: non-willful USD 16,536; willful USD 165,353 (and the 50%-of-balance alternative in the statute continues to apply).
US-6038DForm 8938 attachment duty above USD 50,000 aggregate; USD 10,000 penalty for failure to disclose plus USD 10,000 per 30-day period after notice, capped at USD 50,000 additional; reasonable-cause exception in (g).
US-FBARUSD 10,000 aggregate reporting trigger, April 15 due date with automatic October 15 extension, five-year record retention, and confirmation that producing no taxable income does not remove the filing duty.
US-8938Form 8938 thresholds by filing status and residence (USD 50,000/75,000 single in the US, 100,000/150,000 joint, 200,000/300,000 and 400,000/600,000 abroad) and the USD 10,000 plus USD 10,000/30-days to a USD 60,000 maximum penalty description.
US-3520Form 3520 is required on receipt of more than USD 100,000 from a nonresident alien individual or foreign estate treated as a gift or bequest, and for foreign-trust transactions; it is an information return filed apart from the income tax return.
US-1010340A FinCEN 105 report is required to transport, mail or ship more than USD 10,000 in currency or monetary instruments into or out of the United States at one time; paragraph (d) confirms a funds transfer through normal banking channels that involves no physical transport is outside this section.
US-IRS-FXUS-dollar functional currency, translation at the rate prevailing when an item is received/paid/accrued, and the IRS's list of governmental rate resources.
US-IRS-ABROADUS citizens are taxed on worldwide income regardless of residence, and must report foreign accounts even where no taxable income arises.

Limits of this edition

  • Two market snapshots, both timestamped; BOC states the actual transaction rate governs.
  • One bank's tariff. ICBC's schedule was unreadable and HSBC's PDF 404'd, so no cross-bank comparison is claimed.
  • No Chinese or US tax is computed, because the income base is unknown.
  • Provincial and branch practice is not surveyed; a local branch may apply the same rule differently.
  • US Code was read on Cornell's republication because the authoritative host returned 404 from this sandbox.
  • This is research, not advice, and it cannot make an account eligible that the facts do not make eligible.
