All methods — with pros and cons and who they are for

Every lawful method has the same core requirement: a genuine underlying transaction and the documents to prove it. That is SAFE’s authenticity principle (S01 Art.7). Prohibited methods are listed only with risk information — no execution steps — so you know what to avoid.
