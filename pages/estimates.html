REPRODUCIBLE CALCULATION

How to update the estimate

Use the actual bank’s USD sell rate (CNY per USD × USD target), compare it only with a rate having the same timestamp and stated basis, then add separately quoted outgoing, intermediary and receiving fees. Model tax separately from payment mechanics. Do not multiply the amount by a blanket tax percentage or treat an evidence request as a tax.

All-in cost cannot be confirmed from the information provided. The account bank, source and ownership of funds, tax history, beneficiary bank and route eligibility are unknown. Unknown fees and account-specific taxes stay blank.
