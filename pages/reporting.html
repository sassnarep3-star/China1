Obligations to check

TopicFindingOfficial source
China income taxChinese tax law lists wages, services, interest, dividends, rent and asset transfers as taxable categories; rates and taxable base vary by category and facts.China State Taxation Administration · IIT law ↗
Foreign tax creditEligible foreign income taxes may be credited against U.S. income tax subject to limits. Taxes allocable to income excluded under the foreign earned income exclusion cannot also be credited.IRS · foreign tax credit ↗ IRS · Publication 54 ↗
Foreign earned income exclusionPotentially available for qualified foreign earned income with a foreign tax home and qualifying residence or presence; investment income is not earned income.IRS · Publication 54 ↗
Currency gainThe IRS explains that disposition of nonfunctional currency can result in gain or loss based on amount realized and adjusted basis. Individual exceptions and character require specialist review.IRS · foreign currency disposition ↗
U.S.–China treatyTreaty benefits depend on residency, type of income, conditions and the saving clause. U.S. citizenship alone does not guarantee an exemption.IRS · U.S.–China tax treaty ↗
FBARA U.S. person with required interest or authority files if the aggregate foreign financial account value exceeds $10,000 at any time during the calendar year. Annual due date is April 15 with automatic extension to October 15.IRS · FBAR ↗
Form 8938Separate foreign financial asset reporting may apply; thresholds vary by residence and filing status. Filing it does not replace an FBAR.IRS · Form 8938 reporting ↗
Foreign gift or inheritanceForm 3520 reporting can apply to gifts or bequests exceeding $100,000 from a nonresident alien individual or foreign estate, with aggregation rules. Reporting does not by itself establish a tax.IRS · foreign gifts and bequests ↗
U.S. cash declarationCurrency and monetary instruments transported into or out of the United States over $10,000 require FinCEN Form 105; family declaration rules also apply.CBP · currency reporting ↗
