COMPARISON METHOD

Measure the economic conversion cost

For an identical amount of CNY, calculate reference USD = CNY ÷ reference rate and quoted USD = CNY ÷ quoted rate when both rates are expressed as CNY per USD. Spread cost = reference USD − quoted USD. Add separately charged outbound, intermediary and receiving fees to obtain a transfer-cost estimate. Avoid double-counting fees already deducted from the quote.

Then separately compare any applicable unpaid underlying tax, interest, penalties and U.S. FX consequences. A reference rate is a benchmark, not necessarily an obtainable price. No actual rates, fees, taxes or amounts are known, so no numeric result is presented.
