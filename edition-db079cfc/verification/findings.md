# Irregularities found and what happened to them — 2026-11-11

Severity is about the effect on a reader who acts on the published numbers, not about
effort. "Fixed" means the change is in this pull request and the checker now enforces it.

| ID | Where | Finding | Effect | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P-01 | `guide/data/prices.json`, whole site | The whole guide was priced on one Bank of China board reading from 2026-11-10 17:39:58 (buy 665.71 / sell 668.51). The board had moved to 666.30 / 669.11 by 2026-11-11 10:25:02 — a 0.09% move on the rate at which CNY buys USD. | Every published total was about 0.09% stale: USD 93.36 → 93.39 at USD 10,000 and USD 939.01 → 941.20 at USD 400,000. | **Fixed**: board re-read, all 760 ladder rows recomputed. `verify.py` recomputes every total from `prices.json` and fails if a quoted figure is missing from the source text. |
| P-02 | `guide/build/verify.py` | The ATM check compared against a hard-coded string (`CNY 751.11`) that was itself derived from the old exchange rate. It was not verifying arithmetic; it was verifying that nothing had changed. | Any rate refresh would either fail the build or, if edited by hand, silently bless a wrong figure. | **Fixed**: the expectation is now computed from `prices.json` with exact fractions and half-up cent rounding, so the check tests arithmetic rather than memory. |
| P-03 | `guide/data/prices.json` (Wise), `guide/costs.html` | The Wise ladder only prices an amount if `amount × midpoint` equals one of five sampled CNY amounts exactly. After the rate moved, the old five quotes (66,711 … 266,844) no longer matched, so the whole Wise column would have gone blank. | A blank column reads as "Wise cannot do this", which is false. | **Fixed**: the comparison API was queried again at the five new amounts (66,770.5 / 133,541 / 200,311.5 / 267,082 / 300,000) at 2026-11-11T02:35Z. The limitation is stated on the costs page and in F06. |
| P-04 | `guide/summary.html`, `EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md` | The summary said "the USD 50,000 quota is for domestic individuals and permanent-resident card holders". Effectively right for a *purchase* of FX, but it does not state the other limb: `汇发〔2007〕1号` art. 2 subjects **everyone's** sale of FX (结汇) to the annual total and only a domestic individual's purchase (购汇) to it. | A reader who is a foreign passport holder but sells FX the other way could believe no total applies. | **Fixed**: both limbs are now stated, with the article cited, and the split is the first question on the new questions page (Q01). |
| P-05 | `guide/data/sources.json` | Two of 33 sources were only ever seen as a search excerpt: the PBOC notice on the CNY 20,000 cash limit (`CN-CNYCASH`, via a gqb.gov.cn article) and arts. 32/63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law (`CN-TAXCOLL`). | Figures that depend on them (CNY 20,000 cash per trip; the 0.05%/day late surcharge and the 0.5–5× evasion fine) are marked unverified. | **Open** — flagged as F10 and F11 on the Flagged for Review page. Not filled in, not guessed. |
| P-06 | Branch `arena/1ee3ca2b-china1`, path `edition-m/**` | A complete parallel edition was built and pushed at 2026-11-10 10:30 UTC with its own CI workflow, and was never merged and never opened as a pull request. It is now a day old with no commits. | The repository carries an unreviewed duplicate of the same research that no page links to. | **Open, flagged.** This session did not touch that branch or path, and did not copy figures from it. Taking it over or deleting it is the owner's call. |
| P-07 | Pull requests #39 and #45 | Both are open since 2026-11-10 05:57 and 06:04. #39 conflicts with `main`; #45 only edits `COORDINATION.md`. | Reviewers see two stale pull requests next to the live one. | **Open, flagged.** Left untouched. |
| P-08 | `docs/`, `dossier/`, `edition-b` … `edition-l`, `edition-abd04d5f`, `edition-12670774` | The archived editions were priced on at least four different same-week CNY/USD readings and some present abolished rules as live. | A reader who lands on an archived page instead of the guide gets a different number for the same transfer. | **Open by design.** The archive page says "do not take figures from these pages" and the repository-review page lists the rule-by-rule differences. | P-09 | The tax-filing question (Q10 / F01) | `国家税务总局 国家外汇管理局公告2013年第40号` requires a filing for a single outward payment of USD 50,000 or more and lists a foreign individual's China work pay; art. 3(13) exempts a domestic individual's private use; art. 4 sends a foreign individual's outward payment to the individual FX rules. The text does not resolve which governs a foreign individual remitting their own after-tax salary. | It is the most likely cause of a wasted branch visit, and it cannot be settled from the rule text. | **Open, flagged (F01).** Published as a question with the exact question to put to the bank and to 12366, and no answer inferred. |
| P-10 | House-sale proceeds (Q07 / F17) | The Shanghai government FAQ (sourced to Bank of China) describes a bank process on passport plus sale contract; the 2006 Measures art. 22 require SAFE approval for a foreign individual's house-sale proceeds. Two official pages, two procedures. | The reader cannot tell from the pages whether to queue at the bank or at SAFE. | **Open, flagged (F17).** Both texts are quoted and the reader is told to ask the branch in writing. |
