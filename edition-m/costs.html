How to read the figures

  • Rate used: Bank of China board, 2026/11/10 17:39:58 Beijing time: buys USD at 665.71, sells at 668.51 yuan per USD 100. Midpoint 667.11. Conversion margin one way: 0.21% S30.
  • Never estimated: where a component has no official figure it is blank and links to its flag. A row with a blank shows the confirmed part only.
  • One wire or several: the wire tables price one wire for the whole amount. Each extra wire adds the fixed charges again: CNY 150 cable, the commission, USD 25 and USD 15 S32 S40.
  • Tax is separate: these are transfer and conversion costs. Tax on the money itself and any US currency gain are on the tax page.
