Bank and provider transfers

Convert at a Chinese bank or licensed provider and send the money electronically to your own account.

Documented income: buy USD at your Chinese bank and wire it M01

Lawful with documents $311.32 0.31% at USD 100,000

Who: Passport holders (境外个人) whose yuan is lawful China-source current-account income such as salary, with proof of amount and tax vouchers. S01 S14

Limit: No annual cap in the rule; the amount you can buy follows the amounts on your documents.

PointSource
ForLowest verified all-in cost from USD 20,000 upward: 0.62% at USD 20,000 falling to 0.24% at USD 400,000.S32 S30 S40
ForNo yearly ceiling for a foreign passport holder; the rule ties the amount to documented income.S01
ForThe full-amount option fixes the intermediary-bank cost at USD 25 per wire.S32
AgainstNeeds proof of source with amounts, including tax vouchers. Untaxed income must be regularised first.S01
AgainstThe bank may refuse a transaction it doubts; it must explain why and how to appeal.S01
AgainstThe board rate is indicative; the executed rate is whatever the bank's channel shows at that moment.S30

Wise: pay in yuan by local bank transfer, receive USD in your own account M03

Lawful with documents $597.51 0.60% at USD 100,000

Who: People who are not mainland Chinese, who work or have worked in China, and who hold a Chinese tax record showing tax paid within the last 3 years. S37 S38

Limit: CNY 300,000 per transfer from a bank app (CNY 50,000 via Alipay). Overall limit = salary after tax on the tax records you submit.

PointSource
ForCheapest verified option at USD 10,000: 0.65%.S39
ForWise states it converts at the mid-market rate and shows the fee up front.S38
ForDone online; documents are requested once, then again only when the limit is used up.S37
AgainstSalary only in practice: the limit equals after-tax salary shown on tax records from the last 3 years.S37
AgainstBetween 0.59% and 0.65% at every size, so dearer than a documented bank wire from USD 20,000 upward.S39 S38
AgainstUSD 400,000 needs at least 9 separate transfers because of the CNY 300,000 cap.S37
AgainstPaying in through Alipay costs about three times as much: CNY 1,312.27 against CNY 434.84 quoted for the same CNY 66,711.S39

Send the yuan itself abroad, then convert outside mainland China M04

Lawful with documents $61.46 known part; rest blank F10 at USD 100,000

Who: Foreign individuals remitting lawful China-source yuan income such as pay, under the central bank's 2018 notice. S23 S24

Limit: None stated; the bank applies know-your-customer, know-your-business and due-diligence checks.

PointSource
ForNo conversion in China, so no Chinese bank dealing margin. The central bank's own example says it saved the conversion cost.S24
ForExplicitly permitted for a foreign individual's lawful yuan income.S23
AgainstYou need an account outside mainland China that accepts yuan; which institutions offer one was not researched.S23
AgainstThe offshore conversion rate is set by the receiving institution and was not verified, so the total cost is blank.S24
AgainstDocuments are still needed: the bank must be satisfied the income is lawful.S23

Routes for property, emigration and inheritance money

Capital-type money. Extra documents; two of the three need SAFE approval first.

Proceeds from selling your mainland home M05

Lawful with documents $311.32 0.31% at USD 100,000

Who: Foreign individuals who sell a commercial home in mainland China, including former Chinese nationals who bought it after acquiring foreign nationality. S12 S13

Limit: Sale price minus the taxes of this sale (your share if co-owned), and not more than the amount on the tax certificate.

PointSource
ForHandled directly at the bank; the current SAFE guide lists bank documents only.S12 S14
ForThe whole after-tax price is remittable in one go.S12
AgainstTitle must already be transferred and the sale taxes paid before the bank will act.S12
AgainstThe bank will not remit more than the amount recorded on the tax certificate.S12
AgainstAn older rule still says SAFE approval is needed; current SAFE material says the bank handles it. See Flagged for Review.S02 S14

Capped and physical channels

Lawful but limited by yearly or per-trip caps. Useful for small sums only.

USD banknotes carried out under a bank permit M09

Lawful, capped $209.86 known part; rest blank F14 at USD 100,000

Who: Anyone already entitled to buy the USD (see M01, M08 or M13). Not allowed for emigrant or inheritance transfers. S01 S32

Limit: USD 10,000 per trip with a bank permit; USD 5,000 without. Withdrawal of USD cash: USD 10,000 a day.

PointSource
ForNo wire or receiving-bank fee; Bank of China's permit fee is waived until 31 Dec 2026.S32
ForThe cash selling rate equalled the spot selling rate on the board read.S30
AgainstUSD 400,000 would take 40 trips.S01
AgainstCash is only the transport: you still need the right to buy the USD.S01
AgainstAbove USD 10,000 a SAFE permit is given only for travel to a few kinds of destination.S01
AgainstUS Customs: more than USD 10,000 must be reported on FinCEN Form 105; unreported cash can be seized.S60
