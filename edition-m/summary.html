Executive summary

#FindingSource
1China does not tax the conversion; it controls it. No conversion or remittance tax appears in the rules read. A foreign passport holder may buy foreign exchange against lawful China-source income on proof of source that includes tax vouchers, with no annual ceiling, but has no USD 50,000 no-questions quota.S01 S14 S04
2Cheapest verified route from USD 20,000 up: a documented bank wire. At Bank of China's tariff, with full-amount delivery and a USD 15 receiving fee, the all-in cost is $124.50 (0.62%) at USD 20,000, $311.32 (0.31%) at USD 100,000 and $940.90 (0.24%) at USD 400,000.S32 S30 S40
3At USD 10,000 Wise is cheaper: $65.18 (0.65%) against $93.49 (0.93%). Wise stays near 0.59% at every size, is limited to salary shown on Chinese tax records, and caps each transfer at CNY 300,000.S39 S38 S37
4The same wire cost applies to home-sale proceeds (bank handles it on the tax certificate), to emigrant and inherited assets (after SAFE approval: 5 working days to accept, 20 to decide, no fee) and to yuan bought with foreign currency in the last 24 months.S12 S07 S08 S01
5Capped channels do not scale: overseas ATM cash is limited to CNY 100,000 a year (27 years for USD 400,000, about 1.13% in bank fees alone); USD cash is USD 10,000 a trip (40 trips); yuan cash is CNY 20,000 a trip (134 trips).S15 S32 S01 S19
6The tax exposure is on the money, not the transfer. China: income tax if unpaid (3%–45% on salary-type income, 20% on interest, dividends, rent, asset sales). United States: worldwide income is taxable when earned, with credit for Chinese income tax.S18 S62 S61
7Converting can itself create US income. Yuan acquired at the 2024 average rate (7.189) and converted today (6.6851) shows a gain of $699.46 per USD 10,000, $27,978.56 at USD 400,000. Yuan from 2021 shows a loss. The tax is the gain times your own rate.S52 S53 S30
8Unreported accounts cost more than any transfer fee: up to USD 16,536 per missed FBAR when not willful, and the greater of USD 165,353 or half the balance when willful. Late FBARs carry no penalty if the income was reported and taxed; otherwise the streamlined procedures cost 5% for US residents and nothing for those living abroad.S58 S56 S55
9Prohibited shortcuts carry fines of up to 30% of the amount, and up to 100% in serious cases: splitting through other people's quotas, underground banks, false documents and crypto conversion. SAFE publishes the fines, for example CNY 2.157 million on USD 3.331 million.S04 S21 S25

Which tax paper a foreign individual needs above USD 50,000The 2013 tax-filing notice lists a foreign individual's China pay among payments needing a filing above USD 50,000, and also says foreign individuals follow the personal foreign-exchange rules. Property-sale proceeds: SAFE approval or bank onlyThe 2006 central-bank measures say a foreign individual's home-sale yuan may be converted with SAFE approval.
