Documented China income → Bank of China wire to your own US account

StepWhat to doSource
1Sort every deposit in the account by where it came from. Only documented, lawful income qualifies under this route.S03 S04
2Collect the papers for each source: employment or service contract, income proof, and the tax record or tax-payment certificate issued by the Chinese tax authority.S03 S04
3If tax on some of the income was never paid, settle it with the tax bureau first and obtain the certificate. See the Taxes page for what that costs.S14 S16
4For any single remittance above USD 50,000, ask the branch whether it wants the outbound-payment tax filing form or accepts the tax-payment certificate (flag F15).S17 S03
5Go to the branch that holds the account with your passport and the originals. The bank checks the source of the RMB, not the purpose of the dollars.S04 S03
6Sign the foreign-exchange purchase. The bank sells you dollars at its selling rate of the moment.S09 S03
7Fill in the bank's outward remittance form with your own name, your US account number and the US bank's SWIFT code. The form itself was not reviewed; the bank supplies it.S03
8Choose standard charges, or pay USD 25 more for full-amount arrival so intermediary banks deduct nothing.S10
9Pay the bank fee: 0.1% of the amount (minimum CNY 50, maximum CNY 260) plus CNY 150 cable fee per wire.S10
10The US bank credits the wire and charges its incoming-wire fee (Chase: USD 15, or USD 0 in the cases it lists). Keep every paper.S31
11US filings afterwards: FBAR if your foreign accounts together exceeded USD 10,000 at any time in the year; Form 8938 above its thresholds; report any interest earned.S23 S25

Convert back RMB you exchanged from foreign currency

StepWhat to doSource
1Find the original exchange memo for the foreign currency you converted to RMB. It is valid for 24 months from the exchange date.S03
2Take the memo and your passport to the bank and convert the unused RMB back.S03
3Remit the dollars from your foreign-currency account with your passport, as in the wire steps above.S03 S10

Proceeds of selling your home

StepWhat to doSource
1Complete the sale and the ownership-transfer registration; the registry checks the income-tax clearance of the sale.S14 S18
2Pay the taxes of the sale. Income tax on the gain is 20% of sale price minus original cost and reasonable expenses.S14
3Collect: passport, transfer contract and registration proof, and the tax filing form (above USD 50,000) or other tax-payment proof.S18 S17
4Apply at the bank. The amount may not exceed the sale price minus the taxes of the sale, and must match the tax document.S18
5Convert and wire as in the wire steps above. Confirm the current document list with the bank first (flag F14).S18 S10

Inherited estate

StepWhat to doSource
1Turn the inherited assets into cash in your own name; the property must be lawfully yours and undisputed.S05
2Prepare the documents the Measures list: written application, application form, identity documents, proof of the inheritance, the deceased's property documents and tax certificates. Get the exact list from the SAFE branch before you go.S05
3File with the SAFE branch where the deceased's household was registered. Property documents no longer need notarisation, and heirs may combine applications.S05 S06
4After approval, the bank sells the dollars and sends them straight to your own account abroad — in one or several remittances. No cash withdrawal.S05 S06
5US side: a bequest from a foreign estate above USD 100,000 in the year is reported on Form 3520.S24

Emigrant property transfer (former mainland residents only)

StepWhat to doSource
1Check that you qualify: you moved from mainland China and settled abroad, and the property was yours before you obtained that status.S05
2Prepare: written application, applicant information form, application form, proof that household registration was cancelled, proof of settlement abroad certified by a Chinese consulate, property documents, tax certificates from where the property or income is located.S05
3File with the SAFE branch of your former household registration. Since 2014 there is no head-office filing above CNY 500,000 and no forced staging.S06
4With the approval document the bank converts and remits to your own account abroad, in one or several payments.S05 S06

Company profit or liquidation proceeds

StepWhat to doSource
1Profit: dividends paid abroad above USD 50,000 in one payment need a tax filing with the tax authority first. The bank's own document list was not re-read; ask the company's bank.S17
2Liquidation: after liquidation and tax, the RMB belonging to the foreign investor may be converted and remitted.S01
3The bank converts the investor's RMB and remits it abroad.S01

Wise, sending from CNY

StepWhat to doSource
1Check eligibility: not a mainland Chinese national, working or having worked in China, with a Chinese tax record showing tax paid in the last 3 years.S13
2Open a personal Wise account and add your own account abroad as the recipient — sending to someone else is not possible.S13
3Upload the tax record when asked; first-time verification takes 1–2 working days. Your overall limit is about your after-tax China salary.S13
4Set up each transfer (up to CNY 300,000 from a China bank app, CNY 50,000 from Alipay) and pay it from your Chinese bank. The fee is shown before you confirm.S13

Cash withdrawals abroad with a mainland card

StepWhat to doSource
1Keep all your mainland cards together within CNY 100,000 of overseas cash withdrawals per calendar year and CNY 10,000 per card per day.S08
2Withdraw at an ATM abroad. A BOC Great Wall debit card costs CNY 12 plus 1% per withdrawal, at most CNY 112; the ATM owner may add a surcharge shown on screen.S10
3Deposit the cash in your US account. Do not use anyone else's card or lend yours.S08

Carrying dollar cash

StepWhat to doSource
1Buy the dollars at the bank under one of the entitlement routes — the same documents are required as for a wire.S03
2Withdraw the notes. More than USD 10,000 in a day needs a prior filing with the SAFE branch.S03
3Up to USD 5,000 per crossing needs no permit. For more than USD 5,000 and up to USD 10,000, obtain the carrying permit from the bank (BOC: CNY 10, waived until 2026-12-31).S07 S10
4Do not carry more than USD 10,000: it is not allowed in principle, and more than USD 10,000 must also be reported to US customs on FinCEN Form 105.S07 S29
