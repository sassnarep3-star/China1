Documented income: buy USD at your Chinese bank and wire it M01

Lawful with documents $311.32 0.31% at USD 100,000 · all amounts · pros and cons

Who: Passport holders (境外个人) whose yuan is lawful China-source current-account income such as salary, with proof of amount and tax vouchers.

StepWhat to doSource
1Confirm how the bank classifies you. A foreign passport holder is a 境外个人 and has no USD 50,000 purchase quota, unless holding a Chinese permanent-residence ID (see M13).S02 S14
2Collect proof of where the yuan came from, showing amounts: for salary, the employment contract and pay records plus the tax voucher. The rule's wording is 有交易额的购汇资金来源材料（含税务凭证).S01
3Get the tax voucher from the tax bureau. SAFE Xiamen lists two accepted forms: the 服务贸易等项目对外支付税务备案表 or a tax payment certificate (税收通用缴款书). Ask your bank which it wants for payments above USD 50,000.S14 S16
4Go to the bank that holds the yuan with your passport and the documents. The bank reviews them for authenticity and consistency.S01
5Buy USD at the bank's selling rate (现汇卖出价). The board showed 668.51 yuan per USD 100 at 17:39:58 on 10 Nov 2026.S30
6Instruct a telegraphic transfer (电汇) to your own US account. Bank of China charges 0.1% of the amount (minimum CNY 50, maximum CNY 260) plus CNY 150 cable fee, the same online as at the counter.S32 S33
7Ask for 全额到账 (full-amount arrival): plus USD 25 per wire, or USD 15 if the receiving bank keeps an account at BOC New York. Without it, intermediary banks' deductions are passed on at cost and are not published.S32
8Your US institution credits the wire less its own incoming-wire fee: USD 15 at Chase and Bank of America, none at Schwab.S40 S41 S42
9Keep the papers. The SAFE guide has banks keep their review records for 5 years and asks institutions and individuals to keep transaction documents for 5 years.S01
10On the US side, check FBAR and Form 8938 filing for the Chinese account and work out any currency gain (see the tax page).S50 S52

Same route through HSBC China (Premier or Advance account) M02

Lawful with documents $107.94 known part; rest blank F04 at USD 100,000 · all amounts · pros and cons

Who: As M01, for customers of HSBC Bank (China). Premier needs CNY 500,000 average balance; Advance is offered only to specific groups since 18 Mar 2026.

StepWhat to doSource
1Prepare the same proof of source and tax vouchers as in M01; the foreign-exchange rule is the same at every bank.S01
2Convert at HSBC China's quoted rate and instruct an outward telegraphic transfer.S35
3Pay 0.1% of the amount (minimum CNY 100, maximum CNY 500) plus CNY 120 cable fee.S35
4Choose who bears onward charges: SHA (deducted from the payment), BEN, or OUR. For full-amount USD delivery HSBC pre-collects CNY 200 and bills any shortfall later.S35
5Your US institution applies its incoming-wire fee.S40 S42

Wise: pay in yuan by local bank transfer, receive USD in your own account M03

Lawful with documents $597.51 0.60% at USD 100,000 · all amounts · pros and cons

Who: People who are not mainland Chinese, who work or have worked in China, and who hold a Chinese tax record showing tax paid within the last 3 years.

Official timing: Verification 1–2 working days; money reaches Wise in 0–1 working days; conversion up to 2 working days.

StepWhat to doSource
1Check you qualify: not mainland Chinese, worked in China, tax record with tax paid in the last 3 years.S37
2Open a personal Wise account and set up a CNY to USD transfer to an account in your own name. Transfers to other people are not allowed.S37
3Upload your Chinese tax record. First-time verification takes 1–2 working days. Your overall limit is set from it.S37
4Pay Wise from your own Chinese bank account by local transfer to the unique account details shown (branch 拉卡拉-备付金账户; choose 支付机构转账 if the app asks). Maximum CNY 300,000 per transfer.S37
5Wise converts and pays out USD to your US account. Fee quoted for CNY 66,711: CNY 434.84.S39
6Repeat until your limit is used. The limit does not expire; upload newer tax records to raise it.S37

Send the yuan itself abroad, then convert outside mainland China M04

Lawful with documents $61.46 known part; rest blank F10 at USD 100,000 · all amounts · pros and cons

Who: Foreign individuals remitting lawful China-source yuan income such as pay, under the central bank's 2018 notice.

StepWhat to doSource
1Hold an account in your own name outside mainland China that can receive yuan.S23
2Take your passport and proof of the income (contract, pay records, tax vouchers) to your Chinese bank and ask for a cross-border RMB remittance of lawful income under 银发〔2018〕3号.S23
3Pay the remittance fee. Bank of China prices yuan and foreign-currency cross-border wires on the same line: 0.1% (CNY 50–260) plus CNY 150 cable, or CNY 80 to Hong Kong, Macau or Taiwan.S32
4Convert to USD at the receiving institution. That rate is outside the sources read.S24

Holders of a Chinese permanent-residence ID: USD 50,000 a year without documents M13

Lawful, capped $412.78 0.41% at USD 100,000 · all amounts · pros and cons

Who: Foreign nationals holding a 外国人永久居留身份证. Other foreign passport holders do not have this purchase quota.

StepWhat to doSource
1Confirm you hold a Chinese permanent-residence ID; the purchase quota applies to those holders.S01 S14
2Buy up to USD 50,000 in the calendar year with your ID at the counter or in e-banking.S01
3Wire it to your own US account at the bank's tariff (Bank of China: 0.1%, CNY 50–260, plus CNY 150 cable).S32
4Repeat in the next calendar year; quota resets on 1 January and unused quota lapses on 31 December.S14

Proceeds from selling your mainland home M05

Lawful with documents $311.32 0.31% at USD 100,000 · all amounts · pros and cons

Who: Foreign individuals who sell a commercial home in mainland China, including former Chinese nationals who bought it after acquiring foreign nationality.

StepWhat to doSource
1Sign the sale contract and complete the ownership transfer at the real-estate authority.S13 S12
2Pay the taxes on the sale at the local tax authority and get the proof of tax payment.S13
3If the remittance exceeds USD 50,000, also obtain the Tax Filing Form for External Payments (服务贸易等项目对外支付税务备案表).S13 S12
4Bring to the bank: passport; the registered transfer contract and registration certificate; the tax voucher; a notarised power of attorney and the agent's ID if someone acts for you.S12
5The bank checks three things: amount ≤ price minus this sale's taxes; amount ≤ the tax certificate figure; title already transferred.S12
6Buy USD and wire it to your account; fees as in the Bank of China table.S32 S30

Emigrant asset transfer (移民财产转移) M06

Needs SAFE approval $311.32 0.31% at USD 100,000 · all amounts · pros and cons

Who: People who moved abroad from mainland China and obtained foreign permanent residence or citizenship, for lawful assets they owned in China before obtaining it.

Official timing: SAFE: acceptance within 5 working days; decision within 20 working days, extendable once by 10.

StepWhat to doSource
1Turn the assets into yuan in your own name.S05
2Prepare the file: written application with the 个人财产转移业务申请表; foreign passport or ID; proof of settlement abroad issued or authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate (or Apostille-form proof); the certificate that your household registration was cancelled; proof of income source and property rights; the tax certificate from the tax authority.S07
3If someone will act for you, add an agency agreement signed by both of you and the agent's ID.S07
4File in person at the SAFE branch for your former household-registration place.S09 S07
5SAFE accepts within 5 working days and decides within 20 working days. No fee. The result is a 资本项目外汇业务核准件.S07
6Take the approval to a bank, buy USD and wire it straight to your own account in your country of residence, at once or in instalments within the approved amount.S05 S06 S10
7Bank fees and conversion margin are as in the Bank of China table.S32 S30

Inherited assets transfer (继承财产转移) M07

Needs SAFE approval $311.32 0.31% at USD 100,000 · all amounts · pros and cons

Who: Foreign citizens who lawfully inherit an estate in mainland China.

Official timing: SAFE: acceptance within 5 working days; decision within 20 working days, extendable once by 10.

StepWhat to doSource
1Obtain proof that you inherited: for example an inheritance notarisation or will notarisation.S08
2Turn the inherited assets into yuan and collect the deceased's property-rights documents and the tax certificate.S05 S08
3Prepare your identity papers: foreign passport, ID of your country of residence, and proof of settlement there issued or authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate (or Apostille-form proof).S08
4File the written application and 个人财产转移业务申请表 at the SAFE branch for the deceased's last household-registration place.S09 S08
5SAFE accepts within 5 working days and decides within 20 working days. No fee.S08
6With the approval, buy USD at a bank and wire it to your own account in your country of residence. No cash withdrawal.S10 S06
7In the US, check whether Form 3520 Part IV applies to the bequest.S51

Convert back yuan you originally bought with foreign currency M08

Lawful with documents $311.32 0.31% at USD 100,000 · all amounts · pros and cons

Who: Foreign individuals holding yuan left over from their own earlier foreign-currency exchange, with the original exchange memo.

StepWhat to doSource
1Find the original exchange memos (原兑换水单) dated within the last 24 months.S01
2Take your passport and the memos to the bank and convert the unused yuan back.S01
3Remit the foreign currency from your foreign-currency account; a foreign individual does this on passport alone.S01 S14
4Foreign currency that was wired in and never converted can be returned by the same route on passport alone.S02

USD banknotes carried out under a bank permit M09

Lawful, capped $209.86 known part; rest blank F14 at USD 100,000 · all amounts · pros and cons

Who: Anyone already entitled to buy the USD (see M01, M08 or M13). Not allowed for emigrant or inheritance transfers.

StepWhat to doSource
1Buy the USD on a lawful basis first (documents as in M01 or a memo as in M08).S01
2Withdraw USD cash: up to USD 10,000 equivalent a day on ID.S01
3Carrying USD 5,000 or less: no permit. More than USD 5,000 and up to USD 10,000: get a 携带外汇出境许可证 from the bank. Bank of China lists CNY 10 per permit, free from 1 Apr 2025 to 31 Dec 2026.S01 S32
4Carry the permit with the cash when you leave; customs releases foreign cash within these limits.S01 S20
5On arrival in the US, file FinCEN Form 105 if you carry more than USD 10,000.S60

UnionPay debit card: cash withdrawals at ATMs abroad M11

Lawful, capped $1,120.52 known part; rest blank F11 at USD 100,000 · all amounts · pros and cons

Who: Holders of a bank card issued in mainland China.

StepWhat to doSource
1Use your own mainland-issued card. Limits: CNY 100,000 a calendar year over all your cards, CNY 10,000 per card per day.S15
2Withdraw at an ATM abroad that takes UnionPay. Bank of China charges CNY 12 plus 1% of the amount, at most CNY 112 per withdrawal.S32 S34
3Check the screen for the ATM owner's surcharge before confirming.S32
4Stop at the yearly cap; exceeding it blocks overseas withdrawals for the rest of that year and the next.S15
