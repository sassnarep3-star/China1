## Findings

1. China does not tax the conversion; it controls it. No conversion or remittance tax appears in the rules read. A foreign passport holder may buy foreign exchange against lawful China-source income on proof of source that includes tax vouchers, with no annual ceiling, but has no USD 50,000 no-questions quota. [S01](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm) [S14](https://www.safe.gov.cn/xiamen/file/file/20211022/f4794075e84e40dd9394be523a52731b.pdf) [S04](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2008-08/06/content_2602514.htm)
2. Cheapest verified route from USD 20,000 up: a documented bank wire. At Bank of China's tariff, with full-amount delivery and a USD 15 receiving fee, the all-in cost is $124.50 (0.62%) at USD 20,000, $311.32 (0.31%) at USD 100,000 and $940.90 (0.24%) at USD 400,000. [S32](https://pic.bankofchina.com/bocappd/pricelist/202511/P020251110646838136627.pdf) [S30](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/) [S40](https://www.chase.com/content/dam/chase-ux/documents/personal/checking/ABSF-en.pdf)
3. At USD 10,000 Wise is cheaper: $65.18 (0.65%) against $93.49 (0.93%). Wise stays near 0.59% at every size, is limited to salary shown on Chinese tax records, and caps each transfer at CNY 300,000. [S39](https://wise.com/gateway/v1/price?sourceAmount=66711&sourceCurrency=CNY&targetCurrency=USD&profileCountry=CN) [S38](https://wise.com/us/send-money/send-money-to-the-usa-from-china?sourceAmount=266844) [S37](https://wise.com/help/articles/2955298/guide-to-cny-transfers)
4. The same wire cost applies to home-sale proceeds (bank handles it on the tax certificate), to emigrant and inherited assets (after SAFE approval: 5 working days to accept, 20 to decide, no fee) and to yuan bought with foreign currency in the last 24 months. [S12](http://m.safe.gov.cn/safe/file/file/20250915/16a1dd8e54f94d26969b0a9b781106a8.pdf) [S07](https://www.safe.gov.cn/hebei/file/file/20240816/86fd609207224d71b23cfda39ebe4bbb.pdf) [S08](https://www.safe.gov.cn/hebei/file/file/20240816/186ef46ed22745009f16c6715f2a54f1.pdf) [S01](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm)
5. Capped channels do not scale: overseas ATM cash is limited to CNY 100,000 a year (27 years for USD 400,000, about 1.13% in bank fees alone); USD cash is USD 10,000 a trip (40 trips); yuan cash is CNY 20,000 a trip (134 trips). [S15](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2017/1230/8130.html) [S32](https://pic.bankofchina.com/bocappd/pricelist/202511/P020251110646838136627.pdf) [S01](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm) [S19](http://guangzhou.customs.gov.cn/guangzhou_customs/381679/3795484/3800229/index.html)
6. The tax exposure is on the money, not the transfer. China: income tax if unpaid (3%–45% on salary-type income, 20% on interest, dividends, rent, asset sales). United States: worldwide income is taxable when earned, with credit for Chinese income tax. [S18](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/eng/c102962/c102967/c102997/c103004/c5245849/content.html) [S62](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p54) [S61](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-taxes-that-qualify-for-the-foreign-tax-credit)
7. Converting can itself create US income. Yuan acquired at the 2024 average rate (7.189) and converted today (6.6851) shows a gain of $699.46 per USD 10,000, $27,978.56 at USD 400,000. Yuan from 2021 shows a loss. The tax is the gain times your own rate. [S52](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm) [S53](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/yearly-average-currency-exchange-rates) [S30](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/)
8. Unreported accounts cost more than any transfer fee: up to USD 16,536 per missed FBAR when not willful, and the greater of USD 165,353 or half the balance when willful. Late FBARs carry no penalty if the income was reported and taxed; otherwise the streamlined procedures cost 5% for US residents and nothing for those living abroad. [S58](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1010/subpart-H/section-1010.821) [S56](https://www.taxpayeradvocate.irs.gov/get-help/international/foreign-bank-financial-accounts/) [S55](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-news/IR-14-073.pdf)
9. Prohibited shortcuts carry fines of up to 30% of the amount, and up to 100% in serious cases: splitting through other people's quotas, underground banks, false documents and crypto conversion. SAFE publishes the fines, for example CNY 2.157 million on USD 3.331 million. [S04](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2008-08/06/content_2602514.htm) [S21](http://m.safe.gov.cn/safe/2023/0804/23035.html) [S25](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/2026020619591971323/index.html)

## Open points

- **F01 Your own facts were not provided.** Source of the yuan, whether Chinese tax was paid on it, the identity document your bank holds for you, when the yuan was acquired, your US institution and your filing history are all unknown. Eligibility and tax cells that depend on them are blank. [S01](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm) [S14](https://www.safe.gov.cn/xiamen/file/file/20211022/f4794075e84e40dd9394be523a52731b.pdf)
- **F03 Intermediary-bank deductions on a standard wire.** Without the full-amount option, Bank of China passes on foreign banks' charges at cost. No amount is published, so the standard-wire total is not shown; the tables price the full-amount option instead. [S32](https://pic.bankofchina.com/bocappd/pricelist/202511/P020251110646838136627.pdf)
- **F04 HSBC China's exchange rate.** HSBC China's customer rate for USD was not read. Its conversion margin and therefore its all-in cost are blank; only its published fees are shown. [S35](https://www.hsbc.com.cn/content/dam/hsbc/cn/docs/document-download/tariff-of-accouts-and-services.pdf)
- **F09 Receiving fee for a Wise payout.** Wise pays out by local US bank transfer. The Chase fee schedule read lists wire fees only; whether a receiving institution charges for this kind of credit was not confirmed. The Wise table shows Wise's fee alone. [S40](https://www.chase.com/content/dam/chase-ux/documents/personal/checking/ABSF-en.pdf) [S38](https://wise.com/us/send-money/send-money-to-the-usa-from-china?sourceAmount=266844)
- **F10 Cross-border RMB: where to receive it and at what rate.** Which institutions outside mainland China will receive yuan for a US citizen, and their conversion rates, were not researched. Only the Chinese bank's sending fee is shown. [S23](https://www.safe.gov.cn/henan/2018/0321/306.html) [S24](https://beijing.pbc.gov.cn/beijing/132005/2025111421421910358/index.html)
- **F15 Which tax paper a foreign individual needs above USD 50,000.** The 2013 tax-filing notice lists a foreign individual's China pay among payments needing a filing above USD 50,000, and also says foreign individuals follow the personal foreign-exchange rules. SAFE Xiamen accepts either the filing form or a tax payment certificate. The sources do not settle which one a given bank will ask for. [S16](http://shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfw/zcfgk/jckss/201307/t403993.html) [S14](https://www.safe.gov.cn/xiamen/file/file/20211022/f4794075e84e40dd9394be523a52731b.pdf)
- **F16 Property-sale proceeds: SAFE approval or bank only.** The 2006 central-bank measures say a foreign individual's home-sale yuan may be converted with SAFE approval. SAFE Xiamen (2021) says such sales are handled directly at the bank, and the 2025 SAFE guide lists bank documents only. Treat the bank-only procedure as current and confirm with the bank. [S02](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2006/1225/5319.html) [S14](https://www.safe.gov.cn/xiamen/file/file/20211022/f4794075e84e40dd9394be523a52731b.pdf) [S12](http://m.safe.gov.cn/safe/file/file/20250915/16a1dd8e54f94d26969b0a9b781106a8.pdf)
- **F22 Currency-gain figures are an illustration.** They use IRS yearly average rates in place of the actual rates on the days the yuan was acquired; the IRS table stops at 2025, so yuan acquired in 2026 is not covered. Whether your holding is a personal transaction, and your tax rate, are not decided here, so no tax amount is shown.
