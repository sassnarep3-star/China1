Business and asset routes

Entity-level or asset-monetisation routes. They require a real underlying transaction, not a relabelled personal balance.

B01 · FIE dividend to a foreign individual shareholder

A real foreign-invested enterprise may distribute after-tax profits to a foreign individual shareholder. From 2026-09-01 the old foreign-individual dividend exemption is repealed and 20% IIT applies, subject to any valid treaty relief. This is not a way to relabel personal savings.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S31 · State Taxation Administration / Guangdong S33 · STA Shanghai S35 · STA Tianjin S36 · IRS S37 · IRS F33 F34

B02 · FIE dividend to a foreign corporate shareholder

A real FIE may remit dividends to a nonresident corporate shareholder under direct-investment profit rules. The domestic reduced EIT rate is generally 10% for the specified nonresident income, with treaty analysis separate.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S34 · STA Guangdong S35 · STA Tianjin S36 · IRS S48 · SAFE F34 F35

B03 · Registered capital reduction returned to a foreign investor

A foreign investor may receive proceeds of a lawful capital reduction after registration/change formalities. The 2024 capital-account guidelines contain a foreign-investor withdrawal-remittance section; detailed current document checklist was not fully extracted and is flagged.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S06 · SAFE Beijing S48 · SAFE F35 F36

B04 · Sale of equity in a Chinese entity to a foreign buyer

A real equity sale can generate proceeds remittable under direct-investment/asset-realisation rules after registration and tax. It is not a device to move an unrelated personal balance.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S06 · SAFE Beijing S31 · State Taxation Administration / Guangdong S48 · SAFE F35 F36

B05 · Liquidation of a foreign-invested enterprise

State Council Regulation Art. 22 expressly allows RMB belonging to foreign investors of a lawfully terminated FIE to be purchased and remitted after liquidation and tax payment.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S06 · SAFE Beijing S48 · SAFE F35

B06 · Genuine service or royalty export by a Chinese entity

A Chinese entity that actually provides services or licenses IP to a foreign customer may receive USD export proceeds. Service-trade rules require a real transaction; fabricated contracts are prohibited.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S35 · STA Tianjin S48 · SAFE F14 F35

B07 · Goods export / individual foreign-trade operator / cross-border e-commerce

Registered individual trade operators, cross-border e-commerce and market-procurement traders may settle genuine export proceeds through personal/business FX accounts under the 2020 guidelines. Documented e-commerce/market-procurement settlement does not consume the facilitation quota.

S02 · SAFE / PBOC S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette F14 F35

B08 · Outbound intercompany lending or cross-border cash pooling

Group treasury tools can move funds across borders under registration/quota rules, but they create repayment/repatriation obligations and are not personal savings conversion. Detailed current pooling rules were not fully verified here.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S06 · SAFE Beijing S48 · SAFE F35 F37
