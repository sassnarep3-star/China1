Methodology

Counting

A legal entitlement, an execution rail, an adjacent alternative and a rejected candidate are different objects. They are not added together as independent ways to move the same money. Every candidate still receives a full 40-point cost grid so omissions are visible.

Verification

Only directly checked official regulator/government/court pages and provider-origin tariff/eligibility pages are marked verified. Provider pages verify product terms, not legal entitlement. Unknown values are blank and flagged, never inferred or set to zero.

Cost model

Target USD is the desired receipt amount. CNY principal is unknown without an executable quote. Sender-fee cells show published tariff bounds/zeros only. FX cost, downstream charges, documentation, tax and all-in totals remain blank + UNVERIFIED. Observed Wise quotes are listed separately and not extrapolated.

Reproducibility

python3 edition-g/build/audit.py regenerates the repo-review manifest; python3 edition-g/build/build.py regenerates costs.json and docs; python3 edition-g/build/verify.py gates citations, grids, blanks and links. CI runs the same commands and requires a clean diff.

Edition G is additive. Earlier editions remain intact; see repo audit and Flagged for Review. Blank = unverified, never zero. Not legal or tax advice.
