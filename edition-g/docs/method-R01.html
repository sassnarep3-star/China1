Eligibility

  • Underlying entitlement approved by BOC.
  • Recipient and purpose comply with FX/AML rules.

Pros

  • Transparent published sender fees.
  • Optional USD full-arrival service for some intermediary routes.

Cons

  • FX spread is not fixed by the tariff.
  • Correspondent/receiver fees may apply.
  • Full-arrival prepayment is not a guarantee against every downstream charge.

Step-by-step

  1. Establish the legal basis with BOC (P01-P09 or a business route).
  2. Request an executable CNY-to-USD quote and the TT fee breakdown: commission, cable, optional full-arrival, expected intermediary charges.
  3. Confirm recipient name, account, SWIFT and address.
  4. Submit the remittance instruction and supporting documents.
  5. Track the SWIFT/credit advice and reconcile the U.S. credit against the quote.

Requirements

  • Approved legal basis
  • Recipient bank details
  • Executable quote
  • Fee disclosure
