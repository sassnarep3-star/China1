Execution rails

Banks, card networks, cash and money-transfer operators used to execute an entitlement. A rail does not create a legal right.

R01 · Bank of China personal telegraphic transfer

BOC executes an outward personal TT after the underlying purchase/remittance basis is approved. Published commission is 0.1% of the CNY remittance amount, min CNY50/max CNY260, plus CNY150 international cable. FX and overseas charges are separate.

R02 · Bank of China foreign-currency demand draft

BOC publishes the same 0.1% min50/max260 commission for a draft plus actual postage. A draft is a physical instrument that must be delivered and collected; it is not an instant USD bank credit.

S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S14 · Bank of China F15 F17

R03 · HSBC China Premier / Advance telegraphic transfer

HSBC China Premier/Advance personal outward TT commission is 0.1%, min CNY100/max CNY500, plus CNY120 cable. Other institutions' charges and taxes are excluded.

R04 · HSBC China Private Banking individual telegraphic transfer

HSBC China private-banking individual outward TT commission and cable are published as free. 'Free' excludes other institutions' charges, FX spread and taxes.

S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S18 · HSBC China S46 · People's Bank of China F15 F18

R05 · HSBC Global Transfer to self-name overseas HSBC account

HSBC Global Transfer to a self-name HSBC account in an enabled country/territory is free for Private Banking/Premier; Advance actual charge is USD20 per transaction (CNY145 is only an indicative reference). It moves existing FX between HSBC accounts; CNY must first be lawfully purchased into USD.

S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S18 · HSBC China S19 · HSBC China S46 · People's Bank of China F15 F19

R06 · Wise CNY transfer funded from a Chinese bank app

Wise supports eligible non-mainland-Chinese individuals with China work/tax records sending CNY to their own overseas account. Bank-app funding limit is CNY300k per transfer and an overall net-income limit applies. Public quotes were observed but are not locked and do not verify a 40-point current fee schedule.

R07 · Wise CNY transfer funded from Alipay

Same Wise eligibility as R06, but Alipay funding is capped at CNY50k per transfer. No current numeric fee schedule was verified; the November-2024 formula is historical only.

R08 · Western Union China outbound cash pickup

WU China partner banks offer outbound cash pickup; bank-account payout is temporarily unavailable. The receiver must collect cash and then convert/deposit it, with separate compliance and fees. No numeric China-outbound fee schedule was verified.

S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S25 · Western Union S30 · U.S. Customs and Border Protection S46 · People's Bank of China F24 F25

R09 · Bank of China Great Wall debit card overseas ATM cash

BOC Great Wall card overseas ATM fee is CNY12 + 1% per withdrawal, max CNY112, plus any foreign ATM surcharge. SAFE caps overseas cash at CNY100k/person/year and CNY10k/card/day. This produces cash, not a U.S. bank credit.

R10 · HSBC China debit card overseas ATM cash

HSBC China tariff: Premier at HSBC overseas ATMs free; Premier/Advance at UnionPay overseas ATMs CNY20 + 1%, max CNY110 per withdrawal. SAFE cash caps still apply.

R11 · Carry foreign-currency cash out of China and deposit in the U.S.

After lawful conversion/withdrawal, cash export is permit-controlled: up to USD5k no permit; USD5k-10k bank carry permit; above USD10k generally not allowed except special cases with SAFE permit. U.S. entry/exit over USD10k requires FinCEN 105. BOC's carry-permit fee is waived through 2026-12-31.
