X01 · Splitting across relatives' or friends' quotas

Borrowing or lending facilitation quotas to evade limits/authenticity management is prohibited and can trigger the SAFE watch list. No execution instructions.

X02 · Underground banking / matched off-book settlement (对敲)

Private or disguised FX dealing and unlawful transfer of domestic FX abroad are prohibited. No execution instructions.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S45 · People's Bank of China F42

X03 · Crypto / stablecoin bridge

PBOC-led 2021 notice treats fiat/virtual-currency exchange business and offshore exchanges serving mainland residents as illegal financial activity. Not a lawful CNY-to-USD route. No execution instructions.

S45 · People's Bank of China F42

X04 · Fabricated trade, service contract or invoice

Current-account rules require a real lawful transaction; fictitious trade backgrounds and false documents are prohibited. No execution instructions.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette F14

X05 · Using the facilitation quota for overseas property or securities

The domestic-person quota is a current-account convenience tier. Using it as a capital transfer to buy overseas property/securities misstates purpose and is not a lawful savings-export route. No execution instructions.

S02 · SAFE / PBOC S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette F13

X06 · Relative's gift as a workaround for your own stuck balance

A genuine foreign gift may have U.S. Form 3520 reporting, but the checked sources do not verify a general PRC outward-gift purchase/remittance entitlement for a domestic relative. It is not a proven route for your own balance.

S02 · SAFE / PBOC S43 · IRS F43

X07 · Revolut / Remitly / Xoom as China-outbound CNY rails

Checked provider/search material did not establish a mainland CNY-funded outbound own-account USD corridor for these brands. Inbound-to-China capability is not outbound capability. Unverified, not recommended.

S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette F44

X08 · Adding the U.S. 1% remittance tax to China-to-US costs

The U.S. 1% remittance transfer tax applies to specified physical-instrument-funded remittances sent from the United States. It is not a China-to-US transfer cost.
