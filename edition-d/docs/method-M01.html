Bank FX purchase + SWIFT wire (documented China-source income)

Route M01 · Bank & wire

The default route: buy USD at your Chinese bank against proof of lawful current-account income, then wire it to your US account.

Who can use it
A US citizen who is an overseas individual under the Chinese rules (passport holder) and whose RMB is lawful current-account income earned in China - salary, fees, rent, interest already taxed, pension paid from China.
Legal basis
S01 · PBOC Measures on Individual FX (2006) ↗ S02 · Detailed Rules on Individual FX (2007, as amended) ↗ S04 · Current-Account FX Business Guide 2020 ↗ S05 · SAFE Tianjin Q&A (2024) on Art.59 ↗ S18 · SAFE Xiamen - individual FX service guide (PDF) ↗ S03 · Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration ↗
Ceiling or gate
No annual facilitation quota applies to an overseas individual without a Chinese permanent-residence ID (S05). The USD 50,000/day figure is the ID-only processing threshold for remitting from an FX account, above which documents are required - it is not a prohibition (S02 Art.14).
Tax treatment
No Chinese tax on the conversion. Chinese tax was due on the income when it arose. US: no tax on moving your own principal (§988(e)); reporting duties apply.

Step by step

  1. Classify the money before going to the bank

    Write down what created the RMB: salary, service fees, rent, interest, pension, a property sale, an inheritance, or a gift. The route and the paperwork depend entirely on this, because current-account income and capital-account proceeds are governed by different articles (S01 Art.3).

    S01 · PBOC Measures on Individual FX (2006) ↗

  2. Assemble the income evidence

    For an overseas individual the bank is required to see the source-of-funds evidence together with your ID, and the rule expressly names tax vouchers (S02 Art.13(1); S04 Art.59).

    S02 · Detailed Rules on Individual FX (2007, as amended) ↗ S04 · Current-Account FX Business Guide 2020 ↗

  3. Confirm with the branch that you are being handled as an overseas individual

    The no-quota treatment depends on the passport, not on the branch's habit; the rule applies the annual quota only to holders of a Foreigner's Permanent Residence ID (S05). If the branch applies the USD 50,000 cap, ask it to reconcile that with S04 Art.59 and S05 before proceeding.

    S04 · Current-Account FX Business Guide 2020 ↗ S05 · SAFE Tianjin Q&A (2024) on Art.59 ↗

  4. Ask for the executed rate, the commission, the cable charge and the correspondent deduction in writing

    The published board is indicative only; the bank states that the actual transaction rate governs (S09). Charges beyond the bank's own commission are deducted in transit and are not set by regulation.

    S09 · Bank of China FX rate board ↗

  5. Execute the FX purchase and the outgoing wire in one visit

    The purchase and the remittance are separate operations on the bank's system; confirm the payment purpose code matches the documents.

    S01 · PBOC Measures on Individual FX (2006) ↗ S02 · Detailed Rules on Individual FX (2007, as amended) ↗

  6. Keep the exchange memo

    The memo is what allows reconversion of any unused RMB within 24 months (S02 Art.13(2)).

    S02 · Detailed Rules on Individual FX (2007, as amended) ↗

  7. Report the account and the transfer on the US side

    FBAR if foreign accounts exceeded USD 10,000 in aggregate at any time in the year (S10); Form 3520 Part IV if what you received was a gift or bequest above USD 100,000 from a foreign person (S11).

    S10 · IRS - Report of Foreign Bank and Financial Accounts (FBAR) ↗ S11 · IRS - Gifts from a foreign person ↗

Advantages

  • Cheapest lawful route in this study: the fixed fees are small relative to the balance.
  • Expressly contemplated by the rules for an overseas individual (S04 Art.59), and no purchase-application form is required of an overseas individual (S18).
  • Proceeds go straight into your own US account in USD; no offshore intermediary account needed.

Disadvantages

  • Requires a paper trail tying the RMB to a lawful China-source income event, including tax vouchers (S02 Art.13(1)).
  • Bank-level execution varies: the branch decides what documents are sufficient and may cap a single wire.
  • The spread is set commercially and is not published as a rule (S03 Art.27 sets only the exchange-rate regime).

Documents and evidence

  • Passport
  • Chinese bank account statements showing the RMB balance
  • Evidence of the income source (employment contract, payslip, lease, interest statement)
  • Chinese tax payment certificates or withholding records
  • US bank details: account number, SWIFT/BIC, ABA routing number
