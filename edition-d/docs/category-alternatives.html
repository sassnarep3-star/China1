Adjacent alternatives

Lawful products or choices that do not freely repatriate personal savings as USD.

A01 · QDII / overseas investment plan

QDII lets a licensed domestic institution invest domestic funds offshore within quota. It is an investment product, not a right to receive free USD abroad; redemption proceeds return through the product. Current HSBC subscription suspensions are flagged.

S06 · SAFE Beijing S17 · HSBC China S51 · HSBC China F38

A02 · Greater Bay Area Wealth Management Connect Southbound

Eligible GBA mainland residents may invest in designated offshore products through a closed-loop RMB channel. Redemption/repatriation returns via the same route; it is not free USD repatriation to a U.S. account.

S49 · Guangdong Provincial Government S50 · ICBC (Asia) F39

A03 · Registered employee stock incentive plan

A domestic agent and offshore trustee can operate a registered offshore-listed-company employee plan. Sale proceeds/dividends are repatriated through the dedicated account to the employee's domestic FX account; it is not a general offshore savings retention route.

S47 · SAFE Tianjin S06 · SAFE Beijing F40

A04 · Outward direct investment / special-purpose vehicle

ODI/SPV registration can move investment capital abroad for a genuine approved/filed investment. It creates an offshore asset, not personal USD spending money, and later distributions need their own basis.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S06 · SAFE Beijing S48 · SAFE F35 F41

A05 · Keep CNY in China or spend it lawfully in China

If no lawful outward basis exists, retaining CNY domestically or using it for genuine domestic consumption avoids an unlawful transfer. It does not achieve USD repatriation.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S40 · IRS F03
