Company dividend remittance (if the money belongs to a company you own)

Route M10 · Corporate

If the balance sits in a Chinese company rather than your own name, a profit distribution is the normal route - and it carries withholding tax on the profit.

Who can use it
A foreign investor in a Chinese company with distributable after-tax profits.
Legal basis
S03 · Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration ↗ S01 · PBOC Measures on Individual FX (2006) ↗
Ceiling or gate
No individual quota applies, because the money is corporate. Profit must be after-tax and the distribution must follow company law and the articles.
Tax treatment
Dividends paid out of China carry enterprise income tax withheld at source. The statutory rate and the reduced rate for a non-resident enterprise were not verified against the primary text in this session, so no rate is stated here (flag U07).

Step by step

  1. Confirm the profit is after-tax and distributable

    A dividend can only be paid out of distributable profits.

    no official source located — see flagged items

  2. Withhold Chinese enterprise income tax at source

    The rate was not verified against the primary text this session (flag U07).

    no official source located — see flagged items

  3. Take the resolution and tax receipts to the bank

    The bank processes the conversion and remittance against the corporate documents.

    S03 · Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration ↗

  4. Report on the US side

    A US owner reports the distribution and may claim a foreign tax credit for the Chinese tax (S13).

    S13 · IRS - Foreign tax credit ↗

Advantages

  • Not constrained by the personal USD 50,000 quota.
  • A well-trodden institutional process with banks that handle FDI accounts daily.

Disadvantages

  • Withholding tax is a real cost on the profit distributed - it is a tax on the profit, not on the transfer.
  • Requires audited accounts, a tax clearance and a board resolution.
  • Only usable if the money genuinely belongs to the company; treating personal money as corporate is a compliance problem.

Documents and evidence

  • Board resolution and profit distribution plan
  • Audited financial statements
  • Tax clearance / withholding receipts
  • FDI registration documents
