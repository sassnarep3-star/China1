What was checked, and how

  • Every page in the repository was opened and its central numerical claims compared with the source register in the same edition.
  • No file owned by another edition was edited, renamed or deleted by this edition.
  • Where this edition disagrees with an earlier one — principally on how to treat unverified fees — the disagreement is stated here and in COORDINATION.md rather than silently overwritten.
  • The build is deterministic and the verifier re-derives every published number from the model, so a transcription error cannot survive a commit.

What this is. A source-led research edition on converting renminbi held in mainland China into US dollars. Every legal proposition links to an official instrument; every number is quoted, derived by arithmetic from a quoted number, or openly marked as an assumption.

What this is not. Legal, tax or investment advice for your specific facts. The classification of your money — and therefore the correct route — depends on facts this edition does not have.
