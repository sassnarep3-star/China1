2 · Arithmetic

  • mid = (666.48 + 669.29)/2 = 667.885
  • one-way spread vs mid = (669.29 − 667.885)/667.885 = 0.2104% — used as the central “spread” column
  • full bid–ask width = (669.29 − 666.48)/667.885 = 0.4206% — “round-trip band”; shown in Table 0 footnote logic as the conservative case
  • sell vs reference = (671.68 − 669.29)/671.68 = 0.3558%
  • FX rate used for fee conversion: 669.29/100 = 6.6929 CNY per USD
  • wire fee (counter, international, any currency): fee_CNY = min(max(amount_CNY × 0.001, 50), 260) + 150 (B02)
  • wire fee in USD = fee_CNY / 6.6929
  • L1 tranche plan: tranches of ≤ $50,000 per calendar year, one wire per year per person (S02/S03)
  • L3 emigrant plan: if amount×6.6929 ≤ ¥200,000 → 1 tranche; else tranches of 50% / 25% / 25% mapped to approval-stage releases (S04/S05)
  • L2/L4/L5: single tranche
  • Total cost = Σ wire_USD + amount × one-way spread; percent = total/amount

3 · What is deliberately absent (and why)

  • US receiving-bank fee — no official source exists; publishing an “estimated typical $15–25” would violate this repo's no-inference rule (F-D03).
  • SAFE/bank permit application fees for cash/capital-account approvals — none published in retrieved sources; shown blank (F-D08).
  • Property disposition taxes — wrong category for a “conversion” ladder; quantified by Chinese law at 20% on 财产转让所得 + transaction taxes (F-D07).
  • Market-intervention risk premium — un-priceable; out of scope.
  • QDII in-NAV FX implementation spread — no per-trade official figure (F-D08/F-D15).

4 · Limits and candour

  • Values are arithmetic reproductions from sourced inputs, not quotes from your bank. Bank discretion (documentation sufficiency) is real and unbounded here.
  • Bank-published (tier C) numbers can change without notice; treat August 2026 as a spot check and re-verify the two fee tables before the first wire.
  • Regulation quoted is PRC law as published; local SAFE sub-bureau practice, bank head-office circulars, and your fact pattern may differ.
  • This is research, not legal, tax or investment advice. For an amount ≥ US$50,000 the paperwork is documentary; for ≥ US$100,000 the US-reporting side is the one that destroys value if mishandled — retain a US international CPA/attorney before executing.
