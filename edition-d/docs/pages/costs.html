Why the percentage keeps falling

The commission is capped at CNY 260 and the cable fee is flat at CNY 150. At $10,000 those fixed items are 0.62% of the amount; at $400,000 they are 0.02%. The FX margin is the only component that scales, and it is about 0.21% one way. That is why a single large transfer is cheaper than several small ones — and why anyone charging a percentage to move this money is charging more than the bank does.
