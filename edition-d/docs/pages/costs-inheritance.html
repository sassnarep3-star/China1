Formula used for every row

total = amount × 0.2104% + (min(max(amount × 6.6929 × 0.001, 50), 260) + 150) ÷ 6.6929 + correspondence + receiving-bank fee

Where a route releases in tranches, the bracketed fee term is evaluated per tranche and summed, and the correspondence and receiving-bank fees are multiplied by the number of tranches. The FX margin is applied once to the total, because the whole amount is converted either way.
