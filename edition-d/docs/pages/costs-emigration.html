Every row is derived arithmetic from the sourced unit rates, not an observed quote. Tax on the conversion is USD 0 on both sides (CN-05 Art. 2, US-01), so it is not a column. Where a route releases in tranches, the bracketed fee term is evaluated per tranche and summed, and the correspondence and receiving-bank fees are multiplied by the number of tranches. The FX margin is applied once to the total, because the whole amount is converted either way.
