The recommendation, stated plainly

If the balance is documented, after-tax China-source income: use M01. Ask the bank in writing which identity document governs the account, assemble the source-of-funds file with tax vouchers, buy the USD and send one wire. At $400,000 the modelled total cost is $933 (0.23%).

If it is property you owned before emigrating: M03 is the route the rules were written for, at the cost of three releases and notarisation that is not priced here.

If it is property sold in China: M05, after the 20% IIT on the gain, which is unavoidable and is not a conversion tax.

Before any of that: resolve the US filing history. The FBAR exposure is larger than every fee in this document combined (F05).

Never: split across relatives, use an underground dealer, or bridge through a stablecoin. Those are the only routes here that can cost more than the balance itself.

What this edition adds to the repository

The four earlier editions established the legal taxonomy and refused to invent fees, which left the practical question — how much, at what size, by which sequence — unanswered. Edition D sources a bank tariff, a rate board and a statutory tax table, and builds the missing tables. Where a price still does not exist officially, it stays blank.
