Prohibited routes, statutory exposure

The only figure that exists for these routes is the statutory fine range: 30% to 100% of the amount, plus confiscation.

M01 documented remittanceM02 quota-pacedM03 emigration transferM04 inheritance transferM05 property proceedsM06 convert and holdM09 cashM14-M16 prohibited

These routes are unlawful. Splitting across quotas (CN-03 Art. 62) brings watch-list treatment; private or disguised FX dealing (CN-02 Art. 45) and evading FX control (Art. 39) carry fines of 30% to 100% of the amount plus confiscation. Crypto exchange between legal tender and virtual currency is illegal financial activity (CN-06). No procedure is given for any of them.
