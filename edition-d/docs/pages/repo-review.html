Line-by-line audit of the four earlier editions

Every earlier edition was read, its claims checked against the instrument it cites, and its checker re-run. What follows is what held up and what did not.

What was checked

  • All four checkers were re-run in this checkout: assets/check.py, scripts/verify.py, edition-b/build/verify.py, edition-c/build/linkcheck.py. All four pass, which means the earlier editions are internally consistent.
  • Their cited instruments were opened: CN-01, CN-02, CN-03, CN-04, CN-05, CN-08 and the US statutes. The quotes in the earlier editions match the text in the places checked.
  • Their arithmetic was recomputed from the same BOC board they used.
  • Their external links were tested by retrieval. One primary citation returns a 404 while its own fallback resolves.
  • Their internal consistency was compared across editions: route counts, spread figures and deployment claims.

Why none of this is a criticism of the method

The earlier editions did the hard part correctly: they refused to invent fees, marked unknowns, and separated the law from providers' marketing. The gaps found here are mostly the same gap — the absence of a sourced tariff — plus one arithmetic labelling error and one dead link. Edition D fixes those and leaves the rest of their work in place.

What this is. Published-rule research with arithmetic shown. It is not legal, tax or investment advice, and it is not a bank quote. Rates and tariffs change; the sources are linked for manual review.

Values that could not be confirmed from an official page are blank and listed on Flagged for review. No value is ever inferred.
