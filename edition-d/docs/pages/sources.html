CN-01 · Measures for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange

People's Bank of China (中国人民银行) · PBOC Order [2006] No. 3, effective 2007-02-01

http://m.safe.gov.cn/safe/2006/1225/5319.html

Tier A · full text retrieved

  • Art. 3: current-account individual FX business is administered on the convertibility principle; capital-account business 'according to the process of convertibility'
  • Art. 7: no splitting to evade quota supervision; no false commercial documents to evade authenticity review
  • Art. 9: annual total-amount management; inside the amount, ID only; above it, current-account items need ID plus transaction documents, capital-account items follow Chapter 3
  • Art. 13: an overseas individual may buy FX and remit lawful current-account RMB income earned in China against ID and supporting materials
  • Art. 14: unused inward-remitted FX may be sent back on the original path
  • Art. 22: an overseas individual's sale proceeds from domestic commercial property may be purchased and remitted with SAFE approval
  • Art. 26: outward transfer of an overseas individual's lawful property in China follows the separate property-transfer rules
  • Art. 28: the bank determines the account's subject category from the identity document produced at account opening
  • Art. 37: 境内个人 = holders of a PRC resident identity card; 境外个人 = passport holders (foreign citizens, including stateless persons) and Hong Kong/Macau/Taiwan compatriots

CN-02 · Regulation of the People's Republic of China on Foreign Exchange Administration

State Council of the PRC (国务院) · State Council Decree No. 532, promulgated 2008-08-05

https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2008-08/06/content_2602514.htm

Tier A · full text retrieved

  • Art. 5: the State does not restrict current-account international payments and transfers
  • Art. 12: current-account FX payments must rest on a genuine and lawful transaction basis; the bank must reasonably review the authenticity of documents and their consistency with the payment
  • Art. 22 para. 3: a lawfully terminated foreign-invested enterprise may, after liquidation and tax payment in accordance with State rules, purchase FX and remit the RMB belonging to the foreign investor
  • Art. 39: evading FX control by moving domestic FX or capital abroad — fine up to 30% of the amount; if serious, 30% to 100%; criminal liability where constituted
  • Art. 42: carrying FX into or out of the country in violation of the rules — warning and fine up to 20% of the amount
  • Art. 45: private, disguised or brokered buying and selling of FX in relatively large amounts — warning, confiscation of illegal gains, fine up to 30% (30%-100% if serious)
  • Art. 48: individuals failing to submit valid documents, or submitting untrue documents, may be fined up to CNY 50,000
  • Art. 52(2): 境内个人 means PRC citizens and foreigners who have resided continuously in China for one full year (excluding foreign diplomats and representatives of international organisations)

CN-03 · Guidelines for Current-Account Foreign Exchange Business (2020 edition)

State Administration of Foreign Exchange (国家外汇管理局) · Huifa [2020] No. 14 (汇发〔2020〕14号), issued 2020-08-28

https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm

Tier A · full text retrieved

  • Art. 49: service-trade payments above USD 50,000 equivalent require tax filing verification before the bank processes the payment
  • Art. 54: individual FX settlement, and FX purchase by 境内个人, are subject to an annual facilitation quota of USD 50,000 equivalent each
  • Art. 55: within the annual facilitation quota, the bank processes against valid ID alone
  • Art. 56: a 境内个人 may settle or purchase FX outside the facilitation quota against ID plus source-of-funds or purpose documents
  • Art. 58: a 境外个人 may settle FX outside the facilitation quota against ID plus purpose documents; a single settlement above USD 50,000 must be paid directly to the counterparty's onshore RMB account
  • Art. 59: a 境外个人 may purchase FX against lawful current-account RMB income earned in China, against ID plus source-of-funds documents including tax vouchers; only a 境外个人 holding a Foreigner Permanent Residence ID card uses the annual facilitation quota
  • Art. 60: unused RMB converted back to FX within 24 months against the original exchange receipt; USD 500/day and USD 1,000/day at departure ports on ID alone
  • Art. 62: splitting to evade quota or authenticity management is prohibited; SAFE maintains a watch list, for the year of listing plus two further years
  • Art. 64: a 境内个人 remitting FX from an FX account abroad for current-account purposes — up to USD 50,000/day cumulative on ID alone, above that with transaction documents
  • Art. 65: a 境外个人 remitting FX from an FX account abroad — on valid ID, with no amount threshold in the text; cash remittance up to USD 10,000/day on ID alone
  • Art. 66: individual cross-border e-commerce and market-procurement settlement against transaction materials does not consume the facilitation quota
  • Art. 86: individual cash withdrawal up to USD 10,000/day on ID; above that requires SAFE filing
  • Art. 89: inbound cash above USD 5,000 equivalent must be declared to customs; outbound cash of USD 5,000-10,000 requires a foreign-exchange carriage permit from the bank, and above USD 10,000 a SAFE permit

CN-04 · PBOC official answers on the Interim Measures for the Administration of the Sale and Payment of Foreign Exchange for the Outward Transfer of Individual Property

State Administration of Foreign Exchange (国家外汇管理局) · Index 000014453-2004-00016, published 2004-11-16

https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2004/1116/4134.html

Tier A · full text retrieved

  • Two and only two categories of individual property transfer: emigration property transfer (移民财产转移) and inheritance property transfer (继承财产转移)
  • Applicant must own the property lawfully and it must not be subject to a rights dispute; unproven source of funds and property involved in unsettled criminal or civil proceedings are not accepted
  • Applications go to the SAFE branch in the jurisdiction of the emigrant's original household registration, or of the deceased's household registration before death
  • Below CNY 500,000 equivalent: decided by the provincial SAFE branch. At or above CNY 500,000: provincial review then SAFE head office approval
  • Emigration transfers at or above CNY 200,000: one single application for the whole amount, released in stages — half first, half of the remainder after one year, the rest after two years
  • All property inherited from the same decedent may be applied for once and remitted in one or several instalments; property inherited from different decedents requires separate applications
  • Who may apply: natural persons who have moved from mainland China to a foreign country or to Hong Kong/Macau, for property lawfully owned before acquiring emigrant status; foreign citizens and Hong Kong/Macau residents for lawfully inherited property in China
  • Documents that are not notarised must be notarised, including the power of attorney and the property-right and inheritance documents

CN-06 · Notice on Further Preventing and Handling the Risks of Virtual-Currency Trading and Speculation

People's Bank of China and nine other departments · Yinfa [2021] No. 237 (银发〔2021〕237号), 2021-09-24

https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/4348658/index.html

Tier A · search-index excerpt

  • Virtual currency has no legal-tender status and shall not and cannot circulate as currency in the market
  • Business activity connected with virtual currency — including exchange between legal tender and virtual currency, exchange between virtual currencies, acting as central counterparty, information intermediation and pricing services, token issuance and derivatives — is illegal financial activity, strictly prohibited and to be banned
  • Financial institutions and non-bank payment institutions may not provide account opening, fund transfer, clearing or settlement services for virtual-currency business

US-01 · 26 U.S.C. §988 — Treatment of certain foreign currency transactions

United States Congress (U.S. Code, 2024 edition, GPO) ·

https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm

Tier A · full text retrieved

  • §988(a)(1)(A): foreign-currency gain or loss on a §988 transaction is computed separately and treated as ordinary income or loss
  • §988(c)(1)(C)(i): a disposition of nonfunctional currency is treated as a §988 transaction
  • §988(c)(1)(C)(ii): nonfunctional currency includes coin or currency and nonfunctional-currency-denominated demand or time deposits or similar instruments issued by a bank
  • §988(e)(1): the section does not apply to a §988 transaction entered into by an individual which is a personal transaction
  • §988(e)(2): on a disposition of nonfunctional currency in a personal transaction, no gain is recognised by reason of exchange-rate changes between acquisition and disposition — but that sentence does not apply if the gain that would otherwise be recognised exceeds USD 200
  • §988(e)(3): 'personal transaction' means a transaction entered into by an individual, except to the extent allocable expenses meet §162 or §212

Tier A · full text retrieved

  • §5321(a)(5)(B)(i): a civil penalty for a violation of §5314 shall not exceed USD 10,000
  • §5321(a)(5)(B)(ii): no penalty where the violation was due to reasonable cause and the account balance was properly reported

US-03 · Comparison of Form 8938 and FBAR requirements

Internal Revenue Service ·

https://www.irs.gov/businesses/comparison-of-form-8938-and-fbar-requirements

Tier A · full text retrieved

  • FBAR threshold: aggregate value of financial accounts exceeds USD 10,000 at any time during the calendar year
  • FBAR is filed with FinCEN, not with the income-tax return; received by 15 April with an automatic extension to 15 October
  • FBAR maximum value is converted to USD at the end-of-calendar-year exchange rate
  • Form 8938 threshold for a specified individual living in the United States: more than USD 50,000 on the last day of the tax year or more than USD 75,000 at any time (unmarried); USD 100,000 / USD 150,000 for a joint filer
  • Form 8938 threshold for a specified individual living outside the United States is higher (USD 200,000 / USD 300,000 unmarried)
  • Form 8938 penalty: up to USD 10,000 for failure to disclose plus USD 10,000 for each 30 days after IRS notice, maximum USD 60,000
  • FBAR civil penalties are adjusted annually for inflation; for assessments before 1 August 2016, non-willful up to USD 10,000 and willful up to the greater of USD 100,000 or 50% of account balances

US-05 · Gifts from foreign person (Form 3520, Part IV)

Internal Revenue Service ·

https://www.irs.gov/businesses/gifts-from-foreign-person

Tier A · full text retrieved

  • A U.S. person who receives a large gift or bequest from a foreign person reports it on Part IV of Form 3520
  • Reporting is required where the aggregate received from a nonresident alien or foreign estate exceeds USD 100,000 in the taxable year; each gift over USD 5,000 must be separately identified
