Method and limits

How this was built, and what it cannot tell you

The rules the generator enforces, the arithmetic it performs, and the questions it deliberately does not answer.

Rules enforced in code

  1. A source id must exist in the register or the build fails.
  2. A number is printed only when every input behind it carries a source id.
  3. A missing official price renders as an em dash plus an UNVERIFIED marker; no default, no range, no "typical" value.
  4. Every table row carries at least one source link.
  5. Every page is in the navigation, so no orphan page can exist.

These are re-checked independently by build/verify.py, which recomputes every cost cell from the parameters rather than trusting the HTML.

Arithmetic, stated exactly

mid = (666.48 + 669.29) / 2 = 667.885

one-way margin = (669.29 − 667.885) / 667.885 = 0.2104%

round-trip spread = (669.29 − 666.48) / 669.29 = 0.4198%

commission = min(max(amount × 6.6929 × 0.001, 50), 260) CNY

bank fees in USD = (commission + 150) / 6.6929

The one-way margin is the right figure for a single conversion. The round-trip figure is what two conversions cost, and it is the number the earlier editions labelled as the cost of one.

What this cannot tell you

  • Whether your balance qualifies for any route — that depends on the source of the funds (F03).
  • Whether your bank classifies you as 境内个人 or 境外个人 (F01).
  • What your own bank charges to receive a wire (F07).
  • What notarisation, translation or travel will cost (F14, F20).
  • What your §988 basis is, and therefore whether you have a taxable FX gain (F11).
  • What a Chinese branch will actually do at the counter, as opposed to what the rule says (F10, F16).

Scope and status

Research current to 8 November 2026. Not legal, tax or investment advice. No account was contacted, no funds were moved, and no personal tax position was determined. Where a professional is required — a Chinese notary, a SAFE filing, a US international tax return — this edition says so rather than substituting a calculation for it.

What this is. Published-rule research with arithmetic shown. It is not legal, tax or investment advice, and it is not a bank quote. Rates and tariffs change; the sources are linked for manual review.
