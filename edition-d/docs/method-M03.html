Emigration transfer (移民转移) - whole-balance route after you emigrate

Route M03 · Bank & wire

If you have given up Chinese household registration and settled abroad, SAFE can approve the transfer of your entire pre-emigration domestic property, released in three stages over two years.

Status
CONDITIONAL
Who can use it
A natural person who has moved from mainland China to a foreign country (or HK/Macau) and holds proof of settlement abroad plus cancellation of Chinese household registration.
Legal basis
S07 · Interim Measures on Outward Transfer of Personal Property (2004) ↗ S02 · Detailed Rules on Individual FX (2007, as amended) ↗ S01 · PBOC Measures on Individual FX (2006) ↗
Ceiling or gate
No amount ceiling is stated; approval is by SAFE (local branch up to RMB 500,000, SAFE head office above that - S07 Art.10). Release: up to half first, up to half of the remainder after one year, the rest after two years (S07 Art.6).
Tax treatment
The tax certificate is a precondition, not a new tax: the transfer requires a tax payment certificate or a tax exemption document from the tax authority where the property is located (S07 Art.8(6)).

Step by step

  1. Confirm you qualify as an emigrant

    The measure covers a natural person who has moved from mainland China to a foreign country or to HK/Macau and applies to property owned *before* the emigration status was obtained (S07 Art.2).

    S07 · Interim Measures on Outward Transfer of Personal Property (2004) ↗

  2. Apply once, for the whole amount

    The application must cover the entire property to be transferred; it cannot be topped up later (S07 Art.6).

    S07 · Interim Measures on Outward Transfer of Personal Property (2004) ↗

  3. File with the SAFE branch where your household registration was

    The application goes to the FX branch at the place of your former household registration (S07 Art.5).

    S07 · Interim Measures on Outward Transfer of Personal Property (2004) ↗

  4. Expect head-office approval above RMB 500,000

    Local branches approve up to RMB 500,000 equivalent; above that the file goes to SAFE head office (S07 Art.10).

    S07 · Interim Measures on Outward Transfer of Personal Property (2004) ↗

  5. Take the approval to the bank for the first release

    The bank purchases FX and remits directly to your own account abroad; cash withdrawal onshore is not permitted (S07 Art.11).

    S07 · Interim Measures on Outward Transfer of Personal Property (2004) ↗

  6. Return after 12 and 24 months for the second and third releases

    First release no more than half; after one year no more than half of the remainder; after two years the balance (S07 Art.6).

    S07 · Interim Measures on Outward Transfer of Personal Property (2004) ↗

Advantages

  • Designed for exactly this problem: it moves a whole balance, not USD 50,000 a year.
  • The staged release is two years, not eight or more.
  • The bank must remit abroad directly to your own account - no cash stage (S07 Art.11).

Disadvantages

  • Requires that you have actually emigrated: police certificate cancelling household registration and proof of settlement abroad (S07 Art.8(4)).
  • SAFE approval required, with a full file: application form, property title documents, tax certificate, notarised translations (S07 Arts.8-9).
  • Property that cannot be shown to have a lawful source, or that is subject to litigation, is refused (S07 Art.12).
  • Three releases means three sets of bank charges.

Documents and evidence

  • Written application explaining the source and realisation of the property
  • Completed emigrant property transfer applicant form and FX business application form
  • Passport plus police-issued cancellation of household registration
  • Proof of settlement abroad, issued or authenticated by a Chinese embassy or consulate
  • Property title documents (deed, sale contract, relocation compensation agreement)
  • Tax certificate or tax-paid voucher from the tax authority
  • Notarised translations of any foreign-language document
