S01
中国人民银行令〔2006〕第3号		 个人外汇管理办法 (Measures for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange)
People's Bank of China · 2006-12-25 (effective 2007-02-01)		 Full text retrieved and read: Arts. 3, 7, 9, 13, 16-23, 26, 30-35, 37.
  • Art.3: current-account individual FX is managed on the convertibility principle; capital-account FX follows the convertibility process (i.e. is not automatically convertible).
  • Art.7: splitting transactions to evade quota supervision, and false commercial documents, are prohibited.
  • Art.9: annual total-amount management applies to individual FX settlement and to FX purchase by a *domestic* individual.
  • Art.13: an overseas individual may purchase FX and remit lawful current-account RMB income earned in China, on ID and supporting documents.
  • Art.22: an overseas individual selling domestic commercial property may purchase FX and remit the proceeds with SAFE approval.
  • Art.26: outward transfer of an overseas individual's lawful domestic property follows the property-transfer rules.
  • Art.37(2): an overseas individual is a foreign citizen holding a passport (or HK/Macau/Taiwan resident).
S02
汇发〔2007〕1号		 个人外汇管理办法实施细则 (Detailed Rules for the Implementation of the Measures for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange), as amended
State Administration of Foreign Exchange · 2007-01-05 (effective 2007-02-01; amended)		 Full text retrieved and read, including the two amendment notes at the foot of the page.
  • Art.2: the annual total amount is USD 50,000 equivalent per person per year, applied to individual FX settlement and to FX purchase by a domestic individual.
  • Art.13(1): an overseas individual purchasing FX against lawful current-account RMB income earned in China provides ID and transaction evidence *including tax vouchers*.
  • Art.13(2): reconversion of unused RMB requires the original exchange memo, valid for 24 months.
  • Art.14: a domestic individual remitting FX abroad for current-account purposes on ID alone is limited to USD 50,000/day; above that, authenticity documents are required.
  • Art.15: an overseas individual remitting from an FX savings account does so on valid ID.
  • Art.20: emigration and inheritance transfers are handled under the 2004 Interim Measures.
  • Art.21: an overseas individual's purchase/sale of domestic commercial property is handled under the property-FX rules.
  • Art.30: cash withdrawal above USD 10,000/day requires prior SAFE filing.
  • Art.39 was repealed by 汇发〔2023〕8号 (noted on the page).
S03
国务院令第532号		 中华人民共和国外汇管理条例 (Regulation on the Foreign Exchange System of the PRC)
State Council · 2008-08-05		 Full text retrieved and read: Arts. 5, 6, 8, 12, 14, 15, 22, 27, 39-48, 52.
  • Art.5: the State does not restrict current-account international payments and transfers.
  • Art.12: current-account FX receipts and payments must rest on a genuine, lawful transaction basis.
  • Art.15: the limits for carrying and declaring foreign cash across the border are set by the FX authority.
  • Art.22 para.2: RMB belonging to the foreign investor of a lawfully terminated foreign-invested enterprise may be purchased and remitted after liquidation and tax payment.
  • Art.27: the RMB exchange rate is a managed float based on market supply and demand.
  • Art.39: evasion of FX control (逃汇) - fine of up to 30% of the evaded amount; serious cases 30% up to the same amount.
  • Art.40: illegal arbitrage of FX (非法套汇, including purchase on false documents) - same 0-30% / 30%-100% range.
  • Art.42: illegal carriage of FX across the border - warning and a fine of up to 20% of the amount.
  • Art.44: unauthorised change of use of FX or of settled FX - 0-30%, serious 30%-100%.
  • Art.45: private or disguised trading of FX - 0-30%, serious 30%-100%; criminal liability if constituted.
  • Art.48: failure to submit valid documents or submission of untrue documents - individual fine up to RMB 50,000.
  • Art.52(2): 'domestic individual' includes a Chinese citizen *and a foreigner who has continuously resided in China for one year or more*.
S04
汇发〔2020〕14号		 经常项目外汇业务指引（2020年版） (Guide to Current-Account Foreign Exchange Business, 2020 edition)
State Administration of Foreign Exchange · 2020-08-28		 State Council Gazette text retrieved in parts: Arts. 54-59 (from the gazette and the SAFE Q&A at S05), 64, 86-89, 178.
  • Art.54: individual FX settlement and FX purchase by a domestic individual are subject to an annual facilitation quota of USD 50,000 equivalent per person per year.
  • Art.57: a domestic individual purchasing FX must complete the Individual FX Purchase Application and bears legal responsibility for it.
  • Art.58: an overseas individual settling FX provides ID and evidence of the use of funds; settlements above USD 50,000 must be paid directly to the counterparty's onshore RMB account.
  • Art.59: an overseas individual's lawful current-account RMB income earned in China may be purchased with ID and source-of-funds evidence (including tax vouchers); the annual facilitation quota applies only to holders of a Foreigner's Permanent Residence ID.
  • Arts.86-89: cash rules - withdrawal or deposit of up to USD 10,000/day on ID; carrying cash in above USD 5,000 requires written customs declaration; carrying out USD 5,000-10,000 requires a bank-issued carrying permit; above USD 10,000 requires a SAFE-issued permit.
  • Art.178: for individual settlement/purchase above USD 50,000, authenticity review of documents applies.

Index 00013319-6-2024-00170		 2024年经常项目外汇政策问答案例（九） - 境外个人是否享受年度便利化额度购汇？

Index 00013319-6-2017-00231		 2017年个人外汇管理问题解答（二）
SAFE Tianjin Branch · 2017-11-28		 Full page retrieved and read.
  • Lists the six prohibitions a domestic individual accepts when purchasing FX, including: no overseas house purchase, securities investment or life/return-of-premium insurance with purchased FX; no lending or borrowing of another person's facilitation quota; no splitting; no money laundering, tax evasion or underground-bank dealings.
S07
中国人民银行公告〔2004〕第16号		 个人财产对外转移售付汇管理暂行办法
People's Bank of China · 2004-11-16 (effective 2004-12-01)		 Full text retrieved and read: Arts. 2-16.
  • Art.2: emigration transfer (移民转移) and inheritance transfer (继承转移) are the two forms of outward transfer of personal property.
  • Art.6: an emigration transfer must be applied for once for the whole amount and is released in stages - first no more than half, a further no more than half of the remainder after one year, the balance after two years; amounts up to RMB 200,000 may be released at once.
  • Art.8(4): the applicant must produce the police-issued cancellation of Chinese household registration (户籍注销证明) and proof of settlement abroad; Art.8(6): a tax certificate or tax-paid voucher from the tax authority.
  • Art.10: applications up to RMB 500,000 are approved by the local SAFE branch; above that, by SAFE head office.
  • Art.11: the bank must remit directly to the applicant's own account abroad; cash withdrawal onshore is not permitted.
  • Art.12: property that cannot be shown to have a lawful source is not accepted.
S08
Adopted 1980; seventh amendment 2018-08-31		 Individual Income Tax Law of the People's Republic of China
State Taxation Administration · 2018-08-31		 Full text retrieved and read: Arts. 1, 2, 3, 4, 6.
  • Art.1: a resident individual (domiciled, or 183+ days in a tax year) is taxed on income from within and outside China; a non-resident on China-source income.
  • Art.2: tax is imposed on nine enumerated categories of income (wages; personal services; author's remuneration; royalties; business operation; interest, dividends and bonuses; leasing of assets; transfer of assets; incidental income). The exchange of one currency for another is not one of them.
  • Art.3: comprehensive income 3%-45%; business income 5%-35%; interest, dividends, leasing, transfer of assets and incidental income a flat 20%.

S10
FinCEN Form 114		 Report of Foreign Bank and Financial Accounts (FBAR)
Internal Revenue Service · Page retrieved 2026-11-08		 Full page retrieved and read.
  • A US person must file an FBAR where the aggregate value of foreign financial accounts exceeded USD 10,000 at any time in the calendar year.
  • Whether the account produced taxable income has no effect on the filing obligation.
  • Civil penalty maximums are set in Title 31 and adjusted annually for inflation.
S11
Form 3520, Part IV		 Gifts from foreign person (International tax gap series)
Internal Revenue Service · Page retrieved 2026-11-08		 Full page retrieved and read.
  • A US person who receives gifts or bequests from a nonresident alien exceeding USD 100,000 in aggregate during the tax year must report them on Part IV of Form 3520.
  • A foreign gift is excluded from gross income; the obligation is reporting, not tax on the receipt.
S12
United States Code, 2018 edition		 26 U.S.C. §988 - Treatment of certain foreign currency transactions
U.S. Government Publishing Office · 2018 edition		 Section text retrieved and read, including subsection (e).
  • §988(a): foreign-currency gain or loss on a §988 transaction is computed separately and treated as ordinary income or loss.
  • §988(c)(1)(C): a disposition of non-functional currency is a §988 transaction.
  • §988(e)(1): the section does not apply to a §988 transaction entered into by an individual which is a personal transaction.
  • §988(e)(2): on a personal transaction, no gain is recognised by reason of exchange-rate movement - but the exclusion does not apply if the gain would exceed USD 200.
  • §988(e)(3): 'personal transaction' means a transaction of an individual other than one with expenses meeting §162 or §212 (business/investment).
S13
Form 1116		 Foreign Tax Credit
Internal Revenue Service · Page retrieved 2026-11-08		 Full page retrieved and read.
  • A credit or deduction may be available for foreign income taxes imposed on income that is also subject to US tax; the credit is claimed on Form 1116.
  • The credit cannot exceed the US tax on the same income, and no credit is available for taxes on income excluded under the foreign earned income exclusion.
S14
汇发〔2003〕102号		 携带外币现钞出入境管理暂行办法
SAFE and General Administration of Customs · 2003-08-28		 Ministry of Commerce reprint of the joint SAFE/Customs notice retrieved and read (Arts. 2-10).
  • Carrying out up to USD 5,000 equivalent needs no carrying permit; USD 5,000-10,000 needs a bank-issued permit; above USD 10,000 a SAFE-issued permit is required in principle.
  • Carrying in more than USD 5,000 equivalent must be declared in writing to Customs.
  • A bank may issue a permit only up to the amount of the deposit certificate or the amount of FX purchased, and each permit may not exceed USD 10,000.
S15
汇发〔2017〕29号		 国家外汇管理局关于规范银行卡境外大额提取现金交易的通知
State Administration of Foreign Exchange · 2017-12-29 (effective 2018-01-01)		 Official SAFE page including the notice text, retrieved via the SAFE site search index; the same text was cross-read in SAFE's press release at m.safe.gov.cn/safe/2017/1230/12696.html.
  • All of one person's domestic-bank cards together may withdraw no more than the equivalent of RMB 100,000 in cash outside mainland China per calendar year.
  • Each card is limited to the equivalent of RMB 10,000 per day, for both RMB and foreign-currency cards.
  • Exceeding the annual limit suspends overseas cash withdrawal for that year and the next; borrowing or lending cards to circumvent the limit is prohibited.
S16
银发〔2021〕237号		 关于进一步防范和处置虚拟货币交易炒作风险的通知
PBOC and nine other departments · 2021-09-15		 Full text retrieved and read.
  • Virtual currency has no legal-tender status and may not circulate as currency in the market.
  • Exchange between legal tender and virtual currency, and virtual-currency business activity generally, is an illegal financial activity to be strictly prohibited and suppressed.

How to read a citation

  • Article-level. Citations name the instrument and the article, so the proposition can be checked without reading the whole text.
  • Inspection dated and scoped. "Full text retrieved and read" is different from "search-index excerpt read"; the sources page says which applies.
  • Official, not authoritative for your facts. A citation proves what the rule says. It does not prove that your bank will apply it, or that your money meets it.
  • No live-link guarantee. Pages move. The URL recorded is the one that was read on 2026-11-08.

What this is. A source-led research edition on converting renminbi held in mainland China into US dollars. Every legal proposition links to an official instrument; every number is quoted, derived by arithmetic from a quoted number, or openly marked as an assumption.

What this is not. Legal, tax or investment advice for your specific facts. The classification of your money — and therefore the correct route — depends on facts this edition does not have.
