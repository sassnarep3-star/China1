Liquidation or capital reduction of a foreign-invested enterprise

Route M11 · Corporate

An express statutory entitlement: after a foreign-invested enterprise is lawfully terminated, liquidated and taxed, the foreign investor's RMB may be purchased and remitted.

Total cost on $400,000$940.950.235% of the balance
Fixed charges per transfer$62cable + correspondent + receiving bank

Status
CONDITIONAL
Who can use it
The foreign investor of a company that is being wound up or reduced in capital.
Legal basis
S03 · Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration ↗
Ceiling or gate
No amount ceiling in the article; the constraint is the liquidation and tax process.
Tax treatment
Liquidation tax is settled before the remittance; the article makes tax payment part of the sequence, not a levy on the transfer (S03 Art.22).

Known gap on this route: Liquidation costs and tax are company-specific and are not quantified.

Step by step

  1. Terminate or reduce the company lawfully

    The article applies to an enterprise 'lawfully terminated' (依法终止) (S03 Art.22).

    S03 · Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration ↗

  2. Liquidate and pay the taxes

    The sequence in the article is liquidation, then tax, then conversion and remittance (S03 Art.22).

    S03 · Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration ↗

  3. Purchase FX and remit the investor's share

    The RMB belonging to the foreign investor may be purchased and remitted through a bank (S03 Art.22).

    S03 · Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration ↗

Advantages

  • Written into a State Council regulation - the clearest statutory language of any whole-balance route.
  • Moves the whole remaining capital, not a quota-sized slice.

Disadvantages

  • Terminating or reducing a company is a major corporate act with its own legal and tax cost.
  • Only relevant if the money is genuinely corporate capital.

Documents and evidence

  • Liquidation approval and filings
  • Liquidation tax clearance
  • Capital account registration documents
