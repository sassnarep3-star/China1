What is not claimed

  • No specific tax or penalty amount is computed for your situation, because the classification of the money and the account history are unknown.
  • No fee is invented. Where a tariff could not be retrieved, the figure is an openly labelled assumption and the affected cells are marked.
  • No legal conclusion is stated without the article it comes from.
  • The site does not verify that any source remains live; it records the date and the scope of each inspection.

What this is. A source-led research edition on converting renminbi held in mainland China into US dollars. Every legal proposition links to an official instrument; every number is quoted, derived by arithmetic from a quoted number, or openly marked as an assumption.

What this is not. Legal, tax or investment advice for your specific facts. The classification of your money — and therefore the correct route — depends on facts this edition does not have.
