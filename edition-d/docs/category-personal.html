Personal legal entitlements

Rights to purchase/remit CNY based on who you are and how the money arose.

P01 · Documented current-account RMB income of an overseas individual

A foreign citizen classified by the bank as an overseas individual may purchase and remit lawful current-account RMB income (salary, services, rent, royalties and similar) with identity, source-of-funds and tax evidence. The rule text does not impose the domestic-person USD50k facilitation quota on this documented route.

S02 · SAFE / PBOC S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S31 · State Taxation Administration / Guangdong S46 · People's Bank of China F01 F02 F03

P02 · Emigrant property transfer (移民财产转移)

A natural person who emigrated from mainland China may apply to transfer lawful property owned before obtaining immigrant status. SAFE approval is required; approved funds may be remitted once or in installments to the applicant's own overseas account.

S04 · PBOC / State Council Gazette S05 · SAFE Tianjin S07 · SAFE Hebei S09 · SAFE Tianjin S10 · SAFE Tianjin S11 · SAFE Tianjin F04 F05 F06

P03 · Inheritance property transfer (继承财产转移)

A foreign citizen or HK/Macau/TW resident may apply to transfer lawfully inherited mainland property. The application goes to the SAFE office of the decedent's former household registration; multiple decedents may be combined in one application under the amended rules.

S04 · PBOC / State Council Gazette S05 · SAFE Tianjin S08 · SAFE Hebei S09 · SAFE Tianjin S10 · SAFE Tianjin S11 · SAFE Tianjin S43 · IRS F05 F07

P04 · Sale of mainland commercial housing by an overseas individual

An overseas individual who sells mainland commercial housing may purchase and remit the RMB proceeds under the property-sale foreign-exchange rules. This is distinct from an emigrant transfer of a pre-status balance and requires the property-sale chain and tax evidence.

S02 · SAFE / PBOC S31 · State Taxation Administration / Guangdong S32 · State Taxation Administration F08 F09

P05 · Insurance proceeds paid to an overseas beneficiary

Where the insurance purchase was itself lawful and compliant, SAFE guidance allows an overseas beneficiary to purchase/remit insurance proceeds as lawful RMB income using the policy and insurer payment proof.

S12 · SAFE Henan S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S31 · State Taxation Administration / Guangdong F10

P06 · Reconversion of unused previously exchanged RMB

An overseas individual may reconvert unused RMB originally obtained by exchanging foreign currency, using the original exchange memo within 24 months. Small same-day amounts have simplified document rules.

S02 · SAFE / PBOC S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette F11

P07 · Return of unused originally inbound foreign currency

An overseas individual may remit unused foreign currency that was originally inbound back along the original path. This is not a CNY-to-USD conversion route; it is listed to prevent miscounting already-FX balances.

S02 · SAFE / PBOC S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette F12

P08 · Domestic-person USD50k facilitation quota (identity-mismatch case only)

The USD50k annual facilitation quota applies to domestic individuals as classified by the bank. A U.S. citizen should not assume this is available merely because a PRC ID remains active; classification must be resolved first. The quota is a current-account convenience tier, not a capital-account savings export right.

S02 · SAFE / PBOC S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S46 · People's Bank of China F01 F13

P09 · Above-quota genuine current-account expenditure

A domestic individual may purchase FX above the facilitation quota for genuine current-account expenditures with transaction-amount purpose documents. Tuition, medical care and similar real payments are examples; parking savings abroad is not.

S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S35 · STA Tianjin S46 · People's Bank of China F13 F14
