Bank FX purchase + wire, drip-fed under the USD 50,000 annual quota

Route M02 · Bank & wire

What most people are told to do: spend the USD 50,000-per-person-per-year facilitation quota, year after year.

Who can use it
A domestic individual, or an overseas individual whom the branch treats as quota-bound. It is the only personal route that is purely administrative, but it is slow.
Legal basis
S01 · PBOC Measures on Individual FX (2006) ↗ S02 · Detailed Rules on Individual FX (2007, as amended) ↗ S04 · Current-Account FX Business Guide 2020 ↗ S04 · Current-Account FX Business Guide 2020 ↗ S06 · SAFE Tianjin Q&A (2017) - the six prohibitions ↗
Ceiling or gate
USD 50,000 equivalent per person per year (S02 Art.2; S04 Art.54). A USD 400,000 balance therefore takes 8 calendar years.
Tax treatment
Same as M01: no tax on the conversion itself. Repeated small conversions do not reduce tax, only fees and time.

Known gap on this route: The 8-year schedule assumes one person and no change in the quota, which the regulator may adjust (S02 Art.2).

Step by step

  1. Check how much of this year's quota is unused

    The quota is USD 50,000 equivalent per person per calendar year and resets each January (S02 Art.2).

    S02 · Detailed Rules on Individual FX (2007, as amended) ↗

  2. Complete the Individual FX Purchase Application truthfully

    A domestic individual must complete it and bears legal responsibility for it (S04 Art.57).

    S04 · Current-Account FX Business Guide 2020 ↗

  3. Buy within the quota

    Inside the quota the transaction is processed on ID (S02 Art.2).

    S02 · Detailed Rules on Individual FX (2007, as amended) ↗

  4. Wire to your US account

    The purchase and the payment are separate steps; keep the purpose consistent with the application.

    S01 · PBOC Measures on Individual FX (2006) ↗

  5. Repeat in the next calendar year

    Do not accelerate by using relatives' quotas - that is the prohibited practice of splitting (S01 Art.7; S06).

    S01 · PBOC Measures on Individual FX (2006) ↗ S06 · SAFE Tianjin Q&A (2017) - the six prohibitions ↗

Advantages

  • Least document-intensive per tranche, and the quota is a legal entitlement inside the annual amount.
  • Can be started immediately, online in many banks.

Disadvantages

  • Time: 8 years for USD 400,000, and the quota is per person per year, not per balance.
  • Eight wires means eight sets of fixed charges (cable, correspondent, receiving bank).
  • Buying FX for overseas house purchase, securities or life insurance is expressly prohibited (S06); filling in a false purpose is a false declaration.
  • Borrowing another person's quota to split a balance is expressly prohibited (S06; S01 Art.7).

Documents and evidence

  • ID or passport
  • Completed Individual FX Purchase Application (required of domestic individuals, S04 Art.57)
