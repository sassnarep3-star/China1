Converting through crypto or a stablecoin (USDT)

Route P04 · Prohibited

Frequently proposed online. In China it is an illegal financial activity, and the offshore exchange serving you is itself acting unlawfully.

Statutory fine band0–30%of the amount; 30%–100% where the case is serious S03 · Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration ↗

Ceiling or gate
Exchange between legal tender and virtual currency is an illegal financial activity to be strictly prohibited (S16 Arts.1(2)-(3)); it may also be treated as disguised FX trading (S03 Art.45).

Advantages

  • None. The route is not available lawfully.

Disadvantages

  • Virtual currency has no legal-tender status and may not circulate as currency in China (S16 Art.1(1)).
  • Offshore exchanges serving mainland residents through the internet are committing an illegal financial activity (S16 Art.1(3)).
  • Banks and payment institutions may not process the transfers, so the on-ramp is itself a person willing to break the rules (S16 Art.9).
  • In the US, a receipt treated as a gift or a sale has its own reporting consequences.
