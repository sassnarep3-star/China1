The conversion penalty

Five layers, separated: spread, bank charges, correspondent charges, tax on the event, and penalty exposure.

The brief asked for the conversion penalty to be broken down. It decomposes into five parts, and only two of them are what people usually mean.

The five layers

LayerWhat it isWho sets itSize on $400,000Source
1 · FX spreadThe gap between the bank's buying and selling priceThe bank, commercially$839.70S09 · Bank of China FX rate board ↗ S03 · Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration ↗
2 · Bank commission and cableA percentage fee with a floor and a cap, plus a fixed communication chargeThe bank's tariff$61.26U01
3 · Correspondent and receiving bankFixed charges deducted by the banks that touch the wireEach bank in the chain$40.00S17 · Bank of America personal fee schedule ↗ U02
4 · Tax on the eventChinese IIT on the income or gain that created the balance — owed whether or not you move itStatute20% of the gain, not of the balance S08 · Individual Income Tax Law (official English text) ↗S08 · Individual Income Tax Law (official English text) ↗
5 · PenaltyOnly if the route is unlawfulStatute, discretionary within the band0–30%, or 30%–100% if seriousS03 · Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration ↗

Layers 1–3 are the cost of converting: together $940.95 on $400,000, or 0.235%. Layer 4 is the cost of having earned the money. Layer 5 is the cost of getting it wrong. The brief describes "a considerable amount of taxes"; on the evidence, that description fits layer 4 or layer 5, not layer 1–3.

Why this page has no "penalty calculator" for your balance. The Chinese provisions set a discretionary band, not a formula; the fine depends on the finding, the amount, cooperation and history. Producing a single number would be inventing one. What can be stated exactly is the ceiling of each band, which is what the table above does.

What this is. A source-led research edition on converting renminbi held in mainland China into US dollars. Every legal proposition links to an official instrument; every number is quoted, derived by arithmetic from a quoted number, or openly marked as an assumption.

What this is not. Legal, tax or investment advice for your specific facts. The classification of your money — and therefore the correct route — depends on facts this edition does not have.
