Tax

What is taxed, where, and why the conversion itself is not the taxable event.

Two tax systems, one movement of money. The Chinese system taxes the event that created the money; the US system taxes worldwide income and, separately, requires the account to be reported.

China: the tax is on the event, not the wire

The Individual Income Tax Law lists nine categories of taxable income S08 · Individual Income Tax Law (official English text) ↗. Currency exchange is not one of them, and no instrument in this edition's source register imposes a levy on the act of converting renminbi into foreign currency. The Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration deals with convertibility and rates, not with tax S03 · Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration ↗.

What does exist is a documentary precondition: the emigration and inheritance routes require a tax certificate or tax-paid voucher from the tax authority S07 · Interim Measures on Outward Transfer of Personal Property (2004) ↗, and an overseas individual's purchase requires source-of-funds evidence that expressly includes tax vouchers S02 · Detailed Rules on Individual FX (2007, as amended) ↗. A bank asking for a tax receipt is asking you to prove the money is clean, not charging you for the exchange.

United States: principal is not income

A US citizen is taxed on worldwide income S19 · IRS Publication 54 ↗, and Chinese tax already paid on the same income may be creditable S13 · IRS - Foreign tax credit ↗. But moving your own after-tax money from one account to another is not a taxable event, and §988(e)(2) switches off foreign-currency gain on a personal transaction unless the gain exceeds USD 200 S12 · 26 U.S.C. §988 ↗. A USD 400,000 balance that has sat in renminbi while the rate moved will usually produce a smaller gain than that; if it does not, the gain — not the balance — is what is taxed.

The American exposure is reporting, not conversion: FBAR above USD 10,000 in aggregate at any time in the year S10 · IRS - Report of Foreign Bank and Financial Accounts (FBAR) ↗, and Form 3520 Part IV for a gift or bequest from a foreign person above USD 100,000 S11 · IRS - Gifts from a foreign person ↗. A gift is excluded from income; the obligation is to declare it.

One rate deliberately left blank. Chinese withholding on dividends paid to a non-resident enterprise is real, but the statutory and reduced rates were not verified against primary text in this session, so route M10 states no number. It is published as U07 rather than guessed.

What this is. A source-led research edition on converting renminbi held in mainland China into US dollars. Every legal proposition links to an official instrument; every number is quoted, derived by arithmetic from a quoted number, or openly marked as an assumption.

What this is not. Legal, tax or investment advice for your specific facts. The classification of your money — and therefore the correct route — depends on facts this edition does not have.
