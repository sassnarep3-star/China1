US reporting

FBAR, Form 3520 and the foreign tax credit — the obligations that outlast the transfer.

Three US filings matter when money leaves China. None of them is a tax on the transfer; all of them carry penalties that dwarf the cost of moving the money.

What the FBAR rule does not care about

The IRS states that whether the account produced taxable income has no effect on whether it is a reportable foreign financial account S10 · IRS - Report of Foreign Bank and Financial Accounts (FBAR) ↗. A dormant renminbi savings account that has never earned interest is still reportable. That is the trap in this fact pattern: the money sitting still, unconverted, is exactly the money that generates the reporting duty.

Ordering the work

  1. Reconstruct the account history. Aggregate balances at their highest point in each year determine whether the FBAR threshold was crossed, and which years need attention.
  2. Decide what the money is. Principal, gift, inheritance or income — each has a different US treatment.
  3. File before you move. A transfer does not cure a past non-filing; it tends to surface it.
  4. Keep the Chinese paper. Exchange memos are valid for 24 months for reconversion S02 · Detailed Rules on Individual FX (2007, as amended) ↗, and tax vouchers are what unlock the transfer in the first place.
Not advice. Whether a filing is late, whether a penalty applies, and whether a compliance procedure is available are questions for a US tax professional who can see the account history. This page states the rules, not your position under them.
