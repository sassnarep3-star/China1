Remit USD you already hold (no conversion at all)

Route M06 · Bank & wire

If part of the balance is already in a foreign-currency savings account, there is nothing to convert - you only pay the wire.

Total cost on $400,000$101.260.025% of the balance
Fixed charges per transfer$62cable + correspondent + receiving bank
Transfers1one wire with documents
Status
LAWFUL
Who can use it
Anyone holding USD (or another convertible currency) in a Chinese FX savings account.
Legal basis
S02 · Detailed Rules on Individual FX (2007, as amended) ↗ S04 · Current-Account FX Business Guide 2020 ↗
Ceiling or gate
An overseas individual remitting from an FX savings account does so on valid ID (S02 Art.15). The USD 50,000/day threshold is the ID-only processing limit for a domestic individual (S02 Art.14).
Tax treatment
No Chinese tax and no conversion spread; only the transfer charges. US reporting still applies.

Step by step

  1. Check the currency split of the balance

    Only the foreign-currency portion avoids conversion.

    S02 · Detailed Rules on Individual FX (2007, as amended) ↗

  2. Instruct the wire from the FX savings account

    An overseas individual remits on valid ID (S02 Art.15).

    S02 · Detailed Rules on Individual FX (2007, as amended) ↗

  3. Confirm who pays the charges

    Charges may be shared (SHA) or borne by the sender (OUR); the receiving bank warns that correspondent fees can still be deducted (S17).

    S17 · Bank of America personal fee schedule ↗

Advantages

  • Zero FX spread - the single largest component of the cost disappears.
  • Minimal documentation for an overseas individual (S02 Art.15).

Disadvantages

  • Only works for the portion already held in foreign currency.
  • Correspondent and receiving-bank charges still apply.

Documents and evidence

  • Passport
  • FX account statement
  • US bank details
