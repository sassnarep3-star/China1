Step by step

  1. Decide the amount per trip

    Up to USD 5,000 needs no permit; USD 5,000-10,000 needs a bank permit (S14).

    S14 · Rules on carrying foreign cash across the border (2003) ↗

  2. Obtain the permit before departure

    A bank may issue only up to the deposit or purchase amount, maximum USD 10,000 per permit (S14).

    S14 · Rules on carrying foreign cash across the border (2003) ↗

  3. Declare on re-entry where required

    Carrying in more than USD 5,000 equivalent must be declared in writing to Customs (S04 Art.89).

    S04 · Current-Account FX Business Guide 2020 ↗

  4. Declare to US Customs if above USD 10,000

    A separate US obligation applies.

    no official source located — see flagged items

Advantages

  • No SAFE approval for amounts up to USD 5,000 per exit.
  • The cash and transfer rates at the bank are identical on the sample board (S09), so there is no extra cash spread at that bank on that day.

Disadvantages

  • USD 400,000 would need 40 permitted trips at USD 10,000 each.
  • Illegal carriage of FX across the border carries a warning and a fine of up to 20% of the amount (S03 Art.42).
  • Physical loss and theft risk is total, not proportional.
  • Entering the US with more than USD 10,000 in cash triggers a separate US declaration obligation.

Documents and evidence

  • Passport
  • Bank carrying permit (USD 5,000-10,000)
  • SAFE permit (above USD 10,000 in principle)
