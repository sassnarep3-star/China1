Flagged for review

Everything that could not be verified, plus every assumption the model depends on.

Twelve items that could not be confirmed from an official source, conflict with another instrument, or are deliberate assumptions. Each one is published here with its effect on the numbers, rather than being quietly resolved in the model.

How to use this page. If an item here matters to your decision — especially U09, the definitional conflict that decides whether you get the one-wire route or the eight-year one — take the question to the bank or to a professional in writing. The links are there so you can hand them over.

U09
High		 Definitional conflict: who is a 'domestic individual'The 2008 Regulation defines a domestic individual to include a foreigner who has resided in China for one year or more (S03 Art.52(2)), while the 2006 Measures define an overseas individual by passport (S01 Art.37(2)); S05 applies the no-quota treatment on the basis of the permanent-residence ID. The instruments are not obviously aligned.This is the single most consequential ambiguity in the analysis: it decides whether M01's no-quota treatment or M02's eight-year drip applies. Ask the branch, in writing, which basis it applies.S03 · Regulation on Foreign Exchange Administration ↗ S01 · PBOC Measures on Individual FX (2006) ↗ S05 · SAFE Tianjin Q&A (2024) on Art.59 ↗

What this is. A source-led research edition on converting renminbi held in mainland China into US dollars. Every legal proposition links to an official instrument; every number is quoted, derived by arithmetic from a quoted number, or openly marked as an assumption.

What this is not. Legal, tax or investment advice for your specific facts. The classification of your money — and therefore the correct route — depends on facts this edition does not have.
