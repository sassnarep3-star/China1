F-D02mediumBoC 'counter' fees used; 2026 online pricing unconfirmed
The published personal e-banking table (effective 2014-11-01) charges 1‰ (min ¥50 / max ¥260) + ¥150 telegraph fee for international wires. A 2025 campaign (2025-05→2025-12) waived fees for wires to BoC's own overseas branches and gave 9折 for wires to other banks via online channels. The 2026 renewal was NOT confirmed this session, so the ladder uses the counter standard — your actual online cost may be 10% lower (≈¥369 vs ¥410 per wire) or zero if BoC's overseas branch is in your US city.		Check BoC's current 个人跨境汇款 page before executing; verify whether the 0.9 discount or a branch fee-waiver applies.

F-D04mediumEmigrant/inheritance routes rely on the 2004 Announcement; current SAFE practice not re-verified

F-D13medium§988 analysis depends on your functional currency
26 U.S.C. § 988 (S20) treats FX-denominated transactions as ordinary. Whether converting your CNY deposit generates ordinary income depends on ¥ vs $ functional-currency status (your life/work currency) — a US citizen living in China may have a CNY functional currency, changing both §988 and measurement. This edition does NOT decide your functional currency.		Fix functional currency with a US CPA before the conversion, not after.
