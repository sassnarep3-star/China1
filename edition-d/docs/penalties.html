Depth section · the two regimes that actually bite

Penalties and US exposure, quantified

1 · China regime — when the money moves unlawfully

  • 逃汇 (evasive outbound transfer of foreign exchange), Art. 39: transferring FX that should stay in China out of the country by unlawful means — fine ≤30% of the amount; serious → 30%–100%; criminal exposure if a crime is constituted. penalty exposure [S07]
  • 私自买卖 / 变相买卖 / 倒买倒卖外汇， Art. 45: same two-band structure — ≤30%; serious 30%–100% of the amount; criminal if constituted. [S07]
  • 分拆 / facilitation-quota evasion, 汇发〔2020〕14号 Art. 62: splitting purchases to evade quota or genuineness administration places the person on the “关注名单” governance track with diminished facilitation; details not fully re-verified this session — see F-D10. [S03]
  • 个人外汇管理办法 Art. 26 reference: capital-account emigrant/inheritance property transfers follow the capital-account procedures — using a non-transfer purpose code to move the same money IS the offence under Art. 39/45. [S01] [S07]

2 · US regime — when the account is not reported

  • FBAR (FinCEN 114), 31 CFR 1010.821, assessed ≥ 2025-01-17: non-willful up to $16,536 per annual report (per report, not per account — Bittner v. United States, 598 U.S. 85 (2023)); willful = greater of $165,353 or 50% of the balance. [S13] [S14] [S15]
  • Form 8938 (26 USC 6038D): $10,000 for failure to file; +$10,000 per 30 days the failure continues after IRS notice; maximum additional $50,000 (i.e., up to $60,000); statute threshold $50,000 base (higher thresholds prescribed by the Secretary — filing status and residence matter). Penalty applies even if no tax is due. [S16]
  • Form 3520 (foreign gifts, 26 USC 6039F): aggregate foreign gifts >$10,000 statutory base (adjusted; IRS instructions prescribe operational amounts, typically higher for individuals) — failure to report: the Secretary determines the tax consequences of the gift and 5% per month, max 25% of the gift. A bequest to you from a foreign decedent counts. Reasonable-cause exception exists. [S17] [S18]
  • §988 ordinary characterization: FX gain/loss on the conversion itself is ordinary (not capital) — relevant to tax on investment gain in the account, not to the transfer mechanics. [S20]
  • §6038 backbone: entity-held balances shift to information-return penalties on the entity side (Forms 5471/8865/8858), uncapped stack risk. See F-D14. [S22]

4 · The quiet penalty: staging

The §988-safest and China-cheapest bulk route is not a “penalty” but a wait: 移民转移 releases 50% / 75% / 100% over 24 months (S04/S05). Time-value on $400k at 3% discount rate ≈ $12k of forgone return — still an order of magnitude below one year of willful-FBAR on the same $400k ($200k). The arithmetic, not the fear, should drive the choice.
