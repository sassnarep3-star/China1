Step by step

  1. Confirm the card's remaining annual overseas cash allowance

    The cap aggregates every domestic card in your name (S15).

    S15 · Notice on overseas ATM cash withdrawals (2017) ↗

  2. Withdraw within the daily per-card cap

    RMB 10,000 equivalent per card per day (S15).

    S15 · Notice on overseas ATM cash withdrawals (2017) ↗

  3. Convert locally and keep receipts

    No official source for local ATM or conversion fees was located (flag U04).

    no official source located — see flagged items

  4. Declare cash on entry to the US if it exceeds USD 10,000

    A separate US obligation applies to the physical movement of cash.

    no official source located — see flagged items

Advantages

  • No SAFE application, no bank appointment.
  • Works today, in small amounts.

Disadvantages

  • Structurally slow: about USD 14,942 a year at the board rate, so USD 400,000 needs 27 card-years.
  • Exceeding the annual cap suspends overseas cash withdrawal for the rest of that year and the next (S15).
  • Cash is the least safe and most expensive form of value to move, and carrying more than USD 10,000 into the US triggers a separate US reporting obligation.
  • ATM fees and the local conversion spread are set by the ATM operator and the card network - not verified here (flag U04).

Documents and evidence

  • Chinese bank card
  • Passport
