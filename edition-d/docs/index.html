Converting renminbi held in mainland China into US dollars, for a US citizen, on official sources only.

The short answer

There is no conversion tax. There is a spread, a set of fixed fees, a documentary gate — and, only if you pick an unlawful route, a penalty.

For a US citizen holding lawful current-account renminbi earned in China, the cheapest route on the evidence is a bank FX purchase plus a SWIFT wire handled as an overseas individual: $940.95 on a $400,000 balance — 0.235%. That is the number the phrase "considerable amount of taxes" turns out to describe when the money is taken out lawfully.

Every route, ranked by what it costs

#1 · Bank & wire

Bank FX purchase + SWIFT wire (documented China-source income)

The default route: buy USD at your Chinese bank against proof of lawful current-account income, then wire it to your US account.

LAWFUL #2 · Bank & wire

Bank FX purchase + wire, drip-fed under the USD 50,000 annual quota

What most people are told to do: spend the USD 50,000-per-person-per-year facilitation quota, year after year.

CONDITIONAL #3 · Bank & wire

Emigration transfer (移民转移) - whole-balance route after you emigrate

If you have given up Chinese household registration and settled abroad, SAFE can approve the transfer of your entire pre-emigration domestic property, released in three stages over two years.

CONDITIONAL #4 · Bank & wire

Inheritance transfer (继承转移)

A foreign citizen who has lawfully inherited property in China can apply to SAFE to realise it and remit the proceeds.

CONDITIONAL #5 · Property

Sale proceeds of your own Chinese property (overseas individual)

If you are an overseas individual selling a Chinese home you own, the proceeds can be purchased and remitted with SAFE approval.

CONDITIONAL #6 · Bank & wire

Remit USD you already hold (no conversion at all)

If part of the balance is already in a foreign-currency savings account, there is nothing to convert - you only pay the wire.

LAWFUL #7 · Offshore

RMB to Hong Kong, convert offshore (CNH), then wire to the US

Move RMB to your own Hong Kong account, convert it there at offshore rates, and wire USD onward.

CONDITIONAL #8 · Cash & card

Withdraw RMB abroad with a Chinese bank card, then convert

Use the card at an overseas ATM, then convert the cash locally.

CONDITIONAL #9 · Cash & card

Carry foreign cash out of China

Physical carriage, inside the customs permit thresholds.

CONDITIONAL #10 · Corporate

Company dividend remittance (if the money belongs to a company you own)

If the balance sits in a Chinese company rather than your own name, a profit distribution is the normal route - and it carries withholding tax on the profit.

CONDITIONAL #11 · Corporate

Liquidation or capital reduction of a foreign-invested enterprise

An express statutory entitlement: after a foreign-invested enterprise is lawfully terminated, liquidated and taxed, the foreign investor's RMB may be purchased and remitted.

CONDITIONAL #20 · Prohibited

Splitting a balance across relatives' quotas (分拆结售汇)

The 'ants moving house' pattern. Prohibited, monitored, and the most common way a manageable problem becomes a penalty.

PROHIBITED #21 · Prohibited

Underground banks and private FX dealing (地下钱庄)

A counterparty takes RMB in China and pays USD abroad, netting the two sides. Criminal, and the counterparty risk is total.

PROHIBITED #22 · Prohibited

False documents and false purpose declarations (骗购外汇)

Inventing a study, travel or trade purpose, or using a fabricated invoice, to obtain FX.

PROHIBITED #23 · Prohibited

Converting through crypto or a stablecoin (USDT)

Frequently proposed online. In China it is an illegal financial activity, and the offshore exchange serving you is itself acting unlawfully.

PROHIBITED #24 · Prohibited

Carrying out more cash than the permit allows, or not declaring it

The cash route stops being lawful the moment the permit threshold is crossed.

PROHIBITED #25 · Prohibited

Buying FX for overseas property, securities or life insurance

The purpose restriction is the one most likely to catch an otherwise lawful transfer.

PROHIBITED #30 · Not available

Wise, Revolut, Payoneer or similar transfer apps

Popular in other corridors; not a route out of a mainland bank account.

NOT AVAILABLE #31 · Not available

QDII / QDLP funds

An investment product, not a repatriation route: you buy a fund onshore with RMB; you do not get USD into your US account.

Read before acting. The instruments do not define your facts, and one genuine conflict remains unresolved in this edition: the 2008 Regulation counts a foreigner resident in China for a year as a domestic individual, while the 2006 Measures and SAFE's own 2024 answer treat the passport holder as an overseas individual. Ask your branch which basis it applies, in writing. The issue is published as U09.
