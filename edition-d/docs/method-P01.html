P01 · Documented current-account RMB income of an overseas individual

LAWFUL · CONDITIONALPersonal legal entitlementsF01 F02 F03

A foreign citizen classified by the bank as an overseas individual may purchase and remit lawful current-account RMB income (salary, services, rent, royalties and similar) with identity, source-of-funds and tax evidence. The rule text does not impose the domestic-person USD50k facilitation quota on this documented route.

S02 · SAFE / PBOC S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S31 · State Taxation Administration / Guangdong S46 · People's Bank of China

Eligibility

  • Bank classifies you as 境外个人 under PBOC Order [2006] No. 3 Art. 37, normally by passport/entry-exit documents.
  • Income is lawful current-account RMB earned in mainland China.
  • You can prove the transaction amount and tax treatment.

Pros

  • Direct legal basis for earned income.
  • No USD50k annual cap in the documented overseas-person income rule.
  • Can use a bank TT or an eligible provider rail.

Cons

  • Bank authenticity/tax review is substantive.
  • Old income may be hard to evidence.
  • PRC tax residence and U.S. worldwide-income reporting are separate questions.
  • A still-active PRC resident ID can create classification friction.

Step-by-step

  1. Ask the bank to confirm your individual-FX classification in writing before moving funds.
  2. Gather passport/ID, employment or service contracts, payslips/invoices, bank statements showing receipt, and tax payment/withholding records for the exact income.
  3. Ask the bank for its current checklist for Art. 59 / PBOC Art. 13 purchases; obtain any missing tax certificate from the competent tax office.
  4. Submit the purchase-and-remittance application for the documented net amount; do not describe savings as salary.
  5. Compare the bank's executable CNY-to-USD rate, commission, cable and intermediary-fee treatment with another eligible rail.
  6. Remit to your own U.S. account; keep the purchase slip, remittance receipt and tax records for U.S. reporting.

Requirements

  • Passport and bank-classified overseas-person status
  • Income contracts/payslips/invoices
  • Bank statements tracing the RMB
  • Tax vouchers or competent-tax-office certificate
  • Own U.S. recipient account details
  • FBAR/FATCA analysis
