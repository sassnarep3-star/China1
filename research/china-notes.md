## Scope and evidence discipline

This is a source-backed requirements and route-screening review, not a declaration that the user qualifies for any route. US citizenship does not by itself establish Chinese tax residence, the bank's 境内/境外个人 classification, possession of a Chinese foreign-permanent-resident identity card, immigration-property eligibility, the source of the balance, or prior tax compliance. Those facts were not supplied and remain blank/flagged.

Government/regulator pages were read using the web retrieval tools. Every structured requirement and finding names official source IDs with URLs and locators in `china.json`. No commercial fee or exchange-rate quote is inferred from regulatory rules. No account balance, income category or tax residence is assumed.

## Most consequential conclusions

1. **Separate the underlying income from the transfer.** The FX rules reviewed do not impose a universal conversion tax. Tax depends on what generated the money and when; the bank's documentary controls are a different question. This is a bounded legal-source conclusion, not a statement that every transfer is tax-free.
2. **Documented salary is the first bank route to investigate for an eligible outside individual.** SAFE's July 2026 Q&A requires identity plus transaction-amount source/tax evidence. Do not describe USD 50,000 as an absolute cap on documented current-account income. The foreign-permanent-resident card rule is explicit in SAFE's May 2026 Q&A.
3. **A USD 50,000 threshold is not always the same rule.** Annual facilitation quota and service-trade outbound tax-filing threshold have different purposes. Neither is a universal cap on an approved asset transfer.
4. **Immigration/inheritance property transfer is a separate approval route.** It requires the defined status, lawful asset/inheritance evidence and tax/title records. The 2014 amendment removed the old staged emigration schedule; the RMB 500,000 threshold concerns review routing. The cited guides publish no numerical quantity limit and no administrative approval fee. Bank execution costs are separate and unverified.
5. **Important 2026 correction:** MOF/STA Announcement 2026 No.27 imposes 20% IIT on foreign individuals' FIE dividends/bonuses from 2026-09-01 and repeals the former exemption. Do not reuse older exemption claims. The separate 10% nonresident-enterprise rule is not the individual rate.
6. **Cross-border RMB can be a lawful current-account alternative where both banks support it.** It does not convert RMB into USD automatically and does not authorize undocumented capital flight.
7. **Crypto, underground banks, quota splitting and false transaction documents are not recommended routes.** The official 2021 virtual-currency notice, anti-splitting rules and FX penalties are directly relevant.

## Source-reading decisions and irregularities

- The 2004 gazette text is original law, not a current consolidated text. Its staged-remittance paragraph is read subject to 汇发〔2014〕2号 section 6. The current 2024 approval guides are used for practical documents and timing.
- The 2024 Anhui capital-account guidance was retrieved in multiple chunks; the relevant property-sale/net-proceeds rules were read directly. Local handling details are not assumed to replace national rules.
- The Hebei approval guides quote the national 2024 capital-account guide. Their published timing is an approval commitment, not an end-to-end bank settlement promise. The guides' no-fee statement covers the administrative permission only.
- The dividend change was read on both the Ministry of Finance and STA official sites. This takes precedence over older STA/1994 explanations in search results.
- The enterprise-tax page was retrieved at its opening and its article 91 was located in the indexed official text; it is used only for the narrow enterprise/individual distinction, not a personalized withholding determination.
- General web searches returned numerous SEO pages and obsolete summaries. None is used as primary evidence.
