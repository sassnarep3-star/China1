Sequenced plan

What to do, in what order

The ordering is not arbitrary. Doing step 3 before step 2 is the mistake that turns a paperwork problem into a penalty problem — and the penalties are roughly fifty times larger.

Zero-cost first move Check for a Chinese Foreigner's Permanent Residence ID. If you hold one, the ordinary $50,000-a-year purchase route is open to you under Art.59 and most of this plan is unnecessary. This costs nothing and changes everything.

Step 1  Determine the source of funds — in writing

Pull the statements back to the origin. Not "savings" — the actual event that created each tranche: a payroll deposit, a property completion, an estate distribution, a transfer from before you emigrated.

This single step determines everything downstream, costs nothing, and is the step people skip. It is also the step a bank will effectively perform for you, less sympathetically, at the counter.

Step 2  Fix US reporting before touching the money

  • FBAR — $10,000 aggregate at any time; due 15 April, auto-extended to 15 October; filed with FinCEN, not with your return.
  • Form 8938 — only if you file a return and the applicable threshold is met.
  • If years were missed and it was not willful — Streamlined Filing Compliance Procedures.
  • Do not quiet-disclose. Penalties already assessed are not abated by later joining the streamlined procedures.

Full US obligations →

Why first: an unreported account accrues exposure every year you hold it, independent of any conversion. Three missed years at the non-willful maximum is about $49,608 — against roughly $1,000 of spread on a $100,000 conversion.

Step 3  Obtain the tax clearance certificate

This is the hard gate, and it is not optional. It is a mandatory attachment for both the emigrant transfer (Art.8(6)) and the home-sale remittance (Art.5), and Art.59 remittances require tax documents.

Budget for the Chinese IIT rather than trying to route around it: 20% flat on a property gain, on proceeds less original cost and reasonable expenses. Trying to avoid it converts a 20% cost into a 30–100% exposure.

Step 4  Choose the route and prepare the file

Take the row from Step 1 and build the documents the bank will actually ask for. For any single payment above USD 50,000, add a 服务贸易等项目对外支付税务备案 — filed with the tax bureau, not the bank.

Expect the bank to exercise judgement: Art.68 lets it refuse a transaction it cannot verify, and requires it to explain why and tell you how to appeal.

Step 5  If this is the emigrant-property route, start early

C02 pays out 50% first, half the remainder after 12 months, the balance after 24 months. You take the exchange rate at three dates you do not control. Above RMB 500,000 the application goes from the local SAFE branch to the SAFE head office, so the timeline is not yours.

This phasing risk is usually underweighted against the spread you were originally worried about — and it is genuinely larger.

Step 6  Fix identity data before you send

From 2026-01-01, any outbound remittance of RMB 5,000 / USD 1,000 or more requires the institution to verify the remitter's identity accurately, and name, account, address and payee details travel with the payment. Records are kept 10 years.

Name and address mismatches between your Chinese account and your US bank are now a leading cause of rejection. Reconcile them in advance — it is free and it is the most common avoidable failure.

Never  Improvise a route not on this list

Underground banks, splitting across relatives' quotas, false declarations, crypto bridges, card lending. Penalties attach to the whole amount — up to 100% — and several carry criminal referral.

Professional help, by stage

Now

A US CPA experienced in foreign-account reporting. This is the highest-return dollar you will spend, because it addresses the largest number in the analysis.

Before Step 3

A Chinese-qualified tax adviser for the 完税凭证 and, if a property is involved, the 20% computation and any available cost deductions.

Before Step 4

A Chinese-qualified lawyer to confirm the route fits your facts — particularly for C02, where the hukou decision is one-way.
