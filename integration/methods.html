Methods, pros, cons and steps

43 candidates, not 43 interchangeable ways to export the same savings. A legal entitlement, an execution rail, an adjacent alternative and a rejected scheme are different objects. Select the true source-of-funds route first, then one execution rail. Prohibited schemes have risk information only—no execution instructions.
