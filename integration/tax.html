China and U.S. tax & reporting

Tax follows the income, not the wire. There is no verified universal CNY→USD conversion penalty. Underlying income, property gain, dividends, currency-basis gain and reporting failures are separate issues. A foreign tax credit is not an automatic full offset.

Practical compliance sequence

  1. Reconstruct annual maximum foreign-account balances and review FBAR for every year the Chinese account existed, including the year it is emptied.
  2. Test Form 8938 separately using residence, filing status and asset values.
  3. Trace each income tranche and its PRC tax record; analyze U.S. worldwide income and any limited foreign tax credit.
  4. For CNY disposition, reconstruct acquisition dates/basis and personal versus investment/business character before applying §988.
  5. For genuine foreign gifts/bequests or trusts, analyze Form 3520. Keep bank, conversion and remittance evidence.
