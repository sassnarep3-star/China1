Methodology and limitations

Source hierarchy

  1. Government, regulator, statute, court and official tax guidance for legal/tax claims.
  2. A bank or provider's own tariff/help page for its own prices and product terms only.
  3. No blog, forum, aggregator or law-firm summary supports a consolidated numeric or legal row.

Cost model

  • Targets are gross USD-equivalent principal before fees, not guaranteed net receipt.
  • P = target × CNY6.6903, using BOC's 2026-11-10 07:32:39 China-time indicative telegraphic ask. The actual execution rate controls.
  • BOC sender = min(CNY260, max(CNY50, 0.1%×P)) + CNY150 international cable. Overseas charges are separate; full-arrival is optional and conditional.
  • HSBC Premier/Advance sender = min(CNY500, max(CNY100, 0.1%×P)) + CNY120 cable. Other institutions' charges and taxes are excluded. The USD equivalent uses BOC's ask only for comparison; HSBC's own executable FX was not captured.
  • One transfer is assumed. No quota splitting or repeated-fee estimate is invented. Wise, ATM, cash, drafts and other methods retain their own formulas or blank values in Edition H.
  • FX cost, intermediary/receiver charges, documents, personal tax and all-in totals remain blank where unknown. N/A means not applicable, not zero.

Repository and archive policy

Earlier editions remain unchanged. Material inconsistencies are disclosed on Flagged for Review rather than silently rewritten in archives. Mechanical verification proves deterministic arithmetic, source presence, blank semantics and local-link integrity; it does not establish legal correctness or future external-link uptime.

Safety and legality

Quota splitting, underground settlement, fabricated trade and crypto/stablecoin bridges are not tax-saving recommendations. Prohibited candidates have no operational steps. Business, investment and cash routes require real underlying transactions and cannot be used to relabel an unrelated personal balance.
