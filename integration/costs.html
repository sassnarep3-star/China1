How to use the comparison

  1. First establish the legal entitlement in the method catalog.
  2. Ask each eligible provider for an executable quote for the same CNY amount and same guaranteed USD outcome, at the same time.
  3. Add FX difference, optional full-arrival/correspondent charges, receiving fee, documents and actual underlying tax. Do not add both a personal-entitlement scenario and a separate rail fee twice.
  4. Prefer the lowest verified total among eligible routes—not the smallest advertised sender fee. At USD20,000–40,000 in this scenario, HSBC P/A's published sender fee is lower; at USD10,000 and from USD50,000 upward, BOC's is lower. This says nothing about FX or eligibility.
