Deep analysis & decision logic

Cross-jurisdiction synthesis of the China, US and route evidence. Conditional decision support, not a personalized tax calculation or a cheapest-provider claim.

Headline: The best first step is to identify the lawful source of the CNY and use the matching documented bank/approval route. Conversion fees, income tax, currency gain/loss and reporting penalties are different costs; no single tax-penalty percentage can be verified from the facts supplied.
