One transfer of gross USD-equivalent principal. BOC: min(CNY260, max(CNY50, 0.1% × CNY principal)) + CNY150 cable. HSBC Premier/Advance: min(CNY500, max(CNY100, 0.1% × CNY principal)) + CNY120 cable. CNY principal and USD fee comparisons use BOC's indicative ask CNY6.6903/USD1; HSBC's own executable FX was not captured.

These are not all-in totals. HSBC Private Banking individual TT commission/cable is published as free for eligible clients, but FX, third-party and relationship costs are not thereby zero.

Recommended order of work

  1. Classify each tranche by its true source and confirm the bank's individual-FX identity classification.
  2. Collect transaction-amount source evidence and applicable PRC tax records. Do not relabel savings as salary, trade, a gift or a dividend.
  3. Use the matching entitlement: current income, pre-emigration property, inheritance, property sale, insurance, unused prior conversion, or genuine business transaction.
  4. Only then compare same-time executable BOC, HSBC and eligible provider quotes, including FX and every downstream charge.
  5. Keep purchase slips, SWIFT/receipt records and tax evidence; reconcile U.S. worldwide-income, FBAR/FATCA and any Form 3520 duties.
