Practical next steps — conditional, not an execution guarantee

  1. Classify each portion of the balance by its true source; retain the bank trail.
  2. Have the bank confirm FX identity classification and the applicable route. Article 13 supports documented lawful current income of an overseas individual; other sources require their own rules.
  3. Gather identity, source and applicable tax evidence. For an emigrant or inheritance route, obtain the competent SAFE approval and current checklist.
  4. Compare an executable bank quote with an eligible provider quote for the same USD outcome, at the same time. Include FX, sender, cable, intermediary and receiving charges.
  5. Execute only after approval. Retain conversion slips, remittance receipts and tax records; reconcile U.S. income and foreign-account reporting.
