Getting money out of a China bank account and into US dollars

For a US citizen holding CNY in a mainland account. Which routes are lawful, what each costs at every size up to $400,000, and what tax and reporting follow.

The short answer

  • No rule read for this guide taxes the conversion itself. The tax exposure is on how the money was earned. If China tax on it was already paid, converting adds bank charges only. SAFE Q&A 2026IIT Law
  • Lawful route: take your passport, proof of the amount and your China tax record to the bank, buy USD and wire it to your own US account. The rule states no fixed cap for a foreign passport holder; the bank converts what the documents support. SAFE Q&A 2026Individual FX Measures
  • Cost by Bank of China wire into a Chase account: $93.39 for $10,000 (0.93%), $311.26 for $100,000 (0.31%), $941.20 for $400,000 (0.24%). BOC rate boardBOC tariffChase fees
  • Do not split the amount, borrow other people's quotas, use money changers or crypto. Fines run up to 30% of the amount, and 30%–100% if serious, with possible criminal liability. Individual FX MeasuresFX RegulationPBOC 2026 No. 42
  • In the US: the pages read tax the income in the year it is earned, not the later transfer. File the FBAR and Form 8938 if the account passed the thresholds. IRS citizens abroadIRS 8938 vs FBAR
