An evidence-led guide to converting yuan and moving funds from mainland China to the United States.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Start with the source,
not a workaround.

For documented, lawful China-source current-account income, the first route to assess is a bank purchase of foreign exchange followed by a remittance. A tax receipt can prove the status of the underlying income; it does not, by itself, establish a new tax on conversion.
