What it costs to get money out of China.

The premise is the expensive part. There is no tax on converting your own money in either country, and the real cost of a lawful transfer is under 1% at every size. What stops the money is China's capital-account rule, and what can cost half the balance is a US filing failure. This site prices every route and shows its work.
