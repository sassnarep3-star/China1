# Independent follow-up audit — 2026-11-10

## Scope and limitations

This is a targeted independent review, **not** a line-by-line legal verification of the entire repository. The site contains hundreds of archived HTML files and many historical claims; neither the automated checks nor this review establish that all archived assertions are current or legally accurate. The consolidated portal is the primary edition. Historical versions may conflict with it; see its [Flagged for Review](../integration/review.html). A source link shows provenance, not necessarily support for every claim in a row. Regulatory status and prices may change; professional advice and a fresh written transaction quote are needed.

## Verification and irregularities

- A defect in the consolidated checker treated any row with no links like a header. It now differentiates rows containing data cells from headers, and a regression probe tests the distinction. The check validates HTTPS source-domain presence, not that the linked source supports every figure or proposition.
- The whole-repository local-file crawl checks file existence; it does **not** check every archived page's internal fragment, all externally linked source availability, or factual accuracy. A targeted scan found archived official source links using HTTP rather than HTTPS, including three Edition I source-register rows and two Edition H references; these are preserved as historical material and not asserted to have been independently validated.
- The 40-row consolidated ladder uses an earlier BOC indicative ask and a **single** outbound remittance assumption. Its sender-fee components are scenario arithmetic, not actual quotes, net receipts, tax, penalties, or full transfer costs. HSBC's comparison uses BOC's ask; it is not an HSBC FX quote. Actual totals and case-specific personal tax remain blank and unverified.
- Full source-by-source legal review of all other routes and all archived versions remains outstanding; direct link inspection is limited to the sources above. A blank value must not be interpreted as zero. No personal details, tax evidence, or binding bank quotes were provided, so no one method can be certified as best for this account.
