M01 Annual facilitation quota — current-account FX purchase and remittance个人年度便利化额度购汇汇出 The default retail channel. A domestic individual buys foreign exchange against ID within an annual aggregate quota and remits it abroad for a current-account purpose (travel, study, medical, family support, goods trade, non-investment insurance, advisory services). Lawful · current account Lawful USD 50,000RMB-equivalent USD 50,000 per person per year, aggregated across settlement (结汇) and purchase (购汇). 0% — no PRC individual income tax arises from the act of exchanging CNY for USDThe Individual Income Tax Law taxes nine enumerated categories of income (Art. 2). 'Foreign exchange gain' and 'currency conversion' are not among them. Tax attaches to the underlying income, not the exchange.
Source: S05
 Not gross income. Moving your own after-tax funds is not a taxable event. Any exchange gain on the currency itself is a §988 transaction, but §988(e) exempts an individual's personal transaction from recognition unless the gain exceeds USD 200.
Source: S24
 FBAR (FinCEN 114) if aggregate foreign accounts exceeded USD 10,000 at any time; Form 8938 if the FATCA threshold is met.
Source: S17
 If the declared purpose is false, or the funds are used for an unopened capital-account purpose, the individual may be listed on the watch list and lose the quota for the listing year plus the following two years. Evasion (逃汇) is fined up to 30% of the amount, or 30%–100% if serious.
Source: S01 S12
  • Fast, routine, bank-branch or mobile-banking process.
  • USD 50,000 per person per year with no documentary proof needed inside the quota.
  • No PRC tax on the conversion itself.
  • No US tax on remitting your own after-tax money.
  • USD 50,000 per person per year is small relative to a house-sized balance; a USD 1,000,000 balance takes 20 person-years.
  • The quota is per person — using relatives' quotas to pool funds is a criminal offence, not a planning technique.
  • Purpose must be current-account; overseas property and securities are expressly excluded.
  • Bank bid/offer spread is a real cost but is not published in any official source, so it is not quantified here.

Lawful · current account Conditional unverified 0% — no PRC tax on the conversionTax attaches to the underlying income under IIT Law Art. 2, not to the exchange.

M03 Emigrant property transfer (移民转移)移民财产转移 The dedicated capital-account channel for a person who has emigrated from the mainland (or settled in Hong Kong/Macau) to realise and remit lawful domestic property owned before emigrant status was acquired. Applied for once, remitted in stages. Lawful · capital account Conditional unverified 0% on the conversion, but a tax clearance certificate is a mandatory preconditionArt. 8(6) requires a tax payment certificate or tax clearance certificate from the competent tax authority before SAFE will process the transfer. The transfer itself is not a taxable event; the underlying disposal of property is taxed under IIT Law (property transfer at 20% on proceeds less original cost and reasonable expenses).
Source: S02 S05
 Not gross income; it is your own capital. If the property was sold at a gain after you became a US person, that gain is US taxable (and a foreign tax credit may be available under treaty Art. 22).
Source: S24 S26
 FBAR / Form 8938 as applicable.
Source: S17 S20
 False documents or a duplicate application on the same property to obtain FX = illegal FX arbitrage under Art. 40, up to 30%, or 30%–100% if serious. SAFE will not accept property whose source cannot be proven lawful.
Source: S01 S02
  • The only general-purpose lawful channel that can move an entire lifetime balance, with no USD 50,000 cap.
  • Statutory timeline is finite — the whole balance is out within about two years of first remittance.
  • Requires cancellation of PRC household registration (户籍注销) plus certified proof of foreign residence — a one-way door for a PRC citizen.
  • Requires a PRC tax clearance certificate first.
  • Staged release: 50% now, 25% after a year, 25% after two years.
  • SAFE will refuse if the property is disputed, subject to unresolved litigation, or not provably lawful in origin.
  • Does not apply to a US citizen who never held PRC household registration.

M04 Inheritance property transfer (继承转移)继承财产转移 A foreign citizen, or a Hong Kong/Macau resident, who has lawfully inherited a mainland estate, applies to realise it and remit the proceeds abroad. Applied for once for all property from the same deceased person; may be remitted in one or several payments. Lawful · capital account Conditional unverified 0% on the conversion; tax clearance certificate mandatoryArt. 9(5) requires the deceased's property-right documents and a tax payment or tax clearance certificate from the competent tax authority.
Source: S02
 A bequest is not gross income to the recipient. Inherited assets take a stepped-up basis at date of death, so pre-death appreciation is generally not taxed to the heir.
Source: S22
 Part IV of Form 3520 if the aggregate bequest from the foreign estate exceeds USD 100,000; each gift over USD 5,000 separately identified. FBAR / Form 8938 as applicable.
Source: S21 S22 S17 S20
 False materials or duplicate applications = Art. 40 illegal FX arbitrage, up to 30%, or 30%–100% if serious. Missing Form 3520 = 5% of the gift per month, capped at 25%.
Source: S01 S21
  • No annual cap — the entire inheritance can move.
  • No PRC household-registration cancellation needed (the applicant is already foreign).
  • No US income tax on the bequest itself.
  • Only available to a genuine lawful heir, with notarised kinship and inheritance evidence.
  • Requires the deceased's tax clearance and property-right documents.
  • All property from one deceased must be applied for in a single application.
  • US Form 3520 is separately required and carries a 25% penalty for failure.

This gives foreign-currency exposure but the investor's asset stays inside the PRC financial system, denominated and redeemable in RMB. Not a transfer Lawful unverified Not applicable — no conversion occurs at the investor levelThe investor buys and redeems in RMB; any gain is a PRC securities/investment gain and is not a currency conversion event.
Source: S05
 A foreign (PRC) fund is generally a passive foreign investment company; US holders face PFIC reporting (Form 8621) and PFIC tax treatment. Not a remittance, so no §988 currency leg.
Source: S20
 Form 8938 (the fund interest is a specified foreign financial asset); Form 8621 if the fund is a PFIC. FBAR only if held in a foreign financial account.
Source: S20
 n/a
Source: —
  • Fully lawful way to obtain USD/foreign-asset exposure from onshore RMB.
  • No SAFE application, no quota consumed, no documentary burden on the individual.
  • DOES NOT MOVE MONEY OUT OF CHINA. The asset remains a PRC fund share. This is the single most common misconception about QDII.

Lawful · current account Lawful USD 50,000Per day, not per year: up to USD 50,000 equivalent per day on ID alone; above that, genuine current-account transaction vouchers. 0% — there is no conversionNo conversion means no exchange event at all.

Source: S24
