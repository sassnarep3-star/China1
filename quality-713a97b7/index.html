What the independent checks can—and cannot—show

The reproducible checks render the guide at five screen widths, inspect registered source links and local fragments, and compare the 40-step ladder with its recorded inputs. A passing check is not proof of legal interpretation, source availability, eligibility or an all-in price. This is a targeted review; it is not manual certification of every line in the archived editions.

Practical next steps

  • Collect the documents matching the actual origin and ownership of the money; ask the mainland bank to classify the transaction and specify its requirements.
  • Ask eligible providers for comparable, timestamped end-to-end quotes, identifying the amount sent and the amount received and any extra charges.
  • Have qualified China and US tax advisers review the income, any exchange gain and foreign-account reporting before moving the funds.
