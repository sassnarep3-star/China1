## Headline findings

1. **The conversion is not a tax event.** China taxes the source event (wages 3–45%,
   asset gains 20%, FIE dividends 20%). The U.S. taxes worldwide income. A wire fee is
   not a tax and a tax is not a wire fee.
2. **There is no published USD 50,000 annual purchase cap for an overseas individual.**
   The 50,000 figure is an annual facilitation quota for settlement and for *domestic*
   individuals' purchases. SAFE policy answers state that an overseas individual's
   purchase has no annual cap, but requires identity, source-of-funds and tax evidence.
3. **Published sender-side cost is about 0.23%–0.53% of principal.** On the 10 Nov 2026
   BOC board: one-way FX margin 0.2104%, commission 0.1% (floor CNY 50, cap CNY 260),
   cable CNY 150 outside Greater China.
4. **The source event dominates.** Property gains 20% IIT (or 1% deemed), FIE dividends
   20% from 1 Sep 2026, U.S. tax on the gain with a limited foreign tax credit.
5. **Illegal routes are the only 30%–100% penalty.** Evasion, illegal FX purchase and
   private FX trading carry fines up to 30% of the amount, rising to 30%–100% when
   serious, plus confiscation.
6. **Reporting survives the transfer.** FBAR > USD 10,000 aggregate; Form 8938 above
   the applicable threshold; Form 3520 above USD 100,000 of foreign gifts or bequests.

## Reproduce

```bash
python3 edition-i/build/audit.py    # crawl all committed HTML
python3 edition-i/build/build.py    # regenerate site/ + CSV
python3 edition-i/build/verify.py   # independent checks, exit 0 = pass
```

`verify.py` recomputes every cost row from the published board and tariff, re-parses
the rendered HTML, checks that every route and every row carries a source link, that
unpublished components stay blank, that prohibited routes have no steps, that all
internal links and fragments resolve, and that a rebuild is byte-identical.
