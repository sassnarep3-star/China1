## A. The conversion is not the taxable event

- PRC IIT Law Art. 2 lists nine taxable income categories; exchanging one's own principal is not among them ([CN-IIT-LAW](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/eng/c102962/c102967/c102997/c103004/c5245849/content.html)).
- 26 USC §988(e) excludes a personal transaction from FX-gain recognition unless the gain exceeds USD 200 ([US-988](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm)).
- The IRS requires all amounts to be reported in USD at the prevailing rate ([US-FXCURRENCY](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-currency-and-currency-exchange-rates)).
- Therefore the "considerable tax" is normally tax on the source income, a documentation condition, or a bank price. It is not a tax on converting principal.

## B. China-side legal structure

- PBOC Decree [2006] No.3 Art. 3: current-account individual FX follows convertibility; capital-account individual FX follows the convertibility process ([CN-MEAS-2006](http://m.safe.gov.cn/safe/2006/1225/5319.html)).
- State Council Order 532 Art. 5 permits current-account FX payment/transfer with a real, lawful basis; Arts. 39/40/45 set ≤30% and 30%–100% fine bands for 逃汇, 骗购外汇 and illegal private FX trading ([CN-REG-532](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2008-08/06/content_2602514.htm)).
- SAFE Huifa [2020] No.14 Art. 54 sets a USD 50,000 annual convenience quota for 境内个人; Art. 56 allows documented non-quota current-account purchase; Art. 59 covers 境外个人 lawful China-source RMB income with tax vouchers; Art. 64 sets a USD 50,000/day remittance threshold; Art. 65 covers 境外个人 outward remittance of account FX with ID ([CN-GUIDE-2020](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm)).
- 2026 SAFE Tianjin Q&As confirm quota election, daily remittance threshold and Art. 59 tax-voucher requirement ([CN-FAQ-QUOTA-2026](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0706/3085.html); [CN-FAQ-REMIT-2026](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0422/3039.html); [CN-FAQ-SALARY-2026](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html)).
- PBOC Announcement [2004] No.16 defines 移民转移 / 继承转移 and requires hukou cancellation, settlement proof, property title and tax certificate ([CN-PROP-2004](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2005/content_64294.htm)). SAFE Huifa [2014] No.2 §VI(2) cancels the staged release and the >CNY 500,000 head-office filing ([CN-CAP-2014](http://m.safe.gov.cn/safe/2014/0124/5476.html)); the English translation is reference only ([CN-CAP-2014-EN](https://www.safe.gov.cn/en/2014/0801/741.html)).

## C. China tax

- IIT Law Art. 3 sets a 20% flat rate for interest, dividends, asset transfers, leasing and incidental income ([CN-IIT-LAW](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/eng/c102962/c102967/c102997/c103004/c5245849/content.html)).
- Savings-deposit interest is temporarily exempt from PRC IIT from 2008-10-09 ([CN-INTEREST-2008](https://shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn/tax/zcfw/zcfgk/grsds/200810/t288953.html)).
- MOF/STA 2026 No.27 imposes 20% IIT on foreign-individual FIE dividends from 2026-09-01 and repeals the 1994 exemption ([CN-DIV-2026](https://shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn/zzzb/zcwj/202609/t481448.html)).
- STA/SAFE 2013 No.40 requires tax filing for a single external service-trade payment >USD 50,000, but exempts private 境内个人 study/travel/family visits; 2021 No.19 repealed specified articles and allows one-time electronic filing for repeated payments on one contract ([CN-TAXFILING-2013](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100012/c5194375/content.html); [CN-TAXFILING-2021](https://tianjin.chinatax.gov.cn/11200000000/0300/030004/03000418/20210705111145797.shtml)).

## E. US tax and reporting

- US citizens report worldwide taxable income ([US-WORLDWIDE](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/us-citizens-and-resident-aliens-abroad)); all amounts in USD at the prevailing rate ([US-FXCURRENCY](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-currency-and-currency-exchange-rates)).
- FBAR is required when aggregate foreign accounts exceed USD 10,000 at any time in the calendar year; due April 15, automatic October 15; file with FinCEN, not with the return ([US-FBAR](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/report-of-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts-fbar)).
- Form 8938 thresholds and failure penalty: unmarried US resident >USD 50,000 last day / >USD 75,000 any time; MFJ >USD 100,000 / >USD 150,000; failure up to USD 10,000 plus USD 10,000 per 30 days after notice, max USD 60,000 ([US-8938-FBAR](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/comparison-of-form-8938-and-fbar-requirements)).
- 31 USC §5321: non-willful FBAR statutory USD 10,000 with reasonable-cause exception; willful greater of USD 100,000 or 50% of the account balance at violation ([US-5321](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title31/html/USCODE-2024-title31-subtitleIV-chap53-subchapII-sec5321.htm)).
- 31 CFR §1010.821 inflation-adjusted table for penalties assessed on/after 2025-01-17: non-willful USD 16,536, willful floor USD 165,353 ([US-1010-821](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1010/subpart-H/section-1010.821)).
- Form 3520 Part IV applies to >USD 100,000 gifts/bequests from a nonresident alien or foreign estate; 2026 foreign-company/partnership threshold USD 20,573 ([US-3520-GIFTS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/gifts-from-foreign-person); [US-WORLDWIDE](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/us-citizens-and-resident-aliens-abroad)).
- Streamlined filing compliance procedures require a non-willful certification ([US-STREAMLINED](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/streamlined-filing-compliance-procedures)); Form 1116 may credit qualifying foreign income tax on the same income ([US-FTC](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-tax-credit)).
- IR-2026-48 and the Working Families Tax Cuts page confirm the 1% excise tax from 2026-01-01 applies only to US-origin remittances funded with cash, money orders, cashier's checks or similar physical instruments; China-origin bank wires are outside it ([US-REMIT-TAX](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/treasury-irs-issue-proposed-regulations-on-the-new-remittance-transfer-tax-established-under-the-one-big-beautiful-bill); [US-OBBBA](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/working-families-tax-cuts)).
- CBP states there is no US limit on currency, but USD 10,000 or more requires FinCEN 105; nondeclaration risks seizure, Global Entry revocation and criminal prosecution ([US-CBP-CASH](https://www.cbp.gov/newsroom/local-media-release/dulles-cbp-officers-seize-over-111k-unreported-currency)).
