## 1. The tax premise is wrong
The PRC Individual Income Tax Law taxes nine categories of **income**; exchanging one's own principal is not one of them ([CN-IIT-LAW](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/eng/c102962/c102967/c102997/c103004/c5245849/content.html)). US §988(e) does not recognise a personal-transaction FX gain unless it exceeds USD 200 ([US-988](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm)). What is commonly described as a conversion tax is actually tax on the underlying income, a **tax-voucher requirement** at the bank, or the FX spread plus wire tariff.
- PRC tax on the source remains real: 20% flat on asset transfers, interest, dividends and incidental income ([CN-IIT-LAW](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/eng/c102962/c102967/c102997/c103004/c5245849/content.html)); savings-deposit interest temporarily exempt since 2008-10-09 ([CN-INTEREST-2008](https://shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn/tax/zcfw/zcfgk/grsds/200810/t288953.html)); foreign-individual FIE dividends taxed 20% from 2026-09-01 ([CN-DIV-2026](https://shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn/zzzb/zcwj/202609/t481448.html)).
- Art. 59 requires source-of-funds materials **including tax vouchers** for an overseas individual buying FX against lawful China-source RMB income ([CN-GUIDE-2020](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm); [CN-FAQ-SALARY-2026](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html)).
- The BOC posted board is for reference only and the bank's actual channel rate governs ([BOC-FX-BOARD](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/)). The published counter tariff is a price, not a tax ([BOC-TARIFF-PDF](https://pic.bankofchina.com/bocappd/pricelist/202511/P020251110646838136627.pdf)).

## 2. Classification decides the route
PBOC Decree [2006] No.3 distinguishes 境内个人 / 境外个人 and applies convertibility to current-account items but a staged convertibility process to capital-account items ([CN-MEAS-2006](http://m.safe.gov.cn/safe/2006/1225/5319.html)). USD 50,000/year is an annual **purchase convenience quota** for 境内个人, not an absolute ceiling: documented non-quota current-account purchase is allowed under Art. 56 ([CN-GUIDE-2020](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm); [CN-FAQ-QUOTA-2026](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0706/3085.html)). USD 50,000/**day** is a separate remittance-document threshold for FX already in the account ([CN-FAQ-REMIT-2026](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0422/3039.html)). A US passport holder with a live PRC ID can still be misclassified by the bank; Nationality Law Art. 9 is relevant ([CN-NATIONALITY](https://www.nia.gov.cn/Enquiry/publish/showQuestion.jsp?MZ=9PQAMhZWr6Zk9fibcZHJMA%3D%3D)).

## 4. Penalty ladder and US reporting
- PRC 逃汇 / illegal FX trading: fine ≤30%, serious 30%–100% of the amount ([CN-REG-532](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2008-08/06/content_2602514.htm)).
- Willful FBAR: greater of USD 165,353 adjusted floor or 50% of the **account balance** at the violation ([US-5321](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title31/html/USCODE-2024-title31-subtitleIV-chap53-subchapII-sec5321.htm); [US-1010-821](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1010/subpart-H/section-1010.821)).
- Non-willful FBAR adjusted maximum USD 16,536; Form 8938 failure up to USD 10,000 plus USD 10,000/30 days after notice, max USD 60,000 ([US-1010-821](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1010/subpart-H/section-1010.821); [US-8938-FBAR](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/comparison-of-form-8938-and-fbar-requirements)).
- FBAR threshold: aggregate foreign accounts > USD 10,000 at any time in the year; due April 15, automatic extension to October 15 ([US-FBAR](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/report-of-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts-fbar)). Form 8938 thresholds are higher ([US-8938-FBAR](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/comparison-of-form-8938-and-fbar-requirements)).
- US citizens report worldwide income in USD; Form 3520 Part IV applies to a >USD 100,000 foreign gift/bequest from a nonresident alien or foreign estate ([US-WORLDWIDE](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/us-citizens-and-resident-aliens-abroad); [US-3520-GIFTS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/gifts-from-foreign-person)).
