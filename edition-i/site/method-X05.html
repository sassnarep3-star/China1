Prohibited prohibited

Buying overseas property or investment insurance with purchased FX

Overseas property and securities investment are capital-account uses that are not open to individual purchase.

购汇用于境外买房或投资性保险

Who this can apply to. Not lawful without approval.

Step by step

No execution steps are provided for this entry.

Documents and limits

Documents

  • No documents identified

Limits

  • Prohibited without approval

Pros

None identified.

Cons

  • Capital-account FX payments require approval (S27 Article 22; S01 Articles 16-17, 20).
  • Overseas individuals are separately barred from most domestic financial products (S01 Article 23) — the mirror logic applies outward.
  • Mis-describing the purpose is a false-declaration risk.
