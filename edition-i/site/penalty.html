The conversion penalty, broken down

"Penalty" usually bundles five unrelated things: the bank's FX margin, its commission, the cable charge, the tax on the underlying event, and reporting penalties. Separating them changes the answer.

The only penalty that scales with the amount is the illegal one. Lawful sender-side fees top out at roughly 0.53% at USD 10,000 and fall toward 0.23% at USD 400,000. A fine for evasion is 30%-100% of the amount.

The formula you can apply yourself

Sender-side cost in USD = P × (S − M) / M + min(max(0.001 × P × S, 50), 260) / S + 150 / S

P = USD principal, S = bank's CNY-per-USD selling rate, M = midpoint of the bank's buy and sell. The first term is the FX margin, the second the commission, the third the cable charge. Add the receiving bank's fee and any tax on the source event separately.

What is still unknown

  • Correspondent deduction: depends on routing; no official schedule.
  • Receiving bank fee: depends on your U.S. bank and account tier.
  • Your execution rate: the board is a reference; the trade rate governs.
  • Your tax: depends on basis, residency, source and compliance history.
