Why the tax story is usually wrong

The PRC IIT Law taxes nine categories of income. Exchanging your own principal is not on the list. What the bank is asking for under Art. 59 is a tax voucher proving the income was taxed. On the US side, §988(e) excludes a personal FX gain below USD 200, and the 1% remittance tax created in 2025 applies only to remittances sent from the US using physical instruments — a China→US wire is outside it.

Statutory exposure by amount

These are ceilings computed by arithmetic from statutory percentages, not predicted penalties. The Chinese bands apply to the amount illegally moved; the willful FBAR band applies to the balance in the account. That asymmetry is why US reporting is resolved first.
