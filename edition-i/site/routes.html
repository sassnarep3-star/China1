Routes out of CNY, screened

Every route is classified by legal basis, not by how easy it is to find online. "Prohibited" means there is no lawful execution, so this page gives risk information only.

Read the status, not the headline. A route marked capped is lawful but cannot move USD 400,000 in a reasonable time. A route marked unverified is not recommended — it means no official source or published price was confirmed.

Lawful candidate P01

Bank purchase and wire of documented China current-account income (overseas individual)

境外个人经常项目合法人民币收入购汇汇出

If the RMB is lawful current-account income earned in China and you are an overseas individual, the bank may buy USD with it and remit abroad on documentary proof.

Cost model: boc_fx_wire · Limits: No published annual purchase cap for 境外个人; Documentary review per transaction; Single remittance identity checks above CNY 5,000 / USD 1,000 (S11) · S01 ↗S03 ↗S05 ↗S27 ↗

Conditional P02

Employer payroll remittance of salary (overseas individual)

境外个人工资薪金由单位代发汇出

Some employers remit net salary abroad through a payroll partner; the bank still performs the individual purchase review.

Cost model: boc_fx_wire · Limits: Only current salary flows; Employer-dependent · S03 ↗S09 ↗S11 ↗

Lawful but capped P03

Convert back unused RMB within 24 months of the original exchange

原兑换未用完人民币兑回

RMB left over from a documented foreign-currency exchange can be converted back with the original slip for 24 months.

Cost model: boc_fx_wire · Limits: 24 months from exchange; USD 500/day without receipt; USD 1,000/day at departure points without receipt · S01 ↗S02 ↗S08 ↗S09 ↗

Lawful but capped P04

Return unused inbound foreign currency by the original route

境外个人未使用境外汇入外汇原路汇回

Foreign currency wired in and not used can be sent back the same way with ID.

Cost model: boc_wire_only · Limits: Unused inbound amount only · S01 ↗S09 ↗

Lawful but capped P05

Domestic-individual annual facilitation quota (USD 50,000)

境内个人年度便利化购汇额度5万美元

If you are classified as a domestic individual, USD 50,000 per calendar year can be purchased with ID alone.

Cost model: boc_fx_wire · Limits: USD 50,000 per person per calendar year; Quota does not roll over · S02 ↗S04 ↗S05 ↗S09 ↗

Conditional P06

Domestic-individual purchase above quota with transaction documents

境内个人超额度经常项目购汇

Above the quota, a domestic individual can purchase FX for a genuine current-account purpose with supporting documents.

Cost model: boc_fx_wire · Limits: Purpose-bound; No published cap · S02 ↗S27 ↗S09 ↗

Lawful but capped P07

Permanent-resident ID holder using the facilitation quota

持外国人永久居留身份证适用购汇额度

An overseas individual holding a foreign permanent-resident ID may use the USD 50,000 purchase quota.

Cost model: boc_fx_wire · Limits: USD 50,000 per person per calendar year · S04 ↗S02 ↗S09 ↗

外汇储蓄账户内外汇直接汇出

Cost model: boc_wire_only · Limits: Only the FX amount held · S02 ↗S09 ↗
