M14 · Overseas ATM cash withdrawal from a mainland card

Withdraw cash abroad within CNY 100,000 per person per year and CNY 10,000 per card per day, paying BOC's published 12 CNY + 1% (max 112 CNY) per withdrawal.

lawful (tight limits)   Category: Cards, cash and spending

Eligibility

Mainland-issued debit card; the annual and daily SAFE limits apply across all cards of the person.

#StepOfficial sources
1Note the annual CNY 100,000 aggregate limit and CNY 10,000 per-card daily limit.CN-ATM-2017
2Withdraw at an overseas ATM; BOC charges 12 CNY + 1% of the withdrawal, capped at 112 CNY per withdrawal (tariff effective 2025-11-11).BOC-TARIFF-PDF
