M11 · Wise CNY→USD (licensed partner, tax-record based)

Wise's own pages allow non-mainland-Chinese individuals who work/worked in China and have China tax records to send CNY from their own account to their own overseas account. Single bank-app transfer cap CNY 300,000.

provider-conditional   Category: Licensed provider routes

Eligibility

Not a mainland Chinese national; current/former employment in China; valid Chinese tax record showing tax paid within the last three years; source account in the sender's own name; recipient account also in the sender's own name.

Step by step

Exactly how it works · numbered steps
#StepOfficial sources
1Register, then complete the one-time verification (1-2 working days) with passport and Chinese tax records from the last three years.WISE-GUIDE
2Start a CNY→USD transfer to your own US account; the platform computes an overall limit from China salary and tax paid.WISE-GUIDE
3Pay by a domestic CNY bank transfer from your own account to the unique safeguarding account, or by Alipay; the CNY must come from your own account.WISE-GUIDE
4Wise converts at its displayed mid-market rate and pays out by local ACH/wire to the US; read the exact fee shown for your amount before confirming.WISE-Q-66919 WISE-Q-267716
5Keep the tax records; re-submit updated records when the overall limit is reached.WISE-GUIDE
