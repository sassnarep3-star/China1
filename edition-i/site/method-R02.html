其他中资银行个人跨境汇款费率

Who this can apply to. Account holders at those banks.

Step by step

  1. Ask the branch for its current published personal cross-border remittance schedule in writing.
  2. Compare total cost for the same USD outcome at the same time, including FX rate and all fees.
  3. Do not rely on this site for another bank's price; it was not verified.
