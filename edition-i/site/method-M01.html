M01 · 境内个人 annual USD 50,000 convenience quota

Buy FX at the bank with ID only, within USD 50,000 per person per year, then wire it out. The quota applies only to 境内个人 (PRC ID holders), so a US citizen is normally outside this route.

lawful (classification-dependent)   Category: Current-account routes

Eligibility

Bank must classify the holder as 境内个人; quota is per person per calendar year; purchase purpose and payment purpose must match.

Pros and cons

Pros and cons
AspectDetailOfficial sources
Pros
  • Only an ID document is needed inside the quota
  • No SAFE approval
  • Published bank tariff and posted board rate allow a documented cost estimate

Cons
  • USD 50,000 per year — USD 400,000 would need eight annual tranches under this model
  • A US citizen with a live PRC ID/hukou has a classification problem (see F02)
  • Splitting across other people's quotas is prohibited

Exactly how it works · numbered steps
#StepOfficial sources
1Confirm in writing how the bank classifies the account (境内个人 / 境外个人) and which ID is on file.CN-MEAS-2006 CN-NATIONALITY
2If 境内个人, present the valid ID and file the 个人购汇申请书 truthfully for a current-account purpose.CN-GUIDE-2020 CN-MEAS-2006
3Buy USD within the annual USD 50,000 convenience tier; keep the purchase purpose and payment purpose consistent.CN-GUIDE-2020 CN-FAQ-QUOTA-2026
4Wire the USD out; a single-day cumulative remittance above USD 50,000 needs transaction documents.CN-FAQ-REMIT-2026 CN-GUIDE-2020
5Pay the published BOC counter tariff (0.1%, min CNY 50, max CNY 260) plus CNY 150 international cable charge; overseas bank charges are passed through at actual cost.BOC-TARIFF-PDF
6Report the foreign account for the whole year it exists, including the year it is emptied.US-FBAR US-WORLDWIDE
