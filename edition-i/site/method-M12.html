M12 · HSBC China outward telegraphic transfer (published tariff)

The same lawful purchase/remittance steps through HSBC China, whose 2026 tariff publishes a different fee schedule: free for individual Private Banking customers; 0.1% (min CNY 100, max CNY 500) + CNY 120 cable for Premier/Advance.

provider-conditional   Category: Licensed provider routes

Eligibility

An HSBC China account at the relevant segment and a lawful documented purpose for the FX purchase.
