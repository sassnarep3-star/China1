M06 · Current-account service payment with tax filing (e.g. tuition, medical, family support)

Pay a genuine overseas current-account expense directly. A single external payment above USD 50,000 generally needs a tax filing (a reporting step), while private study/travel/family-visit FX is exempt from that filing.

lawful   Category: Current-account routes

Eligibility

A real payee and a real invoiced service; not a disguised transfer of capital to oneself.

Pros and cons

Pros and cons

Pros
  • Payment goes directly to the real expense, so no separate 'move savings' analysis
  • Tax filing is electronic and free; the 2021 supplement removed repeat filings for one contract
