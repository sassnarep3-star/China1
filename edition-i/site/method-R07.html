Lawful but capped card

Carry foreign-currency cash out

Cash carriage is capped at USD 5,000 without a permit and USD 10,000 with a bank carriage certificate.

携带外币现钞出境

Who this can apply to. Traveller departing China.

Step by step

  1. Purchase the foreign currency lawfully first; cash uses the bank's cash rate.
  2. Up to USD 5,000 equivalent normally needs no carriage permit (S14).
  3. From USD 5,000 to USD 10,000, obtain a 携带外汇出境许可证 from the designated bank (S14).
  4. Above USD 10,000 the permit must come from SAFE, and carrying more than USD 10,000 is exceptional (S14).
  5. Do not split trips or use other travellers to evade the limit (S01 Article 7; S27 Article 42).

Documents and limits

Documents

  • Passport
  • Bank carriage certificate
  • Customs declaration

Limits

  • USD 5,000 per trip normally without a permit
  • USD 5,000-10,000 needs a bank-issued carriage permit
  • Above USD 10,000 needs a SAFE-issued permit and is exceptional
  • Permit valid 30 days, single use

Pros

  • Simple for small amounts
  • No wire fee

Cons

  • Cannot move USD 400,000 lawfully: 80 trips at the USD 5,000 threshold
  • Physical and theft risk
  • Excess carriage is punishable (S27 Article 42)
