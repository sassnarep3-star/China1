How to read a quote you receive

  1. Ask for the USD amount that will actually arrive, not the rate alone.
  2. Ask which party pays the correspondent and receiving fees (OUR / SHA / BEN).
  3. Compare the bank's rate with the PBOC fixing and the BOC board at the same minute; the gap is not all fee.
  4. Add the receiving bank's published incoming-wire fee.
  5. Only then compare with a provider quote for the same delivery date.
