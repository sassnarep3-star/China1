F09 — 2004 staged-remittance schedule vs 2014 reform — conflict

The 2004 Interim Measures (S04 Art. 6) required emigration transfers to go out in stages (first ≤50% of the approved total; after 1 year ≤50% of the remainder; after 2 years the rest). SAFE Huifa [2014] No. 2 Section VI(2) (S03) removed the mandatory staged-remittance requirement (approved amount may be remitted once or in parts). Current local practice may still vary; confirm with the competent SAFE office before relying on one-time remittance of an entire approved amount.

Status: published · non-blocking

F10 — Implementing Rules Art. 39 fine clause is repealed

The endnote on S02 states Art. 39 (RMB 30,000/1,000 default fines) was repealed by SAFE Huifa [2023] No. 8. Earlier editions citing Art. 39 fines as current are wrong on this point; penalty framework now falls back to the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations and other rules (not re-fetched in this edition).
