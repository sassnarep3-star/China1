Step by step — exactly how each lawful route works

Steps are drawn from the official rule text and guidance pages cited beside each procedure. Prohibited schemes get no steps.

M01 — Documented current-account income wire (foreign individual)

  1. Confirm with the bank that the account holder is classified as 境外个人 (foreign individual) under S01 Art. 37, and that the balance has a current-account income trail.
  2. Assemble documents: passport, employment/service contract, income statements, Chinese individual-income-tax payment vouchers for the amounts involved.
  3. At the bank (counter or eligible e-banking channel), submit a FX purchase (购汇) application for the CNY amount with the supporting documents; bank records it through the SAFE personal FX system (S02 Art. 6, 32–36).
  4. Submit the outward remittance (汇出) application to the destination account you own; pay the published handling fee and cable fee (S06).
  5. Keep the exchange slip (兑换水单) and remittance receipt; they support §988 basis records and re-conversion rights (S02 Art. 13(2)).
  6. Reconcile U.S. reporting for the year: worldwide income (S13), FBAR if foreign accounts exceeded USD 10,000 aggregate (S10), Form 8938 if thresholds met (S11), and §988 gain if the CNY appreciated since receipt (S14).

M02 — Annual USD 50,000 convenience quota (domestic individual)

  1. Verify remaining quota for the calendar year with the bank (personal FX settlement system).
  2. Purchase USD within the remaining annual total with ID; conversion at the bank's published sell rate (S07 board is a reference snapshot only).
  3. Remit to the overseas account you own; ≤ USD 50,000/day cumulative from an FX savings account is handled on ID alone (S02 Art. 14).
  4. Repeat in later calendar years for larger balances; keep every exchange slip and remittance receipt.
  5. File U.S. reporting as in M01 step 6.

M03 — Above-quota documented current-account purchase and remittance (domestic individual)

  1. Classify the balance by true income type per S02 Art. 10 and collect the matching evidence (contracts, income proof, tax payment records).
  2. Submit above-quota FX purchase at the bank with ID + evidence; bank records the transaction in the personal FX system.
  3. Remit abroad with authenticity documents; cash remittance over USD 10,000/day needs extra slips per S02 Art. 14 (bank-account remittance ≤ USD 50,000/day by ID, above with proof).
  4. Keep all slips; complete U.S. reporting for the year (S10, S11, S13, S14).

M04 — Mainland property sale proceeds repatriation

  1. Complete the sale: property-ownership review, contract signing, ownership transfer registration (S05 steps 1–2).
  2. Settle the property-sale tax at the local tax authority and obtain the tax-payment proof (S05 step 3).
  3. Obtain the Tax Filing Form for External Payments for Trade in Services and Other Accounts from the tax authority if the amount exceeds USD 50,000 (S05 step 4).
  4. At the bank, purchase FX with the proceeds and remit, presenting passport, contract, registration certificate, tax voucher and (if applicable) the filing form and power of attorney (S05 step 5).
  5. Retain all receipts; report U.S. side: FBAR/8938 for the account, and any U.S. tax effects of the sale (S10, S11). Property-sale gain taxation on the U.S. side depends on facts and is blank (flag F01/F06).

M05 — Emigration property transfer (移民财产转移)

  1. Determine eligibility under S04: the property must pre-date emigrant status and be lawful, undisputed, unrestricted.
  2. Apply to the SAFE branch at the former household registration place (S03 Section VI(1); S04 Art. 5) with identity, emigration and property documents.
  3. Receive the SAFE approval document specifying the approved amount.
  4. At the bank, purchase FX and remit within the approved amount, once or in several remittances (S03 Section VI(2)).
  5. Retain approval, slips and receipts; complete U.S. reporting (S10, S11).

M06 — Inheritance property transfer (继承财产转移)

  1. Obtain inheritance legal documents (court judgment, notarized succession certificate, etc. per S02 Art. 10(3)).
  2. Apply to the SAFE branch at the decedent's last household registration place (S03 Section VI(1); S04 Art. 5).
  3. After approval, sell/liquidate assets if needed, settle any Chinese taxes, purchase FX and remit once or in parts (S03 Section VI(2)-(3)).
  4. Keep the full paper trail; U.S. reporting may include FBAR/8938 and possibly Form 3520 for foreign gifts/bequests — Form 3520 requirement cited in prior editions is IRS-published but not re-fetched in this edition (flag F16).

M07 — Personal foreign-trade settlement account (个人对外贸易经营者)

  1. Register the business and file for foreign-trade operator status (S02 Art. 9(1)).
  2. Open the personal FX settlement account at a designated bank (S02 Art. 25).
  3. Receive export proceeds into the settlement account with trade documents; settle (结汇) per institutional rules without the annual total.
  4. Purchase FX for genuine import payments or remit per trade terms; keep customs declarations and contracts.
  5. Complete international-payments declarations (S01 Art. 8) and U.S. reporting for the accounts and income.

M08 — Family support, gifts and other documented inbound transfers

  1. Have the donor send the transfer with a clear purpose and retain donor income evidence.
  2. Settle at the bank within the annual total on ID; above it, present notarized gift/kinship documents (S02 Art. 10).
  3. Purchase FX and remit per M02/M03 rules as applicable.
  4. Keep records on both sides; U.S. persons receiving foreign gifts may have Form 3520 duties (flag F16).

M09 — Wise from-CNY transfer (licensed-provider route)

  1. Check eligibility: non-mainland-Chinese sender with China work/tax history (S17).
  2. Complete Wise verification once (1–2 working days) and upload qualifying tax records to set the overall limit.
  3. Create the transfer to your own overseas account; pay from the China bank app (≤ CNY 300,000) or Alipay (≤ CNY 50,000).
  4. Compare Wise's live quote against the bank quote for the same net USD before sending; Wise's fee is only knowable at quote time (F07).
  5. Keep records; complete U.S. reporting (S10, S11).

M10 — Bank-card overseas spending with post-return FX repayment

  1. Use the mainland-issued card for genuine overseas purchases or ATM withdrawals.
  2. Repay the card in CNY after returning; the FX-purchase repayment stays outside the annual total (S08).
  3. Keep statements; there is no transfer receipt since no wire occurs.
  4. For large future conversions, this is a consumption channel, not a repatriation channel.

M11 — Physical cash carrying with declaration and permits

  1. Convert CNY to USD cash at the bank within applicable purchase rules (M02/M03 for the purchase leg).
  2. For USD 5,000–10,000, obtain the bank-issued FX Carrying Permit before departure (S19/S20).
  3. Declare cash at customs per the thresholds; keep the declaration form.
  4. Deposit in the destination bank; expect source-of-funds questions for large cash deposits (U.S. banks file currency transaction reports — not fetched here; blank F14).

M12 — Company profit/dividend remittance from a foreign-invested enterprise

  1. Declare dividends via the company with a board/partner resolution; settle corporate-level taxes per Chinese tax rules.
  2. Obtain the tax authority's tax-filing form for external payments.
  3. Have the bank remit the profit (≤ USD 50,000 simplified; above, present resolution + original tax-filing form — S03 Section V).
  4. Bank stamps the tax form with the actual remitted amount and date; keep copies.
  5. U.S. side: dividend income reporting and foreign tax credit (Form 1116, S15); FBAR/8938 for accounts (S10, S11).

M13 — Insurance payout settlement (foreign-exchange insurance proceeds)

  1. Receive the payout into the FX savings account with policy and payment proof (S02 Art. 19).
  2. Settle or remit per quota/above-quota rules (M02/M03).
  3. Keep policy, payout proof and slips; complete U.S. reporting.

M14 — Unused-RMB re-conversion on original exchange slip (foreign individual)

  1. Locate the original exchange slip(s) within the 24-month validity (S02 Art. 13(2)).
  2. At the bank, convert the unused RMB back to USD against the slip.
  3. Remit the FX abroad (S02 Art. 15 for foreign individuals: by ID from FX savings account).
  4. Keep the new slip; report as usual.

M15 — Qualified domestic institutional overseas investment (QDII-type)

  1. Choose a qualified domestic institution product (S02 Art. 17).
  2. Subscribe with RMB or FX; institution performs the conversion and outbound investment formalities.
  3. Hold/ redeem per product terms; proceeds follow the product's return rules.
  4. Report foreign accounts/securities on FBAR/8938 as applicable (S10, S11).
