Conditional capital

Liquidation proceeds of a foreign-invested enterprise

After lawful liquidation and tax, the foreign investor's RMB share may be purchased and remitted.

外商投资企业清算所得汇出

Who this can apply to. Foreign investor in a liquidated FIE.

Step by step

  1. Complete liquidation, tax clearance and deregistration.
  2. Present the liquidation and tax documents to the bank for purchase and remittance.
  3. Retain the distribution computation for U.S. reporting.

Documents and limits

Documents

  • Liquidation report
  • Tax clearance
  • Deregistration

Limits

  • Liquidation distribution only

Pros

  • Explicit provision in S27 Article 22
  • Handles the final exit

Cons

  • Slow and expensive process
  • Tax clearance first
  • Only for genuine equity
