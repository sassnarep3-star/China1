M10 · FIE dividend / profit repatriation (if the CNY came from dividends)

If a foreign individual's balance came from dividends of a foreign-invested enterprise, a 2026 rule imposes 20% IIT and requires withholding; repatriation follows the documented-profit route.

lawful (business-source)   Category: Capital-account routes

Eligibility

A foreign individual shareholder with FIE dividend income and tax records; the company must have withheld or the individual must self-remit.
