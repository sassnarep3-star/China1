What a mechanical audit cannot do

It cannot prove a legal proposition, confirm that an external source still says what it said when cited, or detect a subtly wrong interpretation. It proves that the site is internally consistent and navigable. Those are different claims and this page keeps them separate.

Research, not legal, tax or investment advice. Unknown values are blank, never inferred. Official agencies establish the rules; bank and provider pages establish only their own published terms. A citation does not establish your eligibility for a route.
