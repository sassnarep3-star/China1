F04Two official definitions of domestic individual conflict
The FX Measures (S01 Article 37) define 境内个人 by Chinese identity documents. The FX Regulations (S27 Article 52) define 境内个人 to include foreigners resident in China for one continuous year. A U.S. citizen living in China can therefore be classified differently depending on which instrument the bank applies. The bank's classification determines whether the USD 50,000 quota applies. Treat as a bank-confirmation item, not an assumption.		highclassificationOpenS01 ↗S27 ↗S02 ↗S04 ↗

Cross-edition conflicts, precisely

  • F02: Edition B's 50/25/25 staged release is the 2004 rule, cancelled by 汇发[2014]2号 Section VI.
  • F03: Edition D's exact all-in totals include assumed receiving and intermediary fees that are not published.
  • F04: Two official definitions of "domestic individual" produce different quota outcomes for the same person.
  • F17: Using the PBOC fixing as if it were a bank rate overstates conversion cost by about 0.64 points.

How these will be closed

Each flag names the source that would resolve it. F02 and F03 sit inside other sessions' file scopes, so this edition reports them rather than editing them. F04 and F05 require your bank's written classification and cannot be resolved from public sources.
