M05 · 境外个人 repatriation of unused inward FX / reconversion within 24 months

Art. 14 permits an 境外个人 to remit unused inward FX back out; Art. 60 permits reconverting unused RMB from an original conversion with the original exchange memo within 24 months.

lawful (time-limited)   Category: Current-account routes

Eligibility

Original inward remittance or original conversion memo; the 24-month reconversion validity applies to the memo route.
