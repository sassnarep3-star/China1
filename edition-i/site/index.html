CNY → USD, without the tax myth

Converting your own money is not a taxable event in either jurisdiction; the barrier is China's capital-account inconvertibility and the documentation the bank requires. For a US citizen with documented Chinese earnings, the least-penalty route is the 境外个人 current-account purchase against lawful China-source RMB income, then one direct wire.
