3. Verification standard

  • Government, regulator or statute pages support legal and tax propositions.
  • A provider's own tariff or quote supports only that provider's price or product terms.
  • Every displayed table row carries at least one link to a retrieved official page.
  • Any value not confirmed from an official page is None in the data, renders as a blank cell, and appears on Flagged for Review.
  • Derived arithmetic is allowed only when both inputs and the formula are shown and sourced; it is labelled conditional.
  • Internal checkers prove consistency, arithmetic, citations and link resolution — not external retrievability or the correctness of a personal eligibility determination.

4. Known limits

  • The BOC board is explicitly for reference only; the actual rate is offered at the transaction channel.
  • No personal balance, source of funds, tax residency, US filing history, receiving bank or executable quote was supplied.
  • Capital-account approval is discretionary; no processing time or local document checklist was published in the retrieved instruments.
  • Criminal exposure beyond the statutory referral language is outside scope.
  • This is a research dossier, not legal, tax or investment advice.
