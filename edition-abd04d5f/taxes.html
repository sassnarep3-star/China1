Taxes and penalties

What China and the United States tax, what must be reported, and what breaking the rules costs.

The breakdown of the "conversion penalty". It has three separate parts: (1) the exchange margin and bank fees — about 0.2% to 0.7%, on the Costs page; (2) Chinese income tax on the underlying income, only if it was not already paid; (3) penalties, only if rules are broken. Nothing is charged for the conversion as such.
