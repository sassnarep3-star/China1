Documented China income, bought and wired at a bank Lawful, documented

Who it fits
A foreign passport holder (境外个人) whose RMB is lawful current-account income earned in China, such as salary.
Cap
No yearly cap in the rule text. The amount is what your source documents and tax vouchers show.
Papers
Passport; source-of-funds material showing the amounts (for example employment contract and payslips); China tax vouchers.
For
One wire, lowest sourced cost. No yearly cap in the text. The rule is written for exactly this case.
Against
Works only for income you can document with tax vouchers. The bank may refuse if it doubts the documents.

Proceeds of selling your China home Lawful, documented

Who it fits
A foreign individual who sold a home in China that they lawfully owned.
Cap
Not more than the sale price less the taxes of that sale, and not more than the amount on the tax certificate. Co-owners: your own share.
Papers
Passport; the registered transfer contract and proof of registration; the tax certificate for the sale; a notarised power of attorney if someone acts for you.
For
Handled at the bank against documents. No yearly cap other than the net sale amount.
Against
Tax on the sale must be paid first. An older rule still speaks of SAFE approval, so confirm the procedure with the bank.

Emigrant asset transfer (former Chinese national) Lawful, documented

Who it fits
Someone who moved from mainland China and gained foreign citizenship or permanent residence, for lawful assets owned in China before that.
Cap
The amount SAFE approves. The bank may remit it once or in several payments.
Papers
Written application and the Personal Property Transfer form; foreign ID such as passport; proof of settlement abroad from a Chinese embassy or consulate, or with apostille; certificate that your household registration was cancelled; proof of income source and title; tax certificate.
For
The official route for capital that is not current income. No fee for the approval. One approval can cover the whole amount.
Against
Paper-heavy. Needs hukou cancellation and consular or apostille proof. Applications above CNY 1 million from public officials or their close relatives may be checked with the supervisory authority.

Inherited China estate Lawful, documented

Who it fits
A foreign citizen who lawfully inherited assets in China.
Cap
The amount SAFE approves. The bank may remit it once or in several payments.
Papers
Written application and the Personal Property Transfer form; foreign passport and ID; proof of settlement abroad; proof of inheritance such as a notarised inheritance certificate or will; the deceased's title documents; tax certificate.
For
An official route with no fee for the approval.
Against
Paper-heavy and slow. The official guide's own wording is inconsistent, so ask the branch which conditions it applies.

USD 50,000 yearly quota, on ID alone Lawful, documented

Who it fits
Only a 境内个人 (Chinese citizen with PRC ID) or a foreigner holding a Chinese Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card. A plain US passport holder does not have this purchase quota.
Cap
USD 50,000 of purchases per person per calendar year. Above it, documents of a genuine current-account use are needed.
Papers
Your own valid ID; the purchase application form filled in truthfully.
For
No documents within the quota. Can be done online.
Against
Not open to an ordinary US passport holder. Slow for large sums: one quota per year. Each wire pays its own fees. The stated purpose must be a genuine current-account use.

Change back unused RMB that came from your own foreign currency Lawful, documented

Who it fits
A foreign individual who earlier exchanged foreign currency into RMB and did not use it all.
Cap
The unused part of the original exchange, within 24 months of it. Without the original memo: USD 500 a day, or USD 1,000 on the day of departure past border control.
Papers
Passport and the original exchange memo.
For
Simple, on a memo you already hold.
Against
Covers only money that entered as foreign currency within the last 24 months.

Refund of a home purchase that fell through Lawful, documented

Who it fits
A foreign individual whose purchase money came from abroad and whose purchase was not completed.
Cap
The purchase money returned, plus reasonable interest earned while it was held.
Papers
Passport; the original payment and exchange vouchers; proof that the purchase failed; a written statement if you withdrew.
For
Returns by the original path, with interest allowed.
Against
A narrow case. The money must go back where it came from.

Wise, sending from CNY Lawful, small or narrow

Who it fits
Not a mainland Chinese national; you work or have worked in China; you hold a China tax record from the last 3 years.
Cap
CNY 300,000 per transfer from a China bank app (CNY 50,000 from Alipay). Overall limit: your China salary after tax.
Papers
China tax records; identity verification on first use (1–2 working days).
For
Done from a phone. Runs through a payment provider licensed in China.
Against
Salary only, capped at what the tax records show. The fee and rate are not published in the guide. Large sums need many transfers.

Who it fits

Cap

The card.
For

Carry US dollar banknotes out yourself Lawful, small or narrow

Who it fits
Anyone who has lawfully bought the dollars first (by M01 or M05) and travels in person.
Cap
Up to USD 5,000 without a permit; above USD 5,000 up to USD 10,000 with a permit issued by the bank; more only in special cases with a SAFE permit. Cash withdrawal: USD 10,000 a day on ID.
Papers
Passport; the bank's carrying permit for USD 5,000–10,000.
For
No wire fee.
Against
At most USD 10,000 a trip in ordinary cases. Travel cost and the risk of loss are not counted. Still needs a lawful purchase first.

Splitting amounts, or borrowing or lending yearly quotas Prohibited

Penalty. Watch list for the year of listing and the two following years; during that time every purchase or settlement needs documents with the amounts. Violations are punished under the Regulations. N01 N18 N09

Underground banks, private money changers, offsetting swaps Prohibited

Penalty. Warning, confiscation of illegal gains, fine up to 30% of the amount; serious cases 30% up to the full amount; criminal liability where a crime is made out (Art. 45). N09

No steps are published for prohibited channels.

False or invalid documents to buy foreign currency Prohibited

Penalty. The currency must be changed back; fine up to 30% of the amount; serious cases 30% up to the full amount; criminal liability where a crime is made out (Art. 40). Untrue documents: up to CNY 50,000 for an individual (Art. 48). N09 N18

No steps are published for prohibited channels.

Converting through virtual currency Prohibited

Penalty. Exchange between legal tender and virtual currency in China is an illegal financial activity; institutions may not provide accounts or transfers; offenders are punished and may face criminal liability; losses are your own. N19

No steps are published for prohibited channels.

Carrying cash above the limits without declaring it Prohibited

Penalty. China: warning and a fine of up to 20% of the amount (Art. 42). United States: unreported currency above USD 10,000 can be seized, with civil or criminal penalties. N09 N01 N24

No steps are published for prohibited channels.
