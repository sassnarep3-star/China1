Costs

What each method costs at every USD 10,000 step up to USD 400,000. Total first, then its parts.

Each figure is arithmetic on inputs read from an official page. A part with no official input is left blank and flagged; it is never guessed. So every total is a floor: add your own bank's incoming-wire fee and anything an intermediary bank deducts F05 F06.

How the number is built

cost = (commission + cable fee) ÷ selling rate + amount × FX margin
commission = 0.1% of the RMB amount, not less than CNY 50, not more than CNY 260
