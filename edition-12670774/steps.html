Step by step

What to bring and what happens at each stage, for every lawful method. Steps follow the papers the official text asks for.

Documented China income, bought and wired at a bank Lawful, documented

Bring: Passport; source-of-funds material showing the amounts (for example employment contract and payslips); China tax vouchers.

  1. Collect the papers the rule names: passport, material showing where the RMB came from with the amounts, and the China tax vouchers for that income.
  2. If tax on the income was never paid, settle it first. The tax office collects unpaid tax when an outward payment is reviewed.
  3. Take the papers to your bank and ask to buy US dollars against lawful China income as a foreign individual. The bank checks that the documents are genuine and match the amount.
  4. The bank converts at its selling rate. Compare the rate offered with the board before you accept.
  5. Ask the bank to wire the dollars from your foreign-currency account to your own US account. A foreign individual remits from an FX account on ID.
  6. For one payment above USD 50,000, ask the bank whether it wants a tax filing form as well as the tax vouchers.
  7. Pay the commission and cable fee, keep the purchase and wire receipts, and keep the tax vouchers.
  8. In the US, keep your FBAR and Form 8938 filings up to date for the China account, and report any income the money earned.

Proceeds of selling your China home Lawful, documented

Bring: Passport; the registered transfer contract and proof of registration; the tax certificate for the sale; a notarised power of attorney if someone acts for you.

  1. Complete the sale and the transfer of title at the property registry. The bank will not remit before title has moved.
  2. Pay the taxes of the sale and obtain the tax certificate. It caps what can be remitted.
  3. Take passport, the registered transfer contract, proof of registration and the tax certificate to the bank.
  4. The bank checks that the amount requested does not exceed the price less taxes and does not exceed the tax certificate.
  5. Buy the dollars and wire them to your own US account. Keep the bank's annotated copy of the tax document.
  6. In the US, report the sale on your return; China tax paid on the gain may be creditable.

Emigrant asset transfer (former Chinese national) Lawful, documented

Bring: Written application and the Personal Property Transfer form; foreign ID such as passport; proof of settlement abroad from a Chinese embassy or consulate, or with apostille; certificate that your household registration was cancelled; proof of income source and title; tax certificate.

  1. Confirm the assets were yours before you gained foreign status and are not frozen by a court or supervisory body.
  2. Turn the assets into RMB and keep the sale and bank records that show where the money came from.
  3. Obtain the tax certificate from the tax office where the income arose or the asset sits.
  4. Get the settlement-abroad proof and the household-registration cancellation certificate.
  5. File the application with the SAFE branch of your former household registration. Filing is in person and free.
  6. SAFE decides within 20 working days and may extend once by 10. It issues a single-use approval valid nationwide.
  7. Take the approval to a bank, buy dollars and wire them, once or in parts, within the approved amount.
  8. In the US, keep FBAR and Form 8938 current and report any income or gain realised when the assets were sold.

Inherited China estate Lawful, documented

Bring: Written application and the Personal Property Transfer form; foreign passport and ID; proof of settlement abroad; proof of inheritance such as a notarised inheritance certificate or will; the deceased's title documents; tax certificate.

  1. Obtain proof of your right to inherit, such as an inheritance or will notarisation.
  2. Collect the deceased's title documents and turn the assets into RMB.
  3. Obtain the tax certificate unless the rules say none is needed.
  4. File with the SAFE branch of the deceased's last household registration. Estates from several people can be filed together at one of those branches.
  5. SAFE decides within 20 working days, extendable once by 10, and issues a single-use approval.
  6. Take the approval to a bank, buy dollars and wire them within the approved amount.
  7. In the US, a bequest from a foreign estate above USD 100,000 in a year is reported on Form 3520.

USD 50,000 yearly quota, on ID alone Lawful, documented

Bring: Your own valid ID; the purchase application form filled in truthfully.

  1. Check that you hold a PRC ID or a Chinese Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card. A passport alone does not give the purchase quota.
  2. Buy up to USD 50,000 in the calendar year at the counter or in the bank's app, stating the true purpose on the purchase form.
  3. Wire the dollars for that purpose. Up to USD 50,000 a day leaves an FX account on ID; more needs documents with the amounts.
  4. Repeat in the next calendar year if needed. Never use another person's quota or lend yours.

Change back unused RMB that came from your own foreign currency Lawful, documented

Bring: Passport and the original exchange memo.

  1. Find the original exchange memo. It is valid for changing back for 24 months from the exchange date.
  2. Take passport and memo to the bank and change the unused RMB back.
  3. Remit the foreign currency from your FX account on ID, or carry it within the cash limits.

Refund of a home purchase that fell through Lawful, documented

Bring: Passport; the original payment and exchange vouchers; proof that the purchase failed; a written statement if you withdrew.

  1. Collect the original payment and exchange vouchers and the proof that the purchase failed.
  2. The bank changes the RMB back and returns it the way it came: to the sending account abroad or your original FX account.
  3. Keep the vouchers for your US records.

Wise, sending from CNY Lawful, small or narrow

Bring: China tax records; identity verification on first use (1–2 working days).

  1. Check you qualify: not a mainland Chinese national, China work history, a tax record from the last 3 years.
  2. Open a transfer to an account in your own name. Transfers to other people are not allowed.
  3. Upload the tax record when asked. Your limit is set from salary after tax and does not expire.
  4. Pay from your China bank app, at most CNY 300,000 per transfer, and read the fee and rate on the confirmation screen before you pay.

Cash from ATMs abroad with your China card Lawful, small or narrow

Bring: The card.

  1. Use your own card only. Lending or borrowing cards to get around the cap is forbidden.
  2. Stay inside CNY 10,000 per card per day and CNY 100,000 per person per year. Going over suspends overseas withdrawals for that year and the next.
  3. Check your card's fee table before you travel; fees are not published in the rule.

Sources: N16 N24 · Flags: F08 F34

Carry US dollar banknotes out yourself Lawful, small or narrow

Bring: Passport; the bank's carrying permit for USD 5,000–10,000.

  1. Buy the dollars lawfully first. Carrying cash is only a way to move dollars you were entitled to buy.
  2. Withdraw banknotes, at most USD 10,000 a day on ID.
  3. For more than USD 5,000 and up to USD 10,000, ask the bank for the carrying permit before you travel.
  4. On arrival in the US, report on FinCEN Form 105 if you, or your family or group together, carry more than USD 10,000.

Leave it in RMB and spend it in China No conversion

Bring: None.

  1. Use the balance for costs in China instead of converting it.
  2. Keep filing the FBAR while your foreign accounts exceed USD 10,000 in aggregate at any time in the year, and Form 8938 above its thresholds.
  3. Report interest the account earns on your US return, translated into dollars.

Sources: N11 N12 N20

Pay with the China card when you are abroad Lawful, small or narrow

Bring: The card.

  1. Pay genuine travel and living costs abroad with the card.
  2. Check the card's foreign-transaction fee and rate first; they are not published in the rule.
