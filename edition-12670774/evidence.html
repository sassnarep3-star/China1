  • 准予行政许可的条件: 1. 申请人取得外国永久居留权、外国公民身份或港澳台地区居民身份。2. 申请汇出资金为申请人在取得外国永久居留权、外国公民身份或港澳台地区居民身份之前在境内拥有的合法财产变现。3. 申请汇出资金不属于司法、监察等部门依法限制对外转移的财产变现。

N12 — IRS: Summary of FATCA reporting for U.S. taxpayers (Form 8938)

URL: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/corporations/summary-of-fatca-reporting-for-us-taxpayers — opened 2026-11-10 (chunk 0).

  • Living in the US: unmarried — "more than $50,000 on the last day of the tax year or more than $75,000 at any time during the tax year"; married filing jointly — "more than $100,000 on the last day … or more than $150,000 at any time"; married filing separately — $50,000 / $75,000.
  • Living abroad: not married filing jointly — "more than $200,000 on the last day of the tax year or more than $300,000 at any time during the year"; married filing jointly — "more than $400,000 on the last day … or more than $600,000 at any time".
  • "…report information about those assets on Form 8938, which must be attached to the taxpayer's annual income tax return." "This FATCA requirement is in addition to the long-standing requirement to report foreign financial accounts on FinCEN Form 114".

N17 — Individual Income Tax Law of the PRC (2018 amendment), STA English text

URL: https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/eng/c102962/c102967/c102997/c103004/c5245849/content.html — opened 2026-11-10, chunks 0–1 of 3 (Arts. 1–12).

  • Art. 1: resident = domiciled in China, or not domiciled but resided in China 183 days or more in a tax year → taxed on income from within China or overseas; non-resident → taxed on income from within China.
  • Art. 3: "(1) For comprehensive income … progressive tax rates ranging from 3 percent to 45 percent … (2) For income from business operation … 5 percent to 35 percent … (3) For income from interest, dividends or bonuses, income from leasing of assets, income from transfer of assets, and incidental income, a flat tax rate of 20 percent shall apply."
  • Art. 6: "(2) For income received from salary and wages by a non-resident individual, the amount of taxable income shall be the balance after deduction of RMB 5,000 yuan from his or her monthly income … (4) For income from leasing of assets … after deduction of RMB 800 yuan from the amount received in a single payment not exceeding RMB 4,000 yuan; or after deduction of 20 percent … (5) For income from transfer of assets, the amount of taxable income shall be the balance after deduction of the original value of the asset and reasonable expenses, from the income gained from such transfer. (6) For interest, dividends, bonuses, and incidental income, the amount of taxable income shall be the full amount received in each payment."
  • Art. 10: a taxpayer must file a return, inter alia, "(3) Where the taxpayer obtains taxable income but the withholding agents has failed to withhold and pay the tax; … (5) Where the taxpayer emigrates to another country and cancels his Chinese household registration".

N18 — 个人外汇管理办法 (PBOC Decree [2006] No. 3), SAFE portal

URL: https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2006/1225/5319.html — opened 2026-11-10, read in full.

  • 第七条: 银行和个人在办理个人外汇业务时 … 不得以分拆等方式逃避限额监管，也不得使用虚假商业单据或者凭证逃避真实性管理。
  • 第九条: 对个人结汇和境内个人购汇实行年度总额管理 …
  • 第十三条: 境外个人在境内取得的经常项目项下合法人民币收入，可以凭本人有效身份证件及相关证明材料在银行办理购汇及汇出。
  • 第十四条: 境外个人未使用的境外汇入外汇，可以凭本人有效身份证件在银行办理原路汇回。
  • 第十五条: 境外个人将原兑换未使用完的人民币兑回外币现钞时，小额兑换凭本人有效身份证件 … 超过规定金额的，可以凭原兑换水单在银行办理。
  • 第二十二条: 境外个人购买境内商品房，应当符合自用原则 … 境外个人出售境内商品房所得人民币，经外汇局核准可以购汇汇出。 [2006 wording; later SAFE operating guidance (N08) has the bank handle this directly — the two texts differ, flagged.]
  • 第二十三条: 除国家另有规定外，境外个人不得购买境内权益类和固定收益类等金融产品。
  • 第二十六条: 境外个人在境内的合法财产对外转移，应当按照个人财产对外转移的有关外汇管理规定办理。
  • 第三十七条: （一）境内个人是指持有中华人民共和国居民身份证、军人身份证件、武装警察身份证件的中国公民。（二）境外个人是指持护照、港澳居民来往内地通行证、台湾居民来往大陆通行证的外国公民（包括无国籍人）以及港澳台同胞。
  • 第三十九条: 对违反本办法规定的，由外汇局依据《中华人民共和国外汇管理条例》及其他相关规定予以处罚；构成犯罪的，依法移送司法机关追究刑事责任。
  • IRREGULARITY between official texts: Regulations Art. 52(2) (N09) defines 境内个人 as "中国公民和在中华人民共和国境内连续居住满1年的外国人", while these Measures Art. 37 define it by ID document (Chinese citizens only). For a US-passport holder the Measures' definition makes them a 境外个人 whatever their length of stay. Flagged.

N24 — U.S. Customs and Border Protection: Money and Other Monetary Instruments

  • "Federal law mandates that when entering or leaving the United States you must report amounts exceeding $10,000 to U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP). This requirement applies whether you are: Traveling for business, Sending money abroad, or Bringing savings back home."
  • "Anyone who brings, sends, or receives currency or monetary instruments totaling more than $10,000 at one time, either into or out of the United States, must report it." "An Individual who fails to file a report or submit false or fraudulent information, risk having their currency or monetary instruments forfeited and seized.   • "monetary instruments" = coins and currency, traveler's checks, bearer negotiable instruments etc. (a bank wire is not in the list).
