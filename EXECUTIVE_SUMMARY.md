# China money — executive summary

**Evidence date:** 2026-11-07 UTC. **Known fact:** US citizen with CNY in a mainland-China bank account. Balance, source of funds, residence/status, asset history, bank, destination and prior filings are **not supplied**. This is a source-backed decision guide, not a personalized tax opinion or a claim that any route is available to this user.

## Bottom line

**Do not start by looking for a universal “conversion tax” or the cheapest app. Start by proving what the CNY is and which legal route matches it.** China's exchange-control rules, China's income tax, US federal tax, currency gain/loss, account reporting and commercial FX/transfer costs are separate questions. The reviewed sources do not establish one tax rate on the whole balance merely because it is converted or moved. [SAFE evidence requirements](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html); [IRS worldwide-income rules](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p54); [26 USC 988, official 2024 edition](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/pdf/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.pdf).

For genuine salary, the first candidate is the handling bank's documented conversion-and-wire process. The USD 50,000 figure is a facilitation quota, not an absolute ceiling on documented qualifying current income; the foreign-permanent-resident-card/classification rules still matter. For qualifying pre-immigration assets, use the separate immigration-property approval route. For genuine inheritance, property sale or foreign-investment exit proceeds, use the matching process rather than inventing a salary transaction. [SAFE current-account guide](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2020/art_0dd36157a03f494db7571002c7f785b8.html); [SAFE foreign-PR-card Q&A](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0511/3051.html); [immigration approval guide](https://www.safe.gov.cn/hebei/file/file/20240816/86fd609207224d71b23cfda39ebe4bbb.pdf).

## Lawful ways to reduce avoidable cost or tax friction

- **Use the correct source route and complete records.** Legally non-taxable income does not need a tax certificate merely because it is untaxed, but lawful-source evidence remains necessary. [SAFE](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2025/0715/2834.html).
- **Compare FEIE and FTC on the actual income, without double counting.** FEIE is earned-income relief; the 2026 maximum is USD 132,900 per qualifying person, with eligibility/proration/timing rules. No credit for tax on excluded income. [IRS amount](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/figuring-the-foreign-earned-income-exclusion); [IRS FTC](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-tax-credit).
- **Check treaty eligibility rather than assuming it.** The US–China treaty preserves US taxation of citizens subject to listed exceptions; credit relief operates under US law. [Treaty and protocol](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/china.pdf).
- **Do not create a new taxable event just to move principal.** In particular, from 2026-09-01 foreign individuals' FIE dividends/bonuses face 20% Chinese IIT under the new announcement; the old exemption is repealed. [MOF](http://szs.mof.gov.cn/zhengcefabu/202609/t20260901_3996442.htm); [STA](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c102416/c5252107/content.html).
- **Review genuine blocked-income treatment only if the strict condition is met.** Publication 54 addresses income not readily convertible because of foreign-country restrictions; app refusal or inconvenience is not enough. [IRS Pub. 54](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p54).
- **Get like-for-like quotes and keep reporting current.** FBAR uses an aggregate foreign-account maximum above USD 10,000; Form 8938 has different thresholds. Neither is a transfer-tax allowance. [FinCEN/IRS FBAR](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/report-of-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts-fbar); [FATCA](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/corporations/summary-of-fatca-reporting-for-us-taxpayers).

## Do not use these as “optimization”

Crypto/stablecoin bridges, underground banks, quota splitting, fabricated contracts/sales/dividends and relabelling own savings as gifts/trust distributions are rejected. They add explicit Chinese legal risk and/or US reporting complexity rather than verified tax savings. [PBOC virtual-currency notice](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/4348658/index.html); [FX penalties](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/144953/2817374/index.html); [SAFE anti-splitting guide](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2020/art_0dd36157a03f494db7571002c7f785b8.html); [IRS foreign gifts/trusts](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i3520).

## Cash rules and remaining review flags

The earlier cash-source gap was resolved: China's RMB carrying limit is **RMB 20,000 per person per entry/exit**; foreign-cash exit generally uses **USD 5,000 / USD 10,000 permit tiers**, with prior-entry-declaration and frequent-traveler exceptions. US entry separately requires FinCEN 105 above **USD 10,000 equivalent**, aggregated for families/groups. These are limits/reporting rules, not tax-free allowances. [PBOC announcement](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/chubanwu/114566/114579/114643/2839193/index.html); [foreign-cash rule](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2004/content_63085.htm); [2024 consular confirmation](https://almaty.china-consulate.gov.cn/lsfw/lqgk/202404/t20240415_11281945.htm); [CBP](https://www.cbp.gov/travel/international-visitors/money-monetary-instruments).
