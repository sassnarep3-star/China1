## Answer

1. **No rule read for this guide taxes the conversion itself.** The exposure is tax on the underlying income if it was never taxed; the bank asks for tax vouchers. [SAFE Q&A 2026](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html), [IIT Law](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/eng/c102962/c102967/c102997/c103004/c5245849/content.html)
2. **Lawful route:** passport + proof of the amount + China tax record at the bank, buy USD, wire to your own US account. The cited salary rule does not state a fixed cap; eligibility and bank requirements depend on the funds and individual status. [SAFE Q&A 2026](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html), [SAFE Q&A 2024](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0621/2529.html)
3. **Illustrative cost (Bank of China wire into a Chase account):** $93.46 (0.93%) at $10,000; $311.38 (0.31%) at $100,000; $941.33 (0.24%) at $400,000. [BOC rate board](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/), [BOC tariff](https://pic.bankofchina.com/bocappd/pricelist/202511/P020251110646838136627.pdf), [Chase fees](https://www.chase.com/personal/fees/total-checking)
4. **The Wise comparison-API sample implies a $62.51 loss (0.63%) versus the BOC midpoint at $10,000 and $224.66 (0.56%) at $40,000. These historical, non-simultaneous indicative comparisons exclude any Wise receiving-bank fee; actual rates and fees may differ. CNY 300,000 is a per-transfer limit, not a total limit.** [Wise quotes](https://api.wise.com/v3/comparisons/?sourceCurrency=CNY&targetCurrency=USD&sendAmount=66770.5)
5. **Avoid prohibited conduct:** evasion through splitting or false documents, illegal currency dealing, and prohibited virtual-currency activities. Fines up to 30% of the amount, 30%–100% if serious, possible criminal liability. [FX Regulation](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2008-08/06/content_2602514.htm), [Individual FX Measures](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2006/1225/5319.html), [PBOC 2026 No. 42](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/2026020619591971323/index.html)
6. **US side:** worldwide income may be taxable; a foreign tax credit may be available subject to conditions. Converting currency may create a separate US exchange gain or loss. FBAR generally applies if aggregate foreign accounts exceed $10,000; Form 8938 thresholds depend on residence and filing status. Penalties depend on facts and assessment date. [IRS citizens abroad](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/us-citizens-and-resident-aliens-abroad), [IRS foreign tax credit](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-tax-credit), [IRS 8938 vs FBAR](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/comparison-of-form-8938-and-fbar-requirements), [31 CFR 1010.821](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1010/subpart-H/section-1010.821), [26 USC 988](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm)
