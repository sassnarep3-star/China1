## Answer

1. **No rule read for this guide taxes the conversion itself.** The exposure is tax on the underlying income if it was never taxed; the bank asks for tax vouchers. [SAFE Q&A 2026](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html), [IIT Law](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/eng/c102962/c102967/c102997/c103004/c5245849/content.html)
2. **Lawful route:** passport + proof of the amount + China tax record at the bank, buy USD, wire to your own US account. No fixed cap for a foreign passport holder; the USD 50,000 quota is for domestic individuals and permanent-resident card holders. [SAFE Q&A 2026](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html), [SAFE Q&A 2024](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0621/2529.html)
3. **Cost (Bank of China wire into a Chase account):** $93.39 (0.93%) at $10,000; $311.26 (0.31%) at $100,000; $941.20 (0.24%) at $400,000. [BOC rate board](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/), [BOC tariff](https://pic.bankofchina.com/bocappd/pricelist/202511/P020251110646838136627.pdf), [Chase fees](https://www.chase.com/personal/fees/total-checking)
4. **Wise quoted $62.37 (0.62%) at $10,000 and $224.66 (0.56%) at $40,000; it was cheaper than the Bank of China wire at $10,000 and $20,000, and dearer from $30,000. Wise figures exclude any fee of the receiving bank. Above CNY 300,000 per transfer Wise returns no quote.** [Wise quotes](https://api.wise.com/v3/comparisons/?sourceCurrency=CNY&targetCurrency=USD&sendAmount=66771)
5. **Prohibited:** splitting, borrowed quotas, money changers, false documents, crypto. Fines up to 30% of the amount, 30%–100% if serious, possible criminal liability. [FX Regulation](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2008-08/06/content_2602514.htm), [Individual FX Measures](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2006/1225/5319.html), [PBOC 2026 No. 42](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/2026020619591971323/index.html)
6. **US side:** worldwide income is taxed when earned, with credit for China tax; the wire is not taxed again. FBAR above $10,000 (penalty up to $16,536 non-willful, $165,353 willful); Form 8938 above $50,000/$75,000 if you live in the US, or $200,000/$300,000 if you live outside it; exchange gain ignored up to $200. [IRS citizens abroad](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/us-citizens-and-resident-aliens-abroad), [IRS foreign tax credit](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-tax-credit), [IRS 8938 vs FBAR](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/comparison-of-form-8938-and-fbar-requirements), [31 CFR 1010.821](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1010/subpart-H/section-1010.821), [26 USC 988](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm)

## What has to be answered before you act

24 questions are laid out on `guide/decision.html` with what the rules say, what is missing and how to find it. 8 of them have no official answer yet (flagged); 12 turn on facts only you can supply.

- **D01 Where did the CNY in the account come from?** — Which category your balance falls into. No public source can classify your own deposits. ([Individual FX Measures](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2006/1225/5319.html), [PBOC Ann. 2004-16](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2005/content_64294.htm))
- **D02 Can you prove the source is lawful?** — Nothing in the rules — this is a question about your paperwork. ([FX Regulation](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2008-08/06/content_2602514.htm), [SAFE Q&A 2026](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html))
- **D03 How much is there, and is the account in your name alone?** — Your balance and account structure. Not inferable from any official page. ([Individual FX Rules](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2007/0105/5320.html))
- **D04 Do you hold the Foreign Permanent Resident ID card (外国人永久居留身份证)?** — Whether you hold the card. Also unresolved: the FX Regulation defines a domestic individual to include a foreigner resident in China for one continuous year, while the Individual FX Measures define a domestic individual as a Chinese citizen holding a resident ID card. ([SAFE Q&A 2024](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0621/2529.html), [FX Regulation](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2008-08/06/content_2602514.htm), [Individual FX Measures](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2006/1225/5319.html))
- **D05 Are you a China tax resident this year?** — Your day count and domicile position, which depend on your visa, family and home. No public source can decide it for you. ([IIT Law](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/eng/c102962/c102967/c102997/c103004/c5245849/content.html))
- **D06 Has China tax been paid on it, and can you show the record?** — Whether the tax on your specific balance has been paid. If it has not, the amount of tax and any late-payment surcharge is a question for the tax office; the surcharge and evasion fines were only seen as an excerpt and are flagged. ([SAFE Q&A 2026](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html), [Tax Collection Law](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5195081/content.html))
