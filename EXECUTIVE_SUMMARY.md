# Executive summary — final integration

**Decision date:** 2026-11-10 · **Reader:** a US citizen moving funds from a Chinese bank to USD · **Currency controls depend on immigration/residence and the money’s source, not US citizenship alone.**

## Most important result

There is **no verified universal cheapest route**. First establish that the Chinese bank can document the underlying transaction, source of funds, required tax treatment and both parties’ identity. Then request executable quotes for the same settled amount from every eligible provider. Compare the exchange rate and every sending, intermediary and receiving fee—not just a headline remittance commission.

The [cost dashboard](integration/costs.html) gives 40 USD-equivalent scenarios from $10,000 through $400,000. Its bank figures are **partial sender charges**, not all-in totals. Using BOC’s observed 2026-11-10 cash ask of CNY669.03 per USD100 (CNY6.6903/USD):

BOC: 0.1%, CNY50–260, plus CNY150 cable ([official PDF](https://pic.bankofchina.com/bocappd/pricelist/202511/P020251110646838136627.pdf)). HSBC Premier/Advance: 0.1%, CNY100–500, plus CNY120 cable ([official PDF](https://www.hsbc.com.cn/content/dam/hsbc/cn/docs/document-download/tariff-of-accouts-and-services.pdf)); other HSBC customer tiers and Global Transfers use different terms. Both exclude FX execution, overseas/intermediary charges, documents and tax. BOC’s [quotation](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/) is an observation, not an offered or mid-market rate; it is not a live quote.

## Decision order

1. **Identify the route before optimizing price.** [SAFE 2020 guidance](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5560296.htm) requires genuine transactions and prohibits artificial/split transactions. Overseas-person lawful RMB income such as salary can require contracts, transaction evidence and tax records. Do not delegate conversion to unrelated people or structure around limits.
2. **Separate entitlement from execution.** Emigrant/inherited-property transfer, direct investment liquidation and other capital routes can establish what may be remitted; a bank, provider or card is only the rail. Do not add their costs together.
3. **For existing principal, ask the bank for its lawful remittance procedure.** Compare BOC, HSBC and any eligible Wise route with a live quote. Wise’s [official guide](https://wise.com/help/articles/2955298/guide-to-cny-transfers) describes eligibility, China work/tax history, own-account destination and per-transfer caps, but a fee for this personal transfer is unconfirmed.
4. **Use securities, property, vehicle or company-sale routes only when that transaction is real.** Obtain valuation, closing, tax, registration and bank-review records. Do not create a sham asset sale to move money.
5. **Do not use prohibited workarounds.** Underground banks, falsified invoices, structuring, nominee quotas and virtual-currency evasion appear in the inventory only to document risk; no execution instructions are supplied.

The [method catalog](integration/methods.html) contains 43 distinct candidates and direct links to Edition H’s procedure/cost pages. Every lawful procedure remains subject to the bank and competent authority’s current review. Large or complex capital transactions should receive advice from the handling bank, a China tax adviser and a US international tax adviser before execution.

## Tax and reporting: do not assume zero

Moving one’s own already-taxed principal is not automatically new US income. That is not a blanket exemption: characterize the original income, interest, dividends, asset/company gains, gifts and currency disposition separately. See [IRS citizens abroad](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/us-citizens-and-resident-aliens-abroad), [China IIT law](https://guangdong.chinatax.gov.cn/gdsw/zjfg/2018-08/31/content_a735b4c382d94439b581054c32a4e7f4.shtml), [26 USC §988](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title26/html/USCODE-2024-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.htm) and the [foreign tax credit](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-tax-credit). §988’s personal-transaction exclusion is conditional and is **not a universal USD200 deduction**.

FBAR and Forms 8938/3520 are separate reporting questions. Foreign-person gifts generally require Form 3520 analysis above USD100,000, with aggregation; the 2026 foreign-entity purported-gift threshold is USD20,573 ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/gifts-from-foreign-person)). There is no general USD50,000 “gift tax” threshold established by these sources. The new 1% US remittance tax concerns specified physical-instrument-funded remittances sent **from** the US, not this China-to-US direction ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/treasury-irs-issue-proposed-regulations-on-the-new-remittance-transfer-tax-established-under-the-one-big-beautiful-bill)).

## Requested-borderline and edge-case corrections

- **USD5,000 note-free cash rule:** not certified as a universal exemption. [SAFE](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2017/1230/8130.html) sets overseas bank-card cash withdrawal at CNY100,000 per person per year and CNY10,000 per day, with suspension beyond the annual cap. Cash permits, customs, US physical-currency declaration and sanctions/AML rules are separate.
- **Emigrant transfer:** [SAFE’s 2014 amendment](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2024/0903/2594.html) removed the earlier mandatory 50%/half/remaining staged releases. Current approval, immigration status and evidence still matter.
- **Inheritance:** current status, notarized entitlement and tax/document review are required. The official inheritance guide retains inconsistent pre-status wording and is flagged rather than silently harmonized.
- **Property/car/stock sale:** no generic tax rate or “automatic remittability” is inferred. Each requires an actual sale, basis/proceeds records and route-specific review.
- **Cards:** CNY30,000/CNY80,000 figures are not accepted as universal limits. Card network, issuer, merchant, cash classification, MCC and annual bank-card cash controls differ.
- **Digital dollars/virtual currency:** Mainland restrictions apply; the PBC’s [2026 notice](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/2026020619591971323/index.html) expressly repeals the 2021 notice. No evasion procedure is provided.
