## Interpretation limits

- A source link supports the cited rule, observation or provider term; it does not prove a reader’s immigration status, transaction authenticity, tax basis or bank acceptance.
- Bank tariff arithmetic is not a quote. FX spread, intermediary/receiver fees, document expenses and tax remain blank when unsupported.
- The 40 consolidated scenarios and Edition H’s historical grids are different dated observations and must not be mixed.
- Factual findings, tax character, immigration status and foreign reporting can change the answer. Obtain transaction-specific advice before a large transfer.
