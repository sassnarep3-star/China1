How the total is computed

Bank of China wire, one transfer of A dollars
exchange margin = A × (6.6903 − 6.67625) ÷ 6.67625
commission = min(260, max(50, 0.001 × A × 6.67625)) CNY
cable = 150 CNY · full-amount fee = $25 · receiving fee = $15
total = exchange margin + (commission + cable) ÷ 6.67625 + 25 + 15
