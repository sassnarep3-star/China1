M01. Bank of China: buy USD against documented China income and wire it, full-amount arrival

Recommended Passport holders (overseas individuals) whose RMB is lawful current-account income earned in China, with tax evidence. · cost ladder · F17 F18 F19 F24 F03

#StepSources
1Collect proof that the RMB is lawful China-source income and that tax was paid: contract or pay records plus the tax payment record covering the amount.CN-FX-02 CN-FX-01
2Go to the bank that holds the RMB with your passport. The bank set your account category from the ID shown at opening.CN-FX-01
3Ask to buy USD against those documents. The bank reviews authenticity and consistency, may ask for more, and must give a reason and an appeal channel if it refuses.CN-FX-02
4Wire the USD from your foreign-currency account to your own US account using your ID.CN-FX-02
5Tick full-amount arrival (全额到账) so intermediary banks cannot deduct from the principal: USD 25, or USD 15 when the receiving bank holds an account with Bank of China New York.FEE-02
6Pay the commission of 0.1% (minimum CNY 50, maximum CNY 260) and the CNY 150 cable fee. Keep the purchase memo and wire receipt.FEE-02
7In the US, the receiving bank may charge an incoming-wire fee (Chase: $15). Report the China account on FBAR and Form 8938 if you pass the thresholds.FEE-09 US-02

M02. Bank of China: same purchase, standard wire without full-amount arrival

Lawful Same as M01. · cost ladder · F01 F17 F19

#StepSources
1Follow M01 steps 1–4.CN-FX-02
2Send as a standard wire. Foreign-bank charges are passed on at cost and come out of the principal.FEE-02
3Pay the 0.1% commission (CNY 50–260) and CNY 150 cable fee.FEE-02

M03. HSBC China Premier or Advance telegraphic transfer

Lawful HSBC China Premier or Advance customers; same document gate as M01. Premier needs a CNY 500,000 relationship balance or a CNY 300 monthly fee; Advance is closed to most new customers since 18 Mar 2026. · cost ladder · F02 F03

#StepSources
1Hold or open an HSBC China account in the Premier or Advance tier.FEE-06
2Present the same source and tax documents as in M01 and buy USD.CN-FX-02
3Send a telegraphic transfer: 0.1% of the amount (minimum CNY 100, maximum CNY 500) plus CNY 120 cable.FEE-06
4Optional Pay Exact, CNY 200, so the beneficiary receives the full USD amount; any shortfall in overseas charges is collected from you.FEE-06

M04. HSBC China Private Banking individual telegraphic transfer

Lawful HSBC China Private Banking individual customers; same document gate as M01. · cost ladder · F02 F01 F03

#StepSources
1Present source and tax documents and buy USD.CN-FX-02
2Send a telegraphic transfer; issuance and cable are free for individual Private Banking customers.FEE-06

M05. Wise from-CNY transfer through its licensed China partner

Lawful Non-mainland individuals who work or worked in China and hold a China tax record from the last three years; personal accounts only; pays only to an account you own. · cost ladder · F04 F21

#StepSources
1Open a personal Wise account and start a CNY→USD transfer to your own US account.FEE-07
2Upload proof of work in China and a tax record issued by the China tax authority within the last three years; verification takes 1–2 working days the first time.FEE-07
3Pay from a Chinese bank account in your own name (up to CNY 300,000 per transfer) or from Alipay.FEE-07
4Wise's licensed partner converts; the fee is shown before you pay and Wise states it uses the mid-market rate.FEE-08 FEE-10
5Repeat for further tranches; supply new tax records when the overall limit is reached.FEE-07

M06. Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card holder: USD 50,000 yearly purchase quota

Lawful Overseas individuals holding a Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card. · cost ladder · F08

#StepSources
1Show the Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card; the yearly purchase quota applies to you.CN-FX-02
2Buy up to USD 50,000 in the year on ID alone.CN-FX-02 CN-FX-01
3Wire it out from your foreign-currency account with ID; costs as M01.CN-FX-02 FEE-02
4Do not split, borrow or lend quotas.CN-FX-02

M07. Emigrant property transfer (移民财产转移) with SAFE approval

Recommended People who moved abroad from mainland China and obtained foreign citizenship or permanent residence; covers lawful property owned in China before that status. · cost ladder · F25 F17 F19 F21

#StepSources
1Turn the property into RMB and gather proof of ownership and of the income behind it.CN-FX-07
2Obtain a tax certificate from the tax bureau, unless the total is CNY 150,000 or less or you already hold tax-paid vouchers.CN-TAX-01
3Assemble identity papers: foreign passport or ID, the certificate of settlement abroad issued or certified by a Chinese embassy or consulate (unless exempted under the relevant convention), and the certificate that your household registration was cancelled.CN-FX-07
4File the written application and form at the SAFE branch of your original household registration. One visit, not online, no fee.CN-FX-07 CN-FX-04 CN-FX-06
5SAFE issues the approval document within 20 working days.CN-FX-07
6Take the approval to the bank, buy USD and remit in one wire or several within the approved amount.CN-FX-04
7Wire costs as M01.FEE-02

M08. Inheritance property transfer (继承财产转移) with SAFE approval

Recommended Foreign citizens who lawfully inherit an estate in mainland China. · cost ladder · F25 F14 F23 F10

#StepSources
1Obtain proof of inheritance (for example a notarised succession or will) and the decedent's property documents; turn the estate into RMB.CN-FX-08
2Obtain the tax certificate unless exempt from providing one.CN-FX-08 CN-TAX-01
3Assemble identity papers: foreign passport, residence-country ID, and the certificate of settlement in that country issued or certified by a Chinese embassy or consulate (unless exempted under the relevant convention).CN-FX-08
4File at the SAFE branch of the decedent's last household registration. No fee.CN-FX-08 CN-FX-06
5After approval, buy USD at the bank and remit once or in batches.CN-FX-04
6In the US, report on Form 3520 if bequests from the foreign estate exceed $100,000 in the year.US-04

M09. Proceeds of selling a home bought as an overseas individual

Lawful Overseas individuals who sell their mainland commodity housing. · cost ladder · F06 F13 F21

#StepSources
1Sell the home. Tax is assessed at transfer and the registry checks the income-tax payment certificate before registering.CN-TAX-04 CN-TAX-07
2Keep the transfer contract, registration proof and tax vouchers.CN-FX-11
3For a single outward payment above USD 50,000, complete the tax filing; the tax bureau reviews it within 15 working days.CN-TAX-02
4Submit the purchase application, contract and tax proof for review, then buy USD and wire it.CN-FX-01 CN-FX-11

M10. Dividends from your foreign-invested enterprise

Lawful Foreign individuals holding equity in a foreign-invested enterprise with distributable profit. · cost ladder · F26 F09

#StepSources
1The company resolves to distribute profit.CN-FX-04
2The company withholds 20% individual income tax and files by the 15th of the following month.CN-TAX-05
3Above USD 50,000 the company's bank checks the distribution resolution and the tax filing form; up to USD 50,000 documents are in principle not reviewed.CN-FX-04 CN-TAX-02
4The company's bank remits the dividend to your US account.CN-FX-04
5In the US, report the dividend and claim the credit; only tax at the treaty rate is creditable.US-05 US-07

M12. Send unused inbound foreign currency back by the original route

#StepSources

M14. Withdraw USD cash and carry it out

Capacity-limited Anyone with lawfully purchased foreign currency. · cost ladder · F27 F19

#StepSources
1Withdraw foreign cash, up to USD 10,000 a day on ID.CN-FX-02
2To carry more than USD 5,000 (up to USD 10,000) beyond your last inbound declaration, obtain the carrying permit from the bank. Bank of China lists CNY 10 per permit, waived until 31 December 2026.CN-FX-02 FEE-02
3More than USD 10,000 per trip is allowed only for specified hardship destinations with a SAFE-issued permit.CN-FX-02
4On arrival in the US, report amounts exceeding $10,000; the threshold counts a family or group together.US-09

M15. UnionPay debit card cash withdrawals abroad

Capacity-limited Holders of mainland bank cards. · cost ladder · F05

#StepSources
1Keep the RMB on a UnionPay debit card.FEE-04
2Withdraw abroad, at most CNY 10,000 per card per day.CN-FX-10
3Stop at CNY 100,000 in the calendar year across all your cards; exceeding it suspends overseas cash withdrawal for that year and the next.CN-FX-10
4Bank of China charges CNY 12 plus 1% per withdrawal, at most CNY 112.FEE-04
