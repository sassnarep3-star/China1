Executive summary

Twelve findings, most important first. Each row links to the official text behind it.

#FindingDetailSources
1You are an overseas individual, and that helpsChina's quota of USD 50,000 a year is for Chinese residents and for holders of the Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card. A passport holder converts lawful China income on documents instead, with no yearly cap in the rule.CN-FX-01 CN-FX-02 F08
2The document that matters is the tax recordThe bank asks for proof of the source of the RMB, including tax evidence, checks it, and may refuse with a stated reason. A single payment above USD 50,000 may also need a tax filing.CN-FX-02 CN-TAX-02 F24
3One Bank of China wire has the lowest fully published cost$93.51 on $10,000, $311.86 on $100,000, $943.20 on $400,000. That is exchange margin plus commission, cable fee, the full-amount fee and Chase's receiving fee. HSBC's exchange rate is not published, so its total cannot be compared.FEE-01 FEE-02 FEE-09 FEE-06 F17 F19 F03 F02
4Most of the cost at large sizes is the exchange marginBuying USD costs 0.2104% against the midpoint of the board. The 0.42% often quoted is the full distance between the bank's buying and selling rates. On $400,000 the margin is $841.79 of the $943.20 total.FEE-01
5Do not split a large amount into small wiresThe commission stops at CNY 260 and the other charges are per wire. $400,000 costs $943.20 in one wire, $1,247.44 in four, $1,653.08 in eight.FEE-02
6Wise is cheaper only for small amountsWise's quoted fee is CNY 435.16 on CNY 66,763 and CNY 1,741.67 on CNY 300,000, its limit per transfer. The bank wire is cheaper from about $20,000 upward.FEE-10 FEE-08 FEE-07 F04
7Naturalised citizens and heirs have approval routesAssets owned before you took foreign citizenship, and estates you inherit, leave through a SAFE approval: no fee, a decision within 20 working days, and the money may go in one remittance. The old 50/25/25 staging was abolished.CN-FX-04 CN-FX-07 CN-FX-08 F25
8Converting is not a taxable event; the income isWages were taxed when paid. Dividends from a foreign-invested company bear 20% from 1 September 2026. A home sale bears 20% of the gain, or 1%–3% of the price where cost records are incomplete. Bank interest is exempt for now.CN-TAX-04 CN-TAX-05 CN-TAX-07 CN-TAX-06
9The US taxes the income, not the transferA US citizen reports worldwide income and may credit China income tax. A currency gain on a personal conversion is ignored up to $200. An inheritance is not income but is reported on Form 3520 above $100,000.US-08 US-05 US-06 US-04
10Reporting failures cost more than any feeFBAR above $10,000 in foreign accounts; Form 8938 above its thresholds; currency above $10,000 carried across the border. The FBAR maximum is $16,536 when non-willful.US-02 US-03 US-09 F11
11Shortcuts are where people lose real moneySplitting, borrowed quotas, underground banks, false documents and crypto bridges are prohibited. Fines run to 30% of the amount, or up to the whole amount in serious cases.CN-FX-03 CN-FX-02 CN-FX-09
12What stays unverified27 items are left blank rather than estimated, including intermediary-bank deductions, HSBC's exchange rate, the current desk procedure for home-sale proceeds and the cost of notarised documents.FEE-02 FEE-06 CN-FX-11 all flags

What to do, in order

  1. Work out where the RMB came from and find the matching route on the home page.
  2. Collect the tax records for that income before visiting the bank.
  3. Ask the bank which category your account carries (overseas or domestic individual) and which documents it wants.
  4. Convert and send in one wire with full-amount arrival, to an account in your own name.
  5. File the US reports that apply for the year: FBAR, Form 8938, Form 3520.
