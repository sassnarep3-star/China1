Method

How the figures are produced, what they leave out, and how to check them.

How a number gets onto this site

  1. An official page is read and the relevant passage is stored verbatim in research/evidence_*.json.
  2. Each model input names the stored passage and the exact text that must appear in it. The build stops if the text is missing.
  3. Totals are plain arithmetic on those inputs, shown on Costs. The exchange margin is measured against the midpoint of the bank's own buying and selling rates.
  4. If any input of a total has no published figure, the total is left blank and the row links to a flag. Nothing is estimated or interpolated.
  5. A separate script recomputes every row with exact fractions, checks every link and anchor, and checks that every table row carries a source link.

What the totals include and leave out

  • Included: exchange margin, the sending bank's commission and cable fee, the full-amount arrival fee, and Chase's published incoming-wire fee.
  • Left out: the cost of obtaining documents, travel, and anything your own US bank charges beyond the Chase figure.
  • Tax is shown separately because it depends on what the money is, not on how it is sent.

Limits

  • The exchange board is a snapshot stamped 2026/11/10 07:32:39 and the bank says the executed rate governs.
  • Eight sources were read as official-domain text returned by a search tool rather than opened directly; they are listed in flag F21.
  • Banks decide which documents satisfy them. A rule that allows a transfer does not guarantee a branch will process it.
  • This is general information, not legal or tax advice.

The brief, item by item

  • Research ways to convert CNY into USD and list as many as possible. 19 candidates worked through here, 7 prohibited schemes, 21 further candidates from earlier editions listed as not re-verified
  • Pros and cons of every way. Each method row carries advantages and drawbacks
  • Exact steps for each lawful method. Numbered steps, each with its source; prohibited schemes get none
  • Break down the conversion penalty. Exchange margin, commission, cable, full-amount fee, receiving fee, tax and legal exposure shown separately
  • Estimated total for USD 10,000 to USD 400,000 in USD 10,000 steps. 40 rows per method; totals only where every input is published
  • As a US citizen, the best route with the least tax penalty. Answer first, then the route by source of funds
  • No hallucinations; official sources; links for manual review. Every number is tied to a verbatim quote; the verifier checks each one
  • Every table row has a source link. Checked by the verifier on every generated table row
  • Unconfirmed values stay blank and are flagged. Blank cells link to the flag that explains them
  • Review the repository line by line and flag irregularities. All tracked files outside this directory scanned; counts and examples published
  • Executive summary. One page, most important answer first
  • Mobile-friendly dashboard site with category subpages, no broken links. Static pages, no scripts, link crawl in the verifier
  • Coordinate through COORDINATION.md; claim before work; do not touch others' scopes. Claim, log and release record are in the shared board
  • Open the pull request, merge it, confirm the Pages deployment. Recorded in COORDINATION.md after release
