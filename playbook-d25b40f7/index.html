Moving RMB out of China into US dollars

A decision guide for a US citizen with money in mainland China. Figures as of 10 November 2026. Every figure links to the official page it was read from.

The short answer

  1. Use documents, not the USD 50,000 quota. With a foreign passport you are an “overseas individual” under China's foreign-exchange rules. RMB that is lawful income earned in China can be converted and wired on your ID plus proof of where it came from, including tax records. The rule text sets no yearly cap for this. CN-FX-01 CN-FX-02
  2. Lowest cost among paths with every charge published: one Bank of China wire with full-amount arrival. Total $93.51 on $10,000 (0.94%), $311.86 on $100,000 (0.31%) and $943.20 on $400,000 (0.24%), including a $15 receiving fee at Chase. FEE-01 FEE-02 FEE-09
  3. Send one wire, not several. Fixed charges reach $101.41 per wire once the amount passes $38,944. Splitting $400,000 into eight wires costs $1,653.08 instead of $943.20. FEE-02
  4. Under about $20,000, Wise's from-CNY service costs less. Its fee on $10,000 is $65.18, against $78.51 for the bank wire before any US receiving fee. It is limited to CNY 300,000 per transfer and to what your China tax records support. FEE-07 FEE-10
  5. The route depends on where the money came from. Wages, pre-emigration assets, an inheritance, a home sale and company profits each have their own rule. Pick yours in the table below.
  6. Moving your own money is not taxed. The income behind it is. China taxes dividends at 20% since 1 September 2026 and home-sale gains at 20%. The US taxes worldwide income and credits China income tax. CN-TAX-05 CN-TAX-04 US-08 US-05

What can go badly wrong

  • China: unlawful routes carry fines of up to 30% of the amount, or 30% up to the whole amount in serious cases, plus criminal liability. Splitting amounts or borrowing other people's quotas puts you on a watch list for three years. CN-FX-03 CN-FX-02
  • United States: a China account above $10,000 must be reported on the FBAR. The maximum penalty is $16,536 when non-willful; a willful violation is far higher. Form 8938 and Form 3520 have their own thresholds. US-02 US-03 US-04
  • Not found in the rules read: a bulk route for savings with no income or tax paper trail. CN-FX-02
