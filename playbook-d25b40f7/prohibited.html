Prohibited schemes

Listed so they can be recognised and refused. No steps are given for any of them.

SchemeWhat the rule saysConsequenceSources
X01 Splitting one amount into many small conversions or across several peopleIndividuals may not evade quota or authenticity rules by splitting.Watch list for the year of listing and the two following years; every later FX transaction then needs documents.CN-FX-01 CN-FX-02
X02 Borrowing relatives' or friends' USD 50,000 quotas, or lending yoursLending a quota draws a risk notice, a repeat puts the lender on the watch list; the borrower goes on the watch list directly.Watch list: year of listing plus two years.CN-FX-02
X03 Underground banks, matched offsetting payments, private currency dealingPrivate or disguised FX trading and illegal introduction of FX trades in large amounts.Warning, confiscation of gains, fine up to 30% of the amount; 30% up to the full amount in serious cases; criminal liability.CN-FX-03
X04 Fabricated or invalid documents to buy foreign currencyObtaining FX with false or invalid transaction documents is illegal arbitrage.Forced conversion back, fine up to 30%; 30% up to the full amount in serious cases; criminal liability. Untrue documents alone: up to CNY 50,000 for an individual.CN-FX-03 CN-FX-01
X05 Crypto or stablecoin bridgeExchanging legal tender for virtual currency in China is an illegal financial activity; banks and payment firms may not serve it.Related civil acts are invalid and losses fall on the holder; investigation and criminal liability where applicable.CN-FX-09
X06 Moving money out in breach of the rules, including carrying cash above the limitsTransferring FX abroad in violation of the rules is evasion; unlawful carrying is punished separately.Evasion: repatriation order and fine up to 30%, 30% up to the full amount in serious cases. Carrying: warning and fine up to 20%.CN-FX-03 CN-FX-02
X07 Using other people's bank cards to exceed the overseas cash capIndividuals may not borrow or lend cards to get around the overseas cash-withdrawal limits.Exceeding the yearly cap suspends overseas cash withdrawal for that year and the next.CN-FX-10

Fine ceilings in dollars are on Cost and penalty breakdown.
