Sources deliberately rejected

Numerous search results during this session were law-firm marketing pages, accounting blogs and news aggregators stating FBAR penalty figures, Form 3520 thresholds and Chinese quota rules. Several of them disagreed with each other and at least two disagreed with the statute. They were not used, and no number in this dossier depends on one. Where they were the only source available for a fact, that fact is blank and flagged.

Research dossier, not advice. Every legal proposition is traced to a published instrument with a link, and every number is either quoted from an official source or plainly labelled as derived from one. Values that could not be confirmed from an official source are left blank and listed on the Flagged for review page. Built 2026-11-07.
