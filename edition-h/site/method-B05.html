B05 · Liquidation of a foreign-invested enterprise

lawful-conditional entitlement

State Council Regulation Art. 22 expressly allows RMB belonging to foreign investors of a lawfully terminated FIE to be purchased and remitted after liquidation and tax payment.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S06 · SAFE Beijing S48 · SAFE

Step by step

  1. Lawfully terminate and liquidate the FIE.
  2. Settle debts and taxes; prepare liquidation report and investor distribution.
  3. Complete deregistration and bank capital-account formalities.
  4. Purchase/remit the foreign investor's remaining RMB under Art. 22.

Eligibility / conditions

  • FIE lawfully terminated and liquidated.
  • Taxes paid.
  • Remaining property belongs to the foreign investor.

Pros

  • Express statutory capital-account entitlement.

Cons

  • Liquidation is slow and costly.
  • Entity debts/taxes must be settled first.
  • Not a quick personal transfer.
