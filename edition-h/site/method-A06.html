Personal routes · A06

Salary and bonus remittance backed by the PRC tax record

工资薪金购汇汇出   Lawful

The rule

This is the cleanest instance of A01: the income is a named current-account category, the tax voucher is the evidence, and the bank has a routine process for it.

Source: CN-08 CN-09 BK-01 BK-04

Who can use it

Anyone who has worked in mainland China and has PRC individual income tax records for that salary.

Cap or limit

No cap beyond the documentary review; evidence must match the amount.

Documents

  • Passport
  • Employment contract
  • PRC IIT withholding records for the relevant years
  • Payslips or the employer's income certificate

Pros

  • Banks handle this every day, so the process is predictable
  • The evidence is generated automatically by PRC payroll withholding
  • Removes the 'source of funds' question entirely
  • Cost is the published tariff, typically under 0.5% all-in

Cons

  • Limited to what you actually earned and declared — it will not move an inherited or saved balance
  • Multi-year accumulation needs multi-year evidence
  • If tax was under-withheld, the gap must be settled first

Step by step

  1. Download your PRC individual income tax records for each year concerned from the tax authority's portal, or request them from the withholding employer.
  2. Take the passport, contract and tax records to the bank and request purchase against current-account income.
  3. Purchase and remit to your own US account; retain the receipts.
  4. Claim the foreign tax credit on Form 1116 for PRC tax paid on the same income (US-07).
