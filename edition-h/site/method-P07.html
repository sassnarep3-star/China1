P07 · Return of unused originally inbound foreign currency

lawful-conditional entitlement

Foreign currency already purchased within a lawful quota or retained in an existing account can be remitted with purpose and source evidence. This is not a second USD50,000 entitlement.

S02 · SAFE / PBOC S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S14 · Bank of China S16 · Bank of China

Step by step

  1. Confirm that the foreign currency was lawfully acquired and remains in the same person’s account; gather the original purchase, deposit and tax/source records.
  2. Present the genuine remittance purpose, beneficiary and source evidence to the bank; do not borrow another person’s quota.
  3. Ask the bank to quote its executable outward-TT rate, full-arrival option and third-party charges. Compare BOC, HSBC and other authorized banks.
  4. Send a small test if practical, retain the SWIFT/MT103 and bank advice, and reconcile U.S. reporting. The USD50,000 annual quota is a facilitation limit for purchase, not a universal cap on remitting existing foreign currency.

Eligibility / conditions

  • Funds are still foreign currency, not CNY.
  • Original inbound remittance can be traced.

Pros

  • No new CNY purchase or new annual purchase quota is needed.
  • Existing bank records can support a straightforward outward remittance.

Cons

  • Purpose, beneficiary and original source still require bank review.
  • Existing FX does not eliminate cable, correspondent, tax or reporting obligations.
