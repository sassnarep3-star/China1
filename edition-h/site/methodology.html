Methodology and limitations

Source hierarchy

  1. Government, regulator, statute, court and official tax guidance for legal/tax claims.
  2. Bank/provider official tariff, help and public quote pages for that provider's own product terms only.
  3. No blogs, forums, aggregators or law-firm summaries support numeric or legal rows. S52 is excluded from citations.

Cost model

  • Requested amounts are gross USD-equivalent principal before fees, not net receipt.
  • Personal legal entitlements use a clearly labeled conditional BOC TT scenario so each requested amount has useful arithmetic. This is not proof of eligibility and is not added to a separate rail.
  • BOC CNY scenario uses its 2026-11-09 09:15:45 board: USD100 telegraphic/cash ask CNY669.19, hence CNY6.6919 per USD1. The same ask converts CNY fees to USD for comparison.
  • HSBC's own executable FX was not captured, so CNY principal remains blank. Its fee arithmetic still uses CNY-equivalent principal; the resulting USD fee comparison uses BOC's ask and says so.
  • Wise fees are direct public observations only. No interpolation, regression or extrapolation.
  • FX cost is blank because a retail board and a reference rate do not establish a one-way executable spread. The observed BOC ask was below its reference rate.
  • All-in requires live FX, exact transfer count, correspondent/receiver charges, documents, source-of-funds and tax facts. Those are absent, so all-in is blank.

What “verified” does and does not mean

Verified means the displayed source was inspected for the cited proposition on 2026-11-09 and arithmetic can be reproduced. It does not mean a bank will approve a transaction, a source will always remain online, a tax authority accepts a treaty position, or a quote is executable now.

Safety and legality

Rejected candidates are included to answer “as many as possible” and to warn about common suggestions. They receive no operational steps and no price that could make them appear legitimate. Lawful alternatives such as QDII or Wealth Management Connect are investment channels, not free repatriation of an existing bank balance.
