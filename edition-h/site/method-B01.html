B01 · FIE dividend to a foreign individual shareholder

lawful-conditional entitlement

A real foreign-invested enterprise may distribute after-tax profits to a foreign individual shareholder. From 2026-09-01 the old foreign-individual dividend exemption is repealed and 20% IIT applies, subject to any valid treaty relief. This is not a way to relabel personal savings.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S31 · State Taxation Administration / Guangdong S33 · STA Shanghai S35 · STA Tianjin S36 · IRS S37 · IRS

Step by step

  1. Confirm share ownership and distributable profits in the FIE accounts.
  2. Pass a valid profit-distribution resolution.
  3. Compute and withhold PRC IIT; assess treaty beneficial-owner/residence relief before payment.
  4. Complete outbound tax filing if required and submit resolution/tax documents to the bank.
  5. Remit the net dividend to the shareholder's account and report it in the U.S.

Eligibility / conditions

  • You actually own equity in the FIE.
  • Distributable after-tax profits and shareholder resolution exist.
  • Withholding and tax filing completed.

Pros

  • Legitimate return on real equity.

Cons

  • 20% PRC withholding from September 2026 unless treaty relief applies.
  • Entity audit/profit evidence.
  • U.S. dividend reporting and foreign tax credit limits.
