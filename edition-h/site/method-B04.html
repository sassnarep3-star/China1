B04 · Sale of equity in a Chinese entity to a foreign buyer

lawful-conditional entitlement

A real equity sale can generate proceeds remittable under direct-investment/asset-realisation rules after registration and tax. It is not a device to move an unrelated personal balance.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S06 · SAFE Beijing S31 · State Taxation Administration / Guangdong S48 · SAFE

Step by step

  1. Execute a genuine sale agreement and complete registration change.
  2. File and pay transfer taxes.
  3. Use the bank's current foreign-investor withdrawal/asset-realisation checklist to remit net proceeds.

Eligibility / conditions

  • Genuine equity sale at arm's length.
  • Registration change and tax filing complete.

Pros

  • Monetises a real asset.

Cons

  • Valuation, tax and registration burden.
  • Buyer and funds must be genuine.
