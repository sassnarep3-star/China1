Personal routes · A03

Emigrant property transfer

移民财产转移   Conditional

The rule

A dedicated capital-account route: the emigrant applies once for the whole amount of pre-emigration property, the local SAFE office at the original household-registration location approves it, and the designated bank buys the FX and remits directly to the applicant's own account abroad. Banknotes may not be handed over inside China.

Source: CN-14 CN-15 CN-16 CN-17

Who can use it

A natural person who has moved from mainland China to a foreign country (or to Hong Kong/Macau) and wants to take out lawful property they owned BEFORE obtaining that emigration status.

Cap or limit

No statutory ceiling. Approval is by the local SAFE branch; the old CNY 500,000 upward-filing threshold was abolished in 2014.

Documents

  • Written application stating the reason and a detailed explanation of the property and its realisation
  • Completed 移民财产对外转移申请人情况表 and 个人财产对外转移外汇业务申请表
  • Passport plus the cancelled PRC household registration certificate (户籍注销证明) and proof of settlement abroad certified by a PRC embassy or consulate
  • Property-rights documents (title deed, sale contract, relocation-compensation agreement)
  • PRC tax certificate or duty-paid voucher from the competent tax authority
  • Anything else the local SAFE office asks for

Pros

  • Purpose-built for exactly the 'money stuck in China' problem
  • No upper limit written into the rule
  • The 2014 amendment removed the staged 50/25/25 releases and the notarisation requirement
  • Lands directly in your own overseas account

Cons

  • You must have cancelled your PRC household registration — a hard gate most people cannot meet
  • Covers only property owned BEFORE the emigration status; money earned later does not qualify
  • Local SAFE approval is discretionary and slow; no published service standard
  • Requires a PRC tax clearance on the underlying property; that is where any real tax arises
  • Document costs (translation, certification) are unpublished, so the all-in figure here stays blank

Step by step

  1. Confirm the decisive fact: was the property acquired before you obtained foreign residence, and is your PRC household registration cancelled? If either answer is no, this route is unavailable.
  2. Obtain the PRC tax certificate or duty-paid voucher for the property from the competent tax authority — this is step zero because everything else depends on it.
  3. Lodge the application with the SAFE branch at your ORIGINAL household-registration location, with the document set above.
  4. SAFE issues an approval letter and a 核准件 stating the approved amount.
  5. Take the 核准件 to the designated bank. It buys the FX and remits, once or in instalments at your choice, directly to your own account abroad (CN-16).
  6. File the US side: FBAR for the year(s) the account existed, and report any gain on the property as capital gain.
