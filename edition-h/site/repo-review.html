R01 · HIGH

edition-d/data/parameters.json · edition-d/build/build.py

Finding. The emigrant route is priced with a 50/25/25 staged release above CNY 200,000 and the summary page repeats the schedule.

Evidence. parameters.json emigration.release_fractions = [0.5, 0.25, 0.25]; build.py line ~146 applies them; docs/pages/summary.html: 'releases of 50%, 25%, 25% above CNY 200,000'.

Correct position. 汇发〔2014〕2号 art. 6(2): 取消移民财产转移分次汇出的要求 — the bank may remit the approved amount once or in instalments. The staging is abolished.

Why it matters. It changes the cost, the number of wires and the number of receiving-bank fees on a route this repository presents as a main answer.

Sources: CN-16 CN-14

R02 · MEDIUM

edition-d/data/parameters.json emigration.provincial_threshold_cny

Finding. A CNY 500,000 approval threshold is carried as a live parameter.

Evidence. parameters.json emigration.provincial_threshold_cny = 500000, sourced to CN-04 (the 2004 measure).

Correct position. 汇发〔2014〕2号 art. 6(1) abolished the requirement to file amounts above CNY 500,000 with SAFE head office. Local approval now covers the whole amount.

Why it matters. It implies a head-office step that no longer exists.

Sources: CN-17

What was not changed, and why

Editions A through G are other sessions' work on other branches. Editing their files would break their reproducibility checks, which assert that a rebuild is byte-identical. The correct handling is to record the finding here, publish it, and let the owner or a future integration session apply it. The one exception is the root portal's archive list, which is additive navigation and is asserted by Edition D's own verify step — the edit was made in its generator so the build stays green.

What this is. Published-rule research with the arithmetic shown, built from official government, regulator, statute and provider pages retrieved on 9 November 2026. It is not legal, tax or investment advice, and it is not a bank quote. Rates and tariffs change — every source is linked so you can re-check it by hand.
