Rails · C02

Licensed remittance fintech from CNY (Wise and similar)

持牌汇款机构   Conditional

The rule

Wise's own published eligibility gate: sender must not be mainland Chinese, must be working or to have worked in China, and must produce a PRC tax record within three years. The overall limit is computed from after-tax salary earned in China. The destination must be an account you own.

Source: BK-09 BK-10 BK-11

Who can use it

A non-mainland-Chinese person who has worked in China and holds a PRC tax record showing tax paid in the last three years.

Cap or limit

Provider limits: CNY 300,000 per transfer by bank app, CNY 50,000 by Alipay; total limited by the computed salary-based allowance.

Documents

  • Passport or non-mainland ID
  • PRC tax record within three years
  • Proof of the China work history
  • Own overseas account

Pros

  • Transparent all-in quote before you commit
  • Faster than a bank wire in many corridors
  • Handles the conversion and the delivery in one step

Cons

  • Gated on a PRC tax record — useless for an inherited or undeclared balance
  • The allowance is derived from salary, so it cannot exceed what you earned
  • Only personal accounts; business use is not available
  • Fees are dynamic and unpublished until you build a transfer, so no ladder is shown here
No all-in figure is published. At least one component of this route has no official price, so every cell in its ladder would be an estimate. It is left blank and appears on the Flagged for review page instead.

Step by step

  1. Check the three eligibility gates on Wise's own page before doing anything else.
  2. Create the transfer, add your own overseas account as the recipient, and read the quoted fee and rate.
  3. Upload the tax record when asked; verification takes one to two working days.
  4. Fund the transfer and keep the quote screenshot — it is the only record of the price you were given.
