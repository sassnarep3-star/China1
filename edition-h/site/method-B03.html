Business and entity routes · B03

Genuine service or royalty payment to your own offshore entity

服务贸易项下对外支付   Conditional

The rule

Service-trade payments need a genuine, lawful transaction basis. A bank may waive document review below USD 50,000 equivalent per payment, and must review consistency above it; payments above USD 50,000 also require tax filing with the tax authority.

Source: CN-09 BK-01

Who can use it

Someone with a real offshore company providing a real service to a mainland entity.

Cap or limit

No cap, but evidence and tax filing thresholds at USD 50,000 per payment.

Documents

  • Contract
  • Invoice or settlement list
  • Tax filing record (对外支付税务备案) above USD 50,000
  • Evidence the service was actually delivered

Pros

  • Lawful where the service is real
  • Can be recurring

Cons

  • Only the market value of a real service can move — inventing one is D04
  • Transfer pricing and permanent-establishment issues on the US side
  • PRC withholding tax may apply to the payment
  • Professional fees are unpublished
No all-in figure is published. At least one component of this route has no official price, so every cell in its ladder would be an estimate. It is left blank and appears on the Flagged for review page instead.

Step by step

  1. Sign a genuine contract at arm's length.
  2. Deliver the service and invoice it.
  3. Above USD 50,000 per payment, complete the tax filing with the tax authority first.
  4. Present the pack at the bank; remit and keep the file.

All sources for this route: CN-09 BK-01
