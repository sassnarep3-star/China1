B03 · Registered capital reduction returned to a foreign investor

lawful-conditional entitlement

A foreign investor may receive proceeds of a lawful capital reduction after registration/change formalities. The 2024 capital-account guidelines contain a foreign-investor withdrawal-remittance section; detailed current document checklist was not fully extracted and is flagged.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S06 · SAFE Beijing S48 · SAFE

Step by step

  1. Complete the lawful capital-reduction resolution, creditor/publicity and registration steps.
  2. Obtain the bank's current 2024-guideline checklist for foreign-investor withdrawal remittance.
  3. Resolve tax treatment of capital return versus gain.
  4. Submit registration/tax documents and remit to the investor.

Eligibility / conditions

  • Real FIE capital reduction.
  • Company-law and registration steps completed.
  • Tax character of returned capital vs gain resolved.

Pros

  • Returns real invested capital.

Cons

  • Corporate approvals and creditor procedures.
  • Tax characterisation can be complex.
  • Current operational checklist not fully verified here.
