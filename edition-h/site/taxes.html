China and U.S. tax & reporting

Tax follows the income, not the wire. Moving your own principal does not automatically create a new tax, but the original salary, rent, dividend, property sale, gift or FX gain can matter. A foreign tax credit is not an automatic full offset.

Practical U.S. compliance checklist

  1. Reconstruct annual maximum CNY balances and convert for FBAR using the required year-end rate; aggregate accounts exceed USD10,000 triggers FinCEN Form 114.
  2. Check Form 8938 separately using residence, filing status and year-end/anytime thresholds.
  3. Report worldwide income and analyze any personal FX gain using acquisition basis and dates; USD200 is a transaction threshold, not a deduction.
  4. Keep China tax receipts and evaluate Form 1116. Treaty-reduced rates and FEIE interactions require care.
  5. For genuine foreign gifts/bequests, check Form 3520. Do not relabel your own existing balance as a gift.
