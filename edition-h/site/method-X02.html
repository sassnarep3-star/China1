X02 · Underground banking / matched off-book settlement (对敲)

prohibited rejected

Private or disguised FX dealing and unlawful transfer of domestic FX abroad are prohibited. No execution instructions.

S01 · State Council / gov.cn S45 · People's Bank of China

Step by step

  1. No execution instructions: this is not a lawful CNY-to-USD repatriation route for the stated balance.
  2. Use the matching documented personal, property, inheritance or business route and retain official records.

Pros

  • Identifying this option helps avoid confusing a payment app, investment channel or informal scheme with lawful repatriation.

Cons

  • Private or disguised FX dealing and unlawful transfer of domestic FX abroad are prohibited. No execution instructions.
