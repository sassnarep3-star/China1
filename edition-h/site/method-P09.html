P09 · Above-quota genuine current-account expenditure

lawful-conditional entitlement

A domestic individual may purchase FX above the facilitation quota for genuine current-account expenditures with transaction-amount purpose documents. Tuition, medical care and similar real payments are examples; parking savings abroad is not.

S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S35 · STA Tianjin S46 · People's Bank of China S14 · Bank of China S16 · Bank of China

Step by step

  1. Obtain the real invoice/admission/medical or other purpose document showing the amount.
  2. Complete the purchase application and submit purpose documents to the bank.
  3. Pay the actual foreign beneficiary; keep purchase and payment purposes consistent.
  4. Complete any outbound service-payment tax filing if applicable.

Eligibility / conditions

  • Genuine current-account expenditure.
  • Documents prove amount and purpose.
  • Purchase and remittance purposes match.

Pros

  • No absolute USD50k ceiling for documented current-account use.

Cons

  • Not a repatriation route for savings.
  • False invoices or purpose mismatch are prohibited.
  • Tax filing may apply to service payments.
