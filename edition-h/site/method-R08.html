R08 · Western Union China outbound cash pickup

available-conditional rail

WU China partner banks offer outbound cash pickup; bank-account payout is temporarily unavailable. The receiver must collect cash and then convert/deposit it, with separate compliance and fees. No numeric China-outbound fee schedule was verified.

S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S25 · Western Union S30 · U.S. Customs and Border Protection S46 · People's Bank of China

Step by step

  1. Ask a WU partner bank whether your ID, purpose and U.S. corridor are currently accepted.
  2. Establish the legal purchase/remittance basis and obtain a live fee/FX quote.
  3. Send via the bank's online channel or branch and receive the MTCN.
  4. Receiver collects cash with required ID and declares/records it as required.
  5. Receiver deposits funds; reconcile all conversion/deposit fees.

Eligibility / conditions

  • Partner bank accepts your ID and corridor.
  • Underlying FX/remittance basis approved.
  • Receiver can collect cash lawfully.

Pros

  • Cash pickup network.
  • Partner-bank online channels for some residents.

Cons

  • Bank-account payout temporarily unavailable.
  • Cash collection/deposit adds steps and fees.
  • Passport list excludes HK/Macau/TW passports; local restrictions apply.
  • Fees/FX unverified.
