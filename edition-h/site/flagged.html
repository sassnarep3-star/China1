The flag list

H01 · HIGH · unknown fact

The source of the funds is unknown, so no single route can be named as yours

Every route in this analysis is conditional on how the money was earned and documented. Salary, a property sale, an inheritance and undifferentiated savings lead to four different provisions. No personal fact was supplied and none has been assumed.

Sources: CN-08 CN-09 CN-14

H02 · HIGH · unknown fact

Which identity document the bank holds is unknown

A PRC resident identity card or a permanent-residence ID opens the USD 50,000 facilitation quota; a US passport alone does not, and routes you to the documented path instead. This single fact decides between A01 and A02.

Sources: CN-02 CN-07 CN-09

H03 · HIGH · unknown fact

Prior US filing history is unknown

Whether the FBAR and Form 8938 were filed for prior years, and whether any omission was wilful, changes the exposure from zero to 50% of the balance. No figure is asserted for it.

Sources: US-04 US-09

H04 · HIGH · inherited error (Edition D)

Edition D prices a staged emigrant release schedule that was abolished in 2014

Edition D's cost model for M03 applies releases of 50%, 25% and 25% above CNY 200,000 and states the rule on its summary page. That staging came from the 2004 measure. 汇发〔2014〕2号 art. 6(2) abolished the staged-release requirement: after approval the bank may remit once or in instalments. Edition H does not apply the staging and prices A03 as a single remittance, which is what the current text allows. Edition D's underlying figures for other routes were not altered or relied on here.

Sources: CN-16 CN-14

H05 · MEDIUM · stale rate risk

The FX board moves every business day

The board used here was captured on 2026-11-09 at 09:15:45 Beijing time. The board itself states that the rate actually applied at the counter governs, so every FX-derived figure on this site is a point-in-time arithmetic result, not a quote you will receive.

Sources: BK-04

H06 · MEDIUM · named example only

The receiving-bank fee is one bank's published price

USD 15 is Chase's published incoming international wire fee on the business schedule retrieved. Your own bank's inbound fee was not verified and may be zero. The ladder keeps it as a named component so it can be removed.

Sources: BK-08

H07 · MEDIUM · provider terms

Wise's fee is not published as a static schedule

Wise publishes the eligibility gate and the per-transfer ceilings, but the fee is quoted only when a transfer is built. No ladder is shown for C02 because a publicly verifiable static price does not exist.

Sources: BK-09 BK-10

H08 · MEDIUM · unverified component

Card spending and overseas ATM fees are not priced

The card conversion rate embeds a margin that is not published in advance, and cash-withdrawal caps abroad could not be pinned to an operative notice in this session. Route C03 is therefore blank.

Sources: CN-09

H09 · MEDIUM · unverified component

Document, translation, certification and notarisation costs are not priced

The emigrant and inheritance routes need translations and consular certification whose prices depend on the issuer and the country. Only the bank's own certificate of deposit (CNY 20–40) is published and included.

Sources: CN-15 BK-06

H10 · MEDIUM · policy availability

QDII subscription capacity is not verified

Route A08 depends on an institution having open quota. Availability changes and was not verified for any specific institution in this session.

Sources: CN-07

H11 · MEDIUM · scope limit

Business routes are described at the level of the rule, not the checklist

Routes B01–B03 depend on entity-level practice (liquidation audits, tax clearance, transfer pricing) that is not published as a national price list. Their all-in cost is blank rather than estimated.

Sources: CN-04 CN-09

H12 · LOW · deliberate blank

No PRC tax is computed on the principal

Row TAX-CN-4 is blank: no retrieved provision imposes tax on transferring after-tax principal, and a rate is not invented to fill the cell.

Sources: CN-18

H13 · LOW · scope limit

The full-amount-arrival surcharge is optional

USD 15 or USD 25 applies only if you elect full-amount arrival. Declining it does not make the correspondent charge disappear; it moves an unknown deduction into the principal.

Sources: BK-03 BK-07

H14 · LOW · irregularity in an earlier edition

Earlier editions' headline all-in figures differ from Edition H by a few dollars

Edition D reports $83.45 at USD 10,000 and $932.72 at USD 400,000 using a board captured a day earlier plus its own parameter choices. Edition H recomputes from the 2026-11-09 board and includes a certificate-of-deposit component the earlier model omitted, giving $87.94 and $937.34. The difference is traceable to published inputs, not to a dispute about the rule. Both are shown so the reader can see the drift.

Sources: BK-01 BK-04 BK-06

H15 · LOW · scope limit

Cross-border RMB to a Hong Kong account, then convert, was not verified

A commonly described route is to remit RMB to your own Hong Kong account and convert there. The remittance side and the Hong Kong conversion side each need their own verification and neither was retrieved here, so it is not listed as a priced route.

Sources: CN-01
