P06 · Reconversion of unused previously exchanged RMB

lawful-conditional entitlement

An overseas individual may reconvert unused RMB originally obtained by exchanging foreign currency, using the original exchange memo within 24 months. Small same-day amounts have simplified document rules.

S02 · SAFE / PBOC S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S14 · Bank of China S16 · Bank of China

Step by step

  1. Locate the original exchange memo and match the unused RMB amount.
  2. Present passport/ID and memo at the bank within 24 months.
  3. For same-day cumulative amounts within the regulatory small-amount exceptions, ask the bank to apply the ID-only rule.
  4. Reconvert and, if desired, remit under the applicable overseas-person FX remittance rule.

Eligibility / conditions

  • RMB is unused residue of a prior FX-to-CNY exchange.
  • Original exchange memo is available, or the small-amount exception applies.
  • Reconversion is within the 24-month memo validity.

Pros

  • Restores unused conversion without proving new income.
  • Clear regulatory basis.

Cons

  • Only the originally exchanged unused amount.
  • 24-month limit for memo-based reconversion.
  • Does not help long-held CNY savings without an original exchange trail.
