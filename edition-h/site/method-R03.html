R03 · HSBC China Premier / Advance telegraphic transfer

available-conditional rail

HSBC China Premier/Advance personal outward TT commission is 0.1%, min CNY100/max CNY500, plus CNY120 cable. Other institutions' charges and taxes are excluded.

S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S17 · HSBC China S18 · HSBC China S19 · HSBC China S46 · People's Bank of China

Step by step

  1. Confirm HSBC segment and any monthly service fee.
  2. Establish the legal basis and submit documents.
  3. Obtain executable FX quote and TT/full-arrival fee disclosure.
  4. Send to your U.S. account and reconcile credit.

Eligibility / conditions

  • HSBC China account in the relevant segment.
  • Underlying entitlement and AML review passed.

Pros

  • Published segment tariff.
  • Global account visibility if you also bank with HSBC U.S.

Cons

  • Segment eligibility/monthly fees may apply.
  • FX and intermediary charges separate.
  • Advance account opening is restricted to specified customer groups from 2026-03-18.
