R07 · Wise CNY transfer funded from Alipay

available-conditional rail

Same Wise eligibility as R06, but Alipay funding is capped at CNY50k per transfer. No current numeric fee schedule was verified; the November-2024 formula is historical only.

S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S20 · Wise S46 · People's Bank of China

Step by step

  1. Verify Wise eligibility and remaining limit.
  2. Choose Alipay funding and review the live fee/rate.
  3. Authorize within CNY50k and reconcile the USD credit.

Eligibility / conditions

  • Wise from-CNY eligibility.
  • Alipay funding available and linked.
  • Within CNY50k per-transfer and overall limits.

Pros

  • Alternative funding rail for small amounts.

Cons

  • Low per-transfer cap.
  • Current fee unverified.
  • Same legal entitlement limits as bank-funded Wise.
