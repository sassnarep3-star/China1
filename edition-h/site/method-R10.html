R10 · HSBC China debit card overseas ATM cash

available-conditional rail

HSBC China tariff: Premier at HSBC overseas ATMs free; Premier/Advance at UnionPay overseas ATMs CNY20 + 1%, max CNY110 per withdrawal. SAFE cash caps still apply.

S18 · HSBC China S28 · SAFE S30 · U.S. Customs and Border Protection

Step by step

  1. Confirm segment and ATM network fee.
  2. Check SAFE cash headroom.
  3. Withdraw within caps, retain receipts, and deposit/declare as required.

Eligibility / conditions

  • HSBC China debit card and eligible segment.
  • Within SAFE cash caps.

Pros

  • Premier HSBC-ATM fee waiver.
  • Published UnionPay formula.

Cons

  • Cash caps and foreign surcharges.
  • Not a large-balance repatriation rail.
  • Private-banking entity customers excluded.
