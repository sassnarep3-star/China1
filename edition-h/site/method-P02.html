P02 · Emigrant property transfer (移民财产转移)

lawful-conditional entitlement

A natural person who emigrated from mainland China may apply to transfer lawful property owned before obtaining immigrant status. SAFE approval is required; approved funds may be remitted once or in installments to the applicant's own overseas account.

S04 · PBOC / State Council Gazette S05 · SAFE Tianjin S07 · SAFE Hebei S09 · SAFE Tianjin S10 · SAFE Tianjin S11 · SAFE Tianjin S14 · Bank of China S16 · Bank of China

Step by step

  1. Confirm the qualifying status date and make an inventory of property owned before that date.
  2. Obtain foreign passport/ID or permanent-residence evidence, overseas-settlement certification/apostille as applicable, and PRC hukou-cancellation certificate.
  3. Collect source-of-income and property-right documents for each asset, plus competent-tax-office tax certificates or electronic tax verification.
  4. Prepare the written application and Individual Property Transfer Business Application Form; if using an agent, sign the agency agreement with both parties' ID numbers and date.
  5. Apply to the SAFE office of your original household registration; respond to any correction notice.
  6. After approval, take the approval document to the bank and purchase/remit within the approved amount to your own account in your country/region of residence. Do not withdraw the approved FX as cash in China.
  7. Keep the approval, tax certificates and remittance receipts for U.S. reporting.

Eligibility / conditions

  • Foreign permanent residence, foreign citizenship, or HK/Macau/TW resident status.
  • Property was lawfully owned before the qualifying status was obtained.
  • Property is not restricted by judicial/supervisory authorities.
  • Hukou cancellation and overseas-settlement evidence can be produced.

Pros

  • Capital-account route for a whole pre-emigration balance.
  • No USD50k current-account quota in the approval route.
  • Post-2014 rules allow one or multiple remittances within the approved amount.

Cons

  • SAFE approval and extensive evidence.
  • Only pre-status property; later earnings need another basis.
  • Tax clearance and identity/hukou issues can block the file.
  • Bank execution fees and FX are separate.
