R02 · Bank of China foreign-currency demand draft

available-conditional rail

BOC publishes the same 0.1% min50/max260 commission for a draft plus actual postage. A draft is a physical instrument that must be delivered and collected; it is not an instant USD bank credit.

S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S14 · Bank of China

Step by step

  1. Confirm the U.S. bank will collect the BOC draft and ask its fees.
  2. Establish the legal basis and obtain BOC's executable FX quote.
  3. Request draft issuance; pay commission and actual postage.
  4. Securely deliver the draft and present it for collection; reconcile final credit.

Eligibility / conditions

  • Underlying entitlement approved.
  • Draft issuance and destination collection are available.

Pros

  • Published commission identical to TT.

Cons

  • Postage/collection fees and delay.
  • Loss/stop-payment risk.
  • U.S. bank collection policy must be confirmed.
