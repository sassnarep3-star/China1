R05 · HSBC Global Transfer to self-name overseas HSBC account

available-conditional rail

HSBC Global Transfer to a self-name HSBC account in an enabled country/territory is free for Private Banking/Premier; Advance actual charge is USD20 per transaction (CNY145 is only an indicative reference). It moves existing FX between HSBC accounts; CNY must first be lawfully purchased into USD.

S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S18 · HSBC China S19 · HSBC China S46 · People's Bank of China

Step by step

  1. Open/confirm self-name HSBC China and HSBC U.S. accounts and Global Transfer linkage.
  2. Lawfully purchase USD in HSBC China under the applicable entitlement.
  3. Obtain the CNY-to-USD executable rate and any conversion cost disclosure.
  4. Initiate Global Transfer to Self; confirm the per-transfer fee for your segment.
  5. Reconcile the U.S. credit.

Eligibility / conditions

  • Both China and destination HSBC accounts in your name.
  • Global Transfer to Self enabled for the corridor.
  • Underlying CNY-to-USD purchase is lawful.

Pros

  • Fast same-name HSBC rail.
  • Published Advance fee USD20; PB/Premier free.

Cons

  • Does not solve CNY purchase entitlement.
  • FX conversion is a separate transaction.
  • Country/product limits and HSBC eligibility apply.
