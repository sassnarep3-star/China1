A01 · QDII / overseas investment plan

not-repatriation alternative

QDII lets a licensed domestic institution invest domestic funds offshore within quota. It is an investment product, not a right to receive free USD abroad; redemption proceeds return through the product. HSBC scheduled currency-specific holiday subscription suspensions are flagged; redemption and switching remain available under that notice.

S17 · HSBC China S51 · HSBC China

Step by step

  1. Check current product availability and quota with the institution.
  2. Read subscription/redemption and repatriation terms.
  3. Invest only if the goal is exposure, not repatriation.

Eligibility / conditions

  • Product open and quota available.
  • Investor suitability.

Pros

  • Lawful offshore exposure.

Cons

  • Does not deliver spendable USD to your U.S. account.
  • Market risk and product fees.
  • Subscription may be suspended.
