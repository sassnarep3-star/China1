R06 · Wise CNY transfer funded from a Chinese bank app

available-conditional rail

Wise supports eligible non-mainland-Chinese individuals with China work/tax records sending CNY to their own overseas account. Bank-app funding limit is CNY300k per transfer and an overall net-income limit applies. Public quotes were observed but are not locked and do not verify a 40-point current fee schedule.

S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S20 · Wise S21 · Wise S22 · Wise S23 · Wise S24 · Wise S46 · People's Bank of China

Step by step

  1. Complete Wise from-CNY verification with passport/ID and China tax records.
  2. Confirm remaining overall limit and that the recipient is your own account.
  3. Enter the CNY amount and choose bank-app funding; review the live fee and exchange rate.
  4. Fund from your Chinese bank app within the CNY300k per-transfer limit.
  5. Track the transfer and reconcile the USD credit; cancel unfunded transfers you do not intend to execute so they release limit.

Eligibility / conditions

  • Not mainland Chinese; HK/Macau/TW eligible.
  • Worked/work in China and valid tax record within Wise's lookback.
  • Recipient is your own overseas account or Wise balance.
  • Within per-transfer and overall documented-net-income limits.

Pros

  • Own-account delivery.
  • Public fee examples observed.
  • Digital process.

Cons

  • Not a mainland-national quota workaround.
  • CNY300k bank-app per-transfer cap.
  • Overall limit based on taxed net income.
  • Current full fee schedule unverified; historical formulas not used.
