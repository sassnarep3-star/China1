What you have to file, before and after you move anything

The three that matter, in plain terms

FBAR. If the aggregate value of your foreign financial accounts exceeded USD 10,000 at any point in the calendar year, you file FinCEN Form 114. It goes to FinCEN, not the IRS. Due 15 April, with an automatic extension to 15 October. It applies whether or not the money earns anything and whether or not it ever moves.

Form 8938. A separate statement of specified foreign financial assets, filed with your return. The threshold depends on whether you live inside or outside the United States. It does not replace the FBAR and the FBAR does not replace it.

Form 3520 Part IV. Only for a genuine gift or bequest from a foreign person above USD 100,000 in the year. Your own money coming back to you is not that. Report the receipt; there is no tax on the receipt itself in the ordinary case.

The one people get wrong. The 1% remittance transfer excise tax introduced from 1 January 2026 is widely repeated as a cost of getting money out of China. It is not: it applies to money sent from the United States to a foreign recipient, funded with cash or a similar physical instrument. China-to-US is the opposite direction.
Why the order matters. A willful FBAR penalty is the greater of USD 165,353 (inflation-adjusted) or 50% of the account balance at the time of the violation. At a USD 400,000 balance that is USD 200,000 — more than 200 times the entire lawful cost of moving the same money. Fix the filings first; the transfer is cheap by comparison.
