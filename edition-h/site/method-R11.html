R11 · Carry foreign-currency cash out of China and deposit in the U.S.

available-conditional rail

After lawful conversion/withdrawal, cash export is permit-controlled: up to USD5k no permit; USD5k-10k bank carry permit; above USD10k generally not allowed except special cases with SAFE permit. U.S. entry/exit over USD10k requires FinCEN 105. BOC's carry-permit fee is waived through 2026-12-31.

S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S14 · Bank of China S30 · U.S. Customs and Border Protection S53 · SAFE / Tianjin

Step by step

  1. Lawfully purchase/withdraw USD cash within bank and SAFE rules.
  2. Determine permit tier; obtain bank or SAFE carry permit where required.
  3. Declare cash to Chinese customs as required and carry permit/receipts.
  4. Declare to CBP on FinCEN 105 if over USD10k entering/leaving the U.S.
  5. Deposit at the U.S. bank with source documents.

Eligibility / conditions

  • Lawful source and withdrawal.
  • Cash-export permit rules satisfied.
  • U.S. declaration and deposit compliance.

Pros

  • Physical fallback for small amounts.
  • BOC permit fee currently waived.

Cons

  • Above USD10k generally prohibited absent special case.
  • Security, transport, deposit and declaration burden.
  • Not a conversion entitlement.
