Personal legal entitlements

Rights to purchase/remit CNY based on who you are and how the money arose.

Every candidate has its own step-by-step page and 40-row cost grid. Prohibited candidates have risk information but no execution instructions.
CandidatePros & consSources
P01 · Documented current-account RMB income of an overseas individualentitlement · lawful-conditionalPros: Direct legal basis for earned income. No USD50k annual cap in the documented overseas-person income rule. Can use a bank TT or an eligible provider rail.
Cons: Bank authenticity/tax review is substantive. Old income may be hard to evidence. PRC tax residence and U.S. worldwide-income reporting are separate questions. A still-active PRC resident ID can create classification friction.		S02 · SAFE / PBOC S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S31 · State Taxation Administration / Guangdong S46 · People's Bank of China S14 · Bank of China S16 · Bank of China
P02 · Emigrant property transfer (移民财产转移)entitlement · lawful-conditionalPros: Capital-account route for a whole pre-emigration balance. No USD50k current-account quota in the approval route. Post-2014 rules allow one or multiple remittances within the approved amount.
Cons: SAFE approval and extensive evidence. Only pre-status property; later earnings need another basis. Tax clearance and identity/hukou issues can block the file. Bank execution fees and FX are separate.		S04 · PBOC / State Council Gazette S05 · SAFE Tianjin S07 · SAFE Hebei S09 · SAFE Tianjin S10 · SAFE Tianjin S11 · SAFE Tianjin S14 · Bank of China S16 · Bank of China
P03 · Inheritance property transfer (继承财产转移)entitlement · lawful-conditionalPros: Specific capital-route for inherited assets. Combined applications for different decedents are permitted. Approved amount may be remitted once or in installments.
Cons: Inheritance and property-right evidence can be complex. Tax certificate required unless exempt. Not a route for your own non-inherited savings. U.S. Form 3520 may be required for a foreign bequest.		S04 · PBOC / State Council Gazette S05 · SAFE Tianjin S08 · SAFE Hebei S09 · SAFE Tianjin S10 · SAFE Tianjin S11 · SAFE Tianjin S43 · IRS S14 · Bank of China S16 · Bank of China
P04 · Sale of mainland commercial housing by an overseas individualentitlement · lawful-conditionalPros: Express property-sale remittance basis. Can move sale proceeds rather than wait for annual quota.
Cons: Property tax and transaction taxes may be material. Local SAFE/bank practice and document checklists vary. If the sale is part of emigration, choose the correct legal basis rather than double-counting.		S02 · SAFE / PBOC S31 · State Taxation Administration / Guangdong S32 · State Taxation Administration S14 · Bank of China S16 · Bank of China
P05 · Insurance proceeds paid to an overseas beneficiaryentitlement · lawful-conditionalPros: Specific SAFE guidance for a real insurance payout. Uses the current-income purchase framework.
Cons: Not a savings wrapper; the underlying policy must be lawful. Tax character depends on the payout. Surrender values or investment-linked products may not qualify as insurance proceeds.		S12 · SAFE Henan S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S31 · State Taxation Administration / Guangdong S14 · Bank of China S16 · Bank of China
P06 · Reconversion of unused previously exchanged RMBentitlement · lawful-conditionalPros: Restores unused conversion without proving new income. Clear regulatory basis.
Cons: Only the originally exchanged unused amount. 24-month limit for memo-based reconversion. Does not help long-held CNY savings without an original exchange trail.		S02 · SAFE / PBOC S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S14 · Bank of China S16 · Bank of China
P07 · Return of unused originally inbound foreign currencyentitlement · lawful-conditionalPros: No new CNY purchase or new annual purchase quota is needed. Existing bank records can support a straightforward outward remittance.
Cons: Purpose, beneficiary and original source still require bank review. Existing FX does not eliminate cable, correspondent, tax or reporting obligations.		S02 · SAFE / PBOC S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S14 · Bank of China S16 · Bank of China
P08 · Domestic-person USD50k facilitation quota (identity-mismatch case only)entitlement · lawful-conditionalPros: Simplified documentation within the quota.
Cons: Not a general route for a foreign citizen's stuck savings. Cannot be used to buy overseas property/securities as a capital transfer. Identity mismatch can trigger review.		S02 · SAFE / PBOC S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S46 · People's Bank of China S14 · Bank of China S16 · Bank of China
P09 · Above-quota genuine current-account expenditureentitlement · lawful-conditionalPros: No absolute USD50k ceiling for documented current-account use.
Cons: Not a repatriation route for savings. False invoices or purpose mismatch are prohibited. Tax filing may apply to service payments.		S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S35 · STA Tianjin S46 · People's Bank of China S14 · Bank of China S16 · Bank of China
