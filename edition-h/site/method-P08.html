P08 · Domestic-person USD50k facilitation quota (identity-mismatch case only)

lawful-conditional entitlement

The USD50k annual facilitation quota applies to domestic individuals as classified by the bank. A U.S. citizen should not assume this is available merely because a PRC ID remains active; classification must be resolved first. The quota is a current-account convenience tier, not a capital-account savings export right.

S02 · SAFE / PBOC S03 · SAFE / State Council Gazette S46 · People's Bank of China S14 · Bank of China S16 · Bank of China

Step by step

  1. Resolve nationality/PRC-ID classification with the bank and SAFE before using any quota.
  2. If lawfully classified as domestic, complete the personal FX purchase application truthfully for a current-account purpose.
  3. Purchase within the remaining annual quota and remit for the same genuine purpose.
  4. Do not borrow relatives' quotas or split transactions.

Eligibility / conditions

  • Bank lawfully classifies the applicant as a domestic individual.
  • Purchase is for a genuine current-account purpose within the quota.
  • No splitting or false purpose declaration.

Pros

  • Simplified documentation within the quota.

Cons

  • Not a general route for a foreign citizen's stuck savings.
  • Cannot be used to buy overseas property/securities as a capital transfer.
  • Identity mismatch can trigger review.
