Prohibited · D03

Crypto or stablecoin bridge (USDT and similar)

虚拟货币跨境兑换   Prohibited

The rule

Converting fiat currency into virtual currency, and virtual-currency business activity generally, is classified as illegal financial activity and is strictly prohibited. Overseas exchanges serving mainland residents are treated the same way.

Source: CN-20 CN-04

Pros

Cons

  • Classified as illegal financial activity, with the same fine range as other FX evasion

  • US tax treatment of every disposal is separately reportable
  • Counterparty and custody risk are total
