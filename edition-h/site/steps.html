Exactly what happens, in order

No manual input is needed from you to use this page: it is the sequence itself. Work down it once, at the top, and stop at the first step whose condition you cannot meet — that is the answer to which route you can use.

Step 1 · fix the US side first

  1. List every foreign account, with its maximum value in each calendar year you held it.
  2. File or correct the FBAR for each of those years if the aggregate exceeded USD 10,000 at any point.
  3. Check whether Form 8938 applies at your threshold and living situation, and file it with the return.
  4. Do this before moving money. The penalty is for the omission, not the transfer.

Step 3 · make the purchase at the bank

  1. Go to the branch that holds the account and ask for 境外个人购汇 — overseas-individual purchase — rather than anything described as a quota purchase.
  2. Hand over the evidence pack. The bank reviews it; it may refuse, and the guideline expressly permits a bank to refuse a transaction whose authenticity it doubts.
  3. The applied rate is the counter rate. The public board is indicative only and says so itself.
  4. Ask for full-amount arrival if you want a known total. Without it, a correspondent bank may deduct an unpublished amount from the principal instead.

Step 4 · send the wire

  1. The destination must be an account in your own name.
  2. Commission is 0.1% of the CNY equivalent, minimum CNY 50, maximum CNY 260, plus a CNY 150 cable fee outside Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan.
  3. At USD 400,000 that commission is still only CNY 260 — the cap is why the effective rate falls as the amount rises.
  4. Keep the purchase advice, the remittance advice and the arrival statement together. That bundle is the contemporaneous record for both countries.

BK-01 BK-02

Step 5 · if step 3 is refused

  1. Ask the bank to state, in writing, which document or which element of the transaction is not accepted. That is the input to whatever you do next.
  2. If the money is pre-emigration property, switch to route A03 and start the SAFE application.
  3. If it is a property sale, route A05 with SAFE approval.
  4. If it is an inheritance, route A04.
  5. Do not substitute splitting, a private dealer or crypto. Those convert a documentary refusal into a 20%-100% statutory exposure.
