F-01highThe rule set classifies the same person two waysThe FX Regulations define a domestic individual as a PRC citizen or a foreigner continuously resident in China for one year (CN-01 Art. 52(2)). The 2020 Guidelines give a foreign individual a documented purchase route with no annual cap (CN-02 Art. 59) and then add that a foreign permanent-residence ID holder uses the annual quota. A US citizen who has lived in China for years can therefore be treated as domestic at one counter and foreign at another. Which classification the bank applies decides whether the cap is USD 50,000 or none, and the outcome cannot be resolved from the texts alone.CN-01 ↗ CN-02 ↗unverified

Reading the table

  • Options not taken. Every route that an official instrument does not describe is absent, not guessed at. That is why there is no crypto route here: no instrument was retrieved that would let this edition state the rule, so the item is not asserted in either direction (F-18, F-22).
  • Values not stated. The US receiving-bank fee, the §988 gain, the property gain, the Form 3520 penalty amount and the professional fees are all blank. Each has a reason in the table.
  • Items needing a human. F-01, F-04, F-11 and F-12 depend on a fact or a filing that only the account holder or a professional can resolve.
