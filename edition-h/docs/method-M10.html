Wise CNY-to-USD transfer

Wise 人民币汇出

lawful conditional F-08 F-16

A licensed route with published eligibility, and a narrow one. Wise's own help page says a sender from CNY must not be mainland Chinese, or must have worked in China with a China tax record showing tax paid in the last three years; the maximum single transfer is CNY 300,000; and the money can only go to another account you own. The fee is quoted per transaction and is not published, so no figure is used here.

The rule

  • WISE-01: eligibility, CNY 300,000 single-transfer cap, own-account-only payout, tax record requirement
  • CN-05 Art. 34: the 2026 identity-verification threshold applies to the underlying bank transfer

WISE-01 ↗ CN-05 ↗

Cap or limit in the rule text

CNY 300,000 per transfer from the China bank app; an overall limit based on declared China income and tax paid.

Who this is for

A non-mainland-Chinese sender, or someone who has worked in China with a recent China tax record.

Documents the bank or SAFE will want

  • Passport
  • China tax record showing tax paid within three years
  • Proof that the destination account is yours

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Published, checkable eligibility rather than a rumour
  • Mid-market-style pricing is Wise's stated model
  • Handles the conversion and the transfer in one step

Cons

  • The fee is quote-based, so it cannot be put in the ladder (F-08)
  • Own-account-only payout rules out paying a relative
  • The overall limit depends on your China tax history, which may be far below your balance
  • Mainland Chinese citizens cannot use it as CNY senders

Step by step

  1. Check the eligibility conditions on Wise's CNY page against your own status WISE-01 ↗
  2. Obtain the China tax record for the last three years WISE-01 ↗
  3. Verify identity with Wise (1-2 working days) WISE-01 ↗
  4. Send to your own US or Wise account, up to CNY 300,000 per transfer WISE-01 ↗

Cost

No amount ladder is published for this route: no official price exists for the conversion step, or the route is not an entitlement at all. The cells would be invented, so they are left out and the point is flagged.
