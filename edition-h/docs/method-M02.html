M02 · current

Annual facilitation quota, paced over calendar years

个人年度便利化额度（每人每年等值5万美元）

lawful F-02 F-05

The default route and the one most guides describe. Each person has USD 50,000 of settlement and USD 50,000 of purchase a year, usable on ID alone. It is the cheapest route per dollar because the documentation burden is lowest, and it is the slowest because USD 400,000 needs eight calendar years at one person.

The rule

  • CN-02 Art. 54: annual facilitation quota of USD 50,000 for settlement and for domestic-individual purchase
  • CN-02 Art. 55: ID alone within the quota, at the counter or through electronic banking
  • CN-02 Art. 62: splitting to evade the quota is prohibited and leads to the watch list
  • CN-01 Art. 52(2): a foreigner continuously resident in China for one year is a domestic individual

CN-02 ↗ CN-01 ↗ CN-05 ↗

Cap or limit in the rule text

USD 50,000 per person per calendar year for purchase and USD 50,000 for settlement; unused quota does not carry over.

Who this is for

A domestic individual, meaning a PRC citizen or a foreigner continuously resident in China for at least a year. A foreign permanent-residence ID holder also uses the quota.

Documents the bank or SAFE will want

  • Passport or PRC identity card
  • The individual FX purchase application, with a truthful stated purpose
  • No source documents inside the quota, but the bank may still ask

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Lowest documentation burden of any route
  • Can be done online, repeatedly, without an approval file
  • Predictable: the same arithmetic every year

Cons

  • USD 50,000 a year means USD 400,000 takes eight years and eight sets of fees
  • Cannot be used for overseas property or securities purchase, which are capital-account uses
  • Borrowing another person's quota is prohibited and is the single most common way people turn a lawful transfer into a penalised one

Step by step

  1. Open or confirm the bank's online FX purchase and remittance access CN-02 ↗
  2. Each January, purchase up to USD 50,000 and remit it to your own US account CN-02 ↗
  3. Keep the purchase application and the remittance receipt for each year CN-02 ↗
  4. Repeat in later calendar years; unused quota is lost at the year end CN-02 ↗
  5. Never route a family member's quota for your money: Art. 62 makes both sides watch-listed CN-02 ↗
