M05 · capital

Emigration property transfer (移民转移)

移民财产转移

lawful with approval F-01 F-04 F-11

The general capital-account exit for a person who has emigrated. You apply once for the whole amount, then draw it down in three releases over two years. It is the only route in this edition that is designed to move a large capital sum rather than income, and it needs SAFE approval plus a China tax certificate.

The rule

  • CN-03 Art. 2: definition of an emigration transfer
  • CN-03 Art. 3: the property must be your own, lawful and undisputed
  • CN-03 Art. 6: one application, staged releases, RMB 200,000 one-off tier
  • CN-03 Art. 8: documents, including cancelled PRC household registration, proof of settlement abroad, title documents and a tax certificate
  • CN-03 Art. 10: local approval up to RMB 500,000, SAFE head office above
  • CN-03 Art. 11: the bank must remit to your own account abroad and may not pay cash
  • CN-03 Art. 12: refused where the property is legally restricted or its origin cannot be shown

CN-03 ↗ CN-01 ↗ CN-02 ↗ CN-05 ↗

Cap or limit in the rule text

No cap on the amount that may be approved. Release schedule: no more than half first, then up to half the remainder after a year, the rest after two years; up to RMB 200,000 may be released at once. Approval is local up to RMB 500,000 and by SAFE head office above that.

Who this is for

A person who moved from mainland China to a foreign country (or Hong Kong/Macao) and is transferring lawful property owned before acquiring the emigration status.

Documents the bank or SAFE will want

  • Written application explaining the reason and the source of the property
  • The completed applicant form and application form
  • Cancelled PRC household registration certificate and proof of settlement abroad certified by a PRC embassy or consulate
  • Property title documents: real-estate certificate, sale contract, relocation compensation agreement
  • China tax certificate or tax payment certificate from the competent tax authority
  • Notarised authorisation if an agent acts

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Designed for exactly the large-balance problem: no USD 50,000 ceiling
  • Staged release means the FX exposure can be spread over two years
  • Published tariff applies; no black-market premium

Cons

  • You must actually be an emigrant with cancelled PRC household registration; a US passport alone does not prove this file exists
  • SAFE approval, not bank discretion: months of processing, and head-office review above RMB 500,000
  • Disclosure of the full property and tax history, which cuts both ways
  • Three releases mean three sets of wire fees

Step by step

  1. Confirm the household registration was cancelled and obtain the certificate CN-03 ↗
  2. Obtain proof of settlement abroad, certified by a PRC embassy or consulate CN-03 ↗
  3. Obtain the China tax certificate for the property being transferred CN-03 ↗
  4. File one application for the whole amount at the SAFE branch where your household registration was CN-03 ↗
  5. Receive the approval letter and the capital-account approval document CN-03 ↗
  6. Take the approval to the designated bank and remit release one, no more than half CN-03 ↗
  7. Repeat after 12 and 24 months for the remaining releases, re-submitting the prior approval CN-03 ↗
