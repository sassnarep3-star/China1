M06 · capital

Inheritance property transfer (继承转移)

继承财产转移

lawful with approval F-12

The capital-account route that is written for a foreign citizen: an heir who is a foreign national (or a Hong Kong/Macao resident) applies once to transfer a lawfully inherited PRC estate. If the money in the account came from an inheritance, this is the route the rule text names.

The rule

  • CN-03 Art. 2: definition of an inheritance transfer, expressly for a foreign citizen or Hong Kong/Macao resident
  • CN-03 Art. 6: one application, one or several remittances
  • CN-03 Art. 9: documents, including proof of inheritance and the deceased's property and tax documents
  • CN-03 Art. 10: approval thresholds
  • CN-03 Art. 11: remittance to your own account abroad, no cash

CN-03 ↗ CN-01 ↗ CN-02 ↗

Cap or limit in the rule text

No cap on the amount. One application per deceased person; may be paid in one or several instalments. Approval is local up to RMB 500,000 and by SAFE head office above that.

Who this is for

A foreign citizen or Hong Kong/Macao resident who has lawfully inherited property situated in mainland China.

Documents the bank or SAFE will want

  • Written application describing the inheritance and the deceased's property
  • Application form
  • Passport and proof of residence abroad certified by a PRC embassy or consulate
  • Proof of inheritance: will, notarised succession certificate or court document, notarised if not already notarised
  • The deceased's title documents and China tax certificate
  • Notarised authorisation if an agent acts

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Written around a foreign heir, which matches a US citizen's position
  • No annual cap and no staged-release schedule in the text
  • The tax certificate is of the deceased's property, which may show a low or nil gain

Cons

  • Requires a completed succession with notarised documents; this is the slowest step
  • A dispute among heirs stops the application (Art. 12)
  • Only the inherited estate qualifies, not other money in the same account

Step by step

  1. Complete the succession and obtain notarised proof of inheritance CN-03 ↗
  2. Obtain the deceased's property title documents and the China tax certificate CN-03 ↗
  3. Apply once at the SAFE branch where the deceased was registered CN-03 ↗
  4. Remit against the approval, in one or several instalments, to your own account abroad CN-03 ↗
