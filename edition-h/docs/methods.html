How routes were counted

Earlier editions in this repository publish 22, 33, 35, 16, 31 and 43 routes, because each counted a different grain: instruments, services, service variants or documents. This edition counts a route as one legal characterisation of the money plus one exit mechanism. Variants that change only which bank performs the same act are not separate routes; a route that depends on a fact you may not have is a separate route, because it changes your answer.

What this is. Published-rule research with the arithmetic shown, compiled 2026-11-09 from 15 official and provider-primary documents. Not legal, tax or investment advice, and not a bank quote. Rates and tariffs change; every source is linked for manual review.

Values that could not be confirmed are blank and published on the Flagged for review page. No value is inferred.
