Recommendation

If the money is documented current-account income — salary, personal services, dividends already taxed — use route M01: ask the bank to purchase FX against the source documents under Art. 59 rather than against the quota, and wire it to your own US account. At USD 400,000 the China-side cost is $902.86, which is 0.226%.

If the money is an inheritance from a PRC relative, use route M06, the inheritance transfer: one SAFE application, a notarised succession, the deceased's tax certificate, and no capped schedule.

If you emigrated from mainland China and the property predates that, use route M05, the emigration transfer: a single application with three releases over two years, local approval up to RMB 500,000 and SAFE head office above it.

If none of those facts can be documented, the honest answer is the quota, M02: USD 50,000 a year, USD 400,000 in eight years, and statutory exposure for anyone who tries to shortcut it.

Limits of this summary

  • US receiving-bank fees, notarisation, translation and professional fees are not in any total (F-07, F-25).
