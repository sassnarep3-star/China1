M15 · prohibited

Underground bank, mirror trade or private FX dealer

地下钱庄、对敲、私自买卖外汇

prohibited F-21

Private or disguised FX dealing is named in Art. 45: warning, confiscation of illegal gains, a fine of up to 30% of the amount, 30%-100% in a serious case, and criminal liability where the threshold is met. The quoted 'discount' is a price for legal risk, not a market rate.

The rule

  • CN-01 Art. 45: private, disguised or brokered FX dealing is penalised
  • CN-01 Art. 40: fraud and illegal arbitrage

Not applicable.

Documents the bank or SAFE will want

  • None: no procedure is published here

Pros and cons

Pros

  • A quoted rate that appears better than the bank's

Cons

  • Confiscation of the money involved, not just a fine
  • Criminal exposure
  • The counterparty risk is unsecured: no court will enforce the deal
