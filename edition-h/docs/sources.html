CN-01
A		State Council of the PRC
Regulations of the PRC on Foreign Exchange Administration (State Council Decree No. 532)
中华人民共和国外汇管理条例（国务院令第532号）
  • Art. 5: the State does not restrict current international payments and transfers
  • Art. 12: current-account FX transactions must have a real, lawful transaction basis
  • Art. 17: registration required for outbound direct investment / securities by domestic entities and individuals
  • Art. 22: capital-account FX payments need approval where required; a lawfully terminated FIE's foreign investor may purchase FX and remit after liquidation and tax
  • Art. 39: evasion of FX control - fine up to 30% of the amount, 30%-100% if serious
  • Art. 40: illegal arbitrage / fraudulent purchase of FX - fine up to 30%, 30%-100% if serious
  • Art. 42: illegal carriage of FX across the border - warning, fine up to 20% of the amount
  • Art. 45: private or disguised FX dealing - warning, confiscation of illegal gains, fine up to 30%, 30%-100% if serious
  • Art. 52: definition of 境内个人 (domestic individual) - PRC citizens and foreigners continuously resident in China for one year

Full text read on gov.cn; articles quoted verbatim above were checked in the page body.

CN-02
A		State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), printed in the State Council Gazette
Guidelines for Current-Account Foreign Exchange Business (2020 edition), Hui Fa [2020] No. 14
经常项目外汇业务指引（2020年版）（汇发〔2020〕14号）
  • Art. 49: service-trade payments above USD 50,000 need tax filing verification
  • Art. 54: individual settlement and domestic-individual FX purchase both carry an annual facilitation quota of USD 50,000 each
  • Art. 55: within the quota, ID alone is enough; counter or electronic channel
  • Art. 56: above the quota, a domestic individual may settle or purchase against ID plus source-of-funds / use-of-funds documents
  • Art. 57: the individual FX purchase application form and the individual's legal responsibility for it
  • Art. 58: a foreign individual settles FX against ID plus use documents; single settlements above USD 50,000 must be paid straight to the counterparty's PRC RMB account
  • Art. 59: a foreign individual purchasing FX against lawful current-account RMB income in China uses ID plus source documents including tax vouchers; a foreign permanent-residence ID holder uses the annual facilitation quota
  • Art. 61: close relatives may act for the individual within the quota, with authorisation and relationship evidence
  • Art. 62: splitting to evade the quota is prohibited; SAFE runs a watch list, with the listing lasting the current year plus two more
  • Art. 64: remitting FX already in an account - up to USD 50,000/day on ID alone, above that with supporting documents
  • Art. 66: individual cross-border e-commerce and market-purchase settlement does not consume the facilitation quota when transaction evidence is provided
  • Art. 67: transfers between accounts are limited to your own accounts and those of close relatives
  • Art. 86: cash withdrawal up to USD 10,000/day on ID
  • Art. 88: cash settlement rules, USD 10,000/day threshold
  • Art. 89: carrying cash out - USD 5,000 to USD 10,000 needs a bank-issued carriage permit; above USD 10,000 only a SAFE permit, and only for war-risk or similarly restricted destinations

Articles 54-67, 86-89 were read verbatim in the gazette text. This instrument is the operative current-account rule set and supersedes earlier inconsistent notices.

CN-03
A		People's Bank of China (SAFE portal)
PBOC Announcement [2004] No. 16 - Interim Measures for the Administration of the Sale and Payment of Foreign Exchange for the Transfer of Personal Property Abroad
中国人民银行公告[2004]第16号 个人财产对外转移售付汇管理暂行办法
  • Art. 2: personal property transfer abroad means emigration transfer (移民转移) or inheritance transfer (继承转移); an inheritance transfer is made by a foreign citizen or a Hong Kong/Macao resident who has lawfully inherited a PRC estate
  • Art. 3: the property must be the applicant's own lawful property and undisputed
  • Art. 6: the emigration transfer must be applied for once for the whole amount and paid out in stages - first no more than half, after one year no more than half of the remainder, after two years the rest; amounts up to RMB 200,000 may be paid in one go
  • Art. 6: an inheritance transfer is applied for once and may be paid in one or several instalments
  • Art. 8: documents for an emigration transfer, including cancelled PRC household registration and proof of settlement abroad, property title documents and a tax payment certificate
  • Art. 9: documents for an inheritance transfer, including proof of inheritance and the deceased's tax certificate
  • Art. 10: totals up to RMB 500,000 are approved by the local SAFE branch, above that by SAFE head office
  • Art. 11: the bank must remit directly to the applicant's own account abroad and may not hand out cash
  • Art. 12: applications are refused where a court or supervisory restriction applies, where the case is pending, where the property may not be transferred by law, or where lawful origin cannot be shown
  • Art. 13: false documents or repeat applications for the same property are punished under Art. 40 of the FX Regulations

Full text read on the SAFE portal. The page carries no repeal notice; no later instrument replacing it was retrieved this session, so its current status is treated as in force but is flagged for confirmation (F-04).

CN-04
A		State Taxation Administration of China
Individual Income Tax Law of the PRC (as amended 2018), official English translation
中华人民共和国个人所得税法
  • Art. 1: a resident individual (domiciled, or resident 183+ days in a tax year) is taxed on income from within China and overseas; a non-resident is taxed on China-source income
  • Art. 2: nine categories of taxable income - wages; personal service; author's remuneration; royalties; business operation; interest, dividends and bonuses; leasing of assets; transfer of assets; incidental income. Converting your own money is not one of them
  • Art. 3(3): a flat 20% rate applies to interest, dividends and bonuses, leasing of assets, transfer of assets and incidental income
  • Art. 4: exempt income list

English text read on the STA site; articles 1-4 quoted.

中国人民银行 国家金融监督管理总局 中国证券监督管理委员会令〔2025〕第11号（金融机构客户尽职调查和客户身份资料及交易记录保存管理办法）

Guide to CNY transfers (Wise Help Centre)
  • Sending from CNY is available to senders who are not mainland Chinese, or who have worked in China, with a China tax record showing tax paid in the last three years

US-01
A		U.S. Congress / GPO (govinfo)
26 U.S.C. section 988 - Treatment of certain foreign currency transactions
  • section 988(c)(1)(C): disposing of nonfunctional currency is a section 988 transaction
  • section 988(e)(2): on a personal transaction by an individual, no gain is recognized by reason of exchange-rate change after acquisition, but that exclusion does not apply if the gain would exceed USD 200
  • section 988(e)(3): personal transaction means a transaction by an individual other than one whose expenses meet section 162 or section 212
  • section 988(a)(1): foreign currency gain or loss is ordinary income or loss

Statutory text read. The USD 200 figure is a cliff, not an allowance: exceed it on a transaction and the whole gain is recognized. The page read is the 2018 edition; the provision wording has not changed.

US-03
A		IRS
Comparison of Form 8938 and FBAR requirements
  • Form 8938 thresholds for a specified individual living in the United States: more than USD 50,000 on the last day of the year or more than USD 75,000 at any time (single / married filing separately); USD 100,000 / USD 150,000 for married filing jointly
  • Form 8938 thresholds for a specified individual living outside the United States: USD 200,000 / USD 300,000 single; USD 400,000 / USD 600,000 joint
  • Form 8938 penalty: up to USD 10,000, plus USD 10,000 for each 30 days of non-filing after notice, maximum USD 60,000
  • FBAR threshold: aggregate value of financial accounts exceeds USD 10,000 at any time in the calendar year
  • FBAR civil penalty: for assessments before 1 August 2016, non-willful up to USD 10,000; willful up to the greater of USD 100,000 or 50 percent of account balances
