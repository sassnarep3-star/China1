The conversion penalty, decomposed

Where every cent goes.

The "penalty" is not a tax. It is four published charges, one dated exchange rate, and a compliance exposure that only appears if a form is missing. Here is each layer, with the number and the source.

The layers

FX marginYou buy USD at the bank's selling rate while the fair midpoint is lower. For one conversion the loss is (ask − mid) / mid.0.2104%CN-07 ↗

One layer is deliberately absent from the table: the US receiving bank's inbound fee. No official schedule for a personal account was retrieved, so it is blank rather than estimated (F-07).

The arithmetic, in full

CNY needed to buy USD A = A × 669.19 / 100.
FX margin = A × (669.19 − 667.7850000000001) / 667.7850000000001 = A × 0.2104%.
Commission = min(max(0.001 × CNY, 50), 260) CNY, converted back at 669.19.
Cable = 150 CNY × number of remittances, converted back at 669.19.
China-side total = FX margin + commission + cable.
Share = total / A.
