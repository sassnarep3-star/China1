M03 · current

Remit USD already held in your own FX account

外汇账户内外汇汇出境外

lawful F-03

If the balance has already been converted, this is the cheapest step available: no second conversion, so no FX margin, only the wire tariff. Art. 64 allows up to USD 50,000 a day on ID alone.

The rule

  • CN-02 Art. 64(1): FX in an account remitted abroad up to USD 50,000 a day on ID; above that with documents
  • CN-02 Art. 65: the same for a foreign individual
  • CN-01 Art. 5: current international payments and transfers are not restricted

CN-02 ↗ CN-01 ↗ CN-05 ↗

Cap or limit in the rule text

USD 50,000 per day on ID; above that, transaction documents are required. No annual cap stated for this step.

Who this is for

Anyone holding FX in a Chinese bank account.

Documents the bank or SAFE will want

  • Passport or ID
  • Above USD 50,000 a day: documents evidencing the transaction

Pros and cons

Pros

  • No conversion spread: the FX margin is already sunk
  • Daily limit is high relative to the quota
  • Works for someone who converted onshore years ago and now wants the money out

Cons

  • Only helps if the money is already USD; the typical stuck balance is CNY
  • The bank still verifies identity and may ask about the origin of the FX
  • If the FX came from a capital-account conversion, the account's own use restrictions may still bind

Step by step

  1. Confirm the account is an FX account holding USD, not CNY CN-02 ↗
  2. Remit up to USD 50,000 a day in your own name CN-02 ↗
  3. For larger daily amounts, supply the documents behind the transaction CN-02 ↗
  4. Expect the identity-verification step under the 2026 AML order CN-05 ↗
