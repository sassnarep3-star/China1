M11 · alternatives

Spend from the CNY balance abroad on a UnionPay card

银联卡境外消费

unverified F-17

Spending the balance directly avoids a remittance, but it is consumption, not conversion: it pays for goods and services and cannot deliver USD to your US account. No official rule retrieved this session states whether card spending abroad consumes the facilitation quota, or what the card's FX markup is. Both points are flagged, and no figure is published.

The rule

  • CN-02 Art. 76 lists card-related settlement cases that are not entered in the individual FX system; it does not settle the outbound-spending question

Cap or limit in the rule text

Who this is for

A cardholder spending abroad.

Documents the bank or SAFE will want

  • Card and ID

Pros and cons

Pros

  • No remittance step
  • Useful for living costs while in China or travelling

Cons

  • Does not put USD in your account
  • Quota treatment and card FX markup not verified (F-17)
  • No published price, so no cell in the ladder

Step by step

  1. Ask the issuing bank in writing whether overseas card spending consumes the annual quota CN-02 ↗
  2. Ask for the card's FX conversion and any cross-border fee in writing CN-02 ↗

Cost

No amount ladder is published for this route: no official price exists for the conversion step, or the route is not an entitlement at all. The cells would be invented, so they are left out and the point is flagged.
