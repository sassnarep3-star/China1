M07 · capital

Sale of PRC real property: tax first, and no automatic exit

境内房产转让

lawful conditional F-13

Selling a Chinese property does not itself move money out. It converts property into RMB, and it creates a taxable gain taxed at a flat 20%. The proceeds then have to leave through a current-account route (only if the gain is current-account income, which a property gain is not) or through emigration or inheritance transfer. This edition prices the tax, not the exit.

The rule

  • CN-04 Art. 2(8): income from transfer of assets is taxable income
  • CN-04 Art. 3(3): a flat 20% rate applies to income from transfer of assets
  • CN-01 Art. 17: outbound investment by individuals requires registration; proceeds of a property sale are a capital-account item

CN-04 ↗ CN-01 ↗ CN-03 ↗

Cap or limit in the rule text

20% flat on the gain from transfer of assets; the principal is not taxed.

Who this is for

An owner of PRC real property who is tax resident or non-resident; the sourcing and collection rules apply at the transaction.

Documents the bank or SAFE will want

  • Sale contract and title
  • Tax payment certificate for the transfer
  • Evidence of original cost for the gain computation

Pros and cons

Pros

  • The tax is on the gain, not the price
  • A tax certificate is the document every later route asks for

Cons

  • Selling does not give you a route out; the money stays onshore
  • The gain is unknown here, so no tax figure can be published (F-13)
  • The proceeds are capital-account money, so the USD 50,000 quota cannot be used to buy FX with them

Step by step

  1. Complete the transfer and settle the 20% tax on the gain CN-04 ↗
  2. Keep the tax certificate: it is required by the emigration and inheritance routes CN-03 ↗
  3. Choose an exit route that matches the money's character, not the quota CN-01 ↗

Cost

No amount ladder is published for this route: no official price exists for the conversion step, or the route is not an entitlement at all. The cells would be invented, so they are left out and the point is flagged.
