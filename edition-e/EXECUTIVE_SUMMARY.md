## Answer

The reviewed rules do not impose a tax merely on converting or remitting your own principal. The hard problem is proving the lawful source and using the correct current/capital channel. ## Important findings

1. **Do not start with the USD 50,000 slogan.** For a qualifying overseas individual, documented lawful mainland current-account RMB income follows Article 59 rather than a generic USD 50,000 exit cap. Classification and bank review still control. [S02](https://www.safe.gov.cn/shenzhen/file/file/20201204/2b3bb1ba2c18435fb80ff6d521410ca4.pdf), [S03](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0713/3088.html).
2. **Capital savings need a real capital route.** Emigrant transfer, inheritance and actual property/corporate transactions are narrow. A US passport alone does not create an emigrant-transfer entitlement. [S06](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0318/3017.html), [S08](https://www.safe.gov.cn/tianjin/2026/0319/3019.html).
3. **Current provider tariffs are retrievable, but not all-in.** BOC personal TT is 0.1% capped at CNY 260 plus CNY 150 international cable; SC electronic OUR components vary by tier. FX execution and receiving charges remain unknown. [S15](https://pic.bankofchina.com/bocappd/pricelist/202511/P020251110646838136627.pdf), [S18](https://av.sc.com/cn/content/docs/cn-cb-tariff-zh.pdf).
4. **Closed-loop investment is not US repatriation.** WMC proceeds return to the mainland remittance account; QDII is a product/custody channel; Stock Connect is a trading link. [S27](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/key-functions/international-financial-centre/wealth-management-connect/southbound-scheme/), [S39](http://m.safe.gov.cn/safe/file/file/20170726/8b7131f7c4254724856c41c7f80cacd5.pdf).
5. **US reporting can matter more than conversion fees.** FBAR, Form 8938, Form 3520 and worldwide income rules have separate triggers. The treaty saving clause preserves US citizen taxation. [S30](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/us-citizens-and-resident-aliens-abroad), [S33](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/report-of-foreign-bank-and-financial-accounts-fbar).
6. **Prohibited shortcuts are not cost options.** Splitting, underground dealing and the 2026 crypto/RWA notice are excluded. No operational guidance is given. [S38](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/2026020619591971323/index.html).
