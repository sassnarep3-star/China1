Executive summary

The reviewed rules do not impose a tax merely on converting or remitting your own principal. The hard problem is proving the lawful source and using the correct current/capital channel. Commercial FX spread and tariffs can be shown only as conditional arithmetic; personal tax, executable FX and receiving fees remain blank.

  1. Do not start with the USD 50,000 slogan. For a qualifying overseas individual, documented lawful mainland current-account RMB income follows Article 59 rather than a generic USD 50,000 exit cap. Classification and bank review still control. S02 S03
  2. Capital savings need a real capital route. Emigrant transfer, inheritance and actual property/corporate transactions are narrow. A US passport alone does not create an emigrant-transfer entitlement. S06 S07 S08 S11
  3. Current provider tariffs are retrievable, but not all-in. BOC personal TT is 0.1% capped at CNY 260 plus CNY 150 international cable; SC electronic OUR components vary by tier. FX execution and receiving charges remain unknown. S15 S18
  4. Closed-loop investment is not US repatriation. WMC proceeds return to the mainland remittance account; QDII is a product/custody channel; Stock Connect is a trading link. S27 S39 S40
  5. US reporting can matter more than conversion fees. FBAR, Form 8938, Form 3520 and worldwide income rules have separate triggers. The treaty saving clause preserves US citizen taxation. S30 S33 S34 S35
  6. Prohibited shortcuts are not cost options. Splitting, underground dealing and the 2026 crypto/RWA notice are excluded. No operational guidance is given. S01 S02 S38
