Decision fork

1 · Documented current-account income?

Start with Article 59 source/tax evidence and the bank’s classification. Earned routes.

2 · Real capital transaction?

Emigration, inheritance, property sale or corporate liquidation must be genuine. Capital routes.

3 · Investment or cash/card?

Closed-loop investment is not US repatriation; cash/ATM limits are narrow. Investment · Cash/cards.

Important findings

Do not start with the USD 50,000 slogan

For a qualifying overseas individual, documented lawful mainland current-account RMB income follows Article 59 rather than a generic USD 50,000 exit cap. Classification and bank review still control.

S02 S03

Capital savings need a real capital route

Emigrant transfer, inheritance and actual property/corporate transactions are narrow. A US passport alone does not create an emigrant-transfer entitlement.

S06 S07 S08 S11

Current provider tariffs are retrievable, but not all-in

BOC personal TT is 0.1% capped at CNY 260 plus CNY 150 international cable; SC electronic OUR components vary by tier. FX execution and receiving charges remain unknown.

S15 S18

Closed-loop investment is not US repatriation

WMC proceeds return to the mainland remittance account; QDII is a product/custody channel; Stock Connect is a trading link.

S27 S39 S40

US reporting can matter more than conversion fees

FBAR, Form 8938, Form 3520 and worldwide income rules have separate triggers. The treaty saving clause preserves US citizen taxation.

S30 S33 S34 S35

Prohibited shortcuts are not cost options

Splitting, underground dealing and the 2026 crypto/RWA notice are excluded. No operational guidance is given.

S01 S02 S38

Conditional BOC reference rows

One international TT, base sender tariff only; optional USD full-arrival surcharge and receiving charges excluded. Full forty-step grids.
