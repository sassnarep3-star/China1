R01 · BOC documented salary / current-account purchase and wire

conditional

Who: Overseas individual with lawful mainland current-account RMB income

Mechanism: Buy FX against ID and transaction-value source materials, then remit through BOC.

Limit: No stated annual ceiling in Article 59 for qualifying overseas-individual current-account income; bank authenticity review applies.

Documents: ID; employment/source documents; tax evidence unless nontaxable; payment purpose; beneficiary details.

  • Direct documented path
  • Current personal tariff retrieved

Cons

  • Bank may require more documents
  • FX quote and receiving fees unknown

China tax: Income tax attaches to the underlying income, not to moving already-taxed principal. Personal tax is blank.

US tax: US worldwide income/reporting may apply; moving principal is not a second income event by itself. Personal tax is blank.
