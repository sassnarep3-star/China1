R09 · Nonresident house-sale proceeds

conditional

Who: Nonresident seller of mainland property with completed transfer/tax steps

Mechanism: Remit documented sale proceeds after transfer registration and tax steps.

Limit: Transaction-specific; no generic savings limit. External-payment filing described above USD 50,000 where covered.

Documents: ID; transfer contract/registration; tax voucher; filing form where required; agent POA if used.

Pros

  • Government-hosted sale FAQ
  • Established documentary path

Cons

  • Only actual sale proceeds
  • Local practice and inherited cases need confirmation

China tax: Property-transfer gain may be taxed; personal tax blank.

US tax: US gain/reporting rules separate; personal tax blank.

approval

Who: Person with qualifying pre-emigration mainland property and former household registration

Mechanism: Apply to former-hukou SAFE office; after approval bank remits directly to applicant’s own overseas account.

Limit: Within approval; 2014 circular allows lump sum or batches. Old staged schedule not confirmed current.

  • Express capital route

  • Documents and local office control
  • Old staged-release claims conflicted

China tax: Underlying asset taxes may apply; transfer itself is not a generic income tax. Personal tax blank.

US tax: US gain/reporting rules separate; personal tax blank.

R11 · Inheritance property transfer

approval

Who: Qualifying foreign/HK/Macao/Taiwan heir of mainland estate

Mechanism: Apply at decedent’s former-hukou SAFE office; bank remits directly to applicant’s own overseas account.

Limit: Within approval; different decedents may be combined at one competent office under the 2026 FAQ.

Documents: ID; inheritance proof; decedent title/source; tax evidence; application; agent POA if used.

Pros

  • Specific inheritance route
  • 2026 filing-office FAQ retrieved

Cons

  • Requires qualifying inheritance
  • Not a gift or savings workaround

China tax: No blanket inheritance-tax rate is asserted; underlying asset taxes may apply. Personal tax blank.

US tax: Foreign inheritance above USD 100,000 may require Form 3520; income later produced is separate. Personal tax blank.
