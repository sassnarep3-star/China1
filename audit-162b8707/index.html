5. How this was checked

The pages above were opened from this session on 2026-11-12 and the quoted text recorded in guide/data/sources.json with a read date. guide/build/verify.py re-runs the build, fails if the committed pages differ from a fresh build, requires an official link on every table row, requires a Flagged link on every blank cell, requires the board and Wise figures to appear verbatim in the quoted source text, and recomputes all 370 cost totals with exact fractions. It cannot tell whether a cited page still supports its quote — that is what sections 1 and 2 above record, page by page.

The full working record, including the twenty-one sources not reopened, is in REVIEW.md and at github.com/sassnarep3-star/China1/audit-162b8707.
