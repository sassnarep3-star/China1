Step by step

Exactly what to do for each lawful route, in order. Each step links to the rule or price list behind it.

Before any route: the money must have a lawful, documented source and the tax on it must be paid. Bring originals. Do not split one amount into several to avoid review. SAFE Q&A 2026Individual FX Measures

M01 · Bank of China: convert and wire to your own US account Lawful

Who: Foreign passport holder whose CNY is lawful China current-account income (for example salary) and who can show the amount and the tax paid.

StepWhat to doOfficial source
1Collect proof that the CNY is lawful China income, showing amounts: employment contract or payslips and your individual income tax payment record.SAFE Q&A 2026Individual FX Rules
2Take your passport and the documents to the bank that holds the account.Individual FX Measures
3For a single payment above USD 50,000, ask the bank whether it needs a tax-filing form; if so, file it with the local tax office first.STA/SAFE Ann. 40
4Ask the bank to convert the CNY to USD at its telegraphic selling rate. The board rate is a reference; the rate at the moment of the transaction applies.BOC rate board
5Order a SWIFT wire to your US account and choose full-amount delivery so correspondent charges are a fixed USD 25 instead of a deduction.BOC tariff
6Pay the commission (1‰, minimum CNY 50, maximum CNY 260) and the CNY 150 cable fee.BOC tariff
7Send the whole documented amount in one wire. Do not split it into smaller pieces to avoid review.Individual FX Measures
8When the wire arrives your US bank may charge an incoming-wire fee (Chase: USD 15).Chase fees
9Keep the conversion slip and wire receipt, and file the FBAR and Form 8938 if the account passed the thresholds during the year.IRS 8938 vs FBAR

M02 · HSBC China (Premier or Advance): convert and wire Lawful

Who: Same legal basis as M01, for customers of HSBC China. Premier needs an average balance of CNY 500,000.

StepWhat to doOfficial source
1Prepare the same evidence as for M01: passport, proof of the income amount and tax vouchers.SAFE Q&A 2026
2Ask for the bank's selling rate before you confirm and compare it with the Bank of China board.BOC rate board
3Order the outward telegraphic transfer; pay 0.1% (minimum CNY 100, maximum CNY 500) plus CNY 120 cable.HSBC China tariff
4If you want the full USD amount delivered, the bank pre-collects CNY 200 toward overseas agent charges and bills any shortfall later.HSBC China tariff
5Complete the US filings as in M01.IRS 8938 vs FBAR

M15 · Convert back CNY you originally exchanged from foreign currency Lawful with the exchange memo

Who: Foreign individual holding unused CNY that was obtained by exchanging foreign currency in China.

StepWhat to doOfficial source
1Find the original exchange memo (兑换水单) from when you turned foreign currency into CNY; it is valid for converting back for 24 months from the exchange date.Individual FX Rules
2Take your passport and the memo to the bank and convert the unused CNY back to foreign currency.Individual FX Rules
3Wire the USD to your own account as in M01.BOC tariff

M05 · House-sale proceeds Lawful with documents

Who: Foreign individual who sold a home on the mainland.

StepWhat to doOfficial source
1Pay the tax on the sale and obtain the tax voucher. Gain is taxed at 20% of proceeds less original value and reasonable expenses.IIT Law
2Bring passport, the house transfer contract and registration certificate, and the tax voucher.Shanghai gov FAQ
3If the amount exceeds USD 50,000, file the tax-filing form for external payments.Shanghai gov FAQSTA/SAFE Ann. 40
4The bank (with SAFE approval where required) converts and remits the proceeds.Individual FX Measures
5In the US, report the sale on your return and claim a credit for the China tax.IRS citizens abroadIRS foreign tax credit

M06 · Emigrant property transfer (移民财产转移) Lawful — approval needed

Who: A person who emigrated from China and transfers property lawfully owned before emigrating.

StepWhat to doOfficial source
1Apply to the SAFE branch of your former household registration.SAFE 2014 No. 2
2Submit proof of lawful source and tax certificates or tax-payment vouchers.PBOC Ann. 2004-16
3After approval the bank may remit the approved amount in one or several wires.SAFE 2014 No. 2
4Funds go directly to your own account abroad; foreign cash may not be withdrawn in China.PBOC Ann. 2004-16

M10 · Wise (from a China bank app or Alipay) Lawful — if eligible

Who: Non-mainland-Chinese nationals who work or worked in China and have a China tax record from the last 3 years.

StepWhat to doOfficial source
