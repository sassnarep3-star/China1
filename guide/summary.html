Executive summary

You are a US citizen with CNY in a mainland bank account. This page gives the answer first, then the numbers, then the risks.

Answer

  1. There is no separate conversion tax in the rules read. What people call the "conversion penalty" is made of: tax on the underlying income if it was never taxed, bank charges, fines if a prohibited route is used, and US filing penalties if reports are missed. IIT LawFX Regulation31 CFR 1010.821
  2. Use the documented bank route. As a foreign passport holder you may buy foreign exchange with lawful China current-account income on showing your ID and proof of the amount including tax vouchers. No fixed cap is stated. SAFE Q&A 2026Individual FX Rules
  3. The USD 50,000 yearly quota is not your limit. It applies to domestic individuals and to holders of the Foreign Permanent Resident ID Card. Individual FX RulesSAFE Q&A 2024
  4. Send it in one wire. Bank of China's commission is capped at CNY 260 and the cable fee is fixed, so one large wire costs 0.23% at $400,000 against 0.93% at $10,000. Splitting to avoid review is prohibited. BOC tariffIndividual FX Measures
  5. Match the route to the money. Property-sale proceeds, inheritance and pre-emigration assets each have their own route and papers. Shanghai gov FAQPBOC Ann. 2004-16SAFE 2014 No. 2
  6. Keep the US side clean. Report worldwide income in the year earned, consider a foreign tax credit if eligible, and check FBAR reporting if the aggregate foreign-account balance exceeded $10,000. IRS citizens abroadIRS foreign tax creditIRS 8938 vs FBAR
