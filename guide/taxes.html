Taxes and penalties

The "conversion penalty" is not one charge. The parts below depend on the transaction and personal facts; amounts cannot be assumed to be zero.

In one paragraph

Converting and wiring is not itself taxed in the rules read. China taxes the income (salary 3%–45%, gains and investment income 20%) and the bank wants proof that this was paid. The US generally taxes citizens on worldwide income; a foreign tax credit may be available subject to conditions and limits. Currency conversion may separately produce a US exchange gain or loss. What can go wrong: income never taxed, a prohibited route, or missed US reports. IIT LawIRS citizens abroadIRS foreign tax credit
