Before you decide

No personal facts were supplied with the request. Every row below states what is known from official sources, what is still missing, and exactly where to find it. A blank means the answer depends on your own records — not on an estimate. A decision needs five things that no public source can supply for you: where the money came from, whether China tax on it is paid and provable, your China residence status, which US account will receive it, and your US filing history. Answered now, against needing your records

Can be answered now, with no personal dataNeeds your records before a decisionOfficial source
  • The conversion itself is not a separate tax; the tax is on the income if it was never taxed.
  • The lawful bank wire needs a passport, proof of the amount and the tax voucher; the rule read states no cap for a passport holder.
  • The USD 50,000 a year figure is a convenience quota for domestic individuals and permanent-resident card holders.
  • Prohibited routes carry fines of up to 30%, or 30%–100% if serious; they are not cheaper alternatives.
  • Cash cannot move a large balance: USD 5,000 free, up to USD 10,000 with a permit, and nothing above that in principle.
  • The US filing thresholds and penalties are fixed by statute and by the published penalty table.

  • Whether the tax-filing form is required above USD 50,000 (D13).

  • Whether your Wise limit covers the amount (D15).
