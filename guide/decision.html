Before you decide

A decision needs five things that no public source can supply for you: where the money came from, whether China tax on it is paid and provable, your China residence status, which US account will receive it, and your US filing history. The tables below separate those from the questions the rules do answer, and say exactly how to close each gap.

What cannot be settled by research

Five things decide this case and none of them is published anywhere: where the money came from, whether the China tax on it is paid and provable, whether you are a China tax resident, which US account receives it, and whether your earlier US returns are complete. Until those are written down, any route chosen for you would be a guess. The rest of this site prices every route that is lawful once they are known.
