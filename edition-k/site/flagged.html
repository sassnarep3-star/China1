How to close an item

  • Case facts: supply residency, immigration status, the character and source of the funds, the holding bank and the destination bank. Most of the top items collapse once those exist.
  • Live prices: request the same executed amount from each provider on the same day and compare the delivered USD, not the headline rate.
  • Legal text: where two official pages disagree, the later instrument prevails, but the bank in front of you may be working from the earlier one — bring both.
