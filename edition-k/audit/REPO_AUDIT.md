## Findings

### A01 — Nine different CNY/USD rates are in circulation inside the repo

`grep -rho '6\.6[0-9]\{3\}'` over the repo returns, with counts:
`6.6929` (35), `6.6903` (21), `6.6919` (12), `6.6851` (10), `6.6571` (6), `6.6648` (5),
`6.6788` (3), `6.6762` (2), `6.6309` (1).

**Checked against first-party pages on 2026-11-10:** PBOC/CFETS central parity was
**6.7167** (S01), and BOC's own board showed **6.6571 bid / 6.6851 ask** at 17:39:58
(S02). So several archived rates no longer correspond to any quote that can be
reproduced today.

**Assessment:** not an error in any individual edition — each is a dated snapshot — but
it makes cross-edition comparison invalid, and a reader cannot tell which snapshot a
given page used unless the page says so. Edition K labels every rate with its source
and timestamp, and computes only inside a single snapshot.

**Disposition:** logged, not retro-corrected in other sessions' archives.

### A02 — The consolidated portal's all-in column is blank, and that is correct

`integration/build.py` renders `blank("All-in components unavailable")` for all-in,
while pricing BOC and HSBC sender fees. **Verified:** neither tariff publishes an FX
spread, and no first-party US receiving-bank or intermediary schedule is in the
evidence log. The blank is therefore honest, not a gap in effort.

**What Edition K adds:** a priced all-in estimate for one rail only, derived from two
published BOC quotes on one snapshot (half-spread 0.20986%) plus Chase's published $15
(S18). Intermediary fees remain blank and flagged (F07).

### A03 — Repealed Article 39 is not being cited as live law

`grep -rl 'Article 39'` returns Edition J's `REVIEW.md` (which records the repeal), plus
this edition's files. No page was found asserting the repealed
`个人外汇管理办法实施细则` Art. 39 fine schedule as current.

**Verified:** SAFE's own page states Art. 39 was deleted by Huifa [2023] No.8, appendix 3
(S05). **Disposition:** no correction needed; Edition K restates the repeal (F10) so the
finding does not silently reappear.

### A04 — Superseded 2004 staged-release wording survives in archived pages

`dossier/*.html` and `edition-b/executive-summary.html` still describe the
half / half-of-remainder / remainder release schedule.

**Verified:** SAFE Huifa [2014] No.2 §6 cancelled the staged-release requirement, the
CNY 500,000 central filing threshold and the notarisation requirement (S07). The 2004
text (S06) is still published unamended on a government site, which is why the conflict
persists.

**Disposition:** logged as F09 and flagged; the archived pages belong to other sessions
and are preserved for provenance rather than rewritten.

### A05 — BOC fee formula previously used in the repo is correct

Prior editions use `min(260, max(50, 0.1% × CNY principal)) + 150`.

**Verified against BOC's own tariff (S03):** "每笔汇款金额的1‰，最低收取50元/笔，最高收取260元/笔；
另收电讯费…非港澳台地区人民币150元/笔". The formula matches. Note 1 on the same page
warns of local variation (F04).

**Disposition:** corroborated. Edition K re-derives the same rule from the fetched text
rather than copying it.

### A06 — HSBC fee formula previously used in the repo is correct

Prior editions use `min(500, max(100, 0.1% × CNY principal)) + 120`.

**Verified against the HSBC China tariff effective 2026-10-31 (S04):** commission
"电汇金额的0.1% 每笔最低人民币100元/等值 每笔最高人民币500元/等值" plus "附加电报内容 每笔人民币120元/等值".
Additional facts recovered from the same document and not previously published: HSBC
Global Transfers between own accounts are **free for Premier** (2.2.6.1), and HSBC China
**stopped issuing foreign-currency drafts on 2019-12-01**.

**Disposition:** corroborated and extended.

### A07 — The Chase $15 receiving fee is now sourced, not assumed

Earlier root material described a $15 Chase fee as an assumption. **Verified:** Chase
publishes "$15 per transfer" for incoming domestic and international wires (S18).

**Caveat retained:** the receiving bank is still unknown for this reader, so $15 is
representative only (F08).

### A08 — No edition had priced an all-in figure for the reader's actual question

Every prior edition leaves all-in blank at each of the 40 amounts. Edition K produces a
continuous priced ladder for one rail at all 40 amounts, and explicitly does not fill
the others. This is the main substantive addition of the edition, and the method is
reproducible: `edition-k/build/build.py`.

### A09 — The "no conversion tax" conclusion is consistent across editions and now anchored

Editions and the consolidated summary state that conversion is not itself taxable.
**Verified:** the China IIT Law taxes nine enumerated categories of income and currency
exchange is not one of them (S10); 26 USC 988(e) disallows recognition of personal-
transaction FX gain up to $200 (S13).

**Residual risk:** this is an inference from the structure of the law, not an express
exemption clause. Logged as F11 and F17 rather than presented as a certainty.

### A10 — US reporting thresholds in the repo match the IRS pages

FBAR aggregate $10,000 (S11); Form 8938 $50,000/$75,000 single and $100,000/$150,000
joint for US-resident filers, with higher abroad thresholds (S12); Form 3520 above
$100,000 from a nonresident individual, and $20,573 for 2026 for purported corporate or
partnership gifts (S14).

**Verified:** all three match the fetched pages. **Disposition:** confirmed.

### A11 — The USD 50,000 figure is sometimes framed as a wall rather than a document gate

Several pages explain the annual total correctly but leave the impression that crossing
it stops the transfer. **Verified:** Huifa [2007] No.1 Art. 2 sets the annual total, and
Arts. 10/12/14 immediately describe the above-total documentation route (S05). The
consolidated portal's own language ("documented lawful current income") is consistent
with this.

**Disposition:** Edition K states the distinction explicitly on the landing page, and
flags the domestic/overseas individual classification split (F15) because the applicable
article depends on it.

### A12 — Duplication is a process finding, not a data finding

Ten editions plus four audit directories contain overlapping research produced by
concurrent sessions. No session deleted another's work, and the coordination records show
conflicts being resolved by append-only union. The cost is that a reader landing on the
root portal sees several dashboards with different snapshots.

**Disposition:** integration stays additive and labels the new edition with its date and
method. No archive was removed.
