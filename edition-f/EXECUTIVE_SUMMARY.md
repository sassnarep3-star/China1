**Report date:** 2026-11-08 UTC. **Live target:** https://sassnarep3-star.github.io/China1/edition-f/site/index.html  
**Scope:** US citizen moving CNY to USD with least tax penalty; exhaustive method inventory, conversion-penalty breakdown, $10,000–$400,000 cost ladder, mechanics, tax/reporting, and repository audit.

## 1. The answer in one paragraph

Do not treat “conversion penalty” as one tax. The reviewed PRC IIT Law does not enumerate currency exchange itself as an income category; the real lawful-transfer prices are an FX margin and issuer fees. A US citizen can still owe US tax on worldwide income and on personal foreign-currency gain above the §988(e)(2) USD 200 recognition threshold, and can face large reporting penalties even when no income tax is due. The best lawful route depends on the source of funds: documented China current-account income uses the overseas-individual Art.59 route; property sale uses the 2025 property-sale guide; emigration/inheritance use the 2004 transfer measures; genuine corporate profits/liquidation use corporate routes. The USD 50,000 annual convenience quota is not a universal outbound ceiling, and the USD 50,000/day outbound threshold is a different rule. Sources: [SAFE current guide](https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/file/file/20200831/f0127756271c43ef9b3908d5d157bd91.pdf), [PRC IIT Law](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/eng/c102962/c102967/c102997/c103004/c5245849/content.html), [26 U.S.C. §988](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2020-title26/pdf/USCODE-2020-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.pdf), [IRS FBAR/8938 comparison](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/comparison-of-form-8938-and-fbar-requirements).

## 4. Conversion penalty layers

1. **FX margin:** BOC round-trip proxy 0.4198479%; one-way vs arithmetic midpoint 0.2099239% of ask. Not a tax. [BOC board](https://www.boc.cn/sourcedb/whpj/)
2. **Issuer fees:** BOC 0.1% min ¥50/max ¥260 + ¥150 cable; HSBC Premier/Advance 0.1% min ¥100/max ¥500 + ¥120 cable; SkyRemit ¥79 + observed markup. [BOC](https://pic.bankofchina.com/bocappd/pricelist/202511/P020251110646838136627.pdf), [HSBC](https://www.hsbc.com.cn/content/dam/hsbc/cn/docs/document-download/tariff-of-accouts-and-services.pdf), [SkyRemit](https://www.skyremit.cn/)
3. **Unknown corridor costs:** correspondent/receiving fees, HSBC FX spread, ICBC/WU fees blank. [BOC](https://pic.bankofchina.com/bocappd/pricelist/202511/P020251110646838136627.pdf), [HSBC](https://www.hsbc.com.cn/content/dam/hsbc/cn/docs/document-download/tariff-of-accouts-and-services.pdf), [ICBC](https://www.icbc.com.cn/column/1438058326864052304.html), [WU](https://www.westernunion.com/cn/en/send-money.html)
4. **Underlying PRC tax:** comprehensive 3–45%, business 5–35%, interest/dividends/property transfer/lease/incidental 20%; savings-deposit interest temporarily exempt. [IIT Law](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/eng/c102962/c102967/c102997/c103004/c5245849/content.html), [财税〔2008〕132号](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c102416/c5203286/content.html)
5. **US tax:** worldwide income; FEIE/FTC; personal FX gain >$200 recognition threshold; character conflict (§988 ordinary vs Pub 525 capital). [§988](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2020-title26/pdf/USCODE-2020-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapN-partIII-subpartJ-sec988.pdf), [Pub 525](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p525), [FTC](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-tax-credit), [FEIE](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/foreign-earned-income-exclusion)
6. **Reporting penalties:** FBAR >$10k aggregate; 8938 thresholds; 3520 gifts >$100k; FinCEN 105 cash >$10k. Current adjusted FBAR dollars blank. [IRS comparison](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/comparison-of-form-8938-and-fbar-requirements), [IRM](https://www.irs.gov/irm/part4/irm_04-026-016), [31 USC 5321](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title31/html/USCODE-2024-title31-subtitleIV-chap53-subchapII-sec5321.htm), [CBP](https://www.cbp.gov/travel/international-visitors/money-monetary-instruments)
7. **Unlaw-route fines:** up to 30%, serious 30–100% for evasion/illegal arbitrage/private dealing; crypto notice has no fixed percentage. [State Council Reg](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2008-08/06/content_2602514.htm), [PBOC crypto notice](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/tiaofasi/144941/3581332/4348658/index.html)
