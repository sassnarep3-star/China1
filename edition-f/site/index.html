Bottom line. For documented China salary, the Art.59 bank route is the strongest text-based path and can exceed USD 50,000; for small amounts SkyRemit’s captured scenario can be cheaper than BOC’s capped wire fee, while BOC’s capped fee can win at larger amounts. None of that is a personal tax opinion or an executable quote. Resolve classification, source documents and US reporting first.
