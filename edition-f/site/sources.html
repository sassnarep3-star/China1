CN01
official		经常项目外汇业务指引（2020年版） / Current-Account FX Business Guide (2020)
State Administration of Foreign Exchange		2026-11-08Read both retrieved chunks. Arts.54–67 and 79 are the operative individual-FX provisions.
  • Art.54: annual convenience quota USD 50,000 for individual settlement and domestic-individual purchase.
  • Art.55–57: ID-only inside quota; domestic individual completes purchase application.
  • Art.58: overseas individual settlement above USD 50,000 pays counterparty RMB account directly.
  • Art.59: overseas individual lawful current-account RMB income purchase with ID and source materials including tax vouchers; no annual cap stated; permanent-residence-card holders use quota.
  • Art.60: reconversion of unused RMB against original exchange memo within 24 months; small daily reconversion tiers.
  • Art.61: close-relative proxy; Art.62 splitting leads to watch list for current year plus two years.
  • Art.64: domestic individual outbound FX from FX account USD 50,000/day on ID, above with documents; cash USD 10,000/day.
  • Art.65: overseas individual FX-account outbound on ID.
  • Art.66: e-commerce/market-procurement/border-trade settlement outside convenience quota with evidence.
  • Art.67: domestic FX transfers only to self or close relatives.
  • Art.79: overseas-account witnessing requires foreign visa valid at least three months.
PDF is the 2020 instrument; amendment status is tracked separately at CN02. Branch practice can require more documents than the minimum text.open source
