US reporting: FBAR, 8938, 3520 and cash declarations

The reporting obligations that survive even when the transfer itself produces no income tax.

Reporting is not tax. A transfer can be tax-free and still reportable; a reportable account can produce no taxable income. The largest quantified exposure in this problem is usually non-reporting, not the FX spread.

Practical filing sequence

  1. Inventory every foreign financial account and maximum aggregate value during the year; test FBAR > USD 10,000.
  2. Test Form 8938 against residence/filing-status thresholds; attach to the return if required.
  3. If a foreign individual/estate gift or bequest exceeds USD 100,000 aggregate, test Form 3520.
  4. If carrying > USD 10,000 cash/monetary instruments across the US border, file FinCEN 105; family aggregate applies.
  5. For prior-year gaps, obtain professional advice before amended filings or streamlined procedures; do not assume penalty relief.
