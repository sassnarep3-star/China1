Known limitations

  • BOC board is reference-only and displays a date later than the report date; re-quote before execution.
  • SkyRemit widget was internally inconsistent; only the rate pair and fixed fee are used in a labelled scenario.
  • Wise pricing changed during research and only one amount was captured; no curve is extrapolated.
  • Capital Guide 2024 PDF retrieval did not return every corporate checklist section; corporate steps are framework-level and flagged.
  • Personal tax, eligibility and prior filing history are unknown; no personal liability amount is computed.

Research with linked sources and shown arithmetic. Not legal, tax or investment advice, and not a bank quote. Provider tariffs support only that provider’s prices/product terms; legal propositions cite government/regulator sources. Unknown values are blank and flagged.
