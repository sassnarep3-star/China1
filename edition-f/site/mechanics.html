Step-by-step mechanics

Published documentary sequences for lawful/conditional routes. Prohibited routes are named and penalised, not instructed.

Prohibited and unverified wrappers receive no operational instructions. “Exact” mechanics below are the published documentary sequence, not a guarantee that a branch will approve a particular account.

M01 · Domestic-individual convenience quota purchase + bank wire conditional

Fit: Account classified as 境内个人 (PRC ID); genuine current-account purpose; unused annual quota.

Limit: USD 50,000 equivalent per person per calendar year for purchase inside the convenience quota; above quota needs authenticity documents or another route. Outbound remittance from an existing FX account has a separate USD 50,000/day ID-only threshold.

Cost: BOC and HSBC local wire charges are tariff-derived in the cost ladder; FX spread is a BOC board proxy only; personal tax blank.

M02 · Overseas-individual documented current-account income purchase + remittance (Art.59) conditional

Cost: Same bank tariffs as M01; no transfer tax identified on the exchange itself; underlying IIT and US tax depend on facts.
