Cost ladder: $10,000 to $400,000

Forty exact USD targets. Tariff-derived local charges and a labelled BOC spread proxy are computed; provider scenarios are single-capture; unknown fees and personal tax are blank.

Scenario, not permission or quote. Amounts above USD 50,000 are not automatically available under the domestic convenience quota. The ladder shows arithmetic if a lawful documented route exists. BOC board date mismatch: F04. Unknown all-in fees and personal tax remain blank: F09 F10 F11.

Formulas

  • BOC CNY principal = target USD × 6.6929 (ask 669.29 per 100 USD).
  • BOC local fee = clamp(0.001 × CNY principal, ¥50, ¥260) + ¥150 cable; USD equivalent = fee ÷ 6.6929.
  • BOC one-way spread proxy = target USD × (ask − midpoint) ÷ ask, where midpoint = (6.6929 + 6.6648)/2. Round-trip proxy = (ask − bid)/ask = 0.4198479%.
  • HSBC Premier/Advance local fee = clamp(0.001 × CNY principal, ¥100, ¥500) + ¥120 cable. HSBC FX spread/total blank.
  • SkyRemit principal = target USD ÷ 0.149496; transfers = ceil(principal ÷ 300,000); fee = ¥79 × transfers; FX loss = principal × (0.149900 − 0.149496); fee USD = fee CNY × 0.149496.
